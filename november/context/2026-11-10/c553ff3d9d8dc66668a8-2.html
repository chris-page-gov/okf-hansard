Turn 10 · Lord Katz (Lab)

I am not entirely sure it would be fair to say what the noble Lord was implying. It is the case that the Government, from the Prime Minister downwards, take this issue of wildfires very seriously. In terms of response, firefighters on the front line will be boosted by additional specialist wildfire teams and state-of-the-art wildfire-specific personal protective equipment, backed up by £45 million. Also, the RAF’s Chinook helicopters and A400M aircraft will be equipped so that they can join in the fight against wildfires and be ready when requested to help crews across the UK battle blazes where they find them.

Turn 12 · Lord Katz (Lab)

I thank the noble Baroness for her question and, more importantly, for her work in this area and her long-term commitment to ensuring that we have stronger resilience. To be clear, with regard to the ongoing work of the Government in reforming planning guidance and promoting the building of more houses—particularly social houses—it is absolutely right that we set the right framework for a multitude of resilience factors, whether it is around energy and water conservation or resilience to flood. That is why the Government continue to work with our industry stakeholders, particularly the building sector, to make sure we have the skills, the materials and the know-how so that we have resilient properties in the future.

Turn 14 · Lord Katz (Lab)

I am tempted to say that we should all do a dance, but that would be facetious. I am afraid that I do not have the exact data that the noble Baroness requested in front of me, so I will be more than happy to write to her in reply. This is important. As we discussed yesterday on the question the noble Baroness referred to about drought and water levels, we take both a long and a short-term approach with the water companies. We make sure they are investing, and we have this record level of £104 billion in investment to provide greater reservoir capacity with nine new reservoirs. They also spend their time, money and attention on fixing the problems of water leakage and pipe leakage now. It is so important that we start at the source to conserve water if, as the noble Baroness says, putting issues such as El Niño to one side, we do not have the water fall that we are expecting.
