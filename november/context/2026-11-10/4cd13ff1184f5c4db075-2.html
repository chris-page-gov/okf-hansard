Turn 10 · Speaker unlabelled

To conclude, the support for the International Claims Commission for Ukraine ensures that that those who have suffered are compensated. We have stood with Ukraine throughout Russia’s illegal invasion. We will continue to stand with the Ukrainian people as they defend their country and help build a secure, sovereign and prosperous future. Through the commission, we can strengthen our support for Ukraine, work hand in hand with the international community to advance accountability and help ensure that Russia ultimately pays for the damage it has caused, laying the foundations for a just and lasting peace. I commend the order to the Committee.
