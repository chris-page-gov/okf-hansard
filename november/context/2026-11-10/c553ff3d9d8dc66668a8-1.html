Turn 4 · Lord in Waiting/Government Whip (Lord Katz) (Lab)

My Lords, the Climate Change Committee’s fourth independent assessment of UK climate change risk sets out a worsening picture of climate change impacts. We are committed to improving the nation’s resilience to climate change. That is why we have set out our intention to develop specific, measurable objectives and detailed delivery plans for the fourth national adaptation programme in 2028, in line with the two degrees centigrade of warming by 2050, as advised by the Climate Change Committee.

Turn 6 · Lord Katz (Lab)

My Lords, my noble friend is absolutely right in his analysis. The Government agree that the third national adaptation programme, which was published in 2023, was weak, particularly in its lack of clear targets and limited co-ordination across government departments. For the fourth iteration of the adaptation programme, we are putting in place a stronger framework, built on specific, measurable objectives, which will prepare this country for at least two degrees’ centigrade of warming by 2050. This Government are taking a more comprehensive, joined-up and strategic approach to climate adaptation, based on co-ordinating across systems and outcomes rather than simply addressing isolated risks. We have robust governance in place to ensure that departments are fully engaged at all levels to deliver on climate adaptation.
