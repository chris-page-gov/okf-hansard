Turn 4 · The Lord Privy Seal (Baroness Smith of Basildon) (Lab)

My Lords, it is a privilege to open today’s debate on this short, straightforward but important Bill, which comes to this House following its passage, with cross-party support, in the other place. The purpose of the Bill is simple: it removes historic legal barriers that prevent individuals who profess either the Roman Catholic or Jewish faiths advising the sovereign on certain Church appointments or making such appointments in their own right where their office gives them that function. It would be helpful to look at the historical context, because I know this House has a deep appreciation for our constitutional history.
