Bovine TB Control Strategy for England: Government Response

Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Dame Angela Eagle)

I wish to update the House regarding the Government’s response to the co-designed bovine TB control strategy for England, which was published on 5 November 2026. For too many farmers and their families, bovine TB has brought uncertainty, stress and financial hardship. I have enormous admiration for the resilience shown by those affected. This Government believe that no farming family should have to accept this disease as an unavoidable fact of life and livelihood. This marks an important step towards making that vision a reality. The co-designed strategy published in June this year sets out a long-term vision for England to achieve officially bovine TB-free status by 2038, and the actions needed to get there.
