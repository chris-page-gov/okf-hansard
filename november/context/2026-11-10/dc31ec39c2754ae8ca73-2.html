Turn 10 · Speaker unlabelled

Once more, I hope my friend the noble Viscount will forgive me—I hope to spare his blushes—but his father gave an important lecture, not in 2026 but in 1976, contrasting an elective dictatorship with the constitutional democracy that we all aspire to. Henry VIII powers over matters of such importance as this are a manifestation of an elective dictatorship, not the democracy to which we all aspire.

Turn 13 · Lord Keen of Elie (Con)

My Lords, it occurs to me that the Minister may have missed his vocation: he is able to advance an unstatable case with at least a scintilla of conviction. We cannot accept the explanation advanced. I note the support across the Committee for removing Part 2 of Schedule 1. The suggestion that it might be required to introduce further new laws unknown at the present time was met eloquently by the noble Baroness, Lady Chakrabarti, who pointed out that such laws would almost certainly be introduced by way of primary legislation anyway. I make it clear that we will be returning to this on Report. Meanwhile, I beg leave to withdraw the amendment.
