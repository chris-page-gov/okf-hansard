Turn 5 · Speaker unlabelled

This instrument adds a further 10 enactments to the list of relevant enactments. This legislation relates to areas such as debt issuance, company law payment services and rehabilitation of offenders. We have identified this legislation through our work to prepare for issuing the Government’s digital gilt instrument, or DIGIT. These additions will allow the digital securities sandbox, the first FMI sandbox, to operate more effectively and provide the legislative flexibility needed to enable DIGIT to be issued through a platform operating within the digital securities sandbox.

Turn 6 · Speaker unlabelled

This instrument does not modify the enactments listed in this SI. It brings them within the scope of the existing FMI sandbox powers so that necessary modifications to the digital securities sandbox can be made through the sandbox framework. These modifications will be done via further negative SIs, to be laid subsequent to these regulations being made. These will be targeted changes that will help the digital securities sandbox to fulfil its intended purpose and support the delivery of DIGIT.
