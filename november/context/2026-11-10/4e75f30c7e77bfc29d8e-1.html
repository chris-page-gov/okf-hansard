Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Transport (Keir Mather)

My noble Friend the Minister of State for Transport (Lord Hendy of Richmond Hill) made the following ministerial statement on 9 November 2026. I am pleased to inform the House that today the Government have laid an amendment to the Railways Bill to ensure that discounts for veterans and service personnel are protected in law. Veterans and service personnel currently benefit from railcards that provide one third off most rail fares, but until today have had no statutory protection for this discount. It is vital that those who give so much for our country have their service and sacrifice recognised, and that is exactly what we are doing by safeguarding these discounts in legislation.
