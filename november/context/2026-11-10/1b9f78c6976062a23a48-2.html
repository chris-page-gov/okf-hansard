Turn 9 · Baroness Kramer (LD)

My Lords, the OBR has forecast that, by 2031, the Government’s payment on annual debt interest will rise to a staggering £160 billion. Part of the reason for that very high number is that the Government remain, through the Treasury and the Debt Management Office, absolutely addicted to issuing index-linked bonds, which pay not a fixed coupon but a coupon based on the level of inflation and, even worse, on RPI. No other G7 country does this general idiocy and the pension industry, which once benefited from such bonds, no longer uses them. Can the Minister please explain why the Treasury and the Debt Management Office, which is part of the Treasury, continue with this addiction?
