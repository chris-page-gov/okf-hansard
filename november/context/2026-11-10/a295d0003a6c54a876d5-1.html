Publishing Pay Information in Job Adverts

Commons · 2026-11-10 · Turns 1–1 of 1 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 1 · The Minister for Women and Equalities (Bridget Phillipson)

I have announced the Government’s intention to introduce a new statutory requirement for employers to publish pay information in job advertisements when parliamentary time allows. In the absence of a job advert, the employer must give the candidate this information in writing before the job interview. This announcement follows our consultation on equal pay and pay discrimination, which ran from 14 July to 27 October. Early findings show overwhelming backing, with 94% overall support and 93% of surveyed employers in favour. This measure builds on that momentum, turning existing good practice into standard practice nationwide. Publishing pay information is a small change that removes an everyday frustration felt by jobseekers and is part of the Government’s broader plans to tackle pay discrimination and help get more young people into work.
