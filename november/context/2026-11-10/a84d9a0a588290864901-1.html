Turn 4 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (Baroness Curran) (Lab)

My Lords, it is a pleasure to be with you this afternoon. At its heart, EN-8 is about preparing for a transformative new energy source. Fusion offers the prospect of abundant, low-carbon, safe energy. It has the potential to strengthen our long-term energy security, support economic growth and position the United Kingdom at the forefront of the emerging global industry. Fusion is already supporting UK jobs, supply chains and innovation. Efforts to commercialise it will enable us to capture a significant proportion of the emerging global market. This debate is not about what specific projects should look like or where they should be located. Rather, it is about ensuring that our planning system is ready for the significant opportunities that future fusion energy projects may bring to local areas and to the UK as a whole. Turn 5 · Lord Khan of Burnley (Lab)

My Lords, I welcome the publication of the Draft National Policy Statement for Fusion Energy EN-8. We stand at a critical juncture in our transition to a clean energy future. Fusion energy offers the extraordinary promise of near-limitless low-carbon power without the long-lived high-level radioactive waste associated with traditional nuclear fission.
