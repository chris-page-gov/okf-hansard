Turn 1 · The Minister for Public Health and Patient Safety (Dame Diana Johnson)

On 9 November, the Prime Minister and the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care announced that £40 million of funding will be reinvested in general practice to help GPs proactively identify and support up to 300,000 vulnerable patients this winter. This ringfenced funding will help keep people well at home, prevent serious illness and reduce avoidable hospital admissions as we strengthen preparations for extra pressure on the NHS over winter. Selected patients with conditions including heart disease, frailty or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease will receive additional support from their GP tailored to their health needs, such as medication reviews, health checks and falls prevention support.
