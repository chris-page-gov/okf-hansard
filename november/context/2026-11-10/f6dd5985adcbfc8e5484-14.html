Turn 106 · Lord Phillips of Worth Matravers (CB)

My Lords, perhaps I may add a word in support of this amendment. Most who support the right to trial by jury do so with regard to the interests of the defendant. That is implicit in the term “right to jury trial”. The right protects the defendant from the politically motivated prosecution or the judge who favours the prosecution. The Bill proposes removing the right to jury trial in the case of fraud trials and other trials that are going to be lengthy and complex. It does so because of the procedural disadvantages that flow from having to present to the jury a case that involves complex financial transactions that may well fall wholly outside their experience. Those disadvantages are also felt by the defendant. A trial that goes on month after month is a nightmare for them.
