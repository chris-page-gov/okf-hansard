Turn 4 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Department of Health and Social Care (Baroness Merron) (Lab)

My Lords, the success of neighbourhood health will be measured against the national goals in the neighbourhood health framework, including improved health outcomes and better urgent and emergency care performance. Early local reported evidence from neighbourhood health approaches is encouraging, including reported reductions in emergency department use and in emergency admissions. The learning from the 43-place national neighbourhood health implementation programme is informing wider implementation, alongside an independent national evaluation commissioned through NIHR.

Turn 5 · Lord Lansley (Con)

I am grateful for that response from the Minister. Can I ask about the example of heart valve disease, which affects one in 15 people over the age of 75? Of those, half go undiagnosed, which can lead to severe illness, hospitalisation, expensive treatment and, unfortunately, avoidable early mortality. We have digital diagnostics available in the community now which, in the hands of GPs and pharmacists, can enable much of that lack of diagnosis to be met and therefore preventive treatment to be successfully applied. What are the Government doing to enable the spread of diagnostics into the community, which is vital in that transition from hospital to community, from treatment to prevention and from analogue to digital?

Turn 8 · Baroness Merron (Lab)

It is true that general practice is a cornerstone of neighbourhood health. I am glad that people will now find it easier to access their doctor and have more choice in how and when they book appointments. With reference to digital access, I believe that will greatly help in accessibility to GPs, but we are improving capacity. We are employing more GPs; there are now 2,800 more fully qualified full-time equivalent GPs employed compared with July 2024. That is the highest number since 2015. We have also made changes to the GP contract so that PCNs and practices can hire more GPs and introduced a practice-level GP scheme. We are focusing our efforts on the more under-doctored areas that my noble friend describes.
