Turn 6 · Speaker unlabelled

1: Clause 1, page 3, line 10, at end insert— “(4A) In section 19 (decision as to allocation), after subsection (3), insert—“(3A) If the accused would likely receive a sentence of imprisonment of more than 12 months but not more than three years, is of good character, and has not previously been convicted of an offence, the court must give greater weight to the representations made by the accused under subsection (2)(b) above.””Member’s explanatory statement This amendment seeks to ensure that defendants of good character, without previous convictions, have their representations as to allocation given greater weight, so they are not denied the Crown Court trial a defendant of poorer character with previous convictions would receive.
