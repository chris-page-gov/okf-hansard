Turn 19 · Speaker unlabelled

I also thank the noble Lord, Lord Bruce, for his comments about the Council of Europe; I completely agree. I and the Government are strong supporters of the Council of Europe, as well as the OSCE and other multilateral institutions, some of which have Russia at the table, interestingly. It is an important part of security. I have to say that we have had a decade or so in which multilateral institutions have not had a very fair wind or a very strong influence on some of the major crises. We look to play a part in restoring that, and the Council of Europe’s strong work is an important part of that.

Turn 20 · Speaker unlabelled

I think I have answered some of the cost questions from the noble Earl, Lord Courtown, but I recap that negligible costs are involved in the setting up of the commission—or, rather, that this SI does not necessarily involve costs of any magnitude because the commission is not based in the UK and its privileges and immunities will be managed through existing resources. The question of the administrative costs will be decided by member states down the line.

Turn 21 · Speaker unlabelled

I hope that has covered everything. I appreciate the keen interest that noble Lords have in this claims commission and the cross-party consensus on it. Approval of this draft order is an important part of completing the domestic process before we can ratify the convention. When we do so, we will be better positioned to support Ukraine and stand with our partners in the pursuit of accountability for Russia’s unlawful and brutal invasion in February 2022. I therefore trust that the Committee will support the order.
