Turn 17 · Lord Kakkar (CB)

My Lords, I draw attention to my declared interest as chairman of King’s Health Partners. Section 131 of the 2006 NHS Act requires NHS England to ensure that it has been properly professionally informed in determining how to provide diagnostic, preventive and treatment health services and public health services. It is proposed that this obligation will no longer apply to the Secretary of State when NHS England is abolished. How do His Majesty’s Government propose to ensure that proper, clinically informed information is available in determining the provision of local health services in the future?

Turn 20 · Baroness Merron (Lab)

My noble friend is quite right that expanding the availability of NHS vaccinations through community pharmacy is very much the way forward. That is why in the last flu season we enabled the provision of flu vaccinations through pharmacists to two and three year-olds. It is why in this flu season we are extending that still further, to children and young people in clinical risk groups and for catch-up vaccinations. We have also piloted delivery of the RSV vaccination for older adults and pregnant women, which my noble friend has campaigned on very successfully.
