My Lords, this Order was laid before the House on 14 September 2026 and provides for the continuation of the Secretary of State’s powers under the Terrorism Prevention and Investigation Measures Act 2011, also known as the TPIM powers. The continuation is for a further five years. I think all noble Lords accept that it is the first duty of government to protect the public, and central to that mission is to counter the threat we face from terrorism—a threat which, sadly, is enduring and evolving. My Lords, the Official Opposition support this order. The Terrorism Prevention and Investigation Measures Act 2011 gives the Home Secretary powers that are needed in the small number of cases where the threat is real, but prosecution is not possible.
