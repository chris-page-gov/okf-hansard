Single Living Accommodation: Independent Review

Turn 1 · The Minister for Veterans and People (Mr Calvin Bailey)

The Government recognise that for too long, military accommodation has fallen short of the standards our service people expect and deserve. This Government are committed to making this right, and last year, we published the Defence housing strategy to deliver a generational renewal to fix family housing. We also recognised the need to fundamentally reform accommodation for single and unaccompanied personnel. That is why, in January, the Government commissioned an independent review into single living accommodation, led by Natalie Elphicke Ross OBE. I am pleased to announce that, today, Natalie has published the SLA review report. The Government welcome the review and its recommendations. The Government will consider all 19 recommendations in more detail before setting out a full response in the coming months.
