Turn 34 · Speaker unlabelled

That flexibility is important. Magistrates’ courts’ sentencing powers have previously been varied in response to changing pressures. Amendment 33 would restrict sentencing powers to their current limits. Amendment 35 would go beyond removing the power to increase magistrates’ sentencing powers to 18 or 24 months. It would therefore leave the current 12-month limit permanently in place and remove the ability to vary those powers by regulations altogether, meaning that any future change, whether to increase or reduce sentencing powers, would require primary legislation.
