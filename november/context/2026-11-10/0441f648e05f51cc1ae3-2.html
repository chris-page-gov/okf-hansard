Turn 12 · Baroness Dacres of Lewisham (Lab)

I thank my noble friend because she raises an important issue of equity and fairness. We recognise that not all drivers have access to home charging. If you live in an apartment block and do not have a drive, you cannot just park outside your home and plug in. We are supporting local authorities with funding to install on-street parking. We want to make sure that it is accessible, which is why we are providing a further investment of £100 million over the next two years so that on-street public chargers are available and accessible. We are also looking into tariff porting to enable those who use public charging points to get the best and most affordable prices they can.
