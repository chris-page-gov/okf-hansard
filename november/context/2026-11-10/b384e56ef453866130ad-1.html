Turn 8 · Dr Mullan

It is obvious that the Minister does not want to put on record and own up to the fact that those convicted of coercive and controlling behaviour are, according to our understanding, eligible for early release. We know the patterns that emerge over time. Surely we should tackle this at the first instance and not let these people out early.
