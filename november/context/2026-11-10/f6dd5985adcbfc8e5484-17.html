Turn 134 · Speaker unlabelled

Let me turn to Amendments 13A, 13B and 15A, for which I thank the noble Lord, Lord Marks. These amendments seek to ensure that only cases of appropriate complexity and length qualify for a “judge alone” trial. Amendment 15A would require the defence and prosecution to agree that the estimated trial length is at least five months before a decision for a “judge alone” trial could be made. The Government do not consider that a fixed five-month threshold is appropriate. Such a threshold would arbitrarily restrict the operation of the policy and could exclude cases that are exceptionally burdensome for jurors simply because their estimated length falls slightly below that figure.
