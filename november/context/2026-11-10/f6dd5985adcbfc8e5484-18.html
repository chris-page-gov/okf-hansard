Turn 138 · Speaker unlabelled

I turn to Amendment 31, which would limit the offences that could be added to Schedule 1 to specified regulatory offences. I thank the noble and learned Lord, Lord Thomas of Cwmgiedd, for this amendment and his discussion of it with me yesterday. I know there are broader concerns across the Committee about the existence of this power, and we will have the opportunity to discuss that in full in the next group. For now, let me say this. The purpose of this power is to ensure that the system can keep pace as technological developments continue to change the nature of fraud and financial crime. Restricting the power to existing fraud and regulatory offences would limit the ability to respond to as yet unforeseen developments—for example, new offences enabled by the powerful capabilities of AI. We will return to this in the next group.
