Ebola: Medical Evacuation from DRC of a British Citizen

Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Health and Social Care (Yvette Cooper)

I wish to update the House on a British citizen who is receiving treatment for Ebola at the Royal Free hospital in London. The thoughts of the whole Government are with the patient, their family and colleagues at this difficult time. The patient is a British national and medical doctor who was working at an Ebola treatment centre in the Democratic Republic of the Congo when they contracted the disease. The individual’s employer arranged for their medical evacuation in collaboration with the World Health Organisation, and the Department of Health and Social Care and the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office supported international and commercial partners to effect their safe transfer to the UK.
