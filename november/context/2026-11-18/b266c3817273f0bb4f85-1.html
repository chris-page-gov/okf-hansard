Turn 2 · Henry Tufnell (Mid and South Pembrokeshire) (Lab)

I beg to move, That this House has considered telecommunications connectivity in rural areas. It is a pleasure to serve under your chairmanship, Mr Turner. Reliable broadband and mobile signal are not luxuries; they are essential for work, education, public services and everyday life. Yet in many rural communities, such as those in my constituency, such connectivity is still lacking. Analysis of Ofcom data reveals that mine ranks 444th out of 650 constituencies in the UK for mobile service quality. Just 1% of premises in my constituency have high-quality 5G stand-alone signal, compared with 86% nationally. Those numbers are staggering and the consequences of such poor connectivity are felt every day by my constituents.

Turn 8 · Henry Tufnell

The hon. Member is right. We too often face that rural premium, where rural areas get left behind and forgotten about while those upgrades happen in urban areas. That is not acceptable for her constituents or mine. I look forward to the publication later this year of the Government’s mobile market review, which will assess whether the current market structure is delivering the investment needed to improve mobile connectivity. For communities such as mine in Pembrokeshire, where coverage remains among the worst in the country, the test of that review must be whether it leads to faster investment, better coverage and an end to rural areas being left behind.
