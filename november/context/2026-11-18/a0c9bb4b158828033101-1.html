Turn 6 · Lord Collins of Highbury (Lab)

We are pursuing every available option to deport Shabir Ahmed, and, as I say, it would be inappropriate to give a running commentary on those discussions. Since coming to power, this Government have returned nearly 12,000 foreign national offenders between 1 July 2024 and 31 July 2026. That figure includes both enforced and voluntary returns. Compared with the final two years of the previous Government, foreign national offender returns have increased by 41%.

Turn 7 · Lord Mohammed of Tinsley (LD)

My Lords, I deplore the behaviour of Shabir Ahmed and I wish he was not among us in this country. However, I want to press on the Minister a word of caution. It is easy as a soundbite to say that we want to withdraw international aid, but many of the people who benefit from our international aid in Pakistan or anywhere else are women and girls who gain access to education or healthcare. If the Government are minded to withdraw international aid, will they at least publish what the impact will be? Having seen Shabir Ahmed destroy the lives of women and girls in this country, I do not want him to now go on to destroy the lives of tens of millions of girls in Pakistan.
