Turn 4 · Baroness in Waiting/Government Whip (Baroness Hyde of Bemerton) (Lab)

Education and employment are vital to rehabilitation, each reducing reoffending by up to nine percentage points. Prison education, together with other work-focused activities, builds skills for life including reading, numeracy and work-focused training. Employment partnerships and employer-led skills training programmes develop the skills needed for sustainable work on release. The Government are undertaking a full evaluation of a new prisoner education service and continue to monitor delivery outcomes across education, skills and employment provision to inform future development.
