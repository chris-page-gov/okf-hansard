Turn 166 · Speaker unlabelled

If my noble friend he needs another solution, I believe there is one in France. We all remember in our youth how many strikes a year one used to read about on the French railways—it is much better now. The solution that SNCF and the unions found there was to get an agreement that, if you were going to withdraw your labour, you had to keep one train in three running.
