Turn 2 · Tom Hayes (Bournemouth East) (Lab)

I beg to move, That leave be given to bring in a Bill to make provision about park homes. Residential home parks offer a different way of living: a peaceful setting and beautiful surroundings; usually a garden to call your own; and often a lovely community atmosphere. Around 160,000 people live in mobile homes in England—that is about one and a half times the population of my constituency—but because they are spread across 1,800-plus sites, their needs have barely been spoken about in this Parliament, and they have been exploited and let down by an outdated system in desperate need of reform that is mostly on the side of rogue operators. Residents are being forced to live in unsafe conditions and put up with non-stop non-compliance while enforcement proceedings take too many years to run their course. Legal responsibilities ignored, safety responsibilities ignored, prosecutions ignored, …
