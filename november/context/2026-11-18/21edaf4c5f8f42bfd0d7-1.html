Turn 2 · The Solicitor General (Andy Slaughter)

The Government inherited a justice system on the brink. The record backlogs mean that victims are facing devastating delays for justice. The Government continue to invest in our courts, but vital reforms are needed, too. The Prime Minister and the Justice Secretary have rightly listened to the strength of feeling from victims, the sector and the House and have decided not to establish the Crown court bench division, but we will now act with urgency to implement the rest of our vital reforms, delivering swifter justice for victims with an urgency that was wholly absent from the words and actions of the Conservative party.
