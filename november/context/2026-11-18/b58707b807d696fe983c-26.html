Turn 201 · Sir Bernard Jenkin

We have had this idea that somehow the EU is just the same as NATO or any other international organisation all the way through these debates for decades, and I am afraid that it is based on a very fundamental ignorance. The EU is completely different to any other international organisation. It makes laws that are binding on the member states, whether they like it or not, and its courts overrule the courts of the member states. We are putting ourselves in that position through dynamic alignment, which is why it is a breach of the mandate given to the Government. There is nothing about that in their manifesto, and it is a breach of their manifesto commitments.
