Turn 58 · Helen Morgan (North Shropshire) (LD) · Question 902142

Q13. The e-MERLIN network is the UK’s national radio astronomy facility. Its network of radio telescopes stretches from the Knockin telescope in my constituency of North Shropshire all the way to south Cambridge. Its headquarters are at Jodrell Bank, which has the grade I listed Lovell telescope. I was recently privileged to visit it. Its high-resolution radio imaging is world leading. Sovereign capability in radio astronomy is critical for the UK scientific community, and for national security, given the network’s tracking and observatory capabilities; once lost, they will be lost forever. The network is set to lose its funding in 2028. It would be reckless to lose this capability in an increasingly uncertain world, and it would be vandalism to allow the Lovell telescope to fall into disrepair, so will the Prime Minister commit to finding the money to save it?

Q15. Nearly 10 years ago, brave young women at Oxford University told me that they had been sexually assaulted in their colleges, only to then be silenced by gagging orders supposedly protecting them.
