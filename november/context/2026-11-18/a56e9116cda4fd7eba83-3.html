Turn 21 · The Prime Minister

I certainly pay tribute to all those organisations, and I look forward to welcoming Andy’s Man Club into 10 Downing Street later today. We know that suicide is the leading cause of death for men under 50, and we know that the sessions that those organisations put on make all the difference, connecting men of all backgrounds so that they can talk openly in a way that they could not do in the family home or at work. We are launching the first ever men’s health strategy, which will put more funding into the kind of projects that my hon. Friend has mentioned. May I also compliment him on his Movember efforts? He has 12 days to go—it might even turn into a moustache at some point. [Laughter.]
