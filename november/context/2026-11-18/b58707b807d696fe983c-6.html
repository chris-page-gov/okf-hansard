Turn 48 · Sir Edward Leigh

The Minister is obviously totally committed to the European project, which he is entitled to be. Many people view this reset as the opening move in rejoining the EU. He is my constituency neighbour in Brexit-voting Lincolnshire. I know he will say that he stands by the Government’s red lines, but what is his personal view of the long-term future? He is a Europe Minister and I would like to know whether he believes, as the Prime Minister does, that it would be in our national interest to rejoin the EU eventually. He is completely honest, and we are going to have an honest debate, so does he think that people will have to accept no opt-outs or rebates, and a full commitment, treaty-wise, to Schengen and the euro? Will he give his own personal view, please?
