Turn 12 · Chris Murray

The hon. Lady is quite right; we need to pursue water companies when they try to find workarounds. But I would still much rather have a Government willing to try than an SNP Government who do not even lift a finger. I understand that the hon. Lady is not arguing that this is a panacea solution, but the experience from the Edinburgh and East Lothian area, which is in a housing crisis, is that adding water companies to the planning system has just delayed the planning and lengthened the time it takes to get houses built, and has not had an impact on sewage. What does make a difference is monitoring—we have 100% monitoring in England but only 30% monitoring in Scotland—and investment in infrastructure. I am desperate for a Government to step up and grip the sewage crisis in Scotland. I am just jealous of my friends in England and Wales for having Governments willing to do that.
