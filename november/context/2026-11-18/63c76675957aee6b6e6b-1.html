Turn 7 · Speaker unlabelled

These government amendments do not alter the Bill’s overall purpose. Rather, they allow the framework to be expanded to a wider group of significant recurrent sporting events with international significance in a proportionate way. They also strengthen the framework’s operation, improve safeguards for small businesses, support UK-wide implementation and provide greater legal certainty for those affected by the legislation. I beg to move.
