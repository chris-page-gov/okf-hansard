Stormont Brake Notification: Union Customs Code

Commons · 2026-11-18 · Turns 1–1 of 1 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Northern Ireland (Chris Bryant)

On 6 November 2026, the Speaker of the Northern Ireland Assembly provided to me a notification signed by 34 Members of the Assembly under schedule 6B to the Northern Ireland Act 1998, seeking to prevent reforms to the Union Customs Code from applying. The democratic scrutiny provisions in schedule 6B, commonly referred to as the Stormont brake, place important legal obligations on me as the Secretary of State. The Government take these legal obligations seriously, noting that they are designed to enable MLAs to raise concerns where a regulation would have a significant impact on everyday life in Northern Ireland. I am grateful for the consideration of the issues that Members of the Assembly gave in the notification and would also like to express my appreciation in similar terms to the Windsor Framework Democratic Scrutiny Committee and its members for their prompt conduct of the inquiry …
