Turn 8 · David Chadwick

What really matters in Wales is that the potential cut to community hospital services will have a knock-on impact in other areas of the Union, most obviously England. Lots of people from Powys rely on hospitals in Herefordshire and Shropshire because we do not have a general hospital of our own. Many people who rely on their community hospital do not stay overnight; they go there for blood tests, X-rays or to use the attached minor injuries unit. The ability to access those services in the community is a lifeline for many, particularly those who need regular blood tests and rely on an infrequent bus or a lift from a neighbour to get to the nearest general hospital, which for many people in Powys is in Herefordshire or Shropshire. The removal of these services on the Welsh side of the border will lead to greater demand on the English side.
