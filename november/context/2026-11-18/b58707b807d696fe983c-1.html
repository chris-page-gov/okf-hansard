Turn 8 · Mike Wood

I do agree with that, but I also—[Interruption.] If the Minister wishes to intervene, he may; otherwise I will continue. The additional burdens that are being imposed on the many businesses that do not trade and have never traded directly with the European Union are on top of the 76 new directives that have already been adopted by the EU since the divergence after Brexit. It is the very definition of a blank cheque—a binding commitment without knowing what the costs will be because we cannot know what the future regulations will be. For Brussels, it may be the gift that keeps on giving, but for so many British businesses it will just be the cost that keeps on rising.
