Turn 170 · Sarah Bool

It is a concern that I have on the point about dynamic alignment. We are talking about a say-so, particularly regarding three areas—SPS, electricity trading and youth mobility—but in the wider discussion, this also potentially allows, as we understand from the summit agreement, other agreements to follow on the path of dynamic alignment. In that case, if the Prime Minister is going to be honest and we are talking about rejoining the EU—some of the conversations indicate that that is where he may be going—that completely changes our entire relationship once more. It is important that when we come to dynamic alignment, we understand that it is not just on those issues alone; it could go much further and much wider.
