Turn 245 · Speaker unlabelled

That this House regrets that the previous Government’s agreement with the European Union imposed new costs, checks and red tape on British businesses and held back exporters; notes the electoral mandate of the Government to seek a closer relationship between the United Kingdom and the European Union that will support economic growth, cut costs and deliver benefits for UK businesses and working people, while respecting the manifesto red lines of no return to freedom of movement, the Single Market or the Customs Union in this Parliament; and supports the Government’s position in seeking to achieve progress towards those objectives at the UK-EU Summit on Friday 20 November 2026.
