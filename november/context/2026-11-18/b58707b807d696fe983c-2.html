Turn 10 · Mike Wood

My hon. Friend is absolutely right. I saw this when I met a major food processor in my constituency a few weeks ago, who spoke about the increasing regulations because they do export to Brussels—sorry, to Paris. [Interruption.] The increasing regulations coming out of the European Union—[Interruption.] Will the hon. Member for Walthamstow (Ms Creasy) let me finish my sentence? Because that business exports to Paris, it obviously already has to comply with European regulations, but there are growing new regulations with additional costs, such as the specifications for the coating that must be put on pallets for boxes to be piled on. Those burdens have to be met by exporters. It is not right for those regulations to be extended to every other business. The 92% of food and drink manufacturers in the UK that do not trade with Brussels should not have to face those additional burdens.

Turn 15 · Sir Iain Duncan Smith (Chingford and Woodford Green) (Con)

On the SPS issue, the European Union has always had it in its power to make that a simple operation. It was the bad faith of the EU that meant that the SPS—[Laughter.] Hon.
