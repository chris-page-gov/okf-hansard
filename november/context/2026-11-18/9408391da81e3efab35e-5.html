Turn 34 · Dame Diana Johnson

Recognising the needs of growing communities should be part of the planning that ICBs are involved in. I absolutely accept that that needs to be taken into account. The pressures on the NHS make local community-based services more important, not less. Meeting the challenges will require strong joined-up community services, including community nursing, therapies, rehabilitation, urgent community response, virtual wards, primary care and social care, all working together around the needs of patients. Ultimately, the future of community hospitals should not be considered in isolation; they form part of the broader community health infrastructure, which includes neighbourhood health teams, community providers, primary care, mental health services, social care and the voluntary sector. The question is not simply how many community hospitals we have, but how we use community assets and service …
