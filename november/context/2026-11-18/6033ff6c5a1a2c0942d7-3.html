Turn 24 · Speaker unlabelled

Public ownership ought to mean more than changing the name on the contract. It should mean that decisions about our railways are made in the public interest, with public accountability and the ability to plan services around the needs of passengers and communities, rather than the interests of private operators. The 2024 Act established a clear principle that passenger rail services should be brought into public ownership. This Bill should be building on that commitment, not creating new routes around it.
