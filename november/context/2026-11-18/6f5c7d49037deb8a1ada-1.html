Turn 1 · Sir Julian Lewis (New Forest East) (Con)

On a point of order, Mr Speaker. Yesterday, the Prime Minister of Mauritius gave his National Assembly a lengthy and detailed account—available online—of recent discussions he has had with the United Kingdom’s Prime Minister and our National Security Adviser on the sovereignty of the British Indian Ocean Territory.
