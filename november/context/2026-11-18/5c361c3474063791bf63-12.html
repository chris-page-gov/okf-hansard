On behalf of the Greens, I have tabled an amendment to the Opposition’s motion on Rosebank because, frankly, it is based on myths and misinformation. In this speech, I aim to bring some clarity and accuracy to the debate. On energy security, Rosebank is mostly an oilfield. In January 2024, Conservative Government Ministers admitted that most of the oil extracted from Rosebank would be sold on the international market, not to UK customers. We also know that, whether or not new drilling goes ahead, the UK will still be almost completely dependent on imports to meet our energy needs by 2050. Rosebank’s minimal gas reserves have the potential to reduce UK annual gas import dependency by just 1% on average, and that would be if none of the gas was sold overseas, so it is likely to be even less than that.
