Turn 4 · Andrew Bowie

As ever, my right hon. Friend hits the nail on the head. We are still using gas to heat over 80% of homes in this country. British industry relies on gas to ensure that it can function. It is not that we are using less gas; it is just that we are using more imported gas from overseas, either through pipelines from Norway, or in the form of liquefied natural gas from the United States and elsewhere. It is no surprise that Gary Smith of GMB has described this Government’s energy policy as “batshit”. The opportunity that is open to us is Rosebank. That field, if allowed to proceed, could help Britain turn the corner, reindustrialise, grow and become more energy secure, utilising what is on our doorstep, just as other countries are doing and just like our North sea neighbour, Norway. It is in our best interests—it is in the interests of our economy, our jobs, our security and our climate.
