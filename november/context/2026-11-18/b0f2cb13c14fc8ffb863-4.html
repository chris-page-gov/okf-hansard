Turn 29 · Matthew Pennycook

If the hon. Member will allow me to make a linked point, he can then come back to me if he wants to. Infrastructure constraints can be a material consideration; I think the hon. Member for Chichester said that they could not be, but they can. Where there is clear evidence that a regulatory regime is not operating effectively in a particular area, decision makers may need to depart from the general assumption.
