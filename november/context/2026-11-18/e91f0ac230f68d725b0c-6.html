Turn 43 · Baroness Bennett of Manor Castle (GP)

My Lords, Wales is now starting the Nature Service, delivering part of what the green new deal movement, nature charities and campaign groups have been calling for across these islands—a national nature service. It will have work outside in green spaces, in active jobs that are ideal for many. Yet “nature” appears only four times in the 278-page report and there is nothing referring to the natural world, despite its desperate need for repair for labour. Are the Government at least prepared to consider a significant investment in a national nature service? That is something that could provide good jobs and would be for the public good, rather than what is suggested here of subsidising existing employers, which will largely be giant multinational companies—subsidising their profits rather than working for the public good.

Turn 44 · Baroness Smith of Malvern (Lab)

I think it is fair to say that Alan Milburn’s report, as others have mentioned, is pretty wide-ranging. It feels a little unfair to criticise it on the basis that it did not take up that particular point about a nature service. Where I agree with the noble Baroness is that we have to find a range of opportunities for young people, through work experience, work and training, to experience what it is like to work in a way that enthuses them and makes them want to carry on working. It may well be, for some of them, that that type of activity outside would be the way to do it, but I am afraid I am not in a position to answer her specific questions about the nature service as part of this Statement.
