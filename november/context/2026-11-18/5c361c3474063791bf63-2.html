Turn 10 · Andrew Bowie

The hon. Lady knows that Rosebank will generate around £500 million a year in tax receipts, which is money that this country desperately needs. That money could be used to cut taxes on the hard-pressed British people, if this Government were so minded. The opportunity we have is Rosebank. That field, if allowed to proceed, could help Britain turn the corner, reindustrialise, grow and be secure once again, utilising what is on our doorstep, just as other countries are doing. Anyone who has a grasp on reality and the challenges we face as a country understands that simple fact. It is common sense to use our own resources. Rosebank is projected to produce around 69,000 barrels of oil every day at peak, which is roughly 10% of anticipated oil output from the UK continental shelf.
