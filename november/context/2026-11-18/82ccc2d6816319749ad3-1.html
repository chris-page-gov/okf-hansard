Steel Trade Measure: South Korea

Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Blair McDougall)

My hon. Friend the Minister of State for Trade (Lord Sarwar) has today made the following statement. On 4 September 2026, the UK’s Trade Remedies Authority concluded an anti-dumping investigation on imports of hot-rolled steel plate originating from South Korea, submitting to me its final recommendation. The TRA recommended that a definitive anti-dumping measure be imposed on South Korean hot-rolled plate imports with duties of 5.98% to 25.71% dependent on the South Korean exporter. This recommendation was made on the basis that, in the period of investigation, UK domestic industry was being injured by dumped imports of hot-rolled steel plate from South Korea. In all investigations, the TRA is required to conduct the economic interest test. This assessment considers whether the imposition of a measure would be in the economic interest of the UK.
