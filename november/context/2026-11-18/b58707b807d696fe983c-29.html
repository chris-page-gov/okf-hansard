Turn 230 · Charlie Dewhirst

Unfortunately, a lot of problems relating to exports are due to EU intransigence. I worked closely on this matter before I was elected to this place, and I can assure the hon. Lady that when there are Dutch authorities insisting on the use of red biros and French authorities using blue biros, it is farcical. All of this could have been resolved, without any sort of reset, with a simple negotiation on easing trade frictions and digitising systems, so that our brilliant British produce is not held up at the border. I will give another example. The vet who works at the port of Rotterdam finishes at lunch-time on a Friday, so when the port authority decides to check a lorry load that comes in after that time, it is left until Monday morning. That seems absolutely farcical, and it is the fault of not this Government, but the European Union for the rules that it insisted on putting in place.
