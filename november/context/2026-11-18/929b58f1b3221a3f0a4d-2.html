Turn 12 · The Attorney General

Every victim deserves to have their voice at the heart of the justice system, which is why the national roll-out of the victim transformation programme that I announced in September is so important. It will no longer be a postcode lottery, with dedicated victim liaison officers extended from rape victims to victims of domestic abuse, and clearer, more frequent correspondence for all victims. I am determined to ensure that victims are at the heart of our justice system.
