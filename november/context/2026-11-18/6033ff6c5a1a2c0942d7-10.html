Turn 73 · Lord Hendy of Richmond Hill (Lab)

My Lords, the railway works best when it reflects the needs and priorities of the community it serves. That is why the Bill establishes a statutory role for devolved Governments and strategic authorities within the future railway. I am pleased to announce that the Bill has already received legislative consent from the Scottish Parliament and that Welsh Ministers laid their recommendation to support the Bill on 6 November. The Bill places devolved leaders at the heart of railway decision-making. GBR will be required to have regard to their transport plans and strategies, consult them before taking decisions that significantly affect their areas, and work with them through formal co-operation arrangements.
