Turn 9 · The Minister for Creative Industries, Media and Online Safety (Ian Murray)

I congratulate my hon. Friend the Member for Southport (Patrick Hurley) on securing this important debate. I would like to pay a special tribute to him for the work he has done in this area. We often use the tragic circumstances of the Southport attack as an example of where local media came to the fore. During that crisis, there was an amount of misinformation and disinformation circling. It gave us all hope that local people turned to local news outlets to make sure that they were reading the truth and what was accurate. I pay tribute to the Stand Up For Southport journalists, who recently received money from our local news fund, which forms part of our local news strategy, as well as to the Southport Visiter and BBC North West for the role they played for local people.
