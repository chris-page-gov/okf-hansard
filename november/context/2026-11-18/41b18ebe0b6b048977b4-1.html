West of England Combined Authority Expansion

Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government (Jim McMahon)

Today I am pleased to announce an historic expansion of the West of England combined authority to include North Somerset, extending the benefits of devolution to over 220,000 more people in England. Following a thorough assessment of the joint proposal submitted by WECA and North Somerset council, the Secretary of State is satisfied that the statutory test to expand the West of England combined authority has been met. This momentous step will be the first of its kind for a mayoral combined authority. It will formalise a near decade-long relationship between the two areas, which have worked closely together to see the region and its residents thrive. As part of the expansion, the West of England combined authority will receive an additional £15 million within this financial year, subject to a business case, to help unlock local growth opportunities across the expanded region.
