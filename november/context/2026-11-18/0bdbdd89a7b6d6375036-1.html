Turn 3 · The Attorney General (Ellie Reeves)

This Government are committed to halving violence against women and girls. Since becoming a Law Officer, I have delivered the early victims’ right to review, giving rape victims a second chance of justice, as well as dedicated victim liaison officers for domestic abuse victims, supporting them from charge to trial, and pre-trial meetings with the prosecution for rape victims, which is now being piloted for domestic abuse victims. This Government are getting on with the job.
