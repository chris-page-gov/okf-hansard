14: Schedule 2, page 70, line 17, at end insert— “(1A) The objectives set out under sub-paragraph (1)(a) must include clearly identifiable objectives in relation to the provision of Welsh services and the infrastructure required for the provision of such services (including infrastructure situated outside Wales).(1B) In setting objectives under sub-paragraph (1A), the Secretary of State must have regard to the memorandum of understanding published by the Secretary of State and the Welsh Ministers under section 24.”Member’s explanatory statement This amendment would require the Secretary of State to include separate objectives in relation to Wales and Borders when preparing a statement of objectives for a funding period.
