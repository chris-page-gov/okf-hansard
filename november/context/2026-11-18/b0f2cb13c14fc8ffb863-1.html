Turn 6 · Adrian Ramsay (Waveney Valley) (Green)

I thank the hon. Lady for bringing this important debate to the House. I have an example from the market town of Harleston in my constituency that reinforces the point she makes. The Greater Norwich local plan identifies that existing sewerage constraints and the need for additional water supply are critical factors in the planned housing growth of 700 homes, which is a lot in a market town of 2,500 households. The local plan explicitly requires infrastructure capacity to be addressed to the satisfaction of Anglian Water. Does that not show that water companies must be statutory consultees so that that requirement is established before a development is given permission?
