Turn 46 · Speaker unlabelled

I do not accept this idea that the private sector is short-term and driven only by profit. I was contemplating this as I went in to look at the clothes in Marks & Spencer, to buy my sandwiches at Waitrose and to collect my medicines at Boots. All of these are private sector undertakings which have been with us not for decades but for over a century. The private sector is perfectly capable of long-term investment and generating profits at the same time. Let us have none of this nonsense about the private sector being short-term. Private investors in the railway will want to seek independent assurance before committing their capital. Having the condition that the GBR licence will require collaboration with the private sector is indispensable.
