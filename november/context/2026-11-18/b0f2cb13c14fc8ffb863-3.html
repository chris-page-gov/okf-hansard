Thank you, Mrs Barker. Before we were so rudely interrupted by the other Chamber, I was saying that water companies cannot refuse a planning permission and cannot say no. Instead, they have to explain how they are going to meet the extra demand. In my constituency, and indeed in the wider area, this has led to bizarre schemes, including a plan to ship water from Norway. In practice, it is very hard for anyone to object on capacity grounds alone. When we lay that alongside an overwhelming Government mission to build as many houses as possible in pursuit of a target—which is already unachievable—it is a recipe for disastrous decision making.
