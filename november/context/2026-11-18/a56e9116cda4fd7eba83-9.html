Turn 65 · The Prime Minister

The Health Secretary will soon be meeting Beth to discuss her experiences. We will ensure that my hon. Friend is kept updated and thank her for raising Beth’s cause in the House. We are supporting cutting-edge research, including a first-of-its-kind programme to study the genetic factors behind the condition, and we are exploring a clinical trial for potential new treatments. Progress is being made, but my hon. Friend is right—more is needed.
