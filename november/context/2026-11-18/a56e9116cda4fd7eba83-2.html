Turn 9 · The Prime Minister

I will do everything I can as quickly as possible to get the welfare bill down. We received the landmark report yesterday from Alan Milburn, and I say a huge thank you to him, because he has done a great service to this country and particularly to its young people. Finally—a landmark report that will change the way we think about the coming generation and give them the support that they need to get on in life. I hope that all sides of the House will get behind it. Specifically, I have asked the Cabinet to reprioritise spending plans for ’27-28 to put the beginnings of a fund together to support the youth opportunity service, initially delivering 25,000 quality work placements for 16 and 17-year-olds. We are making progress immediately to get the welfare bill down.
