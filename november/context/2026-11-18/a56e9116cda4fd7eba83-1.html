Turn 3 · Tim Farron

Sir David Evennett was a member of the Education Committee with me when I was first elected. He was a really kind man and I was very fond of him. I send my heartfelt condolences to his family. May I add that we should pay our condolences to the family of John Pardoe, who passed away this week? He served North Cornwall and the Liberal movement so proudly for many years. Every year, more than 600 people with cancer in south Cumbria have to make debilitating round trips of nearly four hours, every day for weeks on end, to the nearest radiotherapy centre in Preston. We have the worst access to radiotherapy in the whole of England, and therefore tragically many of my constituents die before their time. The answer is obvious: we need to open a satellite radiotherapy unit at the Westmorland general hospital in Kendal. Will the Prime Minister agree to meet me, local cancer patients and clinician …

Turn 4 · The Prime Minister

I want to see the end of the postcode lottery in cancer care. I am going to agree with the hon. Gentleman—he and I used to talk about this very issue 15 or so years ago, when I was Health Secretary. I would like to see cancer care more in the community and more satellite units, exactly as he says. I will arrange for a Minister to meet him to discuss this issue. We are investing in new cutting-edge radiotherapy machines in hospitals across England. Perhaps there is an opportunity to do more in his area, and we will look into it.

Turn 6 · The Prime Minister

I have an idea of how difficult this year has been for our farmers. I visited a farm myself in Truro at the height of the summer. I will be fighting in Brussels on Friday for a new arrangement to improve the Tory Brexit deal that actually will support our farmers and our food producers, but I recognise that we can do more to support farmers and farming, and I will work with my hon. Friend to that end.
