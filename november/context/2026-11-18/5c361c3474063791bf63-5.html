Turn 38 · Pippa Heylings (South Cambridgeshire) (LD)

The Conservative motion states that, for three reasons, “energy security, economic growth and jobs”, the House should commit itself to Rosebank “as soon as is practicable”. I listen to Conservative Members, and I ask myself why the party that spent the last decade failing to deliver on the real trilemma of energy affordability, energy security and sustainability finds it so hard to look at the evidence and change course. Why would its Trumpian “drill, baby, drill” approach succeed with this one oilfield when hundreds of new licences did not? I will focus on those three claims, and I will also focus on the elephant in the room in the Conservatives’ motion: the climate impacts of the burning of the oil from the field, the very reason their own original consent for Rosebank was found to be unlawful.
