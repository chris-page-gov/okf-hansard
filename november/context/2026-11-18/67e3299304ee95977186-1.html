Turn 5 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Baroness Hayman of Ullock) (Lab)

My Lords, the land use framework will help to deliver development in the right places faster and at lower cost. It provides clearer priorities for land use in England, practical principles for decision-making and better spatial data, helping to identify suitable locations for development and manage pressures on land. This will support more co-ordinated decisions on permanent land use change, helping to reduce uncertainty and delay while at the same time safeguarding important natural assets and productive farmland.
