Turn 20 · Lord Collins of Highbury (Lab)

I am not sure I understand what the noble Lord is trying to get at. We have deprived Ahmed of his United Kingdom citizenship, a very rare act to take. We are determined to ensure that he is deported, which is why we have put a clause in the Bill that will come to this House. I do not know what more the noble Lord thinks we can do, but we are absolutely determined. As I say, we are in constant discussion with a whole range of countries about returns, and we have succeeded—I have given the figures to the House—in increasing the number of returns in the two years we have been in power, compared to the two years that his Government were in power.
