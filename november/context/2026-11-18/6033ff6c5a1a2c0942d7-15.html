Turn 113 · Lord Moylan (Con)

My Lords, before I come to the meat of the debate, I have Amendments 43 and 44 in this group. In Committee the Minister confirmed that the Office of Rail and Road, which has a vague regulatory responsibility for GBR, would be under a competition duty when creating the retail code of practice. It will be under a competition duty when it creates that code, but it will not be under a competition duty when enforcing it. My two amendments would put it under a competition duty in relation to the enforcement code. The code could change over time and, without an ongoing statutory duty to promote competition, there is a risk that competition could gradually be sidelined, with insufficient safeguards against that happening.
