Transatlantic Slave Trade Reparations

Commons · 2026-11-18 · Turns 9–9 of 9 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 9 · The Attorney General

For years, members of the Commonwealth affected by the slave trade have asked for an honest conversation about the legacies of slavery, and it is right that the Prime Minister is not shying away from those conversations. I do not accept that it opens the door to reparations. The hon. Gentleman talked about the £40 million. We are playing our role as a major global economy in supporting the development of partner countries around the world, and supporting Caribbean countries to address climate change will not only promote growth and stability in the Caribbean but benefit the UK by opening opportunities for collaboration.
