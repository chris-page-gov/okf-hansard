Turn 25 · Ed Davey

I thank the Prime Minister for his answer, but I hope that he will absolutely ditch the trade and co-operation agreement, because it is bad from start to finish. If the cost of living crisis does not convince him that a totally new deal with Europe is needed, surely the security implications of Trump, Putin and Xi cosying up should. Lurking behind their talks in China is the scandal of Nvidia, the US firm accused of shipping $12 billion of advanced AI chips to China. With Donald Trump investing millions in Nvidia and selling shares before news of Jensen Huang’s affair broke, White House corruption is once more in plain sight. Does the Prime Minister accept that the Nvidia scandal is not just a major US security threat but a threat to the whole world, and given that corrupt President Trump will not, can he tell us what steps he is taking to keep Britain safe?

Q9. There are currently 35 brain tumour drug trials in the UK. Of these, about 10 relate to glioblastoma, which has an average life expectancy of just nine months and has seen no improvement in treatment for 30 years. Astonishingly, as of today, nobody appears to know how many people are on those trials. The Department of Health and Social Care does not know, and the National Institute for Health and Care Research does not know, or is at least not telling the Department, so may I ask the Prime Minister if he can find out? I suspect that it is less than 1%, or less than 30 of the 3,000 people diagnosed with this terrible illness in the UK in the last year.

Rejoining is something that his Back Benchers, in their greater number, would support. With the parliamentary arithmetic and public opinion behind him, why does he continue to kick this can even further down the road? Why does he not just set out when we are rejoining the EU?
