Turn 20 · Chris Evans (Caerphilly) (Lab/Co-op)

It is a pleasure to serve under your chairmanship, Mr Efford. In debates such as this, it is easy to look back at the past. There is no future in our past. In Wales, we often talk about the coalfields and the mines. They are long gone. In their place is a quiet revolution: BAE Systems in Glascoed and General Dynamics in Oakdale and Merthyr are providing high-quality jobs. That is why the defence industry must be part of the modern industrial strategy and I welcome the Government’s plans for that. The Government have said that they will plug a £4.7 billion gap in defence funding, and have committed to reaching a 3.5% of GDP target by 2035. That is an opportunity for this Government and for communities such as mine that can build a new industrial revolution in the places that have been left behind by Government.
