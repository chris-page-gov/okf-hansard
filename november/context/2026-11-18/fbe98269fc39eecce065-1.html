Turn 1 · Neil Duncan-Jordan (Poole) (Lab)

On a point of order, Madam Deputy Speaker. I seek your advice on how I might request that the Minister for Border Security and Asylum correct the record on claims made during yesterday’s debate. In speaking about my new clause 62, the Minister said: “I do not accept that the country has a duty to let domestic abusers, shoplifters and sex offenders settle”—[Official Report, 17 November 2026; Vol. 793, c. 829.] New clause 62 would have frozen the suitability requirements as they stood when a person entered the skilled worker route, and under this the Home Secretary would therefore have the power to exercise her discretion to refuse leave on the grounds of criminality. I am sure it was not the Minister’s intention to suggest that my amendment provided a duty to let sex offenders settle in Britain. Turn 2 · Madam Deputy Speaker (Caroline Nokes)

I thank the hon. Member for notice of his point of order. Ministers’ contributions in the Chamber are, of course, not a matter for the Chair, but he has placed his concerns on the record and the Treasury Bench will have heard him. Should it be necessary for the record to be corrected, there are, of course, established mechanisms for doing so.
