Turn 19 · The Attorney General

I join the hon. Gentleman in paying tribute to his predecessor, John Pardoe. Turning to the question, I commend the bravery and courage of all victims who have come forward. Powerful men should not be able to use the law to silence their victims. Non-disclosure agreements should not be used to obstruct justice. The Government also recognise the profound financial and psychological impact that SLAPPs have on individuals. With the Victims and Courts Act 2026, we will ensure that victims in crime can tell anyone what happened, despite signing an NDA, and we remain committed to introducing further legislation in relation to SLAPPs.
