Turn 3 · The Attorney General (Ellie Reeves)

In September I was pleased to announce the national roll-out of the CPS’s victim transformation programme. That includes an enhanced service for victims of domestic abuse, providing them with a dedicated victim liaison officer who can support them from charge to trial. This is a vital step in improving CPS support for victims and placing them at the heart of the criminal justice system.

Turn 4 · Darren Paffey

My constituent Mark Noke was tragically murdered, and his family described the experience of the eight-week trial as harrowing, not just because of its contents, but because support for them changed day by day. They sat in a shared gallery surrounded and intimidated by the perpetrators’ families, and they had to pay for transcripts and rulings that the defendants got for free. The defendants had accommodation; the bereaved family had none. Will the Attorney General meet me and Mark’s family to discuss how their experience can help shape the bereaved family scheme, and will she ensure that every bereaved family gets one dedicated liaison officer from charge to verdict?

Turn 5 · The Attorney General

May I start by saying how sorry I am for what my hon. Friend’s constituents have experienced and for the loss of their loved one, Mark? The CPS is strengthening the bereaved family scheme, including providing dedicated victim liaison officers to have a consistent point of contact from charge to trial. Bereaved families deserve consistent, compassionate support throughout the justice process. I would be happy to meet him and his constituents to hear directly about their experience.
