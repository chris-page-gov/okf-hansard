Turn 67 · Speaker unlabelled

4: Clause 5, page 4, line 18, at end insert— “(4A) Where a relevant local government body proposes to enter into arrangements with Great British Railways under this section and Great British Railways decides neither to enter into the proposed arrangements, nor to enter into arrangements with the body that are substantially similar to the proposed arrangements, Great British Railways must prepare and publish a statement setting out a reasoned explanation of its decision.(4B) The statement under subsection (4A) must be published as soon as reasonably practicable following the decision.”Member’s explanatory statement This amendment would require Great British Railways to publish a reasoned explanation where it has declined to enter into co-operation arrangements with a relevant local government body.
