Turn 62 · Speaker unlabelled

3: Clause 3, page 3, line 14, at end insert— “(5A) A GBR company through which statutory functions of Great British Railways are being exercised under subsection (5) is not to be regarded as acting as agent for Great British Railways when exercising the functions under that subsection.(5B) Subsections (5) and (5A) do not prevent a GBR company, or any other person, from exercising statutory or other functions of Great British Railways as agent for Great British Railways otherwise than under subsection (5).”Member’s explanatory statement This amendment would make it clear that a GBR company exercising functions of GBR under clause 3(5) is not doing so as an agent, while also making it clear that this does not prevent GBR functions from being exercised on an agency basis otherwise than under clause 3(5).
