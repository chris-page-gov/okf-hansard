Turn 17 · Speaker unlabelled

The noble Lord, Lord Addington, also asked how an event of significant national and international interest would be defined. The Bill provides a non-exhaustive list of factors that may be considered when assessing whether an event is of significant international interest. These include the scale and reputation of the event, the number of people, such as participants and spectators, who are expected to attend from outside the UK, and the amount of media coverage that it is expected to receive outside the UK. Other relevant factors may also be considered.
