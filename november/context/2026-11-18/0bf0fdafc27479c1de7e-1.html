Turn 1 · The Minister for Public Health and Patient Safety (Dame Diana Johnson)

I am today announcing the establishment of new neighbourhood support teams that will deliver a care management service to transform the support available to people with the greatest health and care needs. Too many older and vulnerable people are let down by a health and care system that is fragmented and that too often waits for a crisis before it acts. People and their families are left to navigate multiple services and to join up their own care. A small group bears the heaviest cost: around 3% to 5% of the population account for approximately 70% of emergency bed days, and many of their admissions could have been prevented with earlier, co-ordinated support at home. Neighbourhood support teams will change this.
