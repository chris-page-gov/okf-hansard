Turn 4 · Speaker unlabelled

This morning, Alan Milburn published a landmark report setting out a radical blueprint for a new settlement with young people. I thank Mr Milburn and his team for their outstanding work. Sometimes a review deals with an issue so fundamental that it alters what we see as the contract between government and society, and I believe this is one such report, because 1 million young people not in education, employment or training creates a human cost that no country should ask its young people to bear. It robs them of the thing that is most important to their lives: the prospect of a good future. It demands a response that is much more than a conventional departmental response. It has to be one that goes from education to health, to employment and more.
