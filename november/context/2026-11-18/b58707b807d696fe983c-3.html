Turn 19 · Ben Coleman (Chelsea and Fulham) (Lab)

May I beg all hon. Members on both sides of the House to look at the reality of what is being said? We talk about having no say, but if we get the deal right and it goes well we will have the opportunity, for the first time in 10 years, to sit around the table with the European Union at which the standards will be set. I have had this conversation with farmers and farmers’ representatives, and they welcome the opportunity that has been denied them for 10 years to shape the standards. Yes, we will not have a final vote, but we will be back there shaping the standards and having a say, which has been denied them for 10 years as a result of the actions of the previous Government. Let us welcome that.
