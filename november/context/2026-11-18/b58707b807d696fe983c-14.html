Turn 110 · Priti Patel

I will speak briefly about one other aspect, namely migration and jobs. I was the Home Secretary who negotiated with the EU on security co-operation at a time when Labour Members were saying that we would never secure a deal on intelligence sharing and co-operation, which we went on to do.
