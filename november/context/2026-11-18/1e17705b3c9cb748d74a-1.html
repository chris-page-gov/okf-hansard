Turn 6 · Lord Lemos (Lab)

My Lords, I agree with my noble friend Lady Hazarika that we need to have a wider conversation. The question of the staggered age of consent has come to the forefront of public discourse. It is a complicated question, but we can certainly look at it—it has far-reaching consequences. However, as she indicates, there are plenty of things that we could take action on now. The issue at stake is the sexual exploitation and coercion of vulnerable young people by powerful and manipulative older people. There is much that can be done by broadcasters, by online companies and in education.

Turn 8 · Lord Lemos (Lab)

I can confirm that the Law Commission is, as part of its 14th programme of work, reviewing the concept of consent in the criminal law, including in sexual offences, so it will look at the issue that the noble Lord raised. It has confirmed that the review will include a wide range of issues and will provide greater predictability to modernise particularly the criminal law in England and Wales. Of course, the Government will consider its findings carefully.
