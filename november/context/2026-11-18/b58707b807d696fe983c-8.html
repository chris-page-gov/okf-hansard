Turn 57 · Sir Julian Lewis

I am very grateful to the Minister, and I agree with his point about tone and will try to live up to that. Does he accept that, for many people, the decision to vote to leave the European Union was not primarily about economic arrangements; it was about the fact that the European Union has a stated fundamental aim to create a politically unified united states of Europe, which would dissolve the sovereignty of the member countries? That is the reason I voted to leave. Does the Minister have any reason to believe that the European Union has abandoned that long-term political unification ambition?
