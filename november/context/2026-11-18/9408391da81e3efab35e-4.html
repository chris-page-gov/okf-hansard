Turn 26 · Dame Diana Johnson

Discussions are being held among the relevant authorities. I do not have a detailed read-out of the results of those discussions, but it is important that the Welsh Government, the ICBs and the UK Government have those conversations. Making sure that patients can access healthcare as close to home as possible should be at the heart of this. We would all want those discussions to focus on that. It is important to recognise what these hospitals mean to the communities they serve, which rely on them. It is right to recognise that they are staffed by dedicated nurses, therapists, doctors and care staff, supported in many cases by volunteers and local charities. Many have been woven into local life for generations, and I want to put on the record my thanks to everyone who works in or supports a community hospital.
