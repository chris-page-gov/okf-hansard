My Amendment 17 would require GBR to set out in its business plan—I hope I have the right document this time—how it would reduce or minimise its costs to taxpayers. The Minister has said in Committee and elsewhere that GBR will already be under a duty to have regard to the efficient use of public funds, but one can have an efficient use of funds while expanding the demand on the taxpayer. The two are not the same thing.
