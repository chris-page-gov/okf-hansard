Turn 3 · The Minister for Security (Dan Jarvis)

Alongside investment, our Be Ready campaign is helping households to prepare for potential disruption.

Turn 5 · Dan Jarvis

As I think the hon. Gentleman will know and recognise, the Government did publish the nature security assessment—the first globally of its kind—that sets out the importance of ecosystems to the UK’s resilience, national security and prosperity. Like him, I welcome the appointment of Dame Madeleine Alessandri. She will be working closely across Government to support the work we are doing on climate adaptation, including £1.4 billion for more than 600 flood schemes this year, £65 million to support farmers through prolonged dry weather, £710 million to climate-proof schools and £104 billion to improve water security. This Government are acting not only to prepare people for the threats that climate change poses but, with our commitment to net zero, to address the threat of climate change as well.

Turn 7 · Dan Jarvis

I am grateful to my hon. Friend for making those points. Let me say to him that we are taking action to make the UK the most resilient country that it can be, including in the face of our changing climate. That includes investing in nine new reservoirs across the country, more than 600 flood defence projects across England and £2.6 billion to climate-proof rail infrastructure and keep the UK moving. All of this makes us more resilient and contributes to our vital role as a dependable ally.
