Turn 53 · The Chair

With this it will be convenient to discuss the following: Amendment 43, in schedule 2, page 101, line 26, at end insert— “(3A) A relevant authority may only give P, or P and a connected person, a notice if it considers it just and equitable to do so.” This amendment incorporates the “just and equitable” test from the Building Safety Act 2022, so that a relevant authority may only give a notice for group liability where it is just and equitable to do so. Amendment 44, in schedule 2, page 103, line 21, at end insert— “(1A) For the purpose of this Schedule, a “connected person” is not— (a) an institutional investor; (b) a fund; or (c) a build to rent investor.” This amendment clarifies that connected person does not include passive investors and lenders.
