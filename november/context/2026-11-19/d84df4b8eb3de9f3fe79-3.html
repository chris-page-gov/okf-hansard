Turn 17 · Sally Jameson

I thank the hon. Member for raising that case, and I am sorry to hear about her constituent’s experience. We have held more than 1,000 caseworker surgeries, but it is important to note that caseworkers can only provide updates on the current situation and ensure that the hon. Member’s office has all the up-to-date information. If she writes to me with those particular cases, I would be happy to look into them. However, as I said in my previous answer, it is important that we put members first and do not cause any further disruption to the member experience. Capita has been paid to deliver this contract. It will deliver this service to a good standard, and I will be holding it to account every single day until it reaches the standards that were expected in the beginning.

Turn 20 · Sally Jameson

I am sorry to hear of the uncertainty and hardship faced by Samantha and, in fact, so many affected members of the Capita pension scheme. Its performance has been totally unacceptable. We are using all levers at our disposal to ensure that Capita is delivering a good performance at the earliest possible time. As many will know, that includes withholding payments, and we also have a new recovery and rectification plan. The Grant Thornton report was funded by Capita, and we will continue to push Capita to ensure that it is delivered on in full. Capita is very aware of how urgently the Government regard the situation. As I say, every single day, I am holding Capita to account on its progress.
