Turn 41 · Tessa Munt (Wells and Mendip Hills) (LD)

The Government have asked the public to prepare for a black sky event. Many people with very severe ME— myalgic encephalomyelitis—are bedbound and rely on electric pumps for nutrition. If we experience a prolonged national or even regional power outage, they would be at very grave risk. What specific provision is in place for them? Will the Minister ensure that national resilience planning fully accounts for people who are dependent on powered medical equipment?

Turn 42 · Dan Jarvis

I think the hon. Lady is referring to the recent Be Ready campaign that we launched. This is about raising awareness of simple steps that households can take in their everyday lives to prepare for everyday emergencies. It is part of a process by which we ensure that our country is as resilient as possible, because we cannot have national resilience and national security without resilience at the most localised levels. These are issues that the Government take incredibly seriously. That is why we invested £1 billion in pandemic preparedness and launched the home defence programme. We are doing work in Government now that we have not been doing for almost 25 years. We are determined to ensure that our country is as resilient as it can be, given the nature of the world we are living in.
