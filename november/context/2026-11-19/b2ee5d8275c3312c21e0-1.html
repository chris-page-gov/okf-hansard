Turn 1 · The Minister for Policing and Crime (Sarah Jones)

Following an announcement earlier this year, I am pleased to launch a consultation on a range of events that are due to take place during this Parliament for which it may be appropriate to make use of the power under section 172 of the Licensing Act 2003 to extend licensing hours on a national basis in England and Wales to mark occasions of exceptional international or national significance. These occasions are an opportunity for communities to come together in collective celebration and provide a welcome boost to the hospitality sector. To date the power has been used largely to mark royal events such as His Majesty the King’s coronation and the latter stages of major football tournaments. Subject to the consultation this would mean that fans of football can celebrate the women’s world cup in 2027, and rugby fans can celebrate the rugby world cup later that year.
