The Cabinet Office has already said that it is considering bringing administration in-house, and the Public and Commercial Services Union has put forward a practical plan to start now. Turn 4 · Sally Jameson

Terminating the contract immediately could trigger an operational vacuum and jeopardise monthly pension payments for hundreds of thousands of our public servants, and I am not willing to do that. We must put the members first at every single stage. We are evaluating the long-term options, but an alternative delivery structure cannot just be readied overnight, which I am sure the hon. Gentleman will appreciate. Our immediate priority remains forcing operational recovery and holding Capita strictly to account. Simultaneously, my officials are looking at the long-term options for the pension scheme.
