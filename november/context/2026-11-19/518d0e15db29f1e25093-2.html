Turn 10 · Lord Jackson of Peterborough (Con)

Anecdotally, one of the issues that comes up—I am not criticising GPs; there is some superb primary care provision for patients with dementia—is that, once medication is put in place, it is not often reviewed. Medication has a big impact on different patients over different lengths of time. Is that an area of consideration? I know it is a very granular issue, but if people’s medication is not reviewed in a timely way, it can impact their capacity to stabilise the condition.
