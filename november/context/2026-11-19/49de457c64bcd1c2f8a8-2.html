Turn 10 · Baroness Hayman of Ullock (Lab)

The Environment Agency’s mapping uses a unique combination of local knowledge and national capability. It draws on outputs from more than 1,000 detailed river and coastal flood models and more than 200 local surface-water models that have been developed with local authorities so that we have as much local knowledge as we can. It also incorporates information on flood defences. There is expertise within the Environment Agency and local authorities’ offices because we want to make sure that the mapping reflects conditions on the ground as accurately as possible.
