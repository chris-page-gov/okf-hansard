Turn 10 · Warinder Juss (Wolverhampton West) (Lab)

The UK Youth Parliament was established over 25 years ago to give young people a stronger voice in decisions affecting their lives. Elected members campaign on issues such as knife crime, mental health, education and the cost of living. However, its future is uncertain, as its funding is to be stopped. Given that the Government want to introduce votes at 16, does the Leader of the House agree that we should be encouraging our young people to engage with democracy? Will he please arrange for me to meet urgently the Under-Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport, my hon. Friend the Member for Lewisham North (Vicky Foxcroft), to discuss the future of the UK Youth Parliament?
