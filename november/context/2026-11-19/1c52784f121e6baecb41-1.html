Turn 2 · The Parliamentary Secretary, Cabinet Office (Sally Jameson)

The Government’s ambition is to deliver growth in every postcode by moving power, funding and decision making closer to people and places. The Cabinet statement on rewiring the state sets out our plans to develop a new approach to devolution that will empower local leaders, strengthen strategic authorities, improve public services and enable stronger local economies across the UK. We are working closely with mayors and local leaders to deliver that.

Turn 4 · Sally Jameson

The Prime Minister was clear in his Motherwell speech and at the National Economic Council that devolution to communities and regions is at the centre of his vision for the whole of the UK. That does not mean swapping power from one Parliament to another; as my hon. Friend says, it means going deeper and putting power down into those regions. The Government will always seek to work collaboratively with the Scottish Government, respecting their devolved competence throughout, to progress this work in Scotland and put people at the centre of the places where they live. We are taking forward the work that has come out of the NEC through our offer of joint work to drive regional empowerment through policy sprints, which is being convened by No. 10 North.

Turn 7 · Sally Jameson

The statement the Prime Minister made, and what was set out at the beginning, was that mayors would not be able to offer a rebate. That remains the position. However, we are working with local mayors and strategic authorities across the country to look at how we can devolve further power—the position of this Government is devolution by default. We will set out the “Rewiring the State” delivery plan very shortly. In that plan, people will be able to see the full suite of powers across Government that will be devolved to regions and local leaders, which is where we believe those powers and resources will be best delivered.
