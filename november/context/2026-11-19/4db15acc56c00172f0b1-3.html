Turn 17 · Helen Morgan

One issue that has arisen a number of times in my casework is that a person with a learning disability might need a family member or advocate with them because they are unable to communicate what they have or have not been experiencing so far. For example, they may have been taking a certain type of medication but have not been able to communicate effectively that that is what they have been doing. Would the Minister be able to ensure that there is space for an advocate in place where that is appropriate?
