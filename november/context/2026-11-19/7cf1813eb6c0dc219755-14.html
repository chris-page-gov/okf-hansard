Clauses 80 to 82 are supporting provisions to ensure that part 4 of the Bill continues to function as intended. Clause 80 is an updating power to future-proof the legislation. It ensures that the co-operation framework can continue to work properly if responsibility for enforcing parts 1, 2 or 3 of the Bill were to change. Without the clause, a future change to the authorities responsible for relevant functions could leave part 4 referring to bodies that no longer exercise those functions. Clause 81 provides a route for the co-operation provisions in part 4 to be repealed. That will take effect only if chapter 1 of part 1 is repealed. This is because it may no longer be proportionate to retain all the associated co-operation arrangements if chapter 1 is repealed. That repeal is not automatic.
