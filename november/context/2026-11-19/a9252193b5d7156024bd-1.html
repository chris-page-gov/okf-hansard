Turn 2 · The Minister for Artificial Intelligence (Kanishka Narayan)

The Government are taking decisive steps to assess and improve the resilience of the UK’s digital infrastructure. That includes commitments on cyber in the Budget and commitments on AI security, as well as a direction on telecoms with the 2026 statement of strategic priorities for Ofcom. We will continue to work closely across Government and with regulators and industry to identify risks, reduce them, secure networks and maintain digital services.

Turn 3 · Helen Morgan

The public switched telephone network will finally be turned off in January, which means everybody will be dependent on their broadband to make a phone call. Twice in the last five years, many of my constituents have been without power for several days following big storms. If the mobile masts go down without power as well, we cannot even make a mobile phone call, and yet the mobile masts are not considered to be a priority when the power is being switched back on. Will the Minister look at that? Many of my constituents and people in other rural areas cannot make a phone call in the event of a power outage, and it is a really serious problem.

Turn 6 · Kanishka Narayan

The Security Minister, who is sitting next to me, is the lead Minister for cyber across Government, but I know that DCMS, as the lead cyber Department for Government and public services, will also take the concerns seriously.
