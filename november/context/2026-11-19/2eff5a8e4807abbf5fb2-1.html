Turn 2 · The Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster and Minister for the Cabinet Office (Louise Haigh)

We are exploring how greater public control can ensure that our essential services—housing, water, energy and transport—run in the interest of those who use them. The taskforce is committed to hearing diverse perspectives. I am driving the taskforce, which is supported by civil servants, drawing on expertise across central, regional and local government, business, civil society and academia, and its work will inform the 10-year plan that will be published next month.

Will the Minister clarify whether campaign organisations, including Windrush Against Sewage Pollution and Ilkley Clean River Group, which have a great deal of knowledge of the sector and have campaigned for many years on the issue, would be involved in the taskforce in any meaningful way?
