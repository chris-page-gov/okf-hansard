Turn 1 · Charlie Dewhirst (Bridlington and The Wolds) (Con)

On a point of order, Madam Deputy Speaker. At Prime Minister’s questions yesterday, the Prime Minister told the House that UK exports have gone down since the UK left the EU. However, UK exports in our last full year of membership totalled £723 billion; last year, that figure rose to £913 billion, an increase of £190 billion, or 26%. Even adjusted for inflation, that is still an increase of 2%, so perhaps the Prime Minister might wish to return to the House and correct the record.
