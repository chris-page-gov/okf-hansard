Turn 40 · Sally Jameson

As my hon. Friend will know, London has one of the longest standing devolution settlements. The mayor is accountable to voters in every borough. He is scrutinised by the London Assembly and works in partnership with boroughs where responsibilities might overlap. As I said in a previous answer, I am happy to meet London MPs to discuss their thoughts about this issue further. Through “Rewiring the State”, we will work with London partners so that the settlement keeps pace with the changes we are making and, of course, London’s role as a global city, building on the package agreed at the Budget.
