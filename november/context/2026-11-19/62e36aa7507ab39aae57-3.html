Turn 17 · Baroness Stedman-Scott (Con)

By way of an example, there are five vacancies for every 100 young people in Rotherham, and the area is not alone in that respect. The Government’s ambitions for the Milburn review and getting young people into work are completely understood and supported. But what will the Government do to help Rotherham, and other places around the country in such a position, create the additional jobs that are needed?

Turn 18 · Baroness Smith of Malvern (Lab)

I reiterate the answer I gave earlier to the noble Baroness’s noble friend. Of course, there is the challenge of making sure that we continue to grow the economy, put in place the conditions necessary for businesses to create jobs, and build vacancies and opportunities for young people. All those are at the heart of not just our response to Milburn but this Government’s economic policy as a whole. I reiterate the encouraging response we have already seen from employers, which I mentioned yesterday evening: the 250,000 jobs that the hospitality, retail and tourism industries have pledged for young people who are neither earning nor learning at the moment. That is a good example of the commitment that employers, alongside others, are showing to delivering a future for our young people.
