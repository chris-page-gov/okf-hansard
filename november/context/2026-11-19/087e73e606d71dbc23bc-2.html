I will come on to answer that question. On the level of research funding, I remind noble Lords that funding decisions are made independently of the Government, as per the Haldane principle. A recent independent review by the UK Centre for Ecology & Hydrology’s long-term observation portfolio concluded that soils remain a priority area for future development, particularly agricultural soils.
