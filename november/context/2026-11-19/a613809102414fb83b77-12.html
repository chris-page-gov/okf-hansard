Turn 89 · Josh Dean (Hertford and Stortford) (Lab)

The Holy Trinity night shelter in Bishop’s Stortford provides much-needed support and stability to those who are most in need in our community. It is no exaggeration to say that its work changes lives. I was delighted to see those who run the shelter awarded a King’s award for voluntary service last week. Will the Leader of the House, and the whole House, join me in congratulating Dave, Michelle and all the volunteers at the night shelter, and in thanking them for their hard work?

Turn 90 · Sir Alan Campbell

I am sure that the whole House will join me in congratulating Dave, Michelle and all the volunteers at the Holy Trinity night shelter on a very well-deserved King’s award, which, as I have said, is a very high and prestigious award. I am sure that it means a great deal to them and to the whole community. As my hon. Friend will know, ending rough sleeping is a top priority for the Government and the Prime Minister, and the valuable work of such shelters does not go unnoticed. I pay tribute to the work of people in my hon. Friend’s constituency and across the country, as we bear down on that intolerable problem.
