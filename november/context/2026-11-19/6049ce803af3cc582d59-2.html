Turn 13 · Dan Jarvis

The Be Ready public awareness campaign provides simple and effective advice on practical steps that individuals, households and communities can take to improve their resilience to a range of risks, including those presented by climate change. Not only are the Government acting to prepare people for the threats that climate change poses, but, through our commitment to net zero, we are acting to address climate change.
