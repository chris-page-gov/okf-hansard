Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Health and Social Care (Mr James Frith)

Today, the Government have published its one-year-on report of England’s men’s health strategy, setting out progress and our next phase of action to improve the health and wellbeing of men and boys in England. One year ago, we published England’s first-ever men’s health strategy, with three broad aims: ensuring health services engage men and boys and respond to their needs; empowering men and boys to maximise their own health and wellbeing; and creating the conditions in which men and boys can thrive. The positive response has been fantastic. Over the past year, we have worked across Government and with the NHS, local authorities, employers, academia, industry and the voluntary sector to turn its ambitions into action.
