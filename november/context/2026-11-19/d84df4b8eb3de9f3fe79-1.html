Turn 8 · Jon Pearce

Joseph Cassidy from Buxton in my constituency has given 40 years of public service in the military, police and civil service, yet two days after he took voluntary retirement last year, he was told that the principal civil service pension scheme had somehow miscalculated his lump sum by 30%. Twenty months later, he is still yet to receive an outcome to his complaint or a redundancy payment, leaving his family under immense pressure and without an apology. Will the Minister set out what steps the Department is taking to hold Capita to account and get such long-standing cases resolved, so we can give public servants like Joseph the comfort and security they deserve in retirement?
