Turn 12 · Lord Hendy of Richmond Hill (Lab)

The Government’s commitment is to the northern growth corridor and the Northern Powerhouse Rail announcement in January, which represents the first funded programme for rail improvement in the north for a very long time. All previous plans, whatever they were, were both aspirational and unfunded. I am committing to things that can be actually delivered with money that is actually available, not plans that are long into the future. I will write to the noble Lord about local engagement. It is not my intention to exclude any particular local political section.
