Turn 1 · The Minister for Security (Dan Jarvis)

My noble Friend the Minister of State, Home Office (Lord Hanson of Flint) has today made the following written ministerial statement: Fraud is a borderless crime, and tackling it demands a co-ordinated international response. With over two thirds of UK fraud having an overseas element, we cannot protect the British public through domestic action alone. I am therefore pleased to inform the House that the UK has endorsed the US-led statement of principles on countering criminal online scams and scam centres, strengthening our co-operation with international partners to disrupt the organised criminal networks responsible for fraud around the world. Earlier this year, the US State Department set up a contact group with key partners, to discuss how we can work together to protect our citizens from being defrauded by international criminal networks.
