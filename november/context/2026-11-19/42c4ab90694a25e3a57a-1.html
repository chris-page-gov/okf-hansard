Turn 3 · The Minister for Security (Dan Jarvis)

The Government are taking vital steps to strengthen the UK’s cyber-security. At the Budget, £154 million was provided for cyber-defences, including our new national cyber-shield. We have introduced the Cyber Security and Resilience (Network and Information Systems) Bill to raise resilience and protect essential services, and shortly we will publish the national cyber action plan with clear actions for Government and businesses upgrading the UK’s responses to cyber-threats.

Turn 5 · Dan Jarvis

My hon. Friend is right to raise the nature of the threat in that way. We are updating local resilience leaders regularly, and I will convene them shortly. National resilience cannot be built in isolation; it requires shared leadership and action across every sector. That is why our Be Ready campaign has been built with more than 500 partners from charities, businesses, emergency services, local resilience forums and local councils, and it is why we will continue to work closely with all of them to deliver the campaign. I am determined to make us the most resilient country we can be.

I thank my right hon. Friend for his answer. A “deliberate attempt to disrupt our food supply chain and undermine consumers’ confidence in it” is how the EU Food and Agriculture Commissioner described the recent fridge and freezer cyber-attacks in Europe. This attack caused millions of euros-worth of food to need to be thrown away. I sit on the Public Accounts Committee, and last month we looked at the resilience of the food supply chain to disruption here in the UK. Does my right hon.
