Turn 24 · Dan Jarvis

Let me place on record my profound thanks for the important work that local resilience forums do, not least the vital work they did over the summer and the supporting role they are playing in our Be Ready campaign, including in my hon. Friend’s constituency. The Government are currently leading a post-implementation review of the Civil Contingencies Act 2004, which is considering the merits of putting local resilience forums on a statutory footing. A decision will be taken through the review, and we will present that to Parliament early next year.
