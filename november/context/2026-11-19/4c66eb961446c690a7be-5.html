Turn 36 · Florence Eshalomi

Clauses 37, 38 and 39 collectively ensure that the three regulators of building safety in England can use their powers to enforce part 1 of the Bill. Clause 37 ensures that local housing authorities have the investigatory tools they need to enforce part 1. It does that by extending three existing powers under the Housing Act 2004, allowing local housing authorities to use established and familiar powers, rather than creating a whole new investigative regime. First, the Bill allows local housing authorities to require relevant documents to be produced. That will help them establish the facts, assess compliance and support enforcement.
