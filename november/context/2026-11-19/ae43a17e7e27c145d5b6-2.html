Turn 13 · Daniel Francis

Just to bring together the two issues of workforce and NICE guidelines, the guidelines for young people with cerebral palsy refer to ages zero to 25, but the reality is that there remains a cliff edge for people with cerebral palsy when they get to 18. They are discharged from a paediatric service, often into no real service at all, quite frankly. If they are lucky, they may have a specialist who they see once a year, but that support really does go. I do not expect an answer today, but when we go away and look at those workforce issues, can we look at that challenge of transitioning at 18 so that there continues to be a pathway for those young people with cerebral palsy?
