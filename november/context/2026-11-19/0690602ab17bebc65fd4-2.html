Turn 12 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

I accept that Ofcom will not look at individual complaints, but it is still worth raising those complaints, because the issues are examined collectively by Ofcom and, as I have mentioned already, Ofcom will take down sites, as it has done with 100 sites to date, and fine companies, as it has done with 7 million to date. Ofcom will continue to do that and it will know, independent as it is of government, that there is considerable pressure in this House and in the House of Commons for it to improve its performance in overall regulation of these sites. I hope that is of help. I have taken more than my time.

Turn 14 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

I thank my noble friend for commencing this important debate today.
