Turn 91 · Florence Eshalomi

Clause 66 defines the scope of part 3 of the Bill, which addresses beneficial ownership information. It identifies both the buildings in which the powers can be used and the public authorities that can use them. The powers can be used in relation to self-contained buildings, or self-contained parts of buildings, with at least two dwellings. As ownership can be unclear in residential blocks of any size, there is no height threshold. The clause identifies the authorities that can use those powers. In England, those are local housing authorities, fire and rescue authorities, the Building Safety Regulator and Homes England. The clause allows the Secretary of State to add or remove public authorities through regulations. That keeps the list up to date if bodies or their responsibilities change.
