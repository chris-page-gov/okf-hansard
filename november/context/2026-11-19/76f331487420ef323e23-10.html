Turn 75 · Ben Lake

I thank the Backbench Business Committee for granting the debate, all hon. Members who supported the application, and all right hon. and hon. Members who have contributed today. We have heard a range of opinions about the risks of AI, but acknowledging those risks does not mean that we dismiss the potential rewards. We have heard about the vast array of potential rewards and benefits to society—and indeed to humanity—that frontier AI models can bring. The one thing on which I think there is consensus is that AI is a transformational technology and this House would perhaps do well to debate its consequences more regularly. We might not be able to keep up with the pace of that technology’s development, but it is important that we, as the public’s representatives, discuss it. Question put and agreed to. Resolved, That this House has considered the emerging risks of advanced AI.
