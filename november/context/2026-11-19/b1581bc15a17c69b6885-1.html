Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Defence (Wes Streeting)

Today, I am hosting the Chinese Minister of National Defence, Admiral Dong Jun. It is the first visit of its kind for 26 years, when Labour was last in government. Some will ask whether we should engage so directly with a country whose political system, strategic ambitions and conduct differ so fundamentally from our own. Over the years, Britain’s answer to that question has been inconsistent. This has undermined our economic and security relationship with China and confused our allies, especially those in the Indo-Pacific region who look to us for support, security and stability. Since coming to office, this Government have taken a responsible and clear-sighted approach. We have stepped up our engagement in a consistent and co-ordinated way, led by our values and our interests.
