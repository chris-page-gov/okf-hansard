Turn 5 · Jen Craft (Thurrock) (Lab)

My hon. Friend is speaking most passionately about this subject. Does he not think that there is almost a double inequality here, as children are forced to wait longer than their adult counterparts, but the impact on their development can be significantly greater? If an adult waits for two years, that is two years. If a child of five waits for two years, that is almost half their lifetime. Should that not be reflected in the priority that our health service gives to addressing these issues?
