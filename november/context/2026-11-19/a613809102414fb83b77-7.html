Turn 54 · Samantha Niblett (South Derbyshire) (Lab)

I welcome the news that the Government have called on Google to take down Russell Brand’s YouTube account in the wake of his conviction, but that simply does not go far enough, because his podcast can still be seen on other streaming platforms such as Spotify, Amazon and Rumble, which is deeply disturbing for his victims. This issue also extends to the likes of LinkedIn, as in the case of Naomi Timperley and Brad Burton, who were victims of a convicted criminal who used LinkedIn and other methods to stalk them and try to make their life a living hell. Will the Leader of the House work with me to urge Ministers to bring in regulation and perhaps legislation to ensure that these tech platforms are acting based on not just how they feel about something but what the law dictates they must do, so that victims see justice when criminals are locked away?
