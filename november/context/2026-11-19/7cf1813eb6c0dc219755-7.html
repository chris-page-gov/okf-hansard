Turn 49 · Florence Eshalomi

Clause 55 is about making sure that the right people have the right information about medium-rise buildings. The Hackitt review of building regulations and fire safety identified that accurate and timely information is essential for building safety. The registrar needs reliable information to oversee the safety framework, and residents also need clear information about their building. That is why clause 55 will allow regulations to require the registration duty holder to provide prescribed information to the registrar, residents and other people specified in the regulations. The regulations may say when and how the information must be provided; they may also set its format and standard. The clause also allows regulations to clarify whether information given to the registrar can be used in criminal proceedings.
