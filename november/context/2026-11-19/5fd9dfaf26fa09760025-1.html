Turn 2 · The Minister for Security (Dan Jarvis)

National security is our first duty. We are spending £298 billion on defence over the next four years, and we are providing record funding for our intelligence agencies. We have strengthened state threats legislation and launched the home defence programme, and we are establishing the national centre for information defence.

Turn 4 · Dan Jarvis

My hon. Friend is right about Stevenage. Space is a domain of growing competition that is critical for our national security. The defence investment plan recognises that fact with new investments to support sovereignty, resilience and control, including £2.3 billion invested in satellite communications by 2029-30. We are determined to deliver the space capabilities needed to meet the challenges that we face.
