Turn 4 · The Minister of State, Ministry of Defence (Lord Coaker) (Lab)

My Lords, I start by praising the service personnel and their families who sustain our deterrent. The Government assess that unilateral nuclear disarmament would remove the United Kingdom’s ultimate guarantee of security against the most extreme threats, weaken NATO’s overall deterrence posture and increase strategic risks to the UK and its allies. The only route to ridding the world of nuclear weapons is through gradual multilateral disarmament as the security situation allows. The UK will therefore maintain its independent nuclear deterrent for as long as the global security situation requires it.

My Lords, in this time of enormous uncertainty, and as of near danger as at any time in my life, is it not paramount that the British Government be seen to be unanimously committed to the maintenance of our independent nuclear deterrent? Turn 6 · Lord Coaker (Lab)

Let me start where the noble Lord, with his excellent question, finished and again praise the service personnel who sustain our deterrent, but also their families and what they do. As I said to the noble Lord in my Answer to his Question, the Government, like previous Governments, have sustained the independent nuclear deterrent. It is a fundamental cornerstone of our security and, of course, we will do all we can to maintain it. The noble Lord will have seen that the Government are investing £64 billion between 2026-27 and 2029-30 to maintain that deterrent. That will be about the new Dreadnought programme, but it will also be doing what we can to ensure that the availability of our nuclear submarines is as good as it can be. The fundamental point is that we have been able to maintain that independent deterrent at sea, day in, day out, for decades, and we shall continue to do so.

Turn 8 · Lord Coaker (Lab)

The noble and gallant Lord is absolutely right to point out the situation as Putin would see it, but let it resound from this Chamber that he will not succeed in that demand. The UK’s, and France’s, deterrent is committed to NATO, although the operational decision remains with the British Prime Minister. Putin and those who seek to do our country harm should know that we have a strategic, independent nuclear deterrent that this Government will maintain, and I am sure future Governments will do so as well.
