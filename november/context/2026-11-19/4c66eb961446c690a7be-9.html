Turn 65 · Gareth Bacon

Amendments 46 and 47 were tabled in my name. They have a practical purpose and encourage a common sense approach to the Bill’s wider impact. At present, the clause invites the Secretary of State to carry out a post-implementation review of the Act every five years.
