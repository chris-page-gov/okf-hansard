Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill

Turn 1 · The Minister for Housing and Planning (Matthew Pennycook)

This Government made a clear and unambiguous commitment in its manifesto to act where previous Governments had failed and finally bring the feudal leasehold system to an end. We did so on the basis of a firm conviction that it is only by extinguishing fully the historical iniquities on which the present leasehold system rests that we can ensure that the dream of home ownership is made real for millions of households across the country. The Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill that we are introducing to Parliament today marks the beginning of the end for the feudal leasehold system and is integral to honouring our manifesto commitments in full. The Bill was published in draft on 27 January 2026 for pre-legislative scrutiny by the Select Committee on Housing, Communities and Local Government.
