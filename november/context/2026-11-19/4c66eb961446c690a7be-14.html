Turn 105 · Florence Eshalomi

Part 2 of the Bill establishes a medium-rise building register. We already know the value of having a statutory building register. Since 2023, higher-risk buildings have been required to register with the Building Safety Regulator. That has given us a much clearer picture of the 18-meter-plus building stock and supported the identification of buildings that require remediation. However, there is no equivalent register for residential buildings of heights between 11 metres and 18 metres in England. Homes England has done significant work to identify the vast majority of medium-rise buildings that may require remediation, but without a statutory register of the entire medium-rise stock, we cannot have confidence that every building has been identified.
