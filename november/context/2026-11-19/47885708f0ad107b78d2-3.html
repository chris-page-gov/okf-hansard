Turn 17 · Lord Evans of Rainow (Con)

My Lords, I pay tribute to my noble friend for asking this very good Question. It is good to see him in his place. On 24 June last year, the Government announced they would purchase 12 F35A aircraft for the NATO dual-capable aircraft nuclear mission, with the press release saying: “UK to purchase F-35As and join the NATO nuclear mission”. However, in July of this year, Air Vice-Marshal Jim Beck said that the F35As were not actually purchased to perform that role; rather, they were primarily for training purposes. Can the Minister clarify who is correct, the Government or the Royal Air Force?
