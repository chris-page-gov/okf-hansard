Turn 17 · Dr Beccy Cooper (Worthing West) (Lab)

Following on from the comments of the hon. Member for Brigg and Immingham (Martin Vickers), Samaritans is an essential national charity that is there for us at our times of greatest need. Unfortunately, the Worthing branch is one of those at risk of closure. It has 100 dedicated volunteers available, day and night, to listen to anyone calling in. Those people have dedicated many years of their life to the service, and we risk losing not only their listening skills but the invaluable community networking and outreach that they undertake. I recognise that, as the Leader of the House said in response to the hon. Member, the Government cannot intervene directly, but I ask him to join me in praising the Worthing volunteers, and to encourage the national charity to engage with local MPs on this issue.
