Turn 81 · Florence Eshalomi

The hon. Gentleman will be aware that it is normal for service charges to meet reasonable administrative and management costs associated with running a building. The costs of registering a building and complying with associated information requirements are part of the administrative nature of this Bill. I fully appreciate the concerns he has outlined; hence, we will continue to work with leaseholders and their representatives to ensure that those costs remain reasonable and that, where leaseholders face any unreasonable costs, they are able to challenge them. Most importantly, we want to ensure that any cost for wider remediation work is not passed on to leaseholders.

Turn 83 · Florence Eshalomi

The hon. Gentleman will be aware that, under section 19 of the Landlord and Tenant Act, only relevant costs may be taken into account. Leaseholders already have the right to service charge information under the Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024 and to challenge it. From 1 April 2027, the Act’s provisions on litigation costs will reduce the risk to leaseholders. In relation to medium-rise registration costs, which may be passed on to leaseholders, we are working with professional bodies and representatives such as the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors and the Property Institute to look at a benchmarking system for the costs and to identify any areas for leaseholders to challenge, should they wish to do so.
