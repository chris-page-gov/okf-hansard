Turn 75 · Sir Alan Campbell

Turn 77 · Sir Alan Campbell

The reports of Rahile Omer’s death in custody are very distressing, to say the least. We stand firm on human rights, including standing against China’s repression of people in Xinjiang and Tibet. We continue to raise these issues directly with the Chinese authorities and other international partners. I will ensure that the Foreign Secretary hears the hon. Gentleman’s concerns. As ever, he makes a very important point in a very responsible way, and I will ensure that he gets an update on what this Government are doing to hold the Chinese authorities to account.
