Strategic Defence Review: Munitions Production

Turn 1 · The Minister for Defence Readiness and Industry (Luke Pollard)

I am pleased to inform the House that the Government have today opened the first capital grant window to support new energetic materials production in the United Kingdom. This is the next major step in delivering our strategic defence review commitment to rebuild sovereign UK production of the explosives, propellants and pyrotechnics essential to our munitions. Grants of up to £45 million will help unlock private investment in new manufacturing plants, strengthening our warfighting readiness, national resilience and freedom of action. We know there is significant UK industrial appetite and expertise. Twenty-four feasibility studies have now been completed, giving us unprecedented insight into the opportunities across the UK and what is needed to turn them into production.
