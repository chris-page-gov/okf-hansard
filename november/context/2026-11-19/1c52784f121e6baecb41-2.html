Turn 9 · Sally Jameson

Mayors already have the powers to give grants if they want to. As I said, we continue to work with strategic authorities and mayors on the fiscal devolution road map and the “Rewiring the State” delivery plan, which will be set out in the coming months. I suggest that Mayor Ben Houchen works on delivering for his constituents, which he is not doing very well at the minute.
