Turn 25 · Sir John Hayes (South Holland and The Deepings) (Con)

I am delighted to follow the hon. Member for Blyth and Ashington (Ian Lavery), whom I described earlier as a friend. I share his fears, which are real and realistic. AI is probably the greatest existential threat we face as a nation and, beyond that, as humankind. A lot of people agonise about the weather—I do not particularly —but by comparison with climate change, AI is a far greater threat to humanity; it is of entirely different proportions.
