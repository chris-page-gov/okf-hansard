Turn 9 · Oliver Ryan

I agree with my hon. Friend’s intervention, and he has done well to get his foot in the door—you were particularly understanding, Madam Deputy Speaker. I quite agree and I pay tribute to the fire services in Warwickshire, Yorkshire, Scotland, Hampshire and elsewhere. The first issue is council tax. Lancashire’s band D fire precept for 2026-27 is £94.73, against a national average for combined fire authorities of £100.21. That leaves Lancashire £5.48—for those mathematicians in the Chamber—a year below the average, or roughly 10p a week. The fire authority has asked for flexibility to close that gap in a single year from 2027-28, on top of the £5 increase already assumed in its budget, with the final amount depending on the national average at that point.
