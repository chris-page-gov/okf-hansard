Turn 3 · Jim Shannon (Strangford) (DUP)

I commend the hon. Gentleman for introducing this debate. He is absolutely right to underline the issue of mental health. The stats in Northern Ireland are incredible: we face a devastating mental health crisis among our men, with official figures showing that nearly eight in every 10 suicide deaths are male. Suicide remains the leading cause of death for men under 50; too often, stigma and silence prevent our young men from reaching out before it is too late. Does the hon. Member agree that the Government must work closely with the Northern Ireland Executive and grassroots community groups to deliver accessible, targeted mental health support so that no young man feels he has to suffer alone?
