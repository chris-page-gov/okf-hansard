Turn 29 · The Minister for Homelessness, Democracy, Communities and Faith (Florence Eshalomi)

I thank the hon. Member for his amendment. I will explain the clauses in this group first. Clause 51 requires a registrar to establish and maintain a register of medium-rise buildings in England, which will give the Government and regulators a more complete and authoritative picture of medium-rise building stock. That will provide an important safety net for remediation, helping us to identify buildings that may still require action and those responsible for them. The value of the register goes far beyond the current remediation programme: it will provide a lasting national dataset of medium-rise buildings, which will help Government and regulators to respond more quickly and effectively to wider or emerging safety risks both now and in the future.
