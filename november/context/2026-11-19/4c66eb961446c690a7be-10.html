Turn 74 · Speaker unlabelled

It is right that any impact on house building is closely monitored, including through the Bill. That is why we have tabled new clause 6, which would require the Secretary of State to report on the effects of the Bill on residential building. That would support investors and developers where rules have created uncertainty. It would help Ministers understand the house building landscape, and provide information crucial to sustainable and accelerated house building rates. This is not about questioning the Bill’s intentions, which we support—I want to make that crystal clear; it is about watching how the Bill interacts with other key metrics in the system.
