Turn 45 · The Chair

With this it will be convenient to discuss the following: Amendment 8, in clause 55, page 30, line 15, at end insert— “(1A) The regulations must make provision to require the provision of full copies of any fire risk assessment of external walls, remediation compliance plan, and underlying fire safety data to the registrar, residents and leaseholders of the building.” This amendment, in connection with Amendment 7, would require the Secretary of State to make regulations requiring medium-rise building duty-holders to provide full external wall fire risk assessments, remediation compliance plans and safety reports directly to residents and leaseholders. Clause stand part. Clause 56 stand part.

Turn 46 · Gideon Amos

Amendments 7 and 8 would require the Secretary of State to make regulations requiring medium-rise building duty holders to provide full external wall fire risk assessments. No Bill is complete without a few new acronyms, and this Bill has FRAEWs: fire risk appraisals of external walls, which are the key documents for any resident concerned about cladding in their building, owners and other stakeholders. The amendments would include leaseholders in the group that receives the information and ensure that FRAEWs were included in the information for leaseholders. This is a general information point. As I have said, the provisions in the Bill would provide remediation plans, but I am sure that someone living in a flat will want to know what the assessment of its external walls was, whether or not the remediation plan will deal with it.
