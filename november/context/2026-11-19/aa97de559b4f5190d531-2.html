Turn 12 · Amanda Martin (Portsmouth North) (Lab)

I thank my hon. Friend and the Committee for their work, and all who contributed to the report. As a former teacher, I have campaigned for decades for children to discover the joy of reading. Turn 13 · Helen Hayes

I thank my hon. Friend for her question and for her tribute to James and Terri for everything they do—it is a huge and generous gift to my hon. Friend’s city, and a great blessing, I am sure, to so many children in Portsmouth. Through our report, we set out, based on the evidence, what we believe the infrastructure looks like to ensure that that love of reading can be extended to every child across the country. I think for all members of the Committee who were a part of the inquiry, that became a burning passion—if it was not already, it certainly was by the end of our inquiry. This is certainly an area the Committee will continue to pursue and we will continue to hold the Government to account on going even further than they are already.
