Turn 4 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Baroness Hayman of Ullock) (Lab)

My Lords, the Environment Agency’s flood risk models are used to support flood risk management, investment and planning decisions in order to form emergency planning and response and to provide information to the public. Insurers and lenders draw on a range of evidence, including Environment Agency risk models, as well as commercial risk models. Flood risk is one of the many factors that influence property valuations, insurance underwriting and lending decisions.

Turn 8 · Baroness Hayman of Ullock (Lab)

The main thing we need to do is to provide resilience more broadly so that we have less flooding in the first place. There are many ways we can do that—natural flood risk management, for example—but we also need to ensure that home owners have the right information to assess their flood risk and the ability to access exactly what I was just talking about to make sure their homes are protected as best they can. It is important, as we move to Flood Re finishing in 2039, that we move to a system that protects all home owners, no matter when their house was built.
