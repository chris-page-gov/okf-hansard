Turn 41 · Sir John Hayes

I am grateful to the hon. Gentleman, who is perhaps the most articulate Member of this House, for giving way. Government can do things. Let me offer him—and, though him, the Minister—an example. Over the last 30 years, we have made systems more interconnected, and more interoperable. That is a huge mistake, because it concentrates risk. We see that now in the exposure of all kinds of systems to all kinds of threats; we saw it recently in supermarkets, and we see it often in Government. We can, through policy, change the presumption that making everything more connected makes it more useful and efficient. Actually, it makes it more easily attacked and harmed.
