Turn 9 · Dan Jarvis

As the hon. and gallant Gentleman will know, we work very closely across Government, including with partners in the Ministry of Defence and other Departments, to ensure that we are as resilient as we can be, given the nature of the threats, including those he describes. This is a matter that I personally take very seriously. That is why we are progressing work on the legislative front through the Cyber Security and Resilience (Network and Information Systems) Bill, and we will publish the national cyber action plan very soon.
