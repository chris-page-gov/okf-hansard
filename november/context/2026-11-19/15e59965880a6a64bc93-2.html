Turn 10 · Baroness Merron (Lab)

I am glad to hear that my noble friend benefited in that way. She makes an important point about the centrality of research in delivering our 10-year health plan, particularly that staff need the capacity to undertake it. I know that my noble friend would not expect me to pre-empt the 10-year workforce plan, but I can say that we are working with research funders so as to strengthen clinical academic career pathways through providing additional training places. We are also strengthening post-doctoral support and clearer funding opportunities, because we need the workforce capacity that she refers to.

Turn 11 · Baroness Blackwood of North Oxford (Con)

My Lords, I declare an interest as chair of the Health Data Research Service. The UK has globally competitive health data, but at the moment it is still painfully slow to access. Improving that access and the methods by which we do it is crucial to improving clinical trials. We have made a great deal of progress in setting up HDRS in the first instance, but we will be successful only with partnership across the health system and full-throated support from the Government. Can the Minister confirm that we have their support and how they intend to implement it going forward?

Turn 14 · Baroness Merron (Lab)

All the work that we are doing, including the establishment of the NIHR research infrastructure, the main vehicle for delivering clinical trials, is through the research delivery network. At its core, that is about ensuring the breadth of inclusion for people who should be part of clinical trials, but I also emphasise our work in ensuring that people come forward. We are making it easy to come forward and take part; that includes being able to register through the NHS app—something that was not available beforehand.

Turn 15 · Lord Patel (CB)

My Lords, to follow up on the question from the noble Baroness, Lady Blackwood, does the Minister agree that access to the single patient record, once it is established, will speed up the process of recruiting patients for clinical trials? That is the major cause of the delay in setting up clinical trials.
