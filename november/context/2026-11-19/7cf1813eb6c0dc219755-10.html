Part 2 of the Bill requires occupied medium-rise buildings to be registered. It also requires key building information to be provided and kept up to date. Meeting the duties will involve some small costs, including the registration fee and any other fees or charges levied by the registrar and may also include management costs and legal or other professional fees. Clause 64 allows those costs to be recovered through the service charge. That applies even where the lease does not expressly allow it. The clause applies to leases of seven years or more where the tenant pays a service charge. It does not apply to social housing tenancies.
