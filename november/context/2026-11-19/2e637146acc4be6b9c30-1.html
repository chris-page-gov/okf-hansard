The unit will identify insourcing opportunities, scrutinise departmental insourcing strategies and be a centre of expertise. Turn 4 · Mark Ferguson

I thank my hon. Friend for his engagement on this subject. The insourcing unit is already working across the public sector to support contracting authorities and prepare for the introduction of our public interest test. I declare an interest: I was proud as a trade unionist to have fought for the public interest test, and I am proud as a Minister in this Government to deliver the public interest test. In addition, Departments will set out and publish their five-year insourcing strategies, but that is the start, not the end of our ambition. Delivering that insourcing will benefit those in his Falkirk constituency, my Gateshead Central and Whickham constituency and the constituencies of hon. Members throughout the House. On his specific question, all procurements are subject to a robust set of approvals and we have no evidence that Departments are accelerating any of their decision-making …
