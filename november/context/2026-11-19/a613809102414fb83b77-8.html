Turn 58 · Laura Kyrke-Smith (Aylesbury) (Lab)

My residents still face huge challenges getting driving tests. One constituent, Andrew, logged on to the booking system at 6 am on Monday only to find 12,500 people ahead of him in a 70-minute queue. When he finally got to the front of the queue, there was no availability for tests within a 50-mile radius. The Government are rightly determined to help young people into education, training and employment, but in areas like Aylesbury and the villages, where we have very limited public transport, that often depends on young people being able to drive to college or work. Will the Leader of the House make time for us to further debate the issue and progress it?

Turn 63 · Sir Alan Campbell

My hon. Friend is right to praise the Government’s youth guarantee package, and I join him in doing so. We are taking action by capping maximum interest rates at 6% for plan 2 and plan 3 loans, and reintroducing maintenance grants. However, we continue to look for ways to make the system even fairer for students, who I have to say were let down in a big way by the previous Government. We want to make the system fairer for students, graduates and, indeed, taxpayers, in a financially sustainable way, but I will make sure that Ministers have heard his concerns.

Turn 64 · Clive Jones (Wokingham) (LD)

Will the Leader of the House join me in paying tribute to Lady Elizabeth Godsal MBE, who sadly passed away last week? Lady Elizabeth gave decades of service to local institutions, including 26 years as the high steward of Wokingham. She was a vice-lord lieutenant of the Royal County of Berkshire, high sheriff of Berkshire in 1990, chief president of St John Ambulance, chairman of the board of visitors of Reading Prison, patron of Girlguiding Royal Berkshire, and a former president of the Wokingham Society. Lady Elizabeth made an exceptional contribution to Wokingham and will be greatly missed.
