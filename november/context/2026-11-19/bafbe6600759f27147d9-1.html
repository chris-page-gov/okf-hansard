Turn 3 · The Minister for Security (Dan Jarvis)

The Government are taking decisive steps to strengthen our national resilience. We have launched the home defence programme and overhauled pandemic preparedness plans with a billion pounds in funding. Recently, we launched our Be Ready campaign to help households prepare for everyday emergencies such as flooding, and we are actively co-ordinating resilience planning across Government. I am determined to make our country the most resilient it can be.

I will reflect on what she said and come back to her with a more detailed response.

Turn 7 · Dan Jarvis

My hon. Friend raises an important point. I can assure him that cost of living pressures were absolutely at the forefront of our minds as we designed the campaign, and we have worked very closely with charities such as the Trussell Trust across the country to develop the campaign. We recognise that not everyone can take every step, but if households that can take action do so, it frees up emergency services to help those most in need in a crisis. Many of the steps are free, such as signing up for the priority services register, and we will continue to work with charities such as those in my hon. Friend’s constituency as we deliver the campaign.
