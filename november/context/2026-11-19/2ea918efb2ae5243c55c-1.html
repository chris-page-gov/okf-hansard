Turn 1 · The Prime Minister (Andy Burnham)

The chair of the UK covid-19 inquiry has today published the inquiry’s module 7 report, which investigated the systems and strategies used for testing, contact tracing, and self-isolation across the four nations of the UK. I have laid a copy of the report before both Houses of Parliament. The chair recognises the “unprecedented restrictions and expectations” placed on the public and the profound shift in daily life they required. She highlights the people who felt “forgotten and let down” when isolating and heard accounts of their “unenviable choice of self-isolating and losing income, or going to work and risking spreading the virus.” I recognise the dedication of all those who worked throughout the pandemic to protect the public, including those involved in developing and delivering testing, contact tracing and support for people required to isolate.
