Turn 59 · Speaker unlabelled

On amendment 44, I agree with the hon. Member for Taunton and Wellington—it occasionally happens, where there is common concern—that it would mean that institutional investors, funds and build-to-rent investors could never be connected persons. Its explanatory statement calls them “passive” investors and lenders. A fund that controls a freeholder is not passive. It decides whether work happens or not. If the amendment were to pass, what would prevent every freeholder of a block with unsafe cladding from being restructured into a fund so that its owners are beyond reach? I hope the Conservatives accept that the amendment would create exactly that loophole.
