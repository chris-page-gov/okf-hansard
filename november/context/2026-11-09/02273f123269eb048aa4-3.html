My Lords, one of the commendable objectives defined in the 10-year health plan is the development of women’s health centres. Can the Minister confirm that these will be not just advisory centres but diagnostic and, if possible, treatment centres, and that they will be appropriately staffed and funded?

Turn 19 · Baroness McIntosh of Hudnall (Lab)

My Lords, does my noble friend agree that even where chronic pain has been identified in women, or in men, there is often considerable reluctance to accept the available treatment, because long-term pain is very often treated with drugs which have quite significant side effects? Can my noble friend say what work is being done, possibly supported by the health service, to develop pain relief strategies which do not create those side effects?

Turn 20 · Baroness Merron (Lab)

Women are certainly more likely to experience multiple long-term and limiting conditions, and with that comes pain. In addition, we are absolutely committed to ensuring that there is a standard of care for, for example, gynaecological procedures such as hysteroscopy. We have consulted with the Campaign Against Painful Hysteroscopy for women with lived experiences around painful procedures, and we have gathered their insights. That is an approach we will continue to take as we develop pain management pathways across a whole range of conditions.
