Turn 29 · Baroness Bennett of Manor Castle (GP)

The Minister said that the public expect Ministers to use this power sparingly. That reflects what the noble Lord, Lord Hunt, said about the power being used sparingly in the past. However, to pick up something that the noble Lord, Lord Stevens, said about what has happened across the Atlantic, health is a great deal more political in its provision than perhaps it has been in the past, and these have become controversial, much played out issues. We also have a much more febrile political situation. As a direct example for the Minister, drawing on experience, if we have two hospitals that have children’s emergency services and the suggestion is that only one of them can continue and there is a political contest in this area, is the decision going to be left locally or is the Secretary of State going to intervene?

Turn 30 · Baroness Merron (Lab)
