Turn 40 · Speaker unlabelled

Finally, Amendment 86 would require integrated care boards to report annually on the directions that have applied to them and the consequences those directions have had for their decisions, expenditure, risks, local priorities and outcomes. It would provide an opportunity to look back at the effects of ministerial intervention, rather than simply examining the justification for it at the time. It would allow us to establish whether the direction achieved what it was intended to achieve, whether it had unintended consequences and whether responsibility for those outcomes rested with the board or the Secretary of State, or was shared between them.
