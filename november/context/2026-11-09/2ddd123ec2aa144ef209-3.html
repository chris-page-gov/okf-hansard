Turn 24 · Lord Stevens of Birmingham (CB)

Certainly. The 2022 Act says that a direction “may not be given” by the Secretary of State “in relation to the provision of any drug, medicine or other treatment, or the use of any diagnostic technique, unless NICE has made a recommendation or issued guidance as to its clinical and cost effectiveness and the direction is not inconsistent with that recommendation or guidance”. NICE has to have set out its stall and, if it has, the Secretary of State can direct on the back of it. What the Bill says is that, in any case where NICE has not set out its stall, the Secretary of State has complete freedom to form a personal view about what should or should not be available on the NHS.
