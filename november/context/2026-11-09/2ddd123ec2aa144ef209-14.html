Turn 106 · Speaker unlabelled

Turning to Amendments 33, 36 and 51, tabled by the noble Baroness, Lady Bennett, I feel, to put it mildly, that there is disagreement here as to the best way to ensure the best outcomes for patients. I certainly do not recognise the NHS that the noble Baroness describes. I emphasise that the 10-year health plan is not a year-by-year plan; it is exactly what it says. The Government are absolutely focused on improving access and outcomes and reducing waiting times. That is what the public rightly expect; they want us to prioritise that. We take a practical approach that makes use of spare capacity to meet patient needs.
