Turn 4 · Lord in Waiting/Government Whip (Lord Katz) (Lab)

My Lords, the Government are closely monitoring resilience and water refill, but there is no immediate risk to public water supplies. As of 3 November, reservoir storage across England stood at 60.1%, 17% below average for the time of year, with eight reservoirs classed as exceptionally low. However, this is an improvement on the position a month ago. The National Drought Group, which is convened by my honourable friend Minister Hardy, is meeting weekly to ensure water companies take every action available to minimise the impacts of this drought, while the Environment Agency is prioritising protection of public water supplies in areas facing severe drought.

Turn 6 · Lord Katz (Lab)

Defra and the Environment Agency are working rapidly to improve and accelerate determination times for abstraction permits. We have reduced the average wait and processing times in significant performance outliers, with permitting performance now at its highest level since 2018. The Environment Agency is also improving digital systems to reduce administrative burden and complexity. We are reviewing approaches to high-flow abstraction, trialling dynamic abstraction and making up to £15 million available for on-farm reservoirs, which help farmers capture and store water during wetter periods, alongside taking steps to remove planning barriers to reservoir construction.
