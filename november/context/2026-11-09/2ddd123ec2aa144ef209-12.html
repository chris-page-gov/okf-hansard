Turn 91 · Speaker unlabelled

As I have previously said, we need to take a long-term view here. We have an ageing population, we are aware of more conditions, we have greater emphasis on mental health and its parity with physical health, and we have a public and mainstream political parties taking an increasingly sceptical view of immigration. Even if we are able to get more British NEETs into work, who will pay the taxes for this ageing population? If we continue employing ever more doctors and nurses, we will, as our previous Secretary of State said, eventually run out of money. Fortunately, to take the pressure off the NHS, we see more employers and trade unions offering private health plans. We see individuals often paying for independent healthcare themselves, once again taking the pressure off the NHS.
