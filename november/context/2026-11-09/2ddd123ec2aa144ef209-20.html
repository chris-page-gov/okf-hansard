Turn 158 · Speaker unlabelled

With respect to the question about workforce raised by the noble Lord, Lord Evans, our 10-year workforce plan will put the NHS workforce on a sustainable footing and ensure that we have the staff that it needs. The Secretary of State confirmed to the Health and Social Care Committee that the workforce plan will be published in the new year, which I hope is a helpful update. The noble Lord also asked about the brain cancer review translating into outcomes. There will be a national action plan that follows the expert review which, as the noble Lord acknowledged, will take only three months. But it is the findings of the review that will shape that plan to get new treatments out of the lab and to patients faster.
