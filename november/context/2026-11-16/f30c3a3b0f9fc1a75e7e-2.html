Many parents and carers in my constituency are backing calls to end attendance fines for families whose children cannot attend school because of unmet SEND needs while they wait months—sometimes years—for assessment. What steps is the Minister taking to ensure that families already at breaking point are supported rather than penalised?

Turn 11 · Georgia Gould

The hon. Member is right that we need to have a support-first approach to families; that is what our guidance sets out. We are putting in place a whole range of new support to wrap around families and schools—for example, the new experts-at-hand service is starting to be rolled out by councils around the country; and we are bringing forward work on family first partnerships to provide early help for families—and we are determined that that happens in practice in every part of the country.
