Turn 42 · Anna Turley

The hon. Lady will know of the huge progress made under the Government to end the abuse of our asylum process and to stop the boats. The numbers of people crossing the Channel are down by over 40% this year. Eighty thousand people who had no right to be here have been returned, which is up over 50%. Immigration enforcement has been increased, with an over 80% rise in illegal working arrests. The last Government allowed net migration of 2.5 million in just four years—around one in 30 people in this country came here under the last few years of her Government—so a bit of contrition from the Conservative party would be good.

Turn 43 · Sammy Wilson (East Antrim) (DUP)

This is an appalling and frightening decision, not just because of the effect it will have on the people of this small village but because of what it says about the power of the state to impose life-changing impacts on people because the Government have failed in their policy of stopping the invasion of our country by illegal immigrants. Can the Minister confirm whether those who will be housed in this centre will be issued with the Home Office guidance asking them not to rape, sexually abuse or harass the women and children they come across? What assurance does she think that gives to the women of this village, who will be outnumbered by these people by eight to one? Is it not better to send them back to where they came from when they land on our shores, rather than try to rehouse them?

Turn 44 · Anna Turley

Anyone who has committed a crime on these shores would now be eligible for removal from point zero of their sentence. As I have said, we are removing more people—80,000 people, up more than 50% on the previous Government—so that action will be taken in relation to anyone committing a crime. We take this extremely seriously and we expect anyone coming to these shores to abide by our laws. The right hon. Gentleman seems to be completely unaware that two of these sites are already in operation and the impact on crime has been completely negligible, as expressed by the local chief constable, so his scaremongering does him no service.
