Turn 32 · Alex Mayer (Dunstable and Leighton Buzzard) (Lab)

I was not expecting to be called so early in the debate, so I will make some limited points. We are in the midst of a climate and biodiversity emergency, and I absolutely want as many people as possible to hear the evidence, because it matters, but we need to address a couple of issues. First, I would not want to compel any broadcaster to have to show a briefing, as much as I want as many people as possible to see the information. We need to look to the future: imagine another Government declaring a national emergency on something else that we did not agree with. I want people to find out about climate and nature risks, but we need to be cognisant of that.
