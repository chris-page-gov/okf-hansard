Turn 45 · Rebecca Paul

I am going to come to Edinburgh in a moment, and I do not want to spoil what I am about to say, so I will not give too much away; but I think that, in reality, the levy will end up filling potholes. One operator I spoke to runs 39 holiday parks across Britain and employed approximately 14,000 people last summer, mainly in coastal communities. More than a third of those employees were under the age of 24. These are young people getting their first jobs, gaining confidence, learning skills, and taking their first steps towards financial independence. If the Government are truly committed to getting young people into work, this levy is a backward step. If they genuinely want to create jobs for the next generation, they would do well to take inspiration from our recent proposals to cut employer national insurance for under-25s.
