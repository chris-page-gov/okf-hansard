Turn 80 · John Whitby

I agree. Of course there is hope. We are nowhere near finished yet, but we have to do more. We have to ramp up our transition to clean energy, and we have to stay the course on electric vehicles. We have to put even greater urgency into building out the grid and we have to keep attention on hard-to-abate sectors. I remind the Minister that Peak Cluster is still waiting for a route to market. We need to get on with removals. We need to protect our food production. I urge the Government to reiterate their commitment to tackling the defining challenge of our time and to hold a UK-wide briefing so that the public can be in possession of all the facts, worrying though they often are.
