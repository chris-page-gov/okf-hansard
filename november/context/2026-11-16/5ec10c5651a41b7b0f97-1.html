Turn 1 · The Minister for Creative Industries, Media and Online Safety (Ian Murray)

Children and families have a right to be safe online. Online spaces are as much a part of public life as any high street, and people should be able to communicate, learn and participate without being exposed to serious and preventable harm. That is why the Government are committed to ensuring that online services take effective responsibility for making their platforms safer. Today, I am laying before Parliament amendments to four of Ofcom’s codes of practice and a new code of practice—“Illegal Content Code of Practice for User-to-User Services: Proactive Technologies”—under the Online Safety Act 2023. These measures mark a further important step in cracking down on illegal content online, strengthening protections for children and families against child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, terrorism and other serious illegal harms.
