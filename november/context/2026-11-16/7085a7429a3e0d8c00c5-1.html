Turn 3 · The Minister for School Standards (Georgia Gould)

The UK Government remain steadfast in our support for Ukraine. We are proud to be delivering a GCSE in Ukrainian and intend to work with AQA to introduce the qualification, with first teaching planned for September 2028 and assessments from summer 2029. The new qualification demonstrates our solidarity with Ukraine and our wish to help young Ukrainians maintain their language and cultural heritage while supporting future work, study and engagement with Ukraine.
