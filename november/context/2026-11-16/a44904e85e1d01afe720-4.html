Turn 27 · Stephen Doughty

It is a pleasure to serve under your chairpersonship, Sir Desmond, and to be with the Committee this evening. The draft orders, which were laid before the House on 13 October, will permit the UK Government to make financial contributions of up to £650 million towards the 17th replenishment of the African Development Fund—known as ADF-17. They will also increase the UK’s existing commitment ceiling of the multilateral debt relief initiative of the African Development Fund from £451.87 million to £508.07 million, and for the International Development Association of the World Bank from £2,835.97 million to £3,083.89 million. Finally, they will permit the UK Government to contribute up to £52.6 million for the 13th replenishment of the International Fund for Agricultural Development—known as IFAD13—covering 2025 to 2027.
