Turn 17 · The Earl of Clancarty (CB)

My Lords, the Demos study referred to by the noble Baroness, Lady Gill, showed that regular presenting by serving politicians of not just current affairs programmes but entertainment programmes as well is not tolerated across the rest of Europe. Should we not introduce legislation to stop this? As the noble Baroness said, it is a failure in our democracy.

Turn 18 · Baroness Mackenzie of Sherwood (Lab)

It is entirely reasonable to look at the blurring of the lines between news and current affairs, and how they are being blurred further, for example with chat shows. We need to beware of sweeping up the whole of entertainment and all of those genres. I am sure that many of us would be sad if “Strictly Come Dancing” no longer had access to working or former politicians—only my point of view, obviously. In all seriousness, as was said, the Green Paper has set out some initial thinking in this area and we intend to do further work to get in line with our European partners and ensure the safety of news and impartiality across the genres, as appropriate.
