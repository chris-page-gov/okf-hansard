Turn 36 · Baroness Smith of Llanfaes (PC)

To follow the previous questions from the noble Baroness, Lady Royall, it is positive to hear about the move forward, particularly with regard to the online environment, for women parliamentarians. So as to hear more about the collective aim for tackling violence against women and girls, can the Minister share with us what other actions are being taken collectively to tackle violence against women and girls and to change society across the Commonwealth? I have a second question relating to points raised around the engagement of young people in the Commonwealth. Can the Minister share how young people are not just being engaged with the Commonwealth but are able to influence the outcomes? On the priorities around jobs, investment, climate resilience, democracy and opportunity, how can we ensure that young people can shape those outcomes, rather than just be engaged in the process?
