Turn 1 · The Minister for Security (Dan Jarvis)

Rupert Jones has been appointed as the independent reviewer of terrorism legislation and independent reviewer of state threats legislation, following a robust competition in accordance with the requirements of the governance code on public appointments. Rupert Jones has held a range of legal and judicial offices. These include judge of the upper tribunal, authorised judge of the High Court and legal member of the investigatory powers tribunal. He has also served as Chief Justice of St Helena, Ascension Island and Tristan da Cunha. He will succeed Jonathan Hall KC, who will leave office on 22 November 2026. Jonathan Hall KC has served as independent reviewer of terrorism legislation since 2019 and was the first independent reviewer of state threats legislation, appointed in February 2024.
