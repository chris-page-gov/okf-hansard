Turn 2 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Education (Paul Waugh)

Local authorities must make safe, suitable and free travel arrangements for eligible children. Buses and coaches are regulated as public services vehicles and are expected to meet high safety standards. Licensing authorities are also expected to ensure that taxis and private hire vehicles are safe and roadworthy, and that their drivers are fit and proper.

Turn 3 · Sarah Edwards

It is the 33rd anniversary of the M40 school minibus tragedy, in which 12 children and their teacher lost their lives. I extend my condolences to all those affected. Exemptions remain in the law that allow unqualified and untrained staff or teachers to drive a school minibus, when other drivers would normally be required to have a D1 licence. Despite assurances in February from Department for Education and Department for Transport Ministers that they would meet to address the licensing exemption, no action has so far been taken, and these section 19 permits continue to put the safety of children at risk. I recently heard a report of a school minibus leaking brake fluid for weeks, because there is no set minibus inspection for schools. The official guidance for minibuses is 13 years out of date. Will the Minister please take action on this matter, or do we need to wait for another tragedy …
