Turn 44 · Speaker unlabelled

38: After Clause 6, insert the following new Clause— “Magistrate training and allowances(1) The Lord Chancellor must—(a) consult the Lord Chief Justice to confirm that a sufficient number of magistrates who exercise functions affected by sections 1, 2, 5 and 6 have completed training in the exercise of those functions,(b) consult lay justices about the rates of allowances payable to them, and(c) implement the payment of those rates of allowances to lay justices.(2) The Lord Chancellor must make a written statement to both Houses of Parliament when the duties in subsection (1) have been completed.”Member’s explanatory statement This amendment seeks to probe the relevant training and allowances payable to magistrates.

Turn 47 · Lord Ashcombe (Con)

My Lords, we have heard concerns from noble Lords about the impact these reforms are going to have on magistrates’ courts, especially if they are not prepared for them. Amendment 37, in the name of the noble and learned Lord, Lord Thomas of Cwmgiedd, in the previous group also sought confirmation regarding the training provided to magistrates. These amendments, brought to us by the noble Baroness, Lady Hamwee, seek also to question the allowances that will be available to magistrates. These amendments raise a legitimate concern about the extent to which magistrates are adequately prepared and supported for these reforms. I look forward to the Minister’s response.
