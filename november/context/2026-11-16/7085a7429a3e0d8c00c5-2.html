Turn 10 · Jim Shannon (Strangford) (DUP)

I thank the Minister for her helpful and comprehensive answers. Can the Minister share the strategy and policy for the Ukrainian GCSE that the Government are bringing forward in England with the relevant Northern Ireland Minister, so that we could do the same in Northern Ireland?
