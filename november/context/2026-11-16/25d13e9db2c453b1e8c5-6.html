Turn 42 · Lord Farmer (Con)

I just come back to what I was saying about separating families outside the court system. The presumption is important for them. They do not have a judge looking at it; they are not in the court system. The fact that there is a presumption that the child needs to have access to both parents, where safe, is a bulwark against the parent who has the guardianship of the child abusing the other parent. The presumption says that it is in the child’s best interest. It is for the great number of children outside the court process that this is a safeguard. That is the point I was making.
