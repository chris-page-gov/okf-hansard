Turn 18 · Rebecca Smith (South West Devon) (Con)

Alan Milburn is expected to echo our Conservative new deal for young people pledge to halve employer national insurance for 21 to 24-year-olds. Mr Milburn has already admitted that Labour’s increased minimum wage for young people has imposed a lot of cost on businesses. Is the Secretary of State ready to accept that if the Government are to guarantee work experience, training and employment opportunities for young people, they must make them more affordable for businesses to deliver?

Turn 19 · Lucy Powell

I thought that slightly more humility might have been the order of the day, given that the 1 million young people who are now not in education, employment or training were educated under the previous Conservative Government. That was the generation who had their Sure Start centres closed and all their youth services cut back to the bone, and who in their teens had their education denied because of covid. The hon. Member’s Government refused and cancelled the catch-up programme after covid—with that programme, we would not be in the situation we are in right now.
