On a point of order, Madam Deputy Speaker. Madam Deputy Speaker, Mr. Speaker and yourself are rightly jealous of the Chamber’s rights, in particular that policy announcements should be made here in the House to MPs. Turn 2 · Madam Deputy Speaker (Caroline Nokes)

I thank the hon. Member for her point of her order and for notice of it. She will know that as Chair, I am certainly not responsible for the actions of private companies or indeed Members of the House of Lords. She has placed her concerns on the record, and I am sure Ministers will wish to reflect on them and perhaps respond shortly. As always, my expectation and that of Mr Speaker is that policy announcements will be made in this House by Ministers.
