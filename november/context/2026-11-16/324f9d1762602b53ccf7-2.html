Turn 9 · Lord Palmer of Childs Hill (LD)

My Lords, the Jewish community protective security grant was introduced in 2016 to address an exceptional threat. A decade later, extraordinary security has become a permanent feature of British Jewish life. Public spending continues to rise simply to keep Jewish communities safe. No other measure of success should obscure that fundamental failure. The objective must be to end the need for such protection, not to accept its ever-growing scale as normal. What is the Government’s strategy for reducing the underlying threat, rather than simply spending more to contain it?

Turn 10 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

As the noble Lord will know, I have stood at this Dispatch Box and moved resolutions that the Islamic Revolutionary Guard and the Islamic Movement for Companions of the Right be designated and sanctioned. I have supported the sanctioning of 550 Iranian individuals and entities, including the IRGC, who have been disrupting Jewish community life. I supported putting the Iranian regime in the enhanced tier of the foreign influence registration scheme, and have done so. We are aware of those threats and will continue to tackle them, but, in the meantime, we are committed as a Government—as shown by the fact that we have put in £32.4 million this year—to support Jewish communities to have resilience and safeguarding at the local level.

Turn 12 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

With due respect to the noble Lord, I do not accept that UK Government foreign policy puts a target on the backs of British Jews. We have a right to take a view on the position taken by the Government of Israel. The State of Israel has an absolute right to exist, and we will defend that, but we have a right to take a position on both Israeli policy and those who take a different view on some of the issues in Israel and Palestine at the moment. The key point is that we also have a duty to ensure that we do not tolerate antisemitism, that we support the Jewish community and that those who seek to inflict terrorist or harsh impacts on the Jewish community are held to account by this UK Government.
