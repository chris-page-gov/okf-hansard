Turn 137 · Lord Katz (Lab)

My Lords, I will try to be quick—though maybe not quite as quick as the noble Viscount, Lord Hailsham. I thank the noble Viscount for tabling Amendment 76, which, as we have heard, seeks to cause Clauses 1 to 6 to expire when the backlog in the Crown Court returns to 40,000 cases over a sustained period. These reforms are not short-term emergency measures; the pressures facing the criminal justice system have long-term drivers that are not going away. We have discussed many of them over the two days in Committee, such as the way that digital and other types of evidence have prolonged and extended court cases. Reducing the backlog alone will not alleviate those pressures, and demand will continue to grow.
