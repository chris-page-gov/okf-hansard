Turn 4 · Josh MacAlister

My hon. Friend is absolutely right to raise the issue of persistent absence. Since the election, this Government have made real progress on the issue nationally; 115,000 fewer children have been persistently absent since we came to power. However, there are areas in our country where the issue is deeper. The approach we are taking with Mission Coastal brings partners together at a local level, so that there is learning not just from the top down, but from the ground back up to Government, and we can make the changes needed to ensure that every child gets to school when they need to be there.
