Turn 115 · Baroness Smith of Cluny (Lab)

My Lords, the Government recognise and value the importance of the independence of juries. Juries must be free to deliberate and reach their verdict without pressure or interference, on the basis of the evidence presented in court and the law as directed by the judge. Advocates have an important and distinct role in putting their case before the court and ensuring that defendants receive a fair trial, but it would be inappropriate for advocates to remind juries that they can reach verdicts according to their conscience or to encourage juries to do so. Jurors swear an oath to try the defendant faithfully by applying the law as directed by the judge to only the evidence presented in court, and there must be confidence that cases going through the Crown Court will be decided according to the same legal principles.
