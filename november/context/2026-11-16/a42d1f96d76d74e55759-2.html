Turn 9 · Paul Waugh

AI chatbots are not in our schools. Our guidance is very clear that they should not be used in schools. Although the hon. Gentleman talks about inaction, it was his Government who failed to act in this crucial area. Our ed tech test beds are gathering the evidence, and are ensuring that teachers are in the driving seat, and that anything we do in schools is guided by the safety and efficacy of the technology.
