Turn 1 · The Economic Secretary to the Treasury (Lucy Rigby)

The Government are committed to ensuring that people and businesses across the UK can continue to access the banking services they need. The Government commissioned an independent review chaired by Richard Lloyd OBE to assess whether declining access to in-person banking services is causing consumer detriment, the scale and materiality of any detriment, and which customers are most affected. Further to my statement on 14 May [HCWS14] the Government have today published the “Access to Banking Services Review, Report of the Independent Review”. This statement sets out the Government’s response to the review. The review has found that, while most customers can bank effectively through remote channels, a minority will continue to need access to in-person banking services for the foreseeable future.
