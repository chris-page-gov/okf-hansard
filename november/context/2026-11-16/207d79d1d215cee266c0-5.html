Turn 39 · Ian Murray

The hon. Gentleman talks about people reverting to pen and paper and other systems kicking in. The whole point of having a resilience plan is that when things happen across the tech stack or any of the systems, other systems kick in. That is why we did not see widespread disruption across the whole public sector. Yes, it was an inconvenience. My own emails went down until about 2 o’clock on Friday. I know that because the test email I sent myself on Thursday night arrived at about 2 pm on Friday, and the acknowledgment that it had arrived bounced back. The backlog has been cleared, and Microsoft resolved the problem as quickly as it could, but the whole point of the Government’s cyber action plan is to make sure that all Government Departments, local government and our public sector have resilience, and that the private sector also has resilience.
