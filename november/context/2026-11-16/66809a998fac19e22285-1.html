Turn 5 · Lord Murray of Blidworth (Con)

My Lords, as at Second Reading, I declare my interest as a practising barrister. When I was a very junior barrister, I was a practitioner in the criminal courts, both in the magistrates’ and youth courts and on appeals to the Crown Court, which is relevant to the clauses that we are considering in this group. The changes in the appeal mechanisms from the magistrates’ court to the Crown Court would not, in my submission, reduce the backlog but would undermine faith in our criminal justice system. I say this because in the present system, which works well, a person who is convicted or sentenced in the magistrates’ or youth courts may appeal as of right to the Crown Court. If it is an appeal against conviction, there is a rehearing of the charge before the Crown Court judge and, usually, two lay magistrates.
