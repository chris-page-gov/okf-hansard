Turn 121 · Speaker unlabelled

Finally, Amendments 62B, 64A and 71A are signed only by my noble and learned friend Lord Keen of Elie and not by me, but I can assure the Committee that the distinction is one of authorship and not agreement—I think my name was left off for time reasons. They seek to prevent the Bill coming into force until the Government have published the evidence on which they base their assertion that limiting the right to jury trials will reduce the backlog in the courts. Of course, we dispute that the measures are necessary, but on any analysis, we suggest that the Government should not bring in those provisions without publishing the evidence which is said to underpin them. For those reasons, I commend those amendments.
