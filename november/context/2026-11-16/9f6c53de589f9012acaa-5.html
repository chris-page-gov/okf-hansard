Turn 36 · Alex Mayer

It is important that young people get as much information as possible. When I go to schools, young people are particularly concerned about the climate emergency and really want to make a big difference. We need to give a message of hope, not just fear. We have some fantastic things to talk about: the fact that greenhouse gas emissions have halved since the 1990s is a fantastic achievement by people across this country, and we have scientists working on future plans as well. To go back to my point that we need to reach people who do not agree with me, we need to make sure that the messages get through to people where they are at. That might mean adverts on petrol pumps showing the carbon cost, or maybe “Thank you” messages to all the bus passengers for the difference they make by travelling in a greener way.
