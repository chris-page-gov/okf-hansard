Turn 38 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Education (Paul Waugh)

I thank my hon. Friend for the campaigning work that she has done to bring about the historic expansion of free school meals to children whose families are receiving universal credit. Like me, she was on free school meals herself, and we both have personal knowledge of the impact that that can have on a family’s finances. She can be reassured that, along with the DWP, we are looking closely at the whole idea of auto-enrolment. Some councils, including Gateshead, have made it work, but there is much more to be discussed with the DWP.
