Drone Attack Risks

Turn 25 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

Some £35 million has been invested this year in counterdrone security at high-risk prisons. We have already put in £40 million across 34 sites to look at improving security at prisons with the potential for drones. There is the potential for counterdrone activity, and the police have powers to do that. I will look into the detail because prisons are not in my direct responsibility area, and I will get back to the noble Lord on the point.
