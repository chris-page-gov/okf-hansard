Turn 13 · Dr Savage

I wholeheartedly endorse my hon. Friend’s good food Bill, which is timely and important. What I have outlined are questions of food security, economic stability, public health and national resilience. The Government know that. In January they published a national security assessment, albeit in somewhat edited form, on the degradation, and even the collapse, of global ecosystems. Its conclusions should have commanded national attention. The report found with high confidence that every critical ecosystem that it examined is on a pathway to collapse. It warned that some could begin to collapse from 2030 and concluded that without significant improvements to our food systems and supply chains, Britain would be unlikely to maintain food security if those collapses trigger international competition for food.
