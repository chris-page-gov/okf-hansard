Turn 16 · Anna Turley

My hon. Friend makes an important point. These large sites are designed to be self-contained, with basic services provided on site to reduce demand and pressure on local services and minimise the need for anyone to leave. We work with voluntary organisations to ensure that activities are available on site, helping to create an environment that encourages people to remain on site all day and meets their day-to-day needs. As I said, we know that existing sites have operated very successfully with basic on-site services and 24/7 security designed to minimise the impact on local communities.
