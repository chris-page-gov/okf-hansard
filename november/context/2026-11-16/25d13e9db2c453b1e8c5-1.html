My Lords, I understand and share the concern of the noble Baroness, Lady Brinton—and indeed as expressed by the noble Lord—that the ability to bring private prosecutions is not misused. The threat or instigation of a private prosecution can understandably be deeply troubling for defendants, particularly where the individual bringing the prosecution is known to them. It is therefore crucial that private prosecutions are subject to appropriate scrutiny and safeguards.
