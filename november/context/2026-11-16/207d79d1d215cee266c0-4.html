Turn 27 · Ian Murray

The Bill will return to this House relatively soon. It updates the Network and Information Systems Regulations 2018 to bring some of these companies into scope, including data centres, and contains a provision to extend the scope, if necessary, to include more organisations and more sectors of the economy. It will be back in this House before the end of the year, and we will be able to debate these matters in more detail then, but its purpose is to ensure that we have greater resilience and to update the regulations.

Turn 29 · Ian Murray

I am glad that my hon. Friend managed to talk to his NHS trust and resolve that issue over the weekend. Turn 31 · Ian Murray

I can only re-emphasise what I have said already. The Government are backing our work on the tech stack with taxpayers’ money to see how we can seed good businesses in this country that wish to grow and enter these marketplaces. It is difficult for the Government to do that, because the big suppliers are there for us to secure value for money for the taxpayer.
