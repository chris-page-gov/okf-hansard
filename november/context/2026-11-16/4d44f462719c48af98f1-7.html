Turn 49 · Mr Frith

I thank my hon. Friend for his question and his typically excellent representation of his constituency. As I have said, we cannot expect to underpin trust unless we move at the speed of trust. That is about remaking the case, and giving assurances on data protection and the security by design of the system that we commission, which will be securely built. The federated nature of the platform makes the data more secure. I fully appreciate the concerns that some of his constituents have raised, and I will ensure that we continue to make the case, as we need to bring everybody with us if we are to fully realise the benefits of digital capability in our healthcare system.
