Turn 5 · Baroness Deech (CB)

My Lords, it is a tragedy that I have to ask this Question. The Minister knows the perilous situation faced by some 600 Jewish community buildings in the UK—synagogues, schools, charities and old-age homes—and about the arson attacks, assaults and stabbings, because it is impossible, it seems, to stop the violent protests, marches and Islamic extremists in our midst. Those buildings need protection, 24 hours a day. We are grateful for the support the Government give to the Community Security Trust, but almost all that money goes on guards and personnel. The institutional buildings face significant new costs for reinforced doors, emergency exits, alarms, gates, fences, bomb-proof glass, et cetera. My own small community faces a bill of hundreds of thousands of pounds for those security infrastructure elements. These measures are essential for the community to go about its business safely. …

Turn 6 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

The Government recognise that everybody has a right to enjoy their buildings, synagogues and property without the threat of attack and destruction. That is why the record £32.4 million we have made available for this year is being put in place, along with a further £4 million announced in April, to put additional resources into that area. We keep that under review at all times. I cannot give a commitment about future years until we have the CSR later in this Parliament, but I say to the noble Baroness that this is a vital area of government support. We will use all methods—not just security funding but providing intelligence and support to the police—to ensure that we stop those threats against the Jewish community.

Turn 8 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

I defend the right of individuals to criticise the policies of the State of Israel, and the UK Government have a very clear view on some of those policies. However, that does not excuse or allow for attacks on the Jewish community, which are abhorrent and should be stopped. We need to put resources in, and we have. Over the next three years, we are giving an extra £250 million to police to work in conjunction with Jewish communities, which goes back to the point the noble Baroness, Lady Deech, mentioned. Ultimately, the attacks are the responsibility of those who seek to make them. I will not have those attacks put on the Government of this United Kingdom, who are doing all we can to stop antisemitism in our community.
