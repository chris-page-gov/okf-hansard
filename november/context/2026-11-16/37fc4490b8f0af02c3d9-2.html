Turn 11 · Lord Gardiner of Kimble (Con)

My Lords, I declare my farming interests. It is clear that confidence remains extremely low and farmers are suffering losses, so the idea of profits is really the wrong word, I am afraid. In the Suffolk Chamber of Commerce survey, the question was, “What improvements would matter to businesses in rural and coastal areas in particular?” Some 88% of respondents said, “Better mobile and digital connectivity”, and that would make a big difference to business. I wonder what Defra and other departments are doing to co-ordinate this vital part of enhancing our infrastructure.

It is important that this Government put so much emphasis on cross-departmental working, because another big challenge to rural profitability for businesses is better transport links, which we are also working to improve.

Turn 14 · Baroness Hayman of Ullock (Lab)

We are looking to invest more into horticulture. We see that as an important part of improving food security. It is really important, as well, that we look at how we can best address the loss of abattoirs, which is very complex. We are keen to support small abattoirs but there are skills issues, for example, as well as that of people wanting to work in abattoirs. It is a much bigger problem than just one of supporting abattoirs financially or giving them incentives, but we are looking at that. I know that the Department for Education is also looking at supporting rural schools. There is an awful lot that we need to get in place if rural communities and businesses are to have the right kind of support for the future.
