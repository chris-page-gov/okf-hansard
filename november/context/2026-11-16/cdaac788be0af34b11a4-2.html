Special Schools: Funding

Commons · 2026-11-16 · Turns 9–9 of 9 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 9 · Georgia Gould

Our reforms are trying to do two things. First, we are making the mainstream genuinely inclusive; so many children could have thrived in mainstream but are not able to. We are rolling out new teacher training and Experts at Hand, and extra funding is going into mainstream schools to ensure that they are genuinely inclusive. We also recognise that we need new specialist places. The £3.7 billion is to create 60,000 new specialist places, which can be in inclusion bases linked to mainstream schools or in new special schools. We are investing heavily: we are putting £4 billion into early intervention and £3.7 billion into capital. We just did not see that investment under the previous Government.
