Turn 65 · Speaker unlabelled

I close with the words of the incredibly brave Jack Sykes, whose words were not heard by social services, Cafcass or the family court when he raised those concerns about the risk that his father posed to him and his brother. He spoke some words that have created a legacy. When firefighters found Jack that evening, he was in a truly terrible state, with third-degree burns on 56% of his body, smoke inhalation and lacerations from falling out of the attic hatch in an attempt to drag his younger brother to safety. He managed to say to those firefighters, “My dad did this, and he did it on purpose”.
