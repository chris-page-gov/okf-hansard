Turn 25 · The Minister for Policing and Crime (Sarah Jones)

I beg to move, That the Committee has considered the draft Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (Amendment) Order 2026. It is a real pleasure to serve under your chairmanship, Ms Vaz. The order was laid before Parliament on 27 October. As Members know, drugs are a source of untold harm in our communities, ruining lives and undermining public safety. To address that threat, we must be unrelenting in a range of areas, by restricting the supply of drugs into the UK and disrupting the organised criminal gangs behind it, improving the treatment and recovery support that vulnerable drug users need, and working to reduce the demand for drugs. Legislation is just one part of that work—but a vital one. The order will add 28 new named drugs to the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971.
