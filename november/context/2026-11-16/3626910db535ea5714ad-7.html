Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 49 · Anna Turley

The hon. Gentleman knows that we are proud to undertake our international obligations to support those genuinely fleeing war and persecution. We do everything we can to ensure that those coming here have the right to be here and will be housed in appropriate accommodation. He is right to say that previously there was no consent sought from the communities who had to manage the impact of asylum accommodation on their local communities, with all the costs and impacts on local services. That is why we are making sure, through the use of these former military sites, that people are contained and have a limited impact on local communities.

Turn 53 · Anna Turley

The application has been made via this route because the proposed development is considered urgent and of national importance. We are responding to sustained pressures on the asylum accommodation estate, which many colleagues have raised here in the Chamber today, and the need to reduce reliance on hotels. It will enable relevant planning issues to be considered but it does not predetermine the outcome, so no final decision has been taken on the site’s future use.

Turn 54 · Jim Shannon (Strangford) (DUP)

Like elsewhere in the United Kingdom, communities and constituents in Strangford tell me that they are already feeling the immense strain of the Government’s asylum dispersal policies on local housing, policing and health services, while former military bases are crying out to be used as homes for our veterans. Turn 55 · Anna Turley

I want to be clear with the hon. Gentleman: we recognise the strength of feeling in local communities. We listen hard to them, as we listen to the representations of Members of Parliament. The right to protest is fundamental to our democracy, but protest must not cross the line into some of the violent behaviour we have seen, including in areas in his part of the world. It is important that we continue to listen to people, and that is why we are closing every single asylum hotel and moving asylum seekers into safe, contained accommodation, including these military sites. We want to work with all colleagues in the House to ensure that we house asylum seekers fairly across the country.
