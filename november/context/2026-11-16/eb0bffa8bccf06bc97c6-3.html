High-control Religious Groups: Support for Victims

Commons · 2026-11-16 · Turns 17–18 of 18 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 17 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Justice (Alex Davies-Jones)

I congratulate my hon. Friend the Member for North West Cambridgeshire (Sam Carling) on securing this important debate and shining a light on the experiences of people who have suffered abuse in high-control religious groups. He is right to say that the issue does not fit neatly in one Government Department. It is on all of us to do better, to be aware of the issues faced by vulnerable individuals fleeing those organisations, and to recognise that what they are suffering is abuse, and that they are victims. For someone who has spent their life in one of those environments, finding the courage to leave can mean, as my hon. Friend the Member for Spen Valley (Kim Leadbeater) said, risking everything they have ever known: their family, friends community, and the support networks they rely on. Nobody should have to choose between their freedom and the people they love. But for too many people …
