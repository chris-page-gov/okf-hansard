Turn 94 · Speaker unlabelled

The Courts & Tribunals Service is already subject to significant external oversight, including through the Government Internal Audit Agency, the National Audit Office, the Justice Select Committee and the Public Accounts Committee. All these mechanisms provide valuable, independent inspection of the effectiveness and efficiency of the courts. The criminal justice joint inspectorates are also able to take forward thematic inspections, looking at issues spanning multiple criminal justice agencies, including within HMCTS. The work of these bodies is at their own discretion. However, the NAO has recently announced a new study relating to HMCTS, which will examine value for money and efficiency in relation to workforce planning. The Government certainly welcome this review.

Turn 96 · Speaker unlabelled

Amendments 54 to 57 and 61 in the name of the noble and learned Lord, Lord Bellamy, spoken to very adroitly by the noble Lord, Lord Anderson of Ipswich, seek to expand the scope of the independent review. We are absolutely committed to understanding disparities in the justice system, addressing disproportionality and ensuring that everybody has equality before the law. That is why we introduced a new clause in the other place that committed the Lord Chancellor to commission an independent review to allow us to fully understand whether any of our reforms have created or exacerbated any differences in treatment or outcomes between defendants of different racial groups or socioeconomic status. My noble friend Lord Jabbar spelled out very eloquently why that is so important.
