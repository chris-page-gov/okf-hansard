Turn 33 · Mr Frith

I can give the hon. Gentleman that commitment and my full attention to that issue. One benefit of adopting tech, and the roll-out of safely deployed AI in primary care settings, is that doctors are now able to turn and face their patients. They will not sit side-on, tapping away and administering their records; they will be giving their full attention, which will allow them to understand body language or any cues that they need to understand. One challenge we have is the fragmented system, and one answer that the FDP gives is that it brings together the fetching principle of that fragmented system, to ensure a whole view, whether that is a rural setting, a city, a coastal area, or anywhere else.

Turn 35 · Mr Frith

Yes, of course I will do my bit to champion that meeting. On realising the benefits of using AI with legacy systems, or indeed a federated platform, the point is that this sits on top of legacy systems. It is provided centrally, so that the regions my hon. Friend describes do not have to commission systems for themselves; it is made available for them to benefit from. We will make sure that there is much stronger peer-to-peer advocacy for those trusts using the FDP, so that they can fully realise its benefits. My hon. Friend rightly describes a fragmented system, and the challenge is to ensure that the data is drawn together, so that a whole view can be taken, wherever someone is in the country.
