Turn 9 · Angela Rayner

The Conservatives raised the burden of tax to its highest level since the second world war, so I will take no lectures from them. Mayors can decide on the overnight visitor levy; it will go through a formal consultation period, and any money raised will go back into the local area. We know that tourism can have an impact on transport and some other services, so that money can be directly invested, which will help local people and businesses.

Turn 11 · Angela Rayner

I agree. That is why we are rewiring the state, devolving power and giving it to those who have skin in the game and want to do this. We are not imposing this measure on areas; areas have asked us for the powers, and we are coming forward with them. The powers have to go through a full consultation period before they are implemented. We are bringing forward this Bill to better serve people and place by moving powers closer to them. Entire regions have choices that are imposed on them because they do not have the power to make choices for themselves.
