Turn 135 · Speaker unlabelled

76: After Clause 26, insert the following new Clause— “Expiry of sections 1 to 6(1) Sections 1 to 6 of this Act expire three months after the date on which the condition in subsection (2) is met.(2) The condition is that the number of cases awaiting trial in the Crown Court has been below 40,000 in each of the previous four quarterly reporting periods.(3) For the purposes of subsection (2) the number of cases awaiting trial in the Crown Court is that which is calculated by HM Courts and Tribunals Service.”Member’s explanatory statement The purpose of this amendment is to provide a sunset clause in the event that the “backlog” in the Crown Court returns to the approximate pre-pandemic level of 40,000 cases.

Turn 136 · Viscount Hailsham (Con)

My Lords, in moving Amendment 76, I propose to be extremely brief. The Government’s stated justification for Clauses 1 to 6 is to get the backlog down to a tolerable level. I will take them at their word. If the backlog falls to a tolerable level—around 40,000, which was the pre-Covid level—they should withdraw Clauses 1 to 6 and they should cease to have effect. I beg to move.
