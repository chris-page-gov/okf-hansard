Turn 3 · Greg Smith

I appreciate that answer, but the reality is that since taking office, this Government have scrapped plans for a 152-place SEND school in Buckinghamshire. That school would have been no magic bullet and it would not have solved all our problems, but it would have helped the huge number of families that write to me and come to my surgeries desperate to get their children into that specialist support that they need and deserve. It often ends up costing tens of thousands of pounds in taxes to take those children out of county. Will the Minister reconsider the position that the Government have taken on the new school for Buckinghamshire, and give us the school that we need?

Turn 5 · Alistair Strathern (Hitchin) (Lab)

Too many young people have been held back by decades of under-investment in specialist provision in my communities, so I was delighted, after our campaigning, to secure a record increase in high needs funding for Hertfordshire council and Central Bedfordshire council, including the biggest increase in the country for Herts. This is already making a difference, with the new SEND unit at Hitchin Boys’ school and the expansion of Ivel Valley school in Biggleswade finally confirmed. We know, though, that we need to go further and that, historically, local authorities have not always done a great job of commissioning the places their communities need, so how will the Department for Education hold both my councils to account to ensure they are doing right by the young people in my community?

Turn 6 · Georgia Gould

We have asked every local authority and integrated care board to develop a SEND reform plan that sets out how they should improve services, including their commissioning, and we will be holding them to account regularly. We are getting quarterly data back from those plans, and in our proposals for the new system we want to develop specialist provision packages, which will be really clear on the specialist provision we expect to see available in every part of the country for the young people who need it.
