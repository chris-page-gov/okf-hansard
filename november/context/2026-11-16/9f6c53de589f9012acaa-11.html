Turn 85 · Josh Dean

The right hon. Gentleman makes an important point and it will be on the record. Last year, I attended the national emergency briefing in Westminster. When I left, I felt pretty desolate. At first I thought, “What can we do?” and I felt the same anxiety I had felt as a young person when I first learned about the climate crisis. But on reflection, and over the coming days, the message I took from that screening was not one of hopelessness; it was that the future is still within our grasp. The technologies, the expertise and the solutions to the crisis exist, but a political and societal movement is required to deliver them at scale and pace. Seeing the briefing renewed my determination to make a difference. Imagine the impact of a credible, accessible and evidence-based briefing delivered on a national scale.
