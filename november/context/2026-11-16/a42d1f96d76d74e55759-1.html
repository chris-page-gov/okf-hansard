Turn 2 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Education (Paul Waugh)

We are working across Government to protect children online and in schools. We are raising the age for accessing social media to 16, restricting livestreaming and contact from unknown adults, and providing practical, evidence-based screen use advice for families. Just last week, we announced measures on removing mobile phones from classrooms—our strongest such restrictions yet. This is all part of our effort to ensure that children get the happy, healthy childhood that they deserve.
