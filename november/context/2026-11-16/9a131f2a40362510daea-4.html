Microsoft Outage: Resilience

Lords · 2026-11-16 · Turns 25–26 of 26 in this section.

Turn 25 · Lord Markham (Con)

My Lords, the Government produced a cyber incident response plan in 2026 for precisely this sort of eventuality. How many government departments have produced plans in line with the recommendations? It also recommended a seven-day outage simulation, with a cross-government exercise to game-plan for exactly such a situation. Has this taken place? If not, when is it planned to do so?

Turn 26 · Baroness Lloyd of Effra (Lab)

The noble Lord asked two very specific questions to which I will have to come back to him in detail. What I can say is that officials in the department co-ordinated the response from across government departments in collaboration with the Cabinet Office and in line with the overall government cyber incident plan. One reason that we saw much less disruption, as my noble friend noted earlier in respect of this House, was that back-up plans were in place for many departments, and alternative tools could be used to provide alternative channels for communication across the public sector.
