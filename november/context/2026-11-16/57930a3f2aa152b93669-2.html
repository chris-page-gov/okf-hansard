Turn 14 · Baroness Mackenzie of Sherwood (Lab)

My thanks for that comprehensive question. We need to remind ourselves that for public service broadcasters there are very clear rules about impartiality and very clear rules that prevent the erosion of the distinction between political opinion and the reporting of news. As mentioned earlier, these rules do not cover other forms of news and channels where people access news. Ofcom is an independent regulator. It is independent of the Government and is, of course, answerable to Parliament and reports regularly to it. The Ofcom model has been widely copied around the world and I think is one that we should stick to. We have regular meetings with Ofcom where we discuss the ways in which it can respond to issues in a timely and effective manner.
