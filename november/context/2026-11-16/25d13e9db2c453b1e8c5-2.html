My Lords, I will speak to Amendments 52C, 67A and 70A standing in my name. I am very grateful to Professor David Ormerod, one of the most distinguished criminal lawyers of his generation, for his assistance in developing these amendments. They ask a straightforward question: why is criminal justice the only major jurisdiction of the High Court without a recognised institutional home of its own? The criminal law occupies a unique place within our legal system. Every day, judges sitting in the criminal courts determine questions of guilt and innocence, make decisions affecting personal liberty, protect the public from serious harm and give effect to the rights of victims. The consequences of those decisions are profound, not merely for defendants and victims but for public confidence in the justice system as a whole.
