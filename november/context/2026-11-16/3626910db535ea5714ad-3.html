Turn 17 · Greg Smith (Mid Buckinghamshire) (Con)

MOD Bicester site A is just a mile from the Buckinghamshire border, and many of my communities in Mid Buckinghamshire are threatened by its use for 1,256 illegal migrants. I cannot believe that the Minister said earlier that MHCLG had considered all the representations, when 3,177 of those local voices said no and only one said yes—the Government appear to have gone with that one. Equally, I cannot believe that the Minister is trying to say that those 1,256 men will be incentivised not to leave this small camp, built entirely out of portacabins and modular buildings, and sit around playing ping pong, rather than the things we all know they are actually going to do. Given the clear safety risk, and given that it works out at two and a half times the cost of the equivalent asylum hotels, will the Minister at least come and visit, listen to local voices and turn around on this plan?

Turn 18 · Anna Turley

I am very happy to meet the hon. Gentleman to discuss his representations. As I said, we have listened to the concerns of communities, which are essential to our immigration reforms. That is why we are closing every asylum hotel and moving asylum seekers into alternative accommodation, including military sites. We are working to house asylum seekers fairly across the country, engaging with local authorities to reduce the impact on communities. We have already saved taxpayers more than a billion pounds on asylum support costs since the election.
