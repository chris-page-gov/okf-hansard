Turn 8 · Dr Savage

I am not here to debate the pros and cons of Rosebank, so I will stick to the subject at hand. This moment calls for moral courage: the courage to tell people the truth, however uncomfortable, and trust them to rise to the challenge. The natural systems on which our lives, prosperity and security depend are under threat. We must understand those risks and act while we still have the power to change course. For years we spoke of climate change as something that would happen in the future somewhere else—perhaps somewhere with polar bears or coral reefs.
