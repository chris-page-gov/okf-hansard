Turn 59 · Charlotte Cane (Ely and East Cambridgeshire) (LD)

It is a pleasure to serve under your chairship, Dr Huq. I declare an interest as the chair of the all-party parliamentary group for sustainable flood and drought management. If we do not tackle climate change, my constituency will be under water. That will not be fresh water; it will be salt water, and we will lose the breadbasket of England. It will kill existing nature and flood our precious chalk streams and aquifers with salt water. Hot, dry summers and wet winters will become the norm, with frequent droughts and floods. As we have heard, the UK’s green and pleasant land will be totally changed. People, animals and plants will die. Many countries will become uninhabitable, and people and animals will migrate to parts of the world where the climate is less hostile. The changes that we need to make are challenging, and we need to bring people with us to deliver them.
