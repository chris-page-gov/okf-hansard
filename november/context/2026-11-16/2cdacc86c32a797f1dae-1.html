Turn 4 · Georgia Gould

I am very aware of the f40 group and the work that many colleagues do campaigning for their schools. The national funding formula allocates funding based on schools’ and pupils’ characteristics, including by using proxies for pupils with special educational needs and disabilities. The Department is working on changes to the national funding formula and the high-needs funding system, and we will take into account the points raised by my hon. Friend and the f40 campaigners to ensure that every area is funded. As part of our reform, a greater amount of funding from high needs will go into school budgets to support children with special educational needs.
