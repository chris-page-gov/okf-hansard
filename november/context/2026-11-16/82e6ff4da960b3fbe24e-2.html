Turn 15 · Lucy Powell

My hon. Friend is right to raise that issue. The first of the V-levels will come in from September next year, and the full course details for those are already available. We have further V-levels coming in 2028. We are also reforming the GCSEs in subjects such as computing, so that those practical subjects actually involve practical assessment. More details on that will be published later this year when we publish the new national curriculum.
