Turn 157 · Baroness Fox of Buckley (Non-Afl)

I am not opposed to any judge directing a jury, as they rightly should, not following a script and taking into account the individual case; that is important, as was described. But I imagine that the point of the judge directing the jury in a particular way is not about changing the atmosphere in the courtroom or making people feel better; it is surely to ensure that we get justice in the courtroom. That way, the jury will understand what should be taken into account and what should not. It is going to be uncomfortable. Some of us have experience of being in a courtroom and having those kinds of intimate discussions. That is why a lot of women do not want to go to court at all. But that is surely not the aim of this amendment. Otherwise, it really would be a kind of therapeutic political intervention, rather than one that enhances justice, which is what we all want.
