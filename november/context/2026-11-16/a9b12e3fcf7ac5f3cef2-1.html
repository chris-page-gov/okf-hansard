Steel: Melt and Pour Reporting Requirement

Commons · 2026-11-16 · Turns 1–1 of 1 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Blair McDougall)

My hon. Friend the Minister of State for Trade (Lord Sarwar) has today made the following statement. Today, I am announcing that the Government will introduce a melt and pour reporting requirement for steel products within the scope of the UK’s steel trade measure. The requirement will begin on a voluntary basis from 1 April 2027 and become mandatory from 1 July 2027. Steel is a strategically important sector, supporting communities across the country and providing essential materials for our critical national infrastructure, manufacturing and defence. However, global steel overcapacity continues to distort international markets, depress prices and threaten the long-term viability of domestic steelmaking. This requirement will improve transparency in steel supply chains by identifying where steel was originally melted and poured. It will provide evidence to inform future policy decisions …
