Turn 57 · Lucy Powell

My hon. Friend might be the last, but he is never the least, and he is absolutely right. This Government are determined to keep our young people safe online, which is why we are bringing in the measures on phones in schools, the social media ban and the rest. It is really important that we equip young people with the know-how and skills that they need in media literacy, digital literacy and the rest, so that they can discern disinformation and misinformation and self-regulate.
