Turn 149 · Sally Jameson

We made a commitment in the Labour manifesto to having a wide programme of devolution, and this is about devolving power to local areas. Those who choose to introduce a levy will be able to invest the proceeds in what makes their area special, in improving the visitor experience, and in driving economic growth and local growth. That could mean building new infrastructure, holding and supporting cultural events, security, promoting the area as a destination, or working with local authorities to improve the destination, with cleaner streets and better looked-after parks. Crucially, it will be for local leaders in collaboration with their local authorities to determine how that investment can best support local economies and the visitor economy.
