Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency: Business Plan 2026-27

Commons · 2026-11-16 · Turns 1–1 of 1 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Transport (Justin Madders)

I am pleased to announce the publication of the 2026 to 2027 business plan for the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency. DVSA’s business plan sets out the key business priorities the agency will deliver, including any significant changes it plans to make to its services, and performance indicators by which its performance will be assessed. This plan allows service users and members of the public to understand DVSA’s plans for delivering its core services and managing its finances. The business plan will be available electronically on www.gov.uk and a copy will be placed in the Libraries of both Houses. [HCWS476]
