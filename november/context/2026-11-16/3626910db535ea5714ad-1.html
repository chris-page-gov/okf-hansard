Turn 2 · The Minister for Border Security and Asylum (Anna Turley)

As was announced in June, the Government are considering the use of MOD Bicester for asylum accommodation. As part of that process, an urgent Crown development planning application was submitted to the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government, which was granted on 12 November. MHCLG was the independent, quasi-judicial decision maker, and the application was determined through its established planning process. The Home Office had no role in it or influence over the determination of the application. Incorporating feedback from the consultation and public representation period, MHCLG has set several conditions that the Home Office must discharge if it is to use the site. Turn 6 · Anna Turley

I thank my hon. Friend for his important point. Engagement with local authorities is crucial to everything we are doing. Community impact is embedded throughout the process of site selection, feasibility planning, and, where a site proceeds, mobilisation and operation. I can confirm that we will continue to work closely with local authorities, and also with police, health partners and other key stakeholders, to understand local circumstances, community concerns and any pressures on local services. We will always identify appropriate mitigations.
