Turn 95 · Baroness Goudie (Lab)

My Lords, I support my noble friends Lady Chakrabarti and Lady Kennedy of The Shaws, and the noble Viscount, Lord Hailsham. Juries are periodically irritating not just to politicians; they may annoy judges too. But if judges prevent advocates reminding juries of their ancient prerogative to acquit without fear of reprisals, an important strength of juries would be cancelled. If judges prevent advocates reminding juries of their rights under pain of sanctions, lawyers will be in fear. None of this is ideal and I look forward to the Minister’s comments.
