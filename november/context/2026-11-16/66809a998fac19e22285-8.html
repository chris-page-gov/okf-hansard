Turn 58 · Speaker unlabelled

Finally, on allowances, magistrates are volunteers—they are proud lay people, and we are proud of them and grateful for their contribution—and they do not receive a salary. However, they can claim reimbursement for eligible expenses and financial loss incurred through their judicial service. The noble Baroness, Lady Hamwee, suggested that I recognise the importance of this; I always recognise the importance of the work of volunteers and the need for them to be treated with the greatest respect. Clause 18 will make it easier to update the categories of reimbursable expenses through secondary legislation, avoiding unnecessary delays while retaining parliamentary scrutiny.
