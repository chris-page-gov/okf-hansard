Turn 1 · The Minister for Creative Industries, Media and Online Safety (Ian Murray)

With permission, I will make a statement on the Microsoft 365 outage. On Thursday 12 and Friday 13 November, an outage affecting Microsoft IT systems caused impacts around the world. Microsoft initially explained that the incident was caused by an elevated load on its infrastructure and later provided further details of the three main issues that led to the outage.
