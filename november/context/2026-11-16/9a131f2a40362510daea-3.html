My Lords, a number of people have mentioned a very important issue: keeping sovereign capability. We identified this when we produced our first cybersecurity paper just before we ceased being in government in 2010. Not that much seems to have been done effectively since then in the whole area of cloud control and data centres, and exactly where they are is extremely important. Have we got a policy in place to increase sovereign capability or are we just letting this move along with commercial interests?
