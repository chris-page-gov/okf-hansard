Turn 25 · Rachael Maskell (York Central) (Lab/Co-op)

This Government are committed to listening to local people, which is really important when we think about the sites in not only Bicester but Linton-on-Ouse. Local communities and agencies including in York have set out an evidence base showing why Linton-on-Ouse would be the incorrect site to use: not only will the costs be exorbitant compared with using asylum hotels, putting pressure on the taxpayer, but the infrastructure is completely absent. Will the Minister review the model and ensure that the Government come up with a better model by working with communities to find a solution?

Turn 28 · Anna Turley

The hon. Gentleman knows perfectly well that we have obligations under the 1951 refugee convention and other legislation that was forged to ensure that when we said “Never again” after the second world war, we meant it. Those people have a right to come here to seek asylum, but we have to ensure that we have 24-hour security on the site. Many of those people are incentivised to behave well because they want their claims to be processed. If the hon. Gentleman took the time to look at existing military sites, he would see that there is zero impact on crime.
