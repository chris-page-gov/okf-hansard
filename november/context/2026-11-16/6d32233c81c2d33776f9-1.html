Turn 6 · Georgia Gould

That is exactly why we are putting the new investment into the system, including £4 billion into early intervention. Through the SEND reform plans, we are asking ICBs and local authorities to come together with clear plans for implementation. In the new system, we are proposing national inclusion standards that set out really clearly our expectations on early intervention, and new duties for schools to recognise need and put in place individual support plans. It is so important that we are wrapping that wider support around schools. Families should not have to fight for it; it should just be available.
