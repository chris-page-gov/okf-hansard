Turn 78 · Lord Macdonald of River Glaven (CB)

My Lords, as the noble Baroness, Lady Levitt, said, if we were designing a system of special measures today, we would establish a statutory presumption that child witnesses and complainants eligible under Section 17(4) would be entitled to give evidence via pre-recorded video screens and live link without formal application, while preserving the right of the parties to make representations and the court’s power to refuse to give a direction where this would be contrary to the interests of justice. That is precisely what the noble Baroness’s explanatory statement says. I agree with it and I support this amendment.
