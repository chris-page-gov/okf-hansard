Turn 34 · Anna Turley

I thank my hon. Friend for all the work that he and the Home Affairs Committee have done on this issue. We take all the Committee’s recommendations seriously. He is right: the move to military sites and dispersal accommodation is all about having a sustainable and managed approach to the estate, not the chaos that saw the explosion of hotels around the country—often in areas of deprivation because of low cost—that put a huge strain on local communities, with no consent, as he identified, and at great expense to the taxpayer purse.

Turn 38 · Anna Turley

The hon. Gentleman makes a really important point. There are people fleeing war and persecution whom we have historic and moral obligations to support and provide sanctuary. But we also know that our asylum system has been abused. That is why we are taking steps through the Immigration and Asylum Bill, which will be back before Parliament tomorrow, to ensure that we not only protect the rights of people fleeing war and persecution to have protection in this country, but maintain that public consent, which is being eroded by so many, as he said, and driving grievance.
