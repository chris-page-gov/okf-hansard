Turn 166 · Sally Jameson

No. I will continue to work collaboratively and constructively to ensure that the levy delivers for our local areas, visitor economy and businesses that support it. The Government are delivering on their commitment to shift power out of Whitehall and into the hands of local leaders. The Bill is an important part of that commitment. Crucially, this is about local choice. It will be for local leaders, working with businesses and residents, to decide whether the levy is right for their area and how the proceeds will be used to support their local economy. I am grateful to Members from all parties who have contributed to the debate today, and to those who have given their support to the Bill. I look forward to continuing the constructive debate and scrutiny as the Bill progresses through the House, and I commend the Bill to the House. Question put, That the Bill be now read a Second time.
