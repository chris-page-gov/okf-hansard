Turn 49 · Speaker unlabelled

I thank the Government for recognising in their violence against women and girls strategy that parental alienation is a pseudoscientific term with no basis in law or medicine, and that it should not be recognised. In some cases, these experts are not qualified psychologists and are not regulated by any professional bodies. There have also been some cases in the press where regulated experts have prioritised their own financial interest in the recommendations for the treatment that they give. All these issues have severe consequences for children’s lives and safety.
