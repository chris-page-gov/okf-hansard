Turn 163 · Speaker unlabelled

Finally, let me take just 30 seconds to say a word about the other amendments in the group on judicial training. Judicial training is obviously critical; it is required in various areas and provided at a very high standard. Obviously, I will listen carefully to what the Minister says in response, but the general approach of the Government, certainly when I was on that side of the Chamber, was that, although the funding of training is ultimately a matter for the Government, its content is always left to the judges. Amendment 53H appears to mandate particular training in fairly broad terms, which might overstep and contravene that distinction, which is actually quite important constitutionally.
