Turn 2 · The Minister for School Standards (Georgia Gould)

The Department is investing £4 billion over this year and the next two years to help children and young people with special educational needs and disabilities to receive support. We are training teachers, investing in new specialist places and mainstream schools, rolling out experts at hand and strengthening local partnerships. We are continuing to carefully consider responses to the consultation and engaging widely. We will publish our consultation response and bring forward legislation early in the new year.

Turn 4 · Georgia Gould

Legal duties on schools to identify and support children’s needs are getting stronger. We are developing new national standards for the first time, with new duties on schools to develop individual support plans with families and young people and a stronger role for Ofsted in overseeing inclusion. We want to build a system based on partnership with families and young people at its heart. We have been working closely with families and young people, listening to their reflections on our plans, and we have heard clearly from families how important accountability is. That is why we are taking the time to ensure that we are getting the plans right. We will publish a full response to the consultation.
