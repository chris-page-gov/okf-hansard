Turn 33 · Baroness Barker (LD)

I have not said that I believe that there should not be any change in the recording of statistics; I said that I do not agree with the noble Lord’s Bill. I think all of us who have witnessed and worked for the transformation in abortion services are absolutely open to there being changes in reporting, but they need to be appropriate and carefully worked out, not only with providers but with women who go through the process, to make sure that they are not collected in such a way that they achieve a different aim, which is to deter people from having the service in the first place.

Turn 34 · Lord Moylan (Con)
