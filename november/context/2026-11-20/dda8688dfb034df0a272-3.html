Turn 17 · Lord Dodds of Duncairn (DUP)

My Lords, I thank the Minister for his response. He had a lot to cram into the time that was available, which is not surprising, as this is a massive subject. The disenfranchisement of citizens of the United Kingdom and placing the law-making powers over the people of Northern Ireland in a foreign polity that is not accountable to the people of Northern Ireland and on which Parliament and the Northern Ireland Assembly cannot have any say will naturally evoke a lot of debate. If that were to happen to England, Scotland or Wales, people here would be outraged; they would not tolerate it for a minute. It is therefore right that we should debate these matters, and we will continue to do so until we get a sustainable solution. I am grateful to all noble Lords for their contributions.
