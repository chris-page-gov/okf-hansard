Turn 17 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Baroness Hayman of Ullock) (Lab)

My Lords, I thank all noble Lords who have contributed to what has been an important and constructive debate. In particular, I thank the committee and the noble Baroness, Lady Sheehan, for ensuring that the serious issue of responsibility for waste remains firmly before this House.
