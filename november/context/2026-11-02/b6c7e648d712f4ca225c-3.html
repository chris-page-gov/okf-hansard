Turn 18 · Baroness Bloomfield of Hinton Waldrist (Con)

My Lords, among our nationally significant cultural institutions are our great cathedrals and churches, yet many are at risk of closing their doors due to the costs of maintenance and repairs, such as Southwell Minster, which unsuccessfully applied to the places of worship renewal fund. What is more, the fund is for England only. Does the Minister recognise the cultural contribution of our places of worship across the UK? If so, will she look to restore the previous listed places of worship grant scheme, which was UK wide?

Turn 19 · Baroness Dacres of Lewisham (Lab)

As the noble Baroness will know, a lot of this funding is devolved to the nations and regions. Our capital grant scheme offers more targeted support for the faith sector than the VAT rebate scheme that was mentioned. Over £230 million is being invested in such heritage buildings, with £42 million invested into the heritage revival fund. We must make sure that when different organisations, faiths and places of worship apply for grants, it is dealt with in a fair and balanced way, at arm’s length from the Government and by the Arts Council, so that it is devoid of political interference.
