Turn 5 · Baroness in Waiting/Government Whip (Baroness Dacres of Lewisham) (Lab)

This Government are committed to supporting our world-renowned cultural institutions. Fit-for-purpose infrastructure is vital to that success, which is why we are investing £1.5 billion to address the capital needs of cultural organisations across England. Through our creative foundations fund, the Royal Opera House has been awarded £5 million this year for urgent conservation work and structural repair, safeguarding that premier venue and supporting its progress towards net zero.

Turn 6 · Baroness Fraser of Craigmaddie (Con)

My Lords, the creative foundations fund, while welcome, will not address the stark capital crisis identified by the noble Baroness, Lady Hodge. Where is the longer-term strategic approach, and what can the Minister say in response to the sector, which has called last week’s Budget a missed opportunity, ignoring calls for theatre tax relief to cover the true cost of touring and excluding theatres from business rates reductions? Rather than giving institutions such as the Royal Opera House breathing space, are these short-term measures not more likely to suffocate ballet, opera and theatres?
