Turn 49 · Speaker unlabelled

I must say that the noble Baroness, Lady Fox, produced in passing a wonderful image of the average adult voter conducting independent research on political issues. I am not sure that I have met too many voters while canvassing who have been conducting independent research in detail themselves. Let us not overemphasise how wonderful our electorate is. However, the purpose of the Bill is to encourage the practice and habit of voting as a lifetime exercise for the young, to strengthen the voice of younger generations in our ageing society and to build a more active democracy, and for all those reasons we on these Benches support it.
