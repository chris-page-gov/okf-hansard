Turn 7 · Lord Udny-Lister (Con)

My Lords, in moving Amendment 1, I shall also speak to Amendments 3, 4, 33, 301 and 302, all in my name. On Amendment 1, I am firmly of the view that you should be able to vote in an election only when you are old enough to meet the age criteria to stand as a candidate for that election. Eighteen remains the legal age of adulthood and is currently the age at which a citizen may put themselves forward to be elected to the House of Commons. That connection is important, and it is in alignment with the age criteria to vote. If the Government are unable to support Amendment 1, I hope Ministers will be able to explain why they are so keen to separate the age of voting and candidacy in this way. This is not an argument regarding maturity, though much could be said on that topic; I am moving this amendment in defence of a long-established electoral principle.
