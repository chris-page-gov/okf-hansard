Turn 7 · Speaker unlabelled

I put it to your Lordships that these are not constitutional niceties. These are the questions that go to the very heart of public confidence in our electoral system. We must remember that confidence in our elections depends on ensuring not just that legitimate voters are included but that the rules governing eligibility are properly and consistently enforced. The right to vote is fundamental in our democracy and it is too important to be established by assumption. If the Government are determined to automate registration then they will also be determined to guarantee the integrity of the process. I hope therefore that the Minister will recognise the importance of this distinction and give consideration to the safeguard that I have proposed.
