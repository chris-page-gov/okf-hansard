Turn 4 · The Minister of State, Department for Work and Pensions (Baroness Sherlock) (Lab)

My Lords, this Government are concerned about levels of economic inactivity in our society. It is crucial that our social security system supports and incentivises work. This Government will transform the system from one that writes people off to one that gives people the support and opportunities to live their fullest lives. That means supporting those who can work to do so while always protecting those with the most severe needs.

Turn 6 · Baroness Sherlock (Lab)

My Lords, the Question was about levels of PIP and universal credit, both of which were creations of the previous Conservative Government, under which levels of inactivity went through the roof. The Opposition want to look at what we have done; we have already taken steps to reform the system. We have rebalanced universal credit to reduce the gap between the standard rate and the health-related rate. We have invested in the right to try, given out employment support and launched the biggest crackdown on fraud and error in the social security system. This month, reports will come from the Timms and Milburn reviews and, after that, the Government will show how this system can be created into one that supports work and pushes forward. I urge the noble Baroness to watch this space.

Turn 8 · Baroness Sherlock (Lab)

The noble Lord raises an important point. We are watching carefully and monitoring benefits-related content and emerging risks online, and we are working to ensure that people have access to appropriate support. In the coming weeks, we will also introduce a new code of practice that will clamp down on activity by claims management companies that are trying to get people to pay them to apply for benefits that they can get perfectly well for free. That simply is not there. If the noble Lord looks, he will see that our new “Don’t pay to claim” marketing campaign has already gone live. It will be out there between now and Christmas encouraging pensioners and those who support them not to pay anything for benefits that they can get for free. I am glad the noble Lord raised this: we are acting in this space and will keep monitoring it.
