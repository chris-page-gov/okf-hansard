Turn 2 · Graeme Downie (Dunfermline and Dollar) (Lab)

I beg to move, That leave be given to bring in a Bill to require the Secretary of State to prepare and publish a strategy for recycling decommissioned submarines; and for connected purposes. Every time I drive back to my constituency, there is one sight that tells me I am nearly home. It is not a cathedral or a castle; it is the Goliath crane that stands above Babcock’s dockyard at Rosyth. That crane came to the area just over 15 years ago to help put together the Queen Elizabeth-class aircraft carriers, and it has been part of the west Fife skyline ever since. The dockyard underneath the crane has been there for a good deal longer—more than a century—and the people who work there are some of the most skilled that I have the privilege to represent.
