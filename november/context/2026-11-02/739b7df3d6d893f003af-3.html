Turn 18 · Speaker unlabelled

Quite apart from the fact that scrutiny is meaningless if you cannot change the legislation, this claim is also inaccurate, because the Assembly is given the opportunity to look at only some of the legislation that is imposed on Northern Ireland. Even if all of it went to the Assembly, it would not address the fundamental issue that democracy is not about having the right to elect representatives to scrutinise legislation imposed on them by a foreign Government that you cannot change; it is about electing an Executive who bring forward draft legislation that is scrutinised, amended, passed or rejected.
