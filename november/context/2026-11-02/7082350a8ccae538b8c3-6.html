Turn 46 · Speaker unlabelled

This amendment would bring consistency and timeliness to the electoral counting process, helping to restore voters’ confidence in that process. It is not an unrealistic proposal. At general elections, the law already requires that the counting of votes must begin as soon as practicable within four hours of the close of poll. In 2024, only eight constituencies did not begin counting within the prescribed timescale. Beginning the count at local elections on polling day can be achieved. An efficient voter count improves the efficacy of voting as an accountability measure and a mandate conferred upon representatives. It has an impact on voters’ decisions; I do not think most voters favour lengthy counts, and we have to think of their trust in the electoral process.
