Turn 54 · Speaker unlabelled

I move finally to the very important amendment about the review of undue influence offences. I am sorry that my noble friend Lord Pickles is not in his place, because he would be much better qualified in speaking to this amendment. In passing, I also commend Amendment 136 from the noble Lord, Lord Walney—who is not in his place—which is a similar amendment about religious influence and coercion. This proposed new clause would require Ministers to review the spiritual injury provisions regularly. The Pickles report recommended that the offence retain a reference to spiritual or religious influence. The Representation of the People Act 1983 prohibits spiritual injury as part of its undue influence provisions. The provision was historically an attempt to limit the perceived undue influence of Roman Catholic clergy when introduced as the corrupt and illegal practices Act in 1883.
