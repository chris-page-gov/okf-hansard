Turn 33 · Speaker unlabelled

In the debates on the banning of social media for those under 16, those same teenagers were discussed in this House as being especially vulnerable and insufficiently mature or resilient to handle algorithmic pressure. I am not sure how, in the build-up to an election, they will therefore cope with the pester power of canvassing. It is perfectly right that they should be persuaded and influenced, as 15 year-olds who are soon to be 16 and about to be able to vote, but we have a law which treats such stranger approaches as inappropriate grooming.
