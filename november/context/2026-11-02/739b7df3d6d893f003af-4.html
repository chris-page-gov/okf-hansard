Turn 29 · Speaker unlabelled

On the other hand, it seems that there is confusion about what the exact age is when the state and institutions, indeed the political culture, now view adulthood as beginning. When do we consider adult autonomy to start meaningfully? Formally, it remains at 18 but there are mixed messages. The government-backed Challenge 25, for example, has seen retailers that sell age-restricted products asking for photo ID from anyone who looks under 25, which is increasingly used as some kind of magic age for maturity. Universities increasingly treat 18-plus students as though they are school pupils. Academics are urged to act as though they are in loco parentis, with a duty of responsibility for mental well-being and emotional issues. That sort of infantilisation has led to far too many young adults seeing themselves as dependants, which has fuelled the NEETs problem.
