Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Transport (Keir Mather)

I want to inform the House that my Department has published the transport resilience strategy today. Transport is vital to our way of life. It connects people to family and jobs, and goods to industry, and it supports connectivity, economic growth and health. A secure and resilient transport system is essential to thriving communities and to delivering economic growth across every part of the UK. The resilience action plan, published in July 2025, set out this Government’s strategic approach to building resilience in an increasingly volatile and uncertain world. This transport resilience strategy puts that approach into practice for the transport sector, setting our direction for the next five to 10 years.
