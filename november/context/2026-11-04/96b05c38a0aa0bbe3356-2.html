Turn 9 · Lara Bird (Arbroath and Broughty Ferry) (SNP)

I thank the hon. Member for securing this incredibly time-sensitive and critical debate, and for the points that he has made so far. Does he agree that it was incredibly disappointing for the people in our constituencies who rely on heating oil who were looking to the Budget to provide them with solutions that not a single penny was allocated to support them? Furthermore, does he agree that the UK Government should take lessons from the Scottish Government, who have implemented the emergency heating oil support fund, which so far has helped more than 7,000 people?

Thank you very much, Mrs Barker; aren’t these Divisions annoying things? The hon. Member for West Lancashire (Ashley Dalton) made an extremely fair point in her intervention.
