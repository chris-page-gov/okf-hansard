Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Health and Social Care (Yvette Cooper)

Over the summer I reviewed the Department of Health and Social Care’s reform plans, the legislation and the work underway across the Department and NHS England to deliver the vision set out in the 10-year health plan. I have been clear that our focus must remain on the health and care of patients and communities across the country, and on supporting the staff who deliver those services. On 1 April 2027, subject to the passage of the Health Bill, we will bring NHS England back into the Department of Health and Social Care. As set out in the 10-year health plan, we cannot continue with two parallel national organisations carrying out so many overlapping and unco-ordinated functions. This is particularly important as we strengthen partnerships and integration between the NHS and social care, and shift more care into the community and towards prevention.
