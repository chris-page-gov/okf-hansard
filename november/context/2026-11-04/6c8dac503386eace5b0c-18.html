There will also be a defined list of exemptions including, for example, educational services. The noble Baroness is right to flag up the point that we are in a new area here. Let us take the politics out of school. Let us give kids—and they are kids—space to make up their own minds, and strengthen the hand of teachers against activist parents and sectarianism.
