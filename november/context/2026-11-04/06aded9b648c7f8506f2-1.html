Turn 1 · The Minister for Intergovernmental Relations and European Relations (Mr Hamish Falconer)

On 10 November, I will be meeting with the European Commissioner for Trade and Economic Security, Maroš Šefčovič, for this Government’s third meeting of the withdrawal agreement joint committee. As part of this important meeting, the UK and the EU will take decisions to support our commitment to the Windsor framework. This joint committee marks an important milestone in the lead up to the UK-EU summit on 20 November in Brussels, where we aim to strike important deals that will boost our growth in every postcode, cut red tape and provide more opportunities to young people. On the table are deals covering agrifood and drink products, emissions trading, a youth experience scheme and deeper co-operation on illegal immigration.
