Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport (Lisa Nandy)

On 15 October 2026, my Department wrote to Sky UK Ltd and ITV plc to inform them I was minded to intervene in the anticipated merger between Sky UK Ltd and ITV Media and Entertainment Holdings Ltd under the Enterprise Act 2002 and inviting them to submit representations. Having fully considered their representations, I have decided to issue a public interest intervention notice. My decision to issue a PUN relates to concerns I have that public interest considerations specified in section 58 of the Enterprise Act 2002 may be relevant to the anticipated merger and that these considerations warrant further investigation.
