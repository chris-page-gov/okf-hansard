Turn 1 · The Minister for Policing and Crime (Sarah Jones)

I am today announcing the publication of the Government’s response to the public consultation on a new legal framework for law enforcement use of biometrics, facial recognition and similar technologies in England and Wales. The consultation sought views on which types of data, technologies and activities should be covered by the framework, who the framework should apply to, and what safeguards and oversight should be in place to protect privacy and other rights. It received over 13,800 responses from members of the public, academics, industry, regulators, civil society groups, the police and others. Analysis of responses showed support for a new statutory framework for use of technologies such as facial recognition by the police and others.
