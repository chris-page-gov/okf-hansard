Turn 16 · Baroness Sherlock (Lab)

We will continue to keep the rate of the local housing allowance under review. To give the House a sense of the cost involved, in April 2024 there was a one-year increase in the local housing allowance; that cost an extra £1.2 billion in 2024–25. Over five years, that one rise will cost over £7 billion. I am extremely sensitive to the points being raised. The Government are investing money through a long-term crisis and resilience fund and through local discretionary support to help meet the shortfalls. But in the end, the Government’s priority is to tackle emergency problems in the short term, tackle emergency accommodation, tackle temporary accommodation, invest extensively in tackling homelessness, and provide extra support while the market is sorted.
