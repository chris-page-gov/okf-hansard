Turn 17 · Baroness Wheatcroft (CB)

My Lords, Poundland is owned by Gordon Brothers, which in turn is owned by Stone Point Capital. These companies are effectively private equity organisations and, as is so often the case, they load companies that they purchase with debt that cannot be serviced. Does the Minister agree that this is the essential problem at Poundland?
