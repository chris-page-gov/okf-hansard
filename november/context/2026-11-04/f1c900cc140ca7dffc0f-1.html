Turn 1 · The Minister for Security (Dan Jarvis)

I have today published the Integrated Security Fund’s 2025-26 annual report, which sets out how the fund has supported delivery of the Government’s national security priorities over the past year. The integrated security fund is the Government’s cross-Government national security fund, helping to tackle the most significant threats to the United Kingdom’s security at home and overseas. The UK’s security environment is becoming more contested, complex and unpredictable. The threat from state-linked actors, terrorists and organised criminals is evolving rapidly, while advances in technology create both opportunities and new risks. In response, the Government must act early, work across departmental boundaries and bring together the full range of national security expertise.
