Turn 38 · Speaker unlabelled

I do not deny that secondary legislation has a role in all walks of life and in a lot of the work that your Lordships’ House considers. On democracy, which is the foundation of our society, the law should be clear in primary legislation, not with so much left for Ministers to set; otherwise, it is not just that the Government have a lack of trust in us but, in turn, the voters have a lack of trust in Parliament, which cannot be trusted to sort it out, be clear and make the electors’ voices heard. If there is a lack of trust there, democracy is imperilled.
