Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Secretary to the Treasury (Torsten Bell)

On 19 May 2026 I updated Parliament on the steps being taken by National Savings and Investments to address historical failures in the handling of some bereavement claims and the remediation scheme established to reunite affected estates with funds that should have been returned earlier—HCWS44. I reported that up to 34,000 estates had been affected by this issue, with a total value of £367 million. The Treasury instructed NS&I to put this right swiftly and fairly, requiring a delivery plan detailing how they intended to do so to be published in May1. It also appointed Sir Jim Harra, former first permanent secretary at HM Revenue and Customs, to take over as chief executive of NS&I on an interim basis, to provide a fresh start.
