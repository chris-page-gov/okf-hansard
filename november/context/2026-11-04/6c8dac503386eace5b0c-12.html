Turn 96 · Baroness O'Neill of Bexley (Con)

My Lords, this has been an interesting debate on a variety of issues, and I thank noble Lords for their contributions. Amendment 32 from the noble Lord, Lord Blunkett, would allow sitting Members of this House to vote in general elections. Our disenfranchisement can be traced back centuries in common law as well as to a Motion passed by the other place in 1699 which stated that, “no Peer of this Kingdom hath any Right to give his Vote in the Election for any Member to serve in Parliament”. As Members of your Lordships’ House, we have a voice in Parliament. It is right that we do not also have our voices heard through elected representatives in the other place.
