Turn 1 · Valerie Vaz (Walsall and Bloxwich) (Lab)

This petition is presented on behalf of the residents of Walsall and the United Kingdom to raise the issue of the proposed secondary school on Reedswood Park, an important community space and former golf course. The development would result in the irreversible loss of valuable green space and natural habitat, while increasing the existing pressure on the surrounding infrastructure, with concerns relating to traffic congestion, limited parking capacity and road safety. If a new secondary school is required—the data suggests it is not—alternative sites may be available, including the former Sneyd school site.
