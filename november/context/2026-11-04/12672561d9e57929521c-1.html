Turn 1 · The Minister for Science, Innovation and Investment (Chris McDonald)

Today, the Government are publishing and laying before both Houses of Parliament their response to the consultation on changes to the UK designs framework. This publication marks an important step forward in ensuring that the UK designs framework supports designers and is fit for the digital age. According to a Design Council report, the UK’s “design economy” makes a significant contribution and grew at nearly twice the rate of the UK economy between 2019 and 2023. Of the £136.7 billion that the design economy contributed to the UK in 2023, digital design accounted for 59%. It is vital that the designs system is modernised to support the digital design sector. The design sector is made up predominantly of microbusinesses, which are businesses with fewer than 10 employees.
