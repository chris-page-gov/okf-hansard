Turn 19 · Baroness Nargund (Lab)

My Lords, homelessness and temporary accommodation are not just housing issues; they are public health issues. Housing is an important determinant of health. The report published by our Government in December 2025 showed that during the last Government, from 2019 to 2024, temporary accommodation was a contributing factor to the deaths of 74 children, and 58 of those children were under the age of one. Will the Government consider the public health consequences of homelessness and temporary accommodation, and work jointly with the Department of Health and Social Care and the NHS when planning these prevention strategies?
