Local Government Reorganisation in Devon

Commons · 2026-11-04 · Turns 9–12 of 12 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 9 · Speaker unlabelled

On 7 September 2026, the Secretary of State announced a rapid review of the local government reorganisation programme. The first phase of the review has now concluded that the basis of the programme remains robust and that the existing criteria continue to support the Government’s priorities. The Government therefore remain committed to delivering local government reorganisation using those criteria.
