Turn 74 · Speaker unlabelled

The circumstances that made Commonwealth voting eligibility tolerable on its introduction in 1948 are no longer the circumstances of the United Kingdom today. This distinction is only becoming more acute. In turn, our electoral rules on this issue are becoming more dated and antiquated. It is time to make this change to preserve the integrity of our electoral system, to show that we value British citizenship. We are not making a value judgment on other countries. We are inviting them to collaborate with us in a reciprocal agreement, and that may best serve those to whom representatives should truly be accountable: British citizens. For those reasons, I support these amendments.
