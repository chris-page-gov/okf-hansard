Turn 26 · Blake Stephenson (Mid Bedfordshire) (Con)

It is a pleasure to serve under your chairmanship, Mr Twigg. I thank the Minister for her comments and for extending the powers set out in the 2011 Act. Protecting our country from terrorism is a central responsibility of Government. The public deserve to be safe from these heinous acts. Our commemorations this year marking 25 years since the 11 September attacks—the deadliest terrorist attack for British citizens in our history—should serve as a constant reminder of this solemn duty. Every attack, whether it is the Manchester Arena bombing, Lockerbie, 7/7, Omagh or an array of other dreadful attacks, should prompt us to pay tribute to all those who have lost their lives in the service of our country by dedicating ourselves to preventing such atrocities from happening again.
