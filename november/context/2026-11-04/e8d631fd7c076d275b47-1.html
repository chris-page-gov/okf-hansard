Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Jonathan Reynolds)

In the trade strategy, the Government committed to an evidence-based review of the UK’s approach to promoting responsible business conduct, to assess whether our current framework remains fit for purpose. This highlighted areas where the UK’s approach could be strengthened, and today I am updating the House on the Government’s proposed action to address those findings. In our review, we found the UK’s current framework for preventing forced labour to be fragmented and overly reliant on voluntary action and reporting measures. While the Modern Slavery Act 2015 was world leading at the time of its introduction, international standards and legislative practice have since moved on, leaving the UK out of step with G7 partners and other major trading economies.
