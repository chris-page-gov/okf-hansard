Turn 15 · Speaker unlabelled

Amendment 24, in my name and that of my noble friend Lady Meyer, requires that, before 16 and 17 year-olds are given this responsibility, evidence of their decision-making capabilities, maturity and capacity for independent decision-making is compared with that of 18 year-olds, and the assessment published. This major constitutional step should not be taken without serious consideration of the evidence base. Amendments 280 and 287 enable this requirement for the delayed commencement.
