Turn 4 · Lord in Waiting/Government Whip (Lord Katz) (Lab)

My Lords, devolution allows Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland to take different approaches which reflect particular circumstances, while continuing to benefit from the pooling of resources, the sharing of opportunities and the collective strength that comes from being part of the United Kingdom. The integrity of the United Kingdom is best protected when our four Governments work constructively and effectively together on shared challenges. That is what the Prime Minister is committed to, as he made clear to the Heads of the devolved Governments in the National Economic Council meeting last week.

Turn 6 · Lord Katz (Lab)

My noble friend is right; what matters is what works. The guiding principle for our engagement with the Welsh Government is how to deliver better outcomes on the issues that matter for people in Wales. That is why we are supporting people in Wales with the cost of living and by driving good growth and creating thousands of jobs in the process. We were pleased to discuss our ambitions for industrialisation and good growth with the Welsh Government at the National Economic Council and look forward to building on this in the coming months. I agree with my noble friend that this is also about strengthening and championing the contribution that Wales makes to the UK’s collective security and prosperity in bolstering the UK’s defence, producing our steel and leading the way in industries such as compound semiconductors and life sciences—our union is stronger from having Wales in it.

Turn 8 · Lord Katz (Lab)

If one thinks about devolution across the whole of the United Kingdom, Wales is at risk of becoming one of the most centralised countries. There are opportunities to deepen devolution in Wales and to take a more place-based approach to growth. That is an ambition that the UK and the Welsh Government share. We are keen to explore that in a respectful partnership with the Welsh Government. This means empowering places right across Wales so that they can realise their own growth ambitions.
