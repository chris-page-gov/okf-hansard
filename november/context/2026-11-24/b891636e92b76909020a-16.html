As we would expect, the Ministry of Justice does intend to engage with stakeholders, including affected parents and carers, and the providers of CTFs, to ensure that the final scheme is workable and balances ease of access with safeguards and upholding the principles under the Mental Capacity Act 2005. We anticipate that a suitable person, such as a parent, will be able to apply directly to the relevant providers of CTFs to access funds. The intention is for the scheme to cover both cash and investment CTFs, although that is subject to further engagement with the financial sector on the deliverability of the parameters of the scheme.
