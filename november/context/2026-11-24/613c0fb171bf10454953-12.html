Turn 93 · Sally Jameson

I totally understand the points that the hon. Member raises. I do not want the disruption to get any worse, so whatever we do now has to be about delivering as quickly as possible for scheme members, regardless of what we are working on in parallel in relation to the future of the scheme itself. The Government will seek for Capita to pay back any additional costs we have footed in order to assist it. We have withheld almost £10 million in payments, and Capita is voluntarily receiving 10% less each month in contractual payments. We will use all the levers at our disposal and continue to withhold that money for as long as possible until Capita improves its service and meets the performance indicators that are set out in the contract.
