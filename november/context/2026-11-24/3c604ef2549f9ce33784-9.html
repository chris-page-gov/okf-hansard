Turn 67 · Speaker unlabelled

We do not feel that we can support new clause 14, tabled by the Liberal Democrats. We agree that the relationship between the FCA and the FOS needs to be clearer, but we believe that the changes in the Bill already do that, so the review proposed by the new clause is probably unnecessary. We also believe that the scope of the review is too narrow. Subsection (4) asks for a review of information that is also publicly accessible, and as I said earlier, it is already widely accepted that the FOS has become a quasi-regulator. We therefore do not a report on that; we need action, which is what our new clause 14 seeks to do.

Turn 72 · Lucy Rigby

I beg to move amendment 8, in clause 5, page 4, leave out lines 29 and 30 and insert “expired, or”; (b) after paragraph (a) insert— “(aa) the complainant communicated the substance of the complaint to the respondent before the applicable time limit and any conditions specified in the rules are met, or”; (c) in paragraph (b), omit the words from “despite” to the end. This amendment would require the FCA to make rules providing that, subject to conditions specified in the rules, a complaint made after the applicable time limit may be entertained by the Financial Ombudsman if its substance has been communicated to the respondent before the applicable time limit.
