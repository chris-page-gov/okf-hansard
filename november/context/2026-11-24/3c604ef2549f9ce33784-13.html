Turn 97 · Mark Garnier

This is another important clause. As was said in previous debates, we need to return to a state where the Financial Ombudsman Service moves away from being a quasi-regulator. The FCA is the regulator, and the FOS is the resolution service that sits alongside it. Clause 6 will help to reinforce that by creating a formal referral mechanism. That means that the FOS will have to refer matters to the FCA if there is ambiguity in its rules. The FOS must also refer matters to the FCA where there may be significant market implications, and Government amendments 9 to 13 will help to clarify that further. We see value in this, because we must restore the dynamic of the FCA being the regulator and the FOS being a dispute resolution service, so the Minister will be pleased to hear that we will not vote against those changes.
