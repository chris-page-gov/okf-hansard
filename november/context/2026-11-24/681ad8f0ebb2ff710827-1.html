Turn 7 · Lord Jamieson (Con)

My Lords, my Amendment 51A concerns auto-enrolment of British citizens who are entitled to vote but are resident overseas. In 2024, it was estimated that only around 200,000 of the approximately 3.5 million British citizens abroad were registered. Of that, around half actually cast a vote. Less than 6% were registered and less than 3% voted in the 2024 election. If we really care about democracy and representation, surely we should not ignore these citizens. While there are practical issues around databases, as we highlighted in the proposal to introduce automatic registration as a whole, we should not ignore this group. This is a probing amendment; if it is appropriate to have auto-registration for British citizens within the UK, then it should be appropriate for all British citizens.
