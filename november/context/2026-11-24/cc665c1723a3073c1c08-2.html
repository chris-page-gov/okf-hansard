Turn 9 · Lauren Edwards (Rochester and Strood) (Lab)

I welcome the measures in the Bill, but representatives from my local reserves and cadet community in Medway have raised concerns with me about Government amendment (n), which states that the RFCA may appoint “no more than six representative members” to be drawn from the chairs of regional councils. Their concern is that without stipulating a minimum number of representatives in the legislation, the number could drop to one or even zero, when to meet the standard governance expectation of a higher number of non-executive directors than members of the executive, a minimum of three would be required. I wonder if the Minister will reconsider this aspect of the Bill.
