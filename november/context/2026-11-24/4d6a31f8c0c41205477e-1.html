Turn 8 · Seamus Logan

As I was saying, those 20 years have seen Scotland grow apart from and follow a different path from Westminster policies. It is, frankly, democratic denial, because the British Government have, for almost 30 years, recognised by international treaty that the people of Northern Ireland have the right to choose their own way forward—away from Westminster control, if they so choose. Why not Scotland? It is democratic denial because, for the past 20 years, despite the burden of a political system designed to suppress nationalism, we have continued to dominate the constituency vote. We won 57 of 73 constituencies last May—a constituency victory like so many others in Scotland over the past 20 years, but all the more remarkable because we now have the largest ever majority of MSPs in Holyrood who support independence for Scotland.
