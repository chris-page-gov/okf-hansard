Turn 119 · Bobby Dean

I beg to move amendment 46, in clause 7, page 10, line 22, after “(2)” insert “For complaints about acts or omissions that occurred on or after 31 July 2024,”. This amendment ensures that the new determination test applies only to acts or omissions occurring on or after 31 July 2024 (when the Consumer Duty came into force). Complaints concerning acts or omissions occurring prior to that date will continue to be determined under the current fair and reasonable test.
