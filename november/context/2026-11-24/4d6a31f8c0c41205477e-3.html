Turn 19 · Seamus Logan

No, I will not give way. We will also reduce demand for carbon-based fuels and offer lower electricity bills to our energy-impoverished people. If Westminster wants to go on failing its conventional defence forces while buying a US-maintained nuclear missile system, be my guest—just let us go our own way. There are plenty of nations who are full members of NATO that do not have nuclear weapons: Finland, the Baltic states and Poland, for example. We have faith in our fighting men and women in Scotland and we will invest in our conventional defence forces, unlike the shambolic approach of this Government and the last Government. We cannot afford to be shackled in a relationship in which we are financially controlled and gaslighted at every hand’s turn—it has been the same again in this debate—that we cannot afford to manage on our own or look after our own affairs.
