Turn 130 · Lord Massey of Hampstead (Con)

My Lords, I support Amendments 86 to 88 and 94 in the name of my noble friend Lady Coffey and Amendment 96. All these amendments are concerned with the integrity of our electoral process. In a world of rising impersonation and election fraud, I share these concerns. While I understand the points made by the noble Baroness, Lady Lister, I believe we need to be able to verify the ID of those who are electing our Government. I have spent my career in financial services. If a client offered my firm a debit card or a bank card as proof of who they were, we would politely send them away. A bank card, as the noble Lord, Lord Leigh, has just said, does not prove identity. It proves only that an account exists in a name. It carries no photograph, no date of birth and no address. As my noble friend Lady Coffey mentioned, it potentially carries just an initial, which does not tell you the full name …
