Turn 41 · Sir Stephen Timms

The key will be that the assessment needs to correctly establish whether people have additional costs. If they do, they will be eligible for this benefit, which will make a contribution to those extra costs. Nothing is going to change overnight arising from this. Our aim is a better system that addresses the barriers that disabled people face and that many of them have been in touch with us about. We will set out the details in the White Paper.

Turn 44 · Ian Byrne (Liverpool West Derby) (Lab)

Turn 45 · Sir Stephen Timms

There will, of course, be the full set of assessments published at the time. We are aiming to develop a new assessment that correctly establishes whether people have additional costs arising from disability, which can indeed push people into destitution in the way that my hon. Friend says. If they do, this benefit is there to help with those additional costs. We are determined that that should continue to be the case.
