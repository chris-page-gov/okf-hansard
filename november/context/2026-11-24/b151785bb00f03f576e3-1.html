Turn 1 · The Minister of State, Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (Stephen Doughty)

Today, the Government have designated the Houthis as an entity under the UK’s Yemen sanctions regime. The Houthis continue to undermine peace, security and stability in Yemen. Their reckless aggression is responsible for this conflict, threatening regional security and contributing to one of the world’s worst humanitarian crises. Since July, the Houthis have escalated their military activities, including attacks against the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and offensive operations against the internationally recognised Government of Yemen, and have threatened shipping in and around the Red sea. Houthi actions are preventing freedom of navigation, disrupting global trade and increasing instability across the region. The Houthis are designated at the UN as part of an arms embargo.
