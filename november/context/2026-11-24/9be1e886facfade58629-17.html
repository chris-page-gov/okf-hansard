Turn 129 · Florence Eshalomi

I agree with the hon. Lady about ensuring that a building is safe, but as I have outlined, the Bill sets the core test. External wall fire safety risk must be either eliminated or reduced to a tolerable level on a permanent basis. The Bill takes a proportionate risk-based approach. The safety of residents is paramount, but making a building safe does not necessarily mean eliminating every element of the fire risk or removing all the cladding. That aligns with PAS 9980 and the approach already used in Government remediation programmes. It also gives us the flexibility to focus on keeping residents safe, while avoiding any unnecessary works that take more time, meaning longer remediation and residents’ lives being put on hold.
