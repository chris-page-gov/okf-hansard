Turn 25 · The Minister of State, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Emma Hardy)

I beg to move, That the Committee has considered the draft Environmental Permitting (England and Wales) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2026. It is a pleasure to serve under your chairmanship, Sir Desmond. I would like to acknowledge the work of the parliamentary scrutiny Committees in their review of the draft regulations, which were laid before the House on 20 October. The Committees’ scrutiny is a vital part of our legislative process and ensures that the policy intentions behind secondary legislation are clear and well founded. The draft regulations amend the Environmental Permitting (England and Wales) Regulations 2016. They change processes and rules relating to exemptions from the requirement to hold an environmental permit in England, and they close loopholes to help tackle waste crime.
