We have to work across boundaries to deal with common issues.

Turn 28 · Miatta Fahnbulleh

I thank my hon. Friend for asking that question again. As he knows, the process is quasi-judicial—my favourite word—but the Prime Minister was really clear about our commitment to, and endorsement of, the electrification pledge. We think there is a huge opportunity to drive clean power, but also to ensure consumers benefit from it by having the kit in their homes, such as solar, batteries, EVs and heat pumps. We are clear that that is how we will drive down bills, and critically, it is an investment in every single community—we are creating jobs for our people. This is the opportunity of the future.

Turn 30 · Miatta Fahnbulleh

We are committed to transitioning away from fossil fuels. We have introduced the Energy Independence Bill, and the reason there is a ban on new exploration is that we are clear about that pathway. We are in no doubt about where we need to be in 2050, but within that, we will ensure that we manage our existing fields. We will do all of that in a way that is consistent with our carbon budgets—when we consented Jackdaw, I said I was confident that that decision was consistent with our delivering net zero and with our carbon budgets. We can and will operate within our existing climate commitments.
