Turn 13 · Miatta Fahnbulleh

My hon. Friend is completely right to emphasise the important role of climate finance. We are on a journey, but we have a responsibility to support vulnerable countries and the least developed countries in going on this journey with us. We have exceeded the investment made in climate finance in the last period. Going forward, we have committed £6 billion of international climate finance investment. Alongside that, there is £6.7 billion of UK-backed investment that will go through financial institutions. We are working with the private sector to unlock billions of pounds for clean energy electrification, unlocking grids and supporting adaptation. There is a huge amount that we need to do, and we are absolutely clear about the commitment that we must make to supporting others making this transition.
