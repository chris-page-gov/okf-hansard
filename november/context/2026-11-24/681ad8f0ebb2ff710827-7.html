Turn 56 · Speaker unlabelled

There are many factors affecting how quickly an individual claim can be settled, including when it is submitted, the evidence provided, its complexity and how quickly any queries can be resolved with returning officers. Many of these factors are outside the Government’s control, and it would therefore be self-defeating to legislate for a deadline that might be impossible to meet. Therefore, we cannot accept the amendment. But I assure the noble Lord that we are continually seeking to improve the speed and efficiency of the claims process. We have introduced a digital platform for the submission of expenses, more comprehensive guidance and clearer arrangements around eligible expenditure. The Government will continue to work closely with the sector to build on these improvements and further reduce, as far as possible, the time that it is taking to settle claims.
