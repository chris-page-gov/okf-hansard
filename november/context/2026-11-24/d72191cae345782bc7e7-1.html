Turn 3 · Ben Maguire

I will come on to the fine and important balance between nature preservation and the interests of farmers. Those often go hand in hand, despite many of the strident voices that say otherwise. Importantly for this place, the Government are relying on our protected landscapes to do the heavy lifting in meeting our commitment to protect 30% of our land for nature by 2030, and our uplands are the backbone of honouring those international obligations. Although I welcome the Government’s continued commitment to keeping to £2.7 billion a year for farming and nature until the end of this Parliament, that equates to less than 0.2% of our GDP and is the lowest level of agricultural support in Europe.
