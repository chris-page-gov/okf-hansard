The essence of the Government’s approach has been to establish a new Defence Housing Service, primarily to manage service family accommodation, but as we debated in Committee, it could theoretically be extended at some point to cover single living accommodation. During the Bill’s earlier stages, we were assured repeatedly, both by MOD officials involved in housing provision and by the lead Minister at the time, the hon. and gallant Member for Birmingham Selly Oak (Al Carns), that some £9.2 billion of MOD funding was being put aside over 10 years, primarily for upgrading SFA.
