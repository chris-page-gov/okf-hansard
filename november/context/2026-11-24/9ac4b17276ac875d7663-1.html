Turn 2 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Health and Social Care (Mr James Frith)

I recognise the hon. Member’s concern about the potential impact of the proposed increase in MHRA registration fees, particularly on smaller firms. The MHRA has consulted publicly on the proposals, and the Government will carefully consider the responses. We remain committed to a regulatory system that protects patient safety while supporting a thriving and innovative medtech sector.

Turn 3 · Dave Doogan

I thank the Minister for his answer, and I am glad that the Government will review the consultation responses carefully. The Dental Laboratories Association is aware of the MHRA’s plan to increase the fees for medical device registration from £300 to £511 per global medical device nomenclature level 2 category. That is an increase of 70%, which is materially and operationally unjustifiable, and will fall on the shoulders of independent laboratories, which are already struggling with the cost of doing business. Given the MHRA’s operational independence, what will the Minister do to ensure that it is not inadvertently putting dental laboratories out of business?

Turn 4 · Mr Frith

It is important to remember the context. The landmark Cumberlege review identified a shortfall in the regulation of such devices. It is important that the manufacturers set to benefit from registration are making a contribution that is no longer fully subsidised by the UK taxpayer. Consultation on the cost response will follow shortly.
