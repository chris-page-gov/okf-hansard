Turn 72 · Speaker unlabelled

More generally, I want to press the Minister on the cost of the regulatory burden of the existing AML and KYC—Know Your Customer—checks. That affects not just financial services but the economy more broadly. For example, a home buyer may have to prove their identity as many as 10 times when transacting a property under the KYC rules, such as with their estate agent, lawyer, mortgage broker, mortgage lender, buyers’ lawyers, bank mortgages and all the rest of it. A home buyer will also likely have to prove a source of funds complying with the anti-money laundering rules.
