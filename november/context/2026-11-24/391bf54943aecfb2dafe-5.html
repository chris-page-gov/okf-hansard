Turn 33 · Gideon Amos

I am grateful for the Minister’s response to our amendments. On amendment 10, which would extend the civil liability in section 149 of the Building Safety Act to commercial importers, distributors and corporate entities, I understand that the Minister was concerned that the liability of perhaps a parent company or other corporate entity would be unlimited. First, there are already safeguards in section 149. They would have had to have broken a product requirement. They would have had to have either made a “misleading statement” or manufactured “inherently defective” products. Those conditions are in section 149. Also, it is clear that without amendment 10 there will not be the opportunity to pursue parent companies in the same way, so we sustain our position on that amendment.
