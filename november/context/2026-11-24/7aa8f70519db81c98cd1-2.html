Turn 10 · Dr Ellie Chowns (North Herefordshire) (Green)

Unpaid carers clearly pay a hugely important role in supporting so many people, but too often, they struggle to balance that with employment. Carers UK has suggested that each day, 1,500 people leave paid employment because they cannot get the balance right between their unpaid care duties and their employment duties. Will the Minister support the call of Carers UK and so many other carers’ organisations to introduce paid carer’s leave, to support unpaid carers to remain in employment while supporting their loved ones?
