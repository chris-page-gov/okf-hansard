Turn 32 · Bobby Dean

I do not believe it would be price control under another name; the FCA would still have the flexibility to write rules that work for firms. The new clause would make sure that we maintain core statutory protections in primary legislation, so that consumers can have redress via the courts if they need to, and that we do not transfer the relevant rights wholesale into the FCA rulebook, where they are vulnerable and beyond the purview of parliamentary scrutiny. In conclusion, modern financial evolution has decoupled lending rights from statutory obligations. In taking forward the Bill and making improvements for the modern world, we need to be careful that we maintain crucial protections for consumers in law.
