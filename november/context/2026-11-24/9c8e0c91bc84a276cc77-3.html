Turn 23 · Yvette Cooper

The hon. Member makes an important point. This is an issue I have discussed with NHS England, because it is important that mothers in labour and women across the country do not have to travel huge distances. That means recognising that there are different pressures in rural areas. He is right that there have to be safe staffing levels.
