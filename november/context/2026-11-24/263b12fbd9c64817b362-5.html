Turn 36 · Miatta Fahnbulleh

I thank my hon. Friend for his leadership in this area. He has been a strong and consistent voice for climate action. He is completely right in his analysis and, critically, that the way forward is to develop a coalition of the willing. I believe that such a coalition exists.
