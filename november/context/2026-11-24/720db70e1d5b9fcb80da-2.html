Turn 15 · Sir Stephen Timms

I am grateful to the right hon. Gentleman, who I recall once resigned from the Government over issues related to this area of policy. He is right that there has been a huge increase in the incidence of mental health issues among those applying for PIP, and there is indeed a graph in the review that sets that out. The crucial question for this system is: who has extra costs arising from their impairment, whether it is a mental or physical health impairment? If there are significant additional costs, this benefit should be available to contribute to those costs, but if there are not really any costs, it should not be; I think that is the right way forward.
