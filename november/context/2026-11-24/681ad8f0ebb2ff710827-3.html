My Lords, thank you for the contributions in this debate. As we have heard so far, the group includes a number of amendments that look at the treatment of overseas voters. Turn 18 · Lord Rennard (LD)

Could I ask for a little clarification on what I will call the Minister’s hesitancy over the principle of adding people to the electoral register at the same time as UK citizens living overseas renew their passport? As I understand it, she suggested that the problem is that, to go on the electoral register, people have to provide details of their previous residency or the constituency in which they lived. Could we not, at the same time as we ask UK citizens to renew their passports, ask them to provide such details as their previous constituency or address, thereby simplifying the process and ensuring that they are registered? I cannot see any objection to this, since UK citizens overseas have to provide that information anyway if they register.

Turn 23 · Lord Jamieson (Con)

I think that two and a half minutes, or even three minutes, is acceptable. We have proposed amendments. Across the House, there is general agreement that we should explore better mechanisms to register and to enable people to vote. I thank noble Lords who have participated in this, including my noble friends Lord Massey, Lord Leigh of Hurley and particularly my noble friend Lord Hayward, who raised the point that these systems work elsewhere.
