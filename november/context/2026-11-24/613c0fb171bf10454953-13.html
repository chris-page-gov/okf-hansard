Turn 101 · Sally Jameson

I am sorry to hear about the experience that the hon. Member’s constituent had. I am glad that they now have their money, but I recognise that lots of people are still waiting to be paid. Nine months is totally unacceptable, and I will not make excuses for that. Turning to the future arrangements for the pension scheme and contracts more widely, the Procurement Act 2023 allows the Government to look at poor performance when issuing new contracts, and we will continue to do that. We also have the insourcing unit, which is working across Government and across Departments on how we can bring more contracts in-house, so plenty of work is going on across Government to ensure that we do not find ourselves in this situation again.
