Turn 147 · Baroness Taylor of Stevenage (Lab)

I am very happy to come back and have many discussions, as I am sure I will, with the noble Lord in between Committee and Report, but I will move on now to Amendment 91. We agree that electors and polling station staff must have clear information about which documents are accepted as voter ID. However, banks cannot certify whether an individual is registered or eligible to vote. That is determined through the electoral registration process administered by local authorities. A mark on a bank card cannot therefore reliably prove a person’s eligibility to vote and could risk misleading electors and polling station staff, with potentially serious consequences.
