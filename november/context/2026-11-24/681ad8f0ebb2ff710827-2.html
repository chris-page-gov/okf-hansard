Turn 10 · Speaker unlabelled

My amendment sets out some potential solutions. All that I ask today is that it be considered, and that we look at the international experience of overseas voters in democracies such as New Zealand, Singapore and the Netherlands. UK citizens living overseas are entitled to have their say on vital issues affecting them, such as pensions, taxation and foreign and European policy issues. So I hope the Minister will look favourably on these constructive proposals, and, above all, accept the principle that with the right to vote must also come the means to do so.
