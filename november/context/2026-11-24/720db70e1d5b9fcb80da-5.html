Turn 33 · Sir Stephen Timms

There is a very clear recommendation in the review that we need to do a better job of assessments in cases of that kind. As I said, we will free up some assessment resource as a result of a couple of the recommendations that I referred to, and that will mean that we can do a significantly better job of them. We will need to work out carefully how to do it, but we need to. The criticism that the assessment does not do a good job for people with fluctuating conditions is probably the single most commonly heard criticism, and we are determined to do better.
