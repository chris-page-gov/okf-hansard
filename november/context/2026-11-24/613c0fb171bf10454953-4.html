Turn 27 · Sally Jameson

The Procurement Act 2023 provides Government with the statutory power to launch a formal investigation that can lead to debarment, either for severe breaches of contract or for poor performance. That is different from what happened before; it allow us to take into account poor performance, and not just technical ways that a company has failed. I am not in a position to confirm or deny whether a supplier is under active consideration for, or subject to, a debarment investigation, but I reiterate that it is an option that Government now have when there is poor performance by any strategic supplier.
