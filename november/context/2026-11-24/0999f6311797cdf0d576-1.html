Turn 6 · Lord Parkinson of Whitley Bay (Con)

My Lords, I am grateful to the noble Baroness, Lady Royall of Blaisdon, for setting out the case for her Amendment 68. I did not speak at Second Reading, but in reviewing the Official Report, I saw that she gave notice then that she intended to table this amendment. I am glad that she did, and it gave rise to my Amendment 71 in this group. For the purposes of openness, I should say that I am not an enthusiast for the idea of compulsory voting, but I do not close my mind entirely to any case. I have watched with interest the evidence from Australia and other jurisdictions that the noble Baroness mentioned.

Turn 7 · Speaker unlabelled

In general, I favour choice, including the choice for electors to choose to stay at home if they wish, and think that political parties pay heed to that—or at least they do if they have any sense. I have taken part in many campaigns, party-political and non-partisan, and it is always of interest to campaigners the number of people who have not yet been persuaded to turn out. There is a market there, and a market opportunity for those of us who deal in ideas. Turnout keeps political parties on their toes and keeps the onus on those of us who play an active part, encouraging us to raise our game.

Turn 8 · Speaker unlabelled

Whatever side noble Lords find themselves on on the substantive matter of compulsory voting, I hope that they will look at my Amendment 71, which is allied to this, for it seeks to protect your Lordships and our colleagues in another place from any inadvertent charges of hypocrisy on this matter. The public whom we serve and who follow our proceedings know that we are called on to vote on a wide range of matters. We hold debates such as this and then, once we have concluded them, we make our minds known by walking through Division Lobbies and casting our votes. We do that hundreds of times a year. In the last Session, we did it some 390 times on public Bills alone. That does not count the many other Resolutions and Divisions that we have on top of that. It may surprise some, including some of your Lordships, how few of us actually perform that duty most of the time.
