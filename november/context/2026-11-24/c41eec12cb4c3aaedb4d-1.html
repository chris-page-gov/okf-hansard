Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government (Angela Rayner)

Today the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions and I are announcing an independent investigation into supported exempt accommodation in Birmingham, which we have asked Sir David Pearson to lead. Supported housing provides accommodation alongside care, supervision and support for those who need it. SEA refers to supported housing that is also defined as “exempt accommodation” for housing benefit purposes. It can provide a crucial lifeline to some of the most vulnerable people in our society when they are most in need of support and a stable home. The vast majority of providers deliver this well and form a vital part of our housing system. But Members across the House will have been appalled by recent reports of poor practice in the SEA sector.
