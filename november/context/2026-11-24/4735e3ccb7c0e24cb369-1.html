Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government (Angela Rayner)

I wish to update the House on the serious building safety issues at Royal Gateway in Newham and the action the Government are taking to ensure that residents are safe and properly supported. In October, residents across five buildings at Royal Gateway were required to leave their homes following the identification of serious structural concerns. It is deeply concerning that some residents subsequently remained in the buildings because they had nowhere suitable to go, while others have faced significant costs in securing their own temporary accommodation. No resident should have to choose between remaining in a building that presents a risk to life and having nowhere safe to sleep.
