Turn 73 · Speaker unlabelled

There is a common principle underpinning this debate: electoral information should serve the integrity and effective administration of our elections. We should support sensible safeguards against fraud and ensure that information sharing is effective. We should not confuse additional data collection with better electoral administration and not expand the commission’s powers without ensuring proper accountability. I hope the Minister can address these concerns and give my noble friend Lord Fuller’s amendments serious consideration.
