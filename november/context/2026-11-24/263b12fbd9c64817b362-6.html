Turn 46 · Miatta Fahnbulleh

I think that all parties are now reflecting on how we can improve the process. As I said, the presidency had some proposals about how we could do that, and I suspect that all the UN’s agencies and the institution itself will be thinking about how to do it. However, we should remember where we were before Paris. We were on course for 4°, but collective action—that multilateralism, however painful—has moved us to a place where we are now on course for 2.8°, and if all the countries deliver what they have said they want to deliver in terms of national contributions, it will be 2.1°. Multilateralism does work. It is painful and it is painstaking, but it works.
