Turn 85 · John McDonnell (Hayes and Harlington) (Lab)

May I bring my right hon. Friend back to the potential conflict between his report and Alan Milburn’s report? People have mentioned the Joseph Roundtree Foundation report that says that nearly 4 million people are living in destitution. Any reduction in people’s income will push more into destitution. The withdrawal of PIP and access to the universal credit health element is a withdrawal of a means of support. In addition, it is the withdrawal of a passport benefit to other benefits. Although he has said that support will be provided under the Milburn report, that is not defined, and the level is not indicated in that report. May I ask him to address with his colleagues the contradiction there could be between his report and last week’s report?
