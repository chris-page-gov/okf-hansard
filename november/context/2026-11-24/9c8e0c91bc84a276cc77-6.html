Turn 45 · Mr Frith

We are committed to improving access to NHS dentistry. Across the country, nearly 3 million additional NHS dental treatments were delivered in 2025-26 compared with the year before the 2024 election. Some 81% of those who wanted to access an NHS dental appointment received one, more than 50% of whom were children needing urgent treatment. We will build on this progress and will shortly be publishing the intentions for further reform of the dental contract.
