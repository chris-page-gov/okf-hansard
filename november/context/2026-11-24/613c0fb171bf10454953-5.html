Turn 33 · Sally Jameson

We have currently withheld nearly £10 million in contractual payments due to failure, and Capita has taken a voluntary 10% reduction in its monthly payments from Government, so we will continue to monitor that. I am not in a position to discuss step-in powers at this time. I go back to my earlier answers: we are working across Government to ensure that the relevant contingencies are in place for all our strategic suppliers, and Capita is no different given the announcement yesterday. We will continue to work to ensure that pension scheme members experience as little disruption as possible.

Turn 34 · Dr Ellie Chowns (North Herefordshire) (Green)

Significant numbers of my constituents have been badly affected by Capita’s failures. I have raised this in the Chamber before, and my caseworkers have done lots of work on this. It is an outsourcing omnishambles, frankly. The Minister has set out the steps she is trying to take to sort this out, but fundamentally it highlights a bigger issue: outsourcing is not working and it does not work. The Minister said the performance of Capita has been “totally, totally unacceptable”. In this context, why are the Government still signing new contracts with Capita, such as the recent UK Health Security Agency contract? She has talked about the Government’s commitment to a wave of insourcing, but where is the timetable? Surely it needs to happen now so that we have no more of these failures.

Turn 37 · Sally Jameson

I am sorry to hear of the experience of my hon. Friend’s constituent. I am more than happy to look at that case personally if he writes to me, and I will make sure it is actioned. He highlights Capita’s generally abysmal performance and, as I say, I will make no excuses for Capita in this House or any other forum. It has shown that when competition is insufficient and contracts are signed that potentially should not have been or the relevant checks are not done, as was the case under the last Government, we find ourselves in this position. But this Government are committed to making sure that this scheme gets into a far better position for scheme members, and we are, of course, committed to the biggest wave of insourcing in a generation.

Turn 39 · Sally Jameson

The Cabinet Office and the Government have given a huge amount of resources and that will continue to support the scheme and get the service to a good level. I am more satisfied now than I was when I started this job that Capita is putting in sufficient resources. It has stepped up its game. There is a question to be asked about why it did not put more resources in sooner, and that is something I am sure will be discussed long into the future. The priority now is making sure that the resources of both Capita and the Cabinet Office are used to their maximum potential. As it stands, there is sufficient resource, but both Capita and the officials need to crack on to make sure we get those numbers down.
