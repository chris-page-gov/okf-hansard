Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for the Home Department (Satvir Kaur)

The Under-Secretary of State for Justice, my hon. Friend the Member for Pontypridd (Alex Davies-Jones), and I are today pleased to announce the roll out of domestic abuse protection notices and domestic abuse protection orders from 25 November across England and Wales. The Domestic Abuse Act 2021 legislated for DAPNs and DAPOs to provide stronger and more flexible protection for victims and survivors of domestic abuse. DAPOs are the first cross-jurisdictional protective order that can be made in the criminal, civil and family courts. They can be accessed through a range of routes, with applications being made by the police, victims and survivors themselves, and third parties, including family members, local authorities and the domestic abuse sector.
