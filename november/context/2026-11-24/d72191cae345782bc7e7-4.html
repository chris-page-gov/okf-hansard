Turn 25 · Robbie Moore (Keighley and Ilkley) (Con)

It is a pleasure to serve under your chairmanship, Mr Turner. I congratulate the hon. Member for North Cornwall (Ben Maguire) on securing this important debate. As he rightly said, upland farmers are the guardians of some of our most iconic and cherished landscapes. Upland farmers work and manage poorer soils, steep slopes and challenging topography, and they have to deal with some of the harshest weather. Around 75% of England’s uplands sit in protected landscapes. Uplands hold 53% of our sites of special scientific interest and they carry around 40% of our breeding ewes. They make an essential contribution to food production, biodiversity, landscape management, and the economic and social sustainability of our rural communities.
