Turn 9 · Yvette Cooper

I look forward to the cross-party talks with the hon. Member, and I hope we can build some consensus on the need to create a national care service. Families across Britain have been let down for far too long, partly because political parties and Governments have turned away from this difficult issue rather than asking themselves, “What practical steps can we take? What can we do, step by step, with, for instance, the social care workforce and the neighbourhood support teams, to build services that better reflect today’s needs?”
