Independent Monitoring Panel Report: 1 July to 31 December 2025

Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Northern Ireland (Chris Bryant)

I am pleased to have received the second report from the independent monitoring panel, which was established under the “Safeguarding the Union” Command Paper to scrutinise the operation of the internal market guarantee. The second monitoring period for the guarantee ran from 1 July through to 31 December 2025 and I can confirm that the panel has today published its report for that period. I have placed a copy in the Library of the House for the record. Under the guarantee, the Government undertook that 80% of all freight movements from Great Britain to Northern Ireland would be treated as “not at risk” of moving onwards to the EU, and therefore moving within the UK internal market system.
