Turn 9 · Sarah Dyke

I entirely agree with the hon. Gentleman. Campaign for Better Transport found that more than half of small towns in the region are transport deserts or at risk of becoming one, including 14 of the 23 towns studied in Somerset. The Labour Government want 75,000 new homes built in Somerset over the next 20 years. Those homes must be in the right places, for the right people and fit for the future with the connectivity infrastructure to support them. Otherwise, we are simply building a more car-dependent world. Investing in our railways is not a luxury, it is essential.
