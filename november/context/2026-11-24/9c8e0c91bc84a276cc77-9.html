Turn 71 · Mr Frith

I refer the hon. Member to the statement to the House last week in which the Government announced a new procurement of the NHS federated data platform. Services will continue until 2029, before the contract then ends. The FDP is here to stay. This is not a conversation about the politics of the providers’ executives or the territories of its other customers; it is about continuity of care. This is the right decision. The FDP principle is one to defend, not to attack.
