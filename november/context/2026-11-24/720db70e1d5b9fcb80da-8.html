Will the Minister please confirm that such crude cuts will not be in the White Paper?

Turn 61 · Sir Stephen Timms

The determination that motivates the Milburn report is that young people should have the chance to be in work, and that there should be a substantial improvement in employment support, not least for disabled young people, who often face barriers to work. Those barriers do not need to be there; the system needs to remove those barriers, which is what we are determined to do. I say to my hon. Friend’s constituents that it is very much in the interests of those who depend on this benefit that it be sustainable in the long term. These changes will help to ensure that it is.

Turn 62 · Jeremy Corbyn (Islington North) (Your Party)

I thank the Minister for the inclusive style of the inquiry that he has undertaken, and I hope that this principle will continue into the next, probably much more difficult, stage. When he announced the review, he said that it would all be paid for within the envelope of the Office for Budget Responsibility predictions on expenditure. Will the Minister confirm that PIP will be determined by the needs of the individual as assessed, not driven by some Treasury forecast or restriction? Many people in receipt of PIP are terrified of the future and appalled at the inhuman way they have been treated.
