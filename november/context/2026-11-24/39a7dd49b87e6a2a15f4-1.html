Turn 2 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Health and Social Care (Mr James Frith)

Arrangements for patients registered with a GP in Scotland while residing in England are made by the appropriate English and Scottish authorities. The NHS app is available to all patients registered with a GP practice in England. Patients registered in Scotland should use MyCare.scot.

Turn 3 · Ms Minns

I very much enjoy the convenience of the NHS app, but it strikes me as slightly ironic that I can use it to order a prescription and pick it up on the Scottish side of the border, just 8 miles from where I live, but my constituents living on the English side of the border cannot use the same app to book their NHS appointments. May I press the Minister for a date when the guidance governing cross-border care will be updated?

Turn 4 · Mr Frith

Arrangements on the Scottish border are made through service-level agreements between relevant bodies. These arrangements are expected to be reviewed following completion of the Health Bill to support greater consistency and alignment. I am happy to carry on our conversation at our planned meeting on Wednesday afternoon.
