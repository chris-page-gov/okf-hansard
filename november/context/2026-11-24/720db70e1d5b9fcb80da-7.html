Turn 53 · Sir Stephen Timms

As I have set out, the assessment criteria changes will reflect whether people have additional costs arising from their disability. Getting that right for fluctuating conditions is a very important part of the process. In theory, the assessment is supposed to do that at the moment; in practice, it frequently does not. We will be doing a good deal of work on that, and it is likely that we will put in more assessment capacity to look specifically at this issue.
