Turn 65 · Florence Eshalomi

Clauses 96 and 97 address a practical gap that can leave residents out of their homes for longer than necessary. A prohibition order or notice provides immediate protection by stopping or restricting occupation. However, it does not, by itself, require the underlying hazard or breach to be fixed. The clauses connect immediate protection with an enforcement route to remediation, while preserving the authority’s professional judgment about whether remediation action is practicable and capable of allowing the prohibition to be lifted. Clause 96 amends the Housing Act 2004. Where the statutory conditions are met, a local housing authority must serve an improvement notice alongside a prohibition order.
