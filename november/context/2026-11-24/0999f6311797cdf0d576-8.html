My Lords, like a number of my other amendments to the Bill, my Amendment 70 seeks to ensure the integrity of the electoral register by empowering the Electoral Commission with the statutory tools and data to identify two types of fraud—fraud that undermines the integrity of the elections upon which our democracy depends. My Lords, I support the amendments in the names of the noble Lord, Lord Pack, and the noble Baroness, Lady Pinnock. Clause 50 is a wasted opportunity. As it currently stands unamended, it is a recipe for providing information that very few people are asking for. When people take part in an election, they want to know who their candidates are, what their policies are and who won the election. Often, they want to know details. This is not provided for under the present proposals in the Bill. When one looks at foreign jurisdictions where elections take place, it is astonishing that, for such a small country, we are not able to provide granular technical information about election results.
