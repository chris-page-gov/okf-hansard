Turn 2 · The Minister for Social Care and Mental Health (Alison McGovern)

Carers and unpaid family carers will be at the heart of our national care service. We will publish a five-point plan for the social care workforce next month, setting out immediate steps to improve recruitment, retention and retirement and building on the announcements we have already made on fair pay and pensions. Last week, we were delighted to announce the appointment of my hon. Friend the Member for Shipley (Anna Dixon) as the Government’s champion for unpaid family carers. She will work directly with unpaid carers and support the continued delivery of the unpaid carers action plan, as well as shaping the new national care service.

Turn 6 · Layla Moran (Oxford West and Abingdon) (LD)

The Minister will be aware of the tragic case of Matilda Davis, a parent-carer who did not get the support she needed after telling her social worker that she was having suicidal thoughts. After my hon. Friend the Member for Stratford-on-Avon (Manuela Perteghella) raised that case at Prime Minister’s questions, the Prime Minister committed to mandatory suicide prevention training for frontline social workers. It was the lack of that training that let Matilda down, yet a freedom of information request by her brother Joel has revealed that only three local authorities in the country are making it mandatory. With 41% of parent-carers at some point having thoughts of taking their own lives, will the Minister meet me and my hon. Friend so that we can discuss every opportunity to make good on the Prime Minister’s promise?
