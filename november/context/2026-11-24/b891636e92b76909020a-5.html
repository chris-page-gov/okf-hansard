Turn 39 · Mark Garnier

In principle, we support the abolition of the Payment Systems Regulator. It makes absolutely no sense for the UK to have four different entities regulating payment systems. However, we have a few areas of concern. On price cap powers, amendments 66 and 67 attempt to do the same thing, broadly speaking. The UK payments system is widely seen as delivering strong outcomes and is well positioned to continue delivering innovation and investment for many years to come. The Bill gives the FCA a broad power on price controls without a clearly defined threshold for intervention. We believe that the power should be used only as a last resort. We also believe that it should be exercised only if there is demonstrable evidence of ineffective competition or market failure, and if intervention is necessary and proportionate to address a clearly identified harm.
