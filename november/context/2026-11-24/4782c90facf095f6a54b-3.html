Turn 24 · Speaker unlabelled

Finally, timely and insightful incident reporting will be at the heart of the updated regime. We have standardised the factors that entities will consider to determine whether an incident needs to be reported. In amendments 6, 9 and 13, we have removed mandatory near-miss reporting for data centres, in line with other operators of essential services. Instead, we will launch a voluntary near-miss reporting regime for all organisations covered by the Bill. Customers of data centres and relevant digital and managed service providers will be kept better informed with timely and appropriate information during incidents thanks to new duties in Lords amendments 16, 17 and 18.
