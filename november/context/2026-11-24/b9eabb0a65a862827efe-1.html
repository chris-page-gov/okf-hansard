Turn 6 · Rachel Gilmour

My constituents in west Somerset are served primarily by North Devon district hospital and Musgrove Park hospital, yet North Devon hospital’s maternity unit remains closed and Musgrove continues to face significant pressures. For families in some of the most remote communities on Exmoor, including villages and hamlets such as Porlock and Culbone, the nearest consultant-led maternity care can be more than an hour away by road. Does the Minister accept that it cannot be right in modern Britain that expectant mothers risk giving birth on the side of a B road because maternity services are too distant to reach safely? Will the Government act now to end this unacceptable situation?

Turn 7 · Yvette Cooper

I agree that it is deeply unfair on families that North Devon maternity services continue to be closed. NHS England is meeting daily with the trust leadership to provide support and to ensure progress. The trust has been working on different ways to seek to recruit the additional staff it needs to keep services safe, because fundamentally the problem comes down to issues around recruitment. It has been offering additional financial incentives for relocation. We will continue to support the trust and to maintain that pressure and work in order to get those services safely opened, as rapidly as possible.
