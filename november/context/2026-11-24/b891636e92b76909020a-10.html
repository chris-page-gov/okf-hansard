Turn 80 · Lucy Rigby

Clause 14 makes changes to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the Financial Policy Committee and to provide greater flexibility for cross-representation between the FCA and the Bank’s key decision-making bodies. The FPC identifies and monitors risks that threaten the resilience of the UK financial system as a whole and has the power to take action to counter those risks. This clause reduces the statutory minimum number of meetings that the FPC is required to hold from at least four to at least three each year. Importantly, the FPC itself supports this change. In its March 2026 meeting, the committee noted that moving from at least four rounds of meetings per year to at least three will allow it to better focus on deeper analysis and long-term structural issues while preserving its ability to respond flexibly to developments in the risk environment.
