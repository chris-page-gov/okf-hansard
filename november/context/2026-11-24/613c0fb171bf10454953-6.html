Turn 41 · Sally Jameson

I totally sympathise with the strain this has put on many constituency offices; I have also had a lot of casework in relation to Capita’s failings on the pension scheme. I am a little reluctant to put dates and deadlines on things, because that has happened before and then they were not met, but the approach has changed: we are now looking at the oldest cases first, which tend to be the most complex and therefore could take many hours or sometimes even days to resolve. That is why it may seem that some cases have taken a lot longer than others. I am hoping that, by having that member-led approach and starting with the oldest and most complex cases, more cases will be resolved more quickly as solutions get identified. I have said previously in the House that I expect to see significant improvement in weeks and certainly in a small number of months.
