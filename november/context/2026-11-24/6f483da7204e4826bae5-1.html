Turn 2 · The Minister for Public Health and Patient Safety (Dame Diana Johnson)

May I start by paying tribute to my hon. Friend for all her work on DES and for representing her constituent Jan Hall? My hon. Friend raised this issue at our last oral questions, and I can confirm that the review with the University of Exeter is moving forward, with the publication of protocols setting out how the research will be conducted. Its findings will then be reviewed by a clinical steering group, and will inform decisions on screening and guidance.

I thank the Minister for her answer. It appears that there is still no mammogram screening pathway for DES-exposed women, who are 30 times more likely to get breast cancer. These women require routine screening far before the age of 50. What steps has the Department taken since I last raised the issue of enhanced access and screening for DES-linked cancers?

Turn 4 · Dame Diana Johnson

My hon. Friend is right to raise this issue. To improve clinical awareness, the notice to cancer alliance directors was reissued on 26 October to ensure that affected women are identified and supported, in line with the existing guidance. My officials are engaging with NHS England on concerns about the guidance on DES not being easily accessible, and I would very much welcome a meeting with my hon. Friend and campaigners to hear directly about what more we can do.
