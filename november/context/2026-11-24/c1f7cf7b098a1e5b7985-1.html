Turn 2 · The Secretary of State for Health and Social Care (Yvette Cooper)

Subject to passage of the Health Bill, NHS England will be brought back into the Department of Health and Social Care next April. My statement on 4 November set out the next steps for how those changes will be delivered, but the purpose of the reforms is to help us get back to what the NHS does best—caring for people where they need it most—and ending the duplication and fragmentation of our national health service system.

Turn 8 · Yvette Cooper

The former Health Secretary makes an important point about the challenge of recommendations that are never implemented. We are setting up a new hub within the Department to keep track of recommendations, some of which are duplicates and some of which contradict each other. We will shortly be setting out a first action plan on maternity services based on the work of the maternity and neonatal taskforce. We also expect the National Quality Board to continue.
