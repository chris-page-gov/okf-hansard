Turn 5 · Baroness in Waiting/Government Whip (Baroness Hyde of Bemerton) (Lab)

We remain committed to publishing a revised autism strategy. It will build on the work of the mental health strategy, which will be published this year and set out the Government’s approach to mental health and ill-health, autism and ADHD. We will begin engagement on the revised autism strategy in the new year, including on whether it should cover ADHD. The current strategy remains in effect until a revised autism strategy is published.

Turn 6 · Lord Touhig (Lab)

My Lords, a committee of this House chaired by the noble Baroness, Lady Rock, said in a report on autism a year ago that successive Governments have failed to reduce inequalities for autistic people. I know my noble friend will understand the dismay that there is no new strategy, more so because we are extending the existing strategy, which the committee said was “not fit for purpose”. From discussions I have had with my noble friend, I have no doubt that she is absolutely determined to do her very best for people with autism. Can she assure us that when the strategy is announced it will have a fully costed implementation plan and that there will be full accountability of its delivery so that the problems we faced in the past will not be repeated?

Turn 7 · Baroness Hyde of Bemerton (Lab)

I thank my noble friend for his question and kind comments. The Government recognise the need for an effective and deliverable strategy. As my noble friend will be aware, this is a personal as well as a professional matter for me, as immediate members of my family are autistic. The Government recognise the importance of ensuring that the revised autism strategy is effectively implemented. We have reflected on the implementation issues arising from the previous strategy and want to use that reflection and learning to ensure that, when we approach the new strategy, we take a different approach. We will consider how best to support accountability and oversight as that revised strategy is developed.
