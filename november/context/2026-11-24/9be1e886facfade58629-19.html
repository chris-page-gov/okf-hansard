Turn 149 · The Chair

With this it will be convenient to discuss the following: Clauses 110 to 114 stand part. Amendment 29, in clause 115, page 81, line 36, at end insert— “(9) Notwithstanding subsection (2), Section 11 of this Act does not come into force until the Secretary of State has published draft regulations defining ‘reasonably necessary’ for the purposes of section 8(2).” This amendment would prevent section 11 coming into force until the Secretary of State has published regulations defining what counts as ‘reasonably necessary’ for the purposes of section 8(2). Clauses 115 and 116 stand part. I ask the Minister to speak for 30 seconds.
