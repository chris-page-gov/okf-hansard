Turn 2 · The Minister for Secondary Care (Karin Smyth)

I thank my hon. Friend for meeting me recently with other hon. Friends to discuss the trust. I commend his work in supporting it. Whether it is elective or emergency care, we want all patients seen as quickly as possible. Despite record pressures for emergency care, more patients are being seen within four hours. In Kent and Medway, we have improved performance on the 18-week target from 59.8% to 63.9%, so more people are being seen more quickly. We are determined to improve performance further in Medway and across the country.

There has been increasing demand across the country, including queues at my local Medway Maritime hospital just yesterday. What more can be done to share best practice, including for improved corridor care, so that we do not see repeats of those queues going into the future?

Turn 4 · Karin Smyth

I thank my hon. Friend for the work he is doing with the trust. The trust is working alongside NHS England to use oversight and expertise to improve conditions for patients and staff. There are also the many measures we have introduced to improve flow of patients from contacting primary care through the entire system. We will continue to do that to see sustained progress. His area has had some long-standing challenges that go back many years. We are committed, along with him and other hon. Members, to ensuring that that progress is sustained.
