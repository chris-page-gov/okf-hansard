Turn 105 · Florence Eshalomi

Clause 103 determines that an individual would be fined when their consent, connivance or neglect contributed to the breach. How the individual is liable will be set out clearly in guidance. As I mentioned, we intend to produce dedicated guidance to help leaseholder-owned managing companies to meet their obligations without placing any unfair cost on leaseholders. Many leaseholders are giving up their free time to take an active interest in their buildings. Amendment 56 would go further, but the Government consider that the existing provisions strike the right balance between protecting individuals from automatic liability and ensuring that those who are personally responsible for wrongdoing can be held accountable. Turn 107 · Florence Eshalomi

I should clarify and highlight that clause 103 will not make someone personally liable simply because they are a director. Question put and agreed to. Clause 102 accordingly ordered to stand part of the Bill. Schedule 3 Financial penalties: procedure etc Amendment proposed: 16, in schedule 3, page 108, line 3, leave out from “must” to end and insert “be paid into a dedicated local Building Remediation Fund held by the relevant authority, to be used exclusively for financing step-in remediation works and resident protection measures under Schedule 1.”—(Gideon Amos.) This amendment ensures that financial penalty proceeds collected from failing building owners are retained locally in a dedicated fund reserved exclusively for financing step-in remediation works, rather than being returned to the Consolidated Fund. Question put, That the amendment be made.
