Turn 97 · Lord Jackson of Peterborough (Con)

I promise the Government Whip that I will wrap up. This is a permissive and precautionary principle that we are asking electoral returning officers to observe. I cannot think in good conscience why anyone would necessarily oppose it, because it is not imposing particularly draconian obligations on them just to ensure that electoral fraud does not take place. Electoral officers should use their good offices in the way they see fit to ensure that there is no duplication on the register. On that basis, I take this opportunity to withdraw the amendment.
