Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Health and Social Care (Yvette Cooper)

Today with the agreement of the Education Secretary, the Foreign Secretary, the Home Secretary and the Housing Secretary, I am making the following statement: In June, the Government set out a series of steps to support those most affected by the conflict in Gaza and to contribute to the longer-term recovery of Palestinian society. This included work with the devolved Governments to enable further medical evacuations of seriously ill and injured children from Gaza to the UK; and the renewal of exit support to enable high achieving students from Gaza to take up fully funded scholarships at UK universities for the 2026-27 academic year.
