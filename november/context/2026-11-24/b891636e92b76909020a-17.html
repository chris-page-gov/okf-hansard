Clause 25 concerns skilled person reports under section 166 of FSMA 2000. The reports are an important supervisory tool, and the Government agree that they should be used proportionately. The clause’s first requirement is that before a regulator can commission a skilled person report, the regulator must be satisfied that there is a material risk of serious detriment to regulatory outcomes.
