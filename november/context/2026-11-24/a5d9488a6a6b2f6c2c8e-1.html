Turn 2 · Graham Stuart (Beverley and Holderness) (Con)

Saltend Chemicals Park, just outside Hull, is one of the most important industrial sites in East Yorkshire. It is owned and operated by the px Group. Across its 370 acres, companies including INEOS, the Mitsubishi Chemical Group, Triton Power, Air Products and Yara—though currently mothballed—make chemicals and provide the power, gases and services on which other businesses depend. Saltend matters to the region, because it provides skilled technical jobs. It matters to British industry, because its companies supply one another and businesses far beyond the Humber. It matters to the country, because we need the capacity to make foundational chemicals here in Britain. Last year, Vivergo ceased producing bioethanol and animal feed at Saltend.
