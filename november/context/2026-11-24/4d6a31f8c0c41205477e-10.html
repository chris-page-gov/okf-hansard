Turn 79 · Dave Doogan

Because he is wrong. In 1885, the Secretary of State for Scotland was appointed to the Westminster Government. That was the first stage of trying to patch up the failed Union—this debate is coming up to 150 years old. And then, there was the failed treaty of Perth in 1972, Labour’s wrecking amendment to Home Rule in 1979 and devolution finally happening in 1997. Since that time, more and more powers have been ceded from the centre to the Scottish Government to try to hold this frail Union together. It will not work, but let us be positive.
