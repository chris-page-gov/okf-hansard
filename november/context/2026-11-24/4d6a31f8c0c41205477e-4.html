Turn 25 · Imogen Walker (Hamilton and Clyde Valley) (Lab)

It is a pleasure to serve with you as Chair, Mr Turner. It is hardly a surprise that SNP Members want another independence referendum; in fact, they have never stopped wanting another referendum since the result of the last one. It was a somewhat different story before that, it must be said. I know they do not like it when we use the G-word but, in fairness, they used it first—and repeatedly. The referendum was a “once in a generation opportunity”, according to Nicola Sturgeon in October 2013. In the Scottish Government’s November 2013 White Paper, it was again a “once in a generation opportunity”. Four days before the vote, Alex Salmond described it as a “once in a generation opportunity”. It is hard to see how the independence referendum was anything other than a once in a generation opportunity for the SNP.
