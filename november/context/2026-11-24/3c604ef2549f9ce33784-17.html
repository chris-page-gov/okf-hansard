Turn 129 · Bobby Dean

I thank the Minister for response, but our concerns remain as set out previously. On removing the lines on consumer responsibility, she referred to the fact that consumers are protected elsewhere by the principle in the FCA’s rulebook on being fair to consumers, and that has been relied on historically. However, in this scenario, we are picking out consumer responsibility and putting it in the Bill, instead of relying on it elsewhere—it is part of the consumer duty, for example. I question why we need to single out consumer responsibility when caveats in the other direction on being fair to consumers are not also written explicitly into this part of the Bill. It feels to me that we are tilting the balance too far in the favour of firms. I have made my other arguments already, which the Minister has attempted to address, but I am not quite satisfied at the moment. Our position will remain …
