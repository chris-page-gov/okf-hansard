Turn 55 · Speaker unlabelled

I have spoken with many families about the pressure of finding many thousands of pounds simply to allow their family to be in the same place. I am relieved for those families that this fight is over. I pay tribute to the Royal British Legion team behind the APPG on the armed forces community, and the Minister for Veterans and People, in his previous role as the chair of that APPG; they have used their voice on this issue. Military life often means sacrificing the stability that many of us take for granted. For those who come from overseas to serve in our armed forces, that uncertainty should not extend to the ability to settle in the country that they have served. These men and women represent the very best of us, and they should never be left in any doubt that they and their families belong here.
