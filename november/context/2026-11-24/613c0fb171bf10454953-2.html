Turn 11 · Sally Jameson

I concur with my hon. Friend: the service from Capita has been appalling. I will not make any excuses for that today, or on any day. There is an improvement in non-complex, business-as-usual cases being seen in under 30 days, but there is still a big backlog of complex cases, giving us a mixed picture. I said to the House recently that I expect to see significant improvements by the end of this month and before Christmas. We will continue to monitor that, and will return to the House with an update if Capita’s performance does not appear to be moving in the right direction.

Turn 16 · Sally Jameson

I thank my right hon. Friend for his engagement with me on this matter. As I have said previously, all Government Departments routinely do contingency planning in relation to strategic suppliers, and this case is no different. I want to ensure that what we do now is in the best interests of scheme members, and we are working with Capita to ensure that the situation for scheme members improves as quickly as possible. He knows my views. He knows that I am committed to the Labour party 2024 manifesto, and so to the biggest wave of insourcing in a generation; I have not moved away from that, and neither have the Government. However, in this initial period, we need to ensure that scheme members are the priority, and that services resume for them as quickly as possible.
