Turn 26 · Joe Robertson (Isle of Wight East) (Con)

I thank the Minister for setting out the draft regulations. The Opposition support them and will not seek to divide the Committee. As we have heard, they affect a relatively small group of people but the circumstances are extremely serious. These are people convicted of serious violent or sexual offences, such as manslaughter, attempted murder and rape, who have been detained in hospital rather than sent to prison. Under the current rules, those in prison do not receive benefits but those detained under a hospital order can and do receive benefits towards living costs. That is clearly a position and discrepancy that cannot be justified, and the central change in the draft regulations is therefore right. Turn 27 · Steve Darling (Torbay) (LD)

I would welcome some further commentary from the Minister. This change is not due to kick in until 7 April. Is that the earliest that it is possible for it to come into effect? I understand that is the start of the financial year, but is there any opportunity of drawing it closer? I would also welcome the Minister reflecting on why personal independence payments are seemingly not captured by this instrument. Can the Minister explain the rationale for not capturing personal independence payments in the draft regulations?
