Turn 57 · Blake Stephenson

Does my hon. Friend agree that it is also important to consider the volume and complexity of regulation? I have looked through the written evidence, and the London Market Group gives quite a striking example: one firm employs 78 compliance officers for the UK alone, against 73 compliance officers covering 40 countries across Europe and the middle east. That is perhaps a crude example, but it is one example of the regulator simply not weighing growth seriously. Recognising the step change, perhaps now is the time to focus the regulator’s mind by creating a primary objective.
