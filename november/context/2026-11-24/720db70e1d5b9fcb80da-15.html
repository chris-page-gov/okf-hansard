Turn 120 · Sir Stephen Timms

I am grateful to my hon. Friend for all the interest that he has taken in our work during the course of the review. I was very pleased to have on the steering group a member with a learning disability. The digital profile, which we envisage being populated from the initial gateway on, would be available for the person who has applied for the benefit to use in whatever way they chose: for other bits of the benefits system, for a carer’s assessment if they subsequently went through one, or perhaps for applying for a disabled facilities grant or something of that kind. The point that he makes is a very important one and it is often made to us: people are asked time after time after time to provide the same information to different bits of the system. We think we can do better than that.
