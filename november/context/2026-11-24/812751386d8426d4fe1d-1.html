Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions (Pat McFadden)

Today, the steering group of the Timms review of personal independence payment has published its final report. A copy of the report has been laid before Parliament, and it is available in a range of accessible versions at gov.uk. In the 13 years since its introduction, the world around PIP has changed. Despite this, it had never been fully reviewed. It is in that context that the Timms review was launched in summer 2025 to ensure PIP was fair and fit for the future. For the first time at this scale, the Government co-produced the review with disabled people, the organisations that represent them, and other experts. I want to thank the review’s co-chairs—the Minister for Social Security and Disability, Sharon Brennan and Dr Clenton Farquharson CBE—and the 12 other members of the steering group for their hard work and expertise over the past year.
