Turn 89 · Richard Burgon (Leeds East) (Lab)

PIP is a lifeline for so many people, helping them to meet the extra costs of living with a disability. It supports people in work as well as those who are not in work. I therefore welcome the Timms review’s recognition of PIP as an essential benefit. But that makes me even more alarmed by the proposals in the Milburn review to take that essential support away from so many young disabled people as that risks plunging huge numbers of young disabled people into destitution. Does the Minister not agree that sick and disabled people need that support whatever their age and that extra employment support for young people, which is very welcome, should be funded not by cutting disability support but by taxing the wealthiest?

Turn 92 · Sir Stephen Timms

I thank my hon. Friend for hosting that helpful workshop in her constituency. The key change, as I have said, is that we want a new kind of assessment. It does not need to be dehumanising, as the current one is. It needs to focus on people’s needs and the costs arising from those needs. It also needs to establish properly who has additional costs arising from disability. We want to ensure that the benefit continues to be there to contribute to those additional costs for those who have them.
