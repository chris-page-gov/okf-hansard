Turn 110 · Sir Stephen Timms

The review is clear: it should be the claimant’s choice. The model here is Motability, where that is the case at the moment. At the shaping recommendations workshop that I mentioned, I met somebody who said that what she really would have liked was to have been able to give up her PIP in exchange for a particular kind of lifting bed that she had great difficulty in finding. She eventually did find one at a chain of bed shops; it cost a great deal of money. It may well be that a lot of people want beds of that kind. Maybe that kind of equipment could be provided on this basis as well.
