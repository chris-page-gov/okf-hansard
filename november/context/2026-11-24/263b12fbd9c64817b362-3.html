Turn 19 · Miatta Fahnbulleh

I will keep it brief, Madam Deputy Speaker. We are clear about our carbon budgets, and we will do everything that we can to deliver them. Clean electrification and clean power are absolutely critical. We have set out a road map, and we think that, through that, we can achieve the double win of reducing carbon emissions while responding to the affordability and cost of living crisis. We are taking action at home, but alongside that, we will continue to work with our partners to ensure that there is a coalition of the willing acting on electrification, methane, and our forests and oceans. Critically, we will also work on providing climate finance to support countries that are struggling, vulnerable and at the heart of this crisis in making the transition.

Turn 21 · Miatta Fahnbulleh

I agree with everything that the right hon. Member has said. There is scope to improve the process. One of the tragedies is that there were some good proposals in the cover decision on how we could begin to reform the process, so that it can have the impact that it needs to have. He is absolutely right; the poorest are at the very forefront of this. Adaptation is key, and we are determined to do our part to support adaptation; I chaired the adaptation track, and we came to an agreement on that. There is a question about the finance to support that, and we are committed to supporting that. However, adaptation cannot be a substitute for mitigation. We are about to overshoot 1.5°C, so we have to both adapt and mitigate if we are to have any chance of staying at 1.5°C.
