Turn 20 · Jim Shannon (Strangford) (DUP)

It is a pleasure to serve under your chairship, Ms Jardine. A big thank to you the hon. Member for Rugby (John Slinger) for securing this debate. Every Member of this House knows that while there may be no formal, statutory job description for what we do, the essence of our role is clear. For me, it is as clear as a bell. We are elected to give our voice to our constituents in this sovereign United Kingdom Parliament, to scrutinise legislation, and to hold the Executive to account. Every day of my life in this place—occasionally numerous times—my constituents in Strangford will be mentioned. Surprisingly enough, there are lots of people back home who watch the Parliament channel and let me know what is happening. I am very pleased to have two of my staff members in the Public Gallery. The one on the left is the one who writes all the speeches—Members know how hard that lady works.
