Turn 6 · Lord Young of Cookham (Con)

My Lords, there is much in the review to welcome. But despite what the Minister has said, there is a difference in approach between Milburn on the one hand and Timms on the other, particularly when it comes to young people who are claiming both universal credit and PIP. On that group, Milburn is quite explicit. He proposes “a deliberate exchange” of “less cash tied to … incapacity” for “more immediate” work and health support for young people. The Timms review, applied to exactly the same group, explicitly rules out replacing cash payments, proposing instead that awards remain cash-first with an option to exchange part for services. How does the Minister explain to that same group of people these two totally different approaches?
