Turn 48 · Speaker unlabelled

At the moment, it is unclear how much expense you could reasonably incur without it having to count as election expenses; for example, if you are an election agent who needs to employ a sign language interpreter for meetings and events that you go to. As the noble Baroness, Lady Leaman, pointed out, there is scope under Schedule 4A to the RPA 1983 for the Government to use secondary legislation to address this issue; and, of course, there is always scope for the Electoral Commission to issue guidance. But there is a certain simplicity to having a clear and straightforward amendment through primary legislation. So often, on other topics, the Government explain how they cannot do something so soon because secondary legislation takes time. Agreeing an amendment such as this on Report would cut through that Gordian knot and allow action to be taken very quickly and simply.
