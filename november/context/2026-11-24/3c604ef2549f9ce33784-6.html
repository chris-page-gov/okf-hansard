Turn 48 · Speaker unlabelled

We all want to see an end to predatory credit practices, and the expansion of credit unions can make that happen. Central to their ethos is the idea that they are built on the links between their members—the common bond—be they an employer, a trade union, a city or an area. That is important. As with many things in life, when it comes to financial institutions, bigger is sometimes better, and size matters. Credit unions can spread the risk if they have more members.
