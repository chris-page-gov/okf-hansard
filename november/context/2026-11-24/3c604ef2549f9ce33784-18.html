Turn 140 · Lucy Rigby

Let me set out why clause 9 should stand part of the Bill. Mass redress events occur when an issue, or set of issues, results in a large number of complaints with the potential for significant compensation to be owed. As we have seen, most recently with motor finance, which we discussed earlier, this can have a significant impact on consumers and firms, leading to delays in consumers accessing the redress to which they are entitled. The FCA’s existing tools to investigate potential mass redress events and operate consumer redress schemes have not always supported it to act effectively or quickly, which can exacerbate disruption for consumers and the market, as we are all aware. Clause 9 therefore improves the framework for dealing with mass redress events by introducing a simpler test for the FCA to use to establish a consumer redress scheme.
