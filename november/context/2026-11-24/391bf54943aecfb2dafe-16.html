I will begin with clauses 107 and 108. The changes made by the Bill need to work coherently alongside the wider legal framework, including where consequential changes become necessary as the Bill is implemented. Clause 107 recognises this and provides the flexibility to make those changes without requiring further primary legislation. It allows the Secretary of State to make regulations that make provision consequential on the Act, including amendments to existing legislation. This is a power to make provisions consequential on the Act, not a power to introduce new duties or penalties.
