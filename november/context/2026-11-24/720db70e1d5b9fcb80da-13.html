Turn 97 · Andy McDonald (Middlesbrough and Thornaby East) (Lab)

I wholeheartedly congratulate my right hon. Friend on his statement and the work he has done. The co-production model clearly has much to recommend it. I think I speak for everybody in the House in wanting to see that continued through to the White Paper. May I take my right hon. Friend back to the tension point between the two reviews? We have a different proposition altogether here, for which I am grateful, but there are additional costs from disability, which of course is no respecter of age, and the potential differential between the ages of 24 and 26 under Milburn may lead us to inadvertent age discrimination. I ask him to take those issues on board. Lastly, if there is any ability to take forward his excellent proposals between now and the White Paper, perhaps he might consider doing that.
