Turn 48 · Speaker unlabelled

Amendment 121 raises the important issue of voter choice. The Government understand that some electors may wish to express dissatisfaction with all the candidates standing in an election. However, the purpose of an election is to elect a candidate to represent the electorate. A vote for “none of the above”, in the manner set out in this amendment, would not achieve this purpose.
