Turn 41 · Speaker unlabelled

This is all because of Labour’s crippling, Treasury-driven, in-year efficiency saving targets imposed on the Ministry of Defence, which this year alone total some £3.5 billion. That has led to training exercises being cancelled and warships tied up alongside—anything to save an outlay of cash in-year. Despite all the promises to the contrary during the Bill’s passage, it also led to massive deferrals of spending on military accommodation, which has brought us to the reluctant conclusion that Labour’s much-vaunted new Defence Housing Service is a complete sham; it is no different from what went before. This is just a change of name-plate on the office wall.
