Turn 49 · Chris Murray (Edinburgh East and Musselburgh) (Lab)

It is a pleasure to serve with you in the Chair, Mr Turner. We have to be honest that there are people in Scotland who support independence and those who do not. After 12 years of division and ire, we have to respect that those are legitimate views. We are divided as a nation on that topic. We are having another episode of the single debate that we have had for 12 years in Scotland. Meanwhile, our country has the highest number of drug deaths in Europe. We have higher child mortality than in England, and satisfaction with the national health service has fallen by a half in the past 15 years, with 9% of people waiting more than a year for a hospital appointment in Scotland, compared with 2% in England.
