Turn 35 · Lucy Rigby

My understanding is that there will be sufficient parliamentary oversight as it stands. Let me come back to my point about the motives behind new clause 3. This is about the prescriptive nature of the information requirements. If we preserve in legislation the sanctions that go alongside those requirements, we risk locking into a new system the very requirements that the reforms are intended to improve. It is important to remember throughout this discussion that the unfair relationship provisions will remain in legislation, as will key protections such as sections 56, 75 and 75A of the Consumer Credit Act.
