Turn 2 · The Secretary of State for Health and Social Care (Yvette Cooper)

This morning the UK Health Security Agency published new figures showing that 5,258 people died this summer due to extreme heat. That is three quarters higher than the previous record in 2022, and it is well over double the number of winter deaths last year. I offer my deepest condolences to all those who lost a loved one this summer. The numbers are a stark reminder of the human impact of climate change, which is why I have asked the NHS to prepare for summer as seriously as we do for winter, and why we have our new heat taskforce and an initial £32 million to provide better cooling and ventilation in hospitals. The unprecedented summer has increased the challenges for the NHS for the winter, so we are also investing £1.9 billion over several years in urgent and emergency care facilities, with 25 new and expanded urgent care schemes and centres open or due to become operational this win …
