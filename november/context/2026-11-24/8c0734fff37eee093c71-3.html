Turn 17 · Baroness Sherlock (Lab)

My Lords, there is no “may”—there will be a White Paper in the spring. It will not be in “government spring”—that is, the summer. It will be followed by legislation where needed, and the Government will change the system. We have set up these reports, and there is a huge wealth of information and recommendations in them, which we are considering carefully. We will bring back our proposals, and Parliament will have the opportunity to have its say on them.

Turn 19 · Baroness Sherlock (Lab)

The real joy of co-production is that you have to recognise that not everybody will have the same view. If a team of people had the same view, it would not really add any value. It was an interesting, thoughtful and brave decision to go into co-production. I think a very different report would have emerged if it had been written just by officials or politicians. There is of course a range of views, and the Government will reflect on them all, including the minority views that have been expressed.

Turn 21 · Baroness Sherlock (Lab)
