Turn 87 · Lucy Rigby

I will first say a few words about the clause, which I know the hon. Member for Carshalton and Wallington intends to oppose. The clause introduces an important new referral mechanism between the FOS and the FCA. The principle behind that is very straightforward. Where the ombudsman comes across an issue in its casework that would benefit from consideration by the regulator—the FCA—there should be a clear route for the FOS to be able to raise that issue with the FCA, and there should then be a clear response from the FCA. This clause provides for referrals to be made in two scenarios. First, where the ombudsman considers that patterns emerging through individual complaints may point to a wider market issue across the market, that mechanism will ensure that the FCA is alerted to those issues early so it can decide whether further regulatory action is necessary.
