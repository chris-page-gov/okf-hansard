Turn 102 · Lucy Rigby

I would be delighted to. The minor changes that we are talking about include corrections, clarifications and minor technical updates. It will be for the regulators to determine exactly what meets each of those categories. With her experience, I suspect my hon. Friend will recognise that the regulators are best placed to make determinations about what fits that sort of minor and technical clarification. To give an example, last year the FDA consulted on reducing late filing charges from £250 to £100.
