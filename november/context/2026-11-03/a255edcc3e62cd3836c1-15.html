Turn 114 · Greg Smith

I beg to move amendment 79, in clause 22, page 16, line 23, at end insert— “(2A) Regulations under this section must ensure that— (a) a single authority or licence holder is designated as responsible for litter clearance on each highway specified in a licence; (b) the identity and contact details of that responsible body are published; (c) members of the public may report litter and make complaints directly to that responsible body; and (d) that the responsible body remains accountable for litter clearance notwithstanding any arrangements made with another person to carry out the work.” This amendment would establish clear responsibility and public accountability for litter clearance on licenced highways.
