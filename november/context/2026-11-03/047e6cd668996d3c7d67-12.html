Turn 91 · Speaker unlabelled

27: After Clause 6, insert the following new Clause— “Access to health innovation networks(1) The Secretary of State must make arrangements for a wide single national point of access to the health innovation networks in England.(2) The arrangements must enable a person to—(a) make a single request for support,(b) be directed to the appropriate health innovation network or networks, and(c) receive coordinated support where an innovation has potential application in more than one region.(3) The Secretary of State must publish information about the operation and performance of the arrangements.”Member's explanatory statement This new clause would require a single national point of access through which innovators and other organisations could obtain coordinated support from the health innovation networks in England.
