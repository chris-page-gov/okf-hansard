Turn 598 · Speaker unlabelled

I turn now to clauses 20 to 23, which are the general provisions of the Bill. Clause 20, as I set out, gives the Secretary of State the power to make consequential regulations arising from the Bill. The purpose of this power is to ensure that, once the Bill is enacted, any necessary consequential changes to the statute book can be made to ensure that the provisions of the Act operate effectively and coherently alongside existing legislation. The parliamentary procedure for this power is affirmative where the regulations amend or repeal provision made by an Act of Parliament, and negative in all other cases. We believe that that strikes the right balance between parliamentary scrutiny and a proportionate use of Parliament’s time.

I also commend to the Committee Government amendment 78, which will make a consequential change to the Bill’s extent provisions. It is necessary as a result of new schedule 1, which makes amendments to the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014 and the Companies Act 2006. The amendment will ensure that the Bill’s extent provisions properly reflect those amendments. It is therefore a technical and consequential amendment.
