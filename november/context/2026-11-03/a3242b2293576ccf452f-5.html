Turn 35 · The Minister for Border Security and Asylum (Anna Turley)

It is a pleasure to have you back in the chair again, Dr Murrison. I was actually just concluding my remarks. To round up, legal aid helps to ensure that those seeking protection can access justice, and supports the effective operation of the asylum system, which is fundamentally central to an open, independent and fair justice system. The United Kingdom also has obligations under the UN refugee convention to provide access to justice for those seeking asylum. For the reasons I have given, the Government cannot support this new clause. I respectfully ask that it be withdrawn.
