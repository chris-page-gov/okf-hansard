Turn 75 · Speaker unlabelled

Clause 4 already requires the licensing authority, be that the Secretary of State or the ORR, to consult on a licence before it can be granted. In my view, that is adequate consultation on licence conditions, and consultation at an appropriate time. Requiring the publication of a report before a licence comes into force would delay the process and add inefficiency. It would also be duplicative. As I have said several times already, we have been waiting an awfully long time for this project to proceed, and I am afraid this amendment would further delay an already elongated process.
