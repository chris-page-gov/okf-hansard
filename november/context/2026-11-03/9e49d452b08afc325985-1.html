Turn 2 · The Financial Secretary to the Treasury and Paymaster General (James Murray)

As I was just saying, the Government are backing high streets and small businesses with a package that means that all retail, hospitality and leisure properties in England will see their business rates frozen in real terms, reduced or cut to zero. The Government are also helping employers, particularly small and medium employers, to recruit the workers that they need by expanding local apprenticeship services and providing eligible small and medium-sized enterprises with a £2,000 hiring payment when recruiting young workers.

Turn 4 · James Murray

I thank my hon. Friend for letting us know about the Bird & Barrel and Bexley Brewery, which both sound worth a visit. I am very glad that at the Budget last week the Chancellor was able to offer support specifically for small brewers by announcing an increase in the generosity of small producer relief. The changes made move to a more generous discount taper for products under 3.5% ABV and uprate the marginal discount for products between 3.5% and 8.4% by retail prices index plus an additional 10%. That will provide £10 million of support per year to over 2,000 small alcohol producers across the country.
