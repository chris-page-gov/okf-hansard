Turn 25 · Baroness Neville-Rolfe (Con)

I thank the Minister for her helpful letter last week, answering the range of questions I had raised at Second Reading. I think that that is a symptom for a wide variety of policy issues that this important Bill raises. I have added my name to Amendment 22, on choice, which was so persuasively and sensitively introduced by my noble friend Lord Howe. I believe that private care can complement NHS care, allow treatment nearer to home, and cut waiting times. I particularly support the “have regards” in proposed new subsection (5). I also favour the follow-up review, although there should be more focus on the value and usefulness of the independent sector in a health system that, frankly, is under so much pressure. I have had very positive experiences at Specsavers, which is contracted to do eye and audio tests in a number of NHS trusts.
