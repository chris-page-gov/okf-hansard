Turn 39 · The Chair

With this it will be convenient to discuss the following: Amendment 47, in clause 1, page 2, line 5, leave out lines 5 and 6. This amendment would remove the ORR’s duty to have regard to contributing to sustainable development when carrying out its duties under the Act. Clause stand part. Amendment 48, in clause 2, page 2, line 39, leave out lines 39 and 40. This amendment would remove the Secretary of State’s duty to have regard to contributing to sustainable development when carrying out its duties under the Act. Clause 2 stand part. Amendment 68, in clause 103, page 67, line 18, leave out lines 18 to 21 and insert “after the Secretary of State has published its guidance to the ORR under section 1(5).” This amendment prevents the Act from coming into force until the Secretary of State has published the guidance to the ORR on how it must undertake its regulatory functions under the Act.
