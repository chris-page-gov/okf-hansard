Turn 122 · Justin Madders

The clauses are about the transfer of assets, rights and liabilities. That is why the amendment is in there—to ensure that if there is an outstanding compensation claim, there is a power for the transferee to pay that. We do not want to be in a situation where a party who is trying to exit the arrangements is refusing to meet its obligations. This is a complicated set of amendments, but they are effectively trying to cover all bases in terms of whether there is compensation. It is also about whether a party leaving the agreement is seeking some compensation for costs that they have occurred. This is a fairly broad amendment, but it is necessary. We obviously hope not to have many situations where licences are transferred in whole or part, but it is prudent to have provisions in the Bill to ensure that those circumstances are catered for.
