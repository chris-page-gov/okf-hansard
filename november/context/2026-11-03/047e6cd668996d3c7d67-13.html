Turn 102 · Speaker unlabelled

28: Clause 7, page 4, line 31 at end insert— “(1A) The Secretary of State must publish an independently verified long-term assessment of the workforce needs of the health service and a ten year education and training plan to deliver on those needs.(1B) The first assessment and plan under subsection (1A) must be published within one month of the day on which this Act is passed. (1C) The Secretary of State must keep the assessment and plan under review and revise both at least once every five years.”Member’s explanatory statement This amendment would require the Secretary of State to publish an independently verified assessment of the long-term workforce needs of the health service and a ten-year education and training plan to meet those needs, with the first assessment and plan published within one month of Royal Assent.
