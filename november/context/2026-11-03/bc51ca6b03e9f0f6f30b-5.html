Turn 34 · Lord Scriven (LD)

My Lords, I just have a very quick practical suggestion to deal with some of the issues the noble Lord, Lord Blencathra, and others raised about staff neutrality, because that is what we are talking about. Would it not be wise, rather than going through a long process, to make it part of the NHS constitution that staff have to be neutral in how they deal with fellow staff and patients with whom they come into contact? I ask the Minister to consider that seriously when she reports back not just today but on Report. It is just a practical suggestion.

Turn 40 · Speaker unlabelled

To return to the noble Baroness, Lady Gerada, improving the data that we hold is key. We are developing the unified information standard for protected characteristics programme. That will improve the consistency and quality of data on protected characteristics, including ethnicity and religion or belief. That will apply both to patients and the workforce. The noble Baroness asked about the inclusion of Jewish and Sikh categories in data collection. That is not currently the case, as she is aware, but the consultation I have just mentioned will include reference to the possibility of including Jewish and Sikh categories, as she referred to.
