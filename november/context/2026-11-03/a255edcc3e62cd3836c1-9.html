Clause 7 sets out two types of condition that must be included in every licence. First, every licence must include conditions controlling the tolls that the licence holder may charge. Those conditions may set the amount, or the maximum amount, of a toll. Alternatively, they may require the amount to be approved by the ORR. That means that a licence holder will not have an unrestricted power to set tolls.
