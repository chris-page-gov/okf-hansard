Turn 103 · Speaker unlabelled

I am not trying to be difficult, but clarity on this point really does matter, as we have discussed under previous amendments. Can the Minister be really specific about the definition of “emergency”, so that we do not find ourselves in future operating with licensees asking for force majeure rules for things that they argue are an emergency, but that actually affect everyone, whether they are a licensee under this Bill or any other highways authority out there. They could all put their hands up and legitimately say that it is not their fault, and that it has happened to them and not because of them, which could be argued to be an emergency position. Where is an “emergency” in the scope of this Bill defined?
