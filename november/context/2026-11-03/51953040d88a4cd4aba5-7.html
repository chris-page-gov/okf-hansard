Turn 49 · Lilian Greenwood

I can assure the hon. Member that we are considering the issues for graduates, as well as for younger people leaving school with lower-level qualifications. Tackling the NEET challenge is not only an economic imperative, important as that is; it is about fairness, aspiration and ensuring that every young person has the chance to build a better future. We look forward to the publication of the final “Young People and work” report in the coming weeks. Its findings will provide an important opportunity to consider what more must be done to address the challenges facing young people today, and to ensure that every young person has the opportunity to participate, to progress and to thrive.
