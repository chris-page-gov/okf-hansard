Turn 23 · Speaker unlabelled

These examples are not cited because the individuals held controversial political opinions. I am citing them because, in a democratic society, people are entitled to support Israel, support Palestine, criticise Governments and participate in political debate. This amendment is not about foreign policy, it is not about whether one supports Israel or Palestine, and it is not about criticism of the Government of Israel. The amendment concerns something entirely different: expressions of hatred, prejudice, conspiracy theories and collective hostility directed against Jews just because they happen to be Jews. It concerns conduct that would rightly be condemned if directed against any other ethnic or religious minority.
