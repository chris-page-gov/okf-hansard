Turn 17 · Baroness Finlay of Llandaff (CB)

My Lords, can the Minister confirm that the partner policy that she referred to—which is being developed as guidance and would avoid anything going into statute—will include a requirement that the carer providing principal care for someone when they are discharged from a unit will be assessed for how much they can and are willing to do in providing care and will be taught procedures that may make the care of the person easier? This includes simple things such as how to help someone get up from a chair, how to manage if someone falls on the floor and even how to give injections for breakthrough medication, such as for pain relief if that is indicated—so that carers are not left feeling that they do not know what to do when the person they are caring for comes home.
