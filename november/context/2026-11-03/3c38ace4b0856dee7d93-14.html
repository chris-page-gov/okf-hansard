Turn 110 · Speaker unlabelled

Given the impact that the new clauses would have on UK businesses and public authorities, it is important to consider them alongside wider UK policy and regulation. The Government remain committed to working with international partners and businesses to ensure that global supply chains are free from human and labour rights abuses. We continue to strengthen co-operation on tackling forced labour in supply chains with key partners through a number of multilateral forums. Measures in the Bill that the Committee has already discussed and agreed to will continue to strengthen our understanding and awareness of modern slavery in our supply chains. For those reasons, I respectfully ask for the new clause to be withdrawn.
