Turn 13 · Lord Patel (CB)

My Lords, we recognise that outcomes for people with brain tumours and cancers, particularly children, is a survival time of no more than 13 months to five years. Although it is important to do clinical trials using drugs, the problem we have is the blood-brain barrier, which protects the brain from being affected by these drugs. We need to find novel ways of treatment. Some are being researched; for instance, focused ultrasound, micro-RNA treatments and personalised vaccines developed for particular patients for each of their tumours. We need to support these novel ways of finding treatments for brain tumours that do not apply to other diseases.
