Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero (Miatta Fahnbulleh)

COP31 in Türkiye comes at a pivotal moment for global climate action. The world is experiencing the accelerating impacts of climate change alongside acute pressures from energy insecurity, economic uncertainty and geopolitical instability. This summer showed climate change is no longer a distant threat. Here in Britain, we saw record heatwaves, droughts and wildfires. Homes were destroyed as fires raged, and extreme heat closed schools across the land and strained our NHS and public services. The first duty of Government is to protect our communities. And the reality is that Britain cannot tackle a challenge of this scale alone. Climate change does not respect borders, which is why working with other countries through processes like the conference of the parties remains so important.
