Many times in this very Chamber I have raised the issue of the burden of unpaid caring on one’s health, well-being and finances. As many noble Lords said last week, we know that many unpaid carers do not identify themselves, but women are less likely to do so. Identifying carers is important, but they must not simply become another statistic, because behind every carer there is a family.
