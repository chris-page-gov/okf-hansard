Turn 59 · Speaker unlabelled

I have two questions for the Minister: if a site is suitable, why not publish the evidence, and if it represents value for money, why not publish the costs? The situations in both Oxfordshire and north Yorkshire demonstrate why Parliament should require proper assessments and cost transparencies for every site of this scale. It is also worth mentioning that I am grateful to the right hon. Member for Wetherby and Easingwold (Sir Alec Shelbrooke) for adding his name in support of new clauses 30 and 31. This is about responsible decision making, proper scrutiny of public money and ensuring that local communities are not left dealing with the consequences of decisions in which they have had little say. I urge the Committee to support new clauses 30 and 31.
