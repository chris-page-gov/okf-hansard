Turn 803 · Siân Berry

I beg to move amendment 69, in clause 50, page 52, line 1, at end insert— “(1A) Where any provision of this Act includes a power to make regulations and such provision or regulations would affect— (a) the identification of victims of slavery and human trafficking in Scotland, (b) the provision of support to victims of slavery and human trafficking in Scotland, or (c) any duties on Scottish public authorities relating to tackling slavery and human trafficking in Scotland, the Secretary of State must consult with and receive consent from Scottish Ministers before making such regulations.” This amendment would require the Secretary of State to consult with and receive consent from Scottish Ministers before making regulations relating to slavery and human trafficking which affect Scotland.
