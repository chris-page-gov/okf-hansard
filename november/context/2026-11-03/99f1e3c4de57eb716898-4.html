Turn 27 · Sarah Sackman

It is an honour to serve under your chairmanship, Sir Alec. The two orders before the Committee today are all about open justice. Taken together, they extend the broadcasting regime that already operates successfully in our courts. Specifically, they remove the prohibitions on recording in three areas: first, proceedings in the administrative court which is part of the High Court; secondly, proceedings involving litigants in person in the Court of Appeal; and thirdly, the magistrates court, to enable the recording and broadcasting of sentencing remarks delivered by the chief magistrate in open court.
