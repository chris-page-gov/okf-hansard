Access to Child Trust Funds

Commons · 2026-11-03 · Turns 1–1 of 1 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Justice (Jake Richards)

In 2005, child trust funds were created to give every eligible child a financial asset at the start of adulthood, with around 6.3 million accounts opened for eligible children. However, when child trust funds were established, no specific provision was made for how the money could be accessed where a young person lacked the mental capacity to manage their financial affairs when they turned 18. This has meant that an estimated 63,000 to 126,000 young people have not been able to easily access and benefit from money that was set aside for them. In those circumstances, families have had to go through what can be a lengthy and confusing process of getting a court order. Parents and carers have campaigned for years to secure a fairer and more proportionate route to those funds. This Government have listened to these concerns and are determined to take action.
