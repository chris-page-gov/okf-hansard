Turn 45 · Lord Scriven (LD)

My point about the important value of neutrality is this, and let me give an example. You might not be discriminatory: you have somebody who is Jewish, somebody who is Muslim, somebody who is Christian and somebody of no faith, and you treat them all equally, but you might have something on your body which is not neutral to two of them. You are therefore possibly treating that person differently, from their perspective. If neutrality and fairness is such an important value for the NHS, can the Minister go away and look seriously at making it a key component in the NHS constitution, because it is not there at the moment?
