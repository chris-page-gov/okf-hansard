Turn 96 · Justin Madders

Clauses 16 to 21 engage the fundamental legislative scaffolding required to operate licensed highways by plugging it into the existing legislative framework. The clauses establish clear responsibility for operating and maintaining licensed highways. They give licence holders the legal status and functions they need while retaining specified statutory powers with a public body. The clauses allow a licensee to delegate, without removing its responsibility or the ORR’s oversight, and protect continuity by allowing a strategic highways company to intervene when a licence holder cannot properly perform its functions. The clauses allow relevant responsibility to pass automatically to the relevant strategic highways company when a licence ends without a replacement.
