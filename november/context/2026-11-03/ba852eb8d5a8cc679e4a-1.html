Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Blair McDougall)

During the passage of the Steel Industry (Nationalisation) Act 2026, the Government promised to provide regular updates to Parliament on British Steel. In line with the Act’s provisions, British Steel entered public ownership on 16 July 2026. This statement provides an update on the financial and operational performance of British Steel under public ownership and the company’s governance and strategic direction. Governance and strategic direction In July 2026, Ministers appointed a new board of directors. This was followed by the appointment of Alan Lovell as chair in September. Two further board members are shareholder non-executive directors representing Government, one from the Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade and one from UK Government Investments.
