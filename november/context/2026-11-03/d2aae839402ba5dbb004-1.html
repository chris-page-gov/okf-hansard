Turn 2 · Jim Shannon (Strangford) (DUP)

I commend the hon. Lady for bringing this incredibly sensitive and difficult subject to the House. Back in 2022, we in Northern Ireland introduced legislation for a stand-alone criminal offence, carrying a sentence of up to 14 years. Strangulation is a terrifyingly accurate indicator of escalating abuse and potential lethality. Does the hon. Lady, who is a friend, agree that if we are truly to protect victims, the Government must work hand in hand with the Department of Justice in Northern Ireland and local support charities to launch a robust and targeted public awareness campaign, to ensure that healthcare workers, police officers and victims themselves can recognise the signs of non-fatal strangulation before it turns fatal?
