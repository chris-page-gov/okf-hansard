Turn 2 · Esther McVey (Tatton) (Con)

I beg to move, That leave be given to bring in a Bill to introduce compulsory labelling of halal and kosher meat and products containing halal and kosher meat; and for connected purposes. I am reintroducing this Bill following the considerable support it received when it was previously brought to the House, as the concerns about lack of consumer choice, animal welfare and transparency have not gone away. I therefore hope the House forgives me for repeating much of what I said last time, as the arguments for the Bill have not changed and remain compelling. This Bill does not seek to ban halal or kosher meat; it seeks to introduce clear compulsory labelling so that consumers know exactly what they are purchasing.
