Turn 116 · Speaker unlabelled

My questions to the Minister on this group of amendments are as follows. Will the Government’s long-awaited workforce plan set out a specific number and a funded route for learning disability nurses, together with the vacancy data that I was told does not exist? Will the department look at the Dutch model and explain why the trial funding for these posts was stopped earlier this year? Will the Minister commit to looking into reinstating the funding for these training posts? Will she write to me, and to the Committee, on whether the Government believe that a co-ordinating learning disability physician should be in place in England? People with a learning disability are dying 19 years too early. Their needs should not be left out of any workforce plan.
