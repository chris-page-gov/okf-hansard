Turn 19 · Lord Farmer (Con)

My Lords, I thank all those who contributed so powerfully to this debate. I think that all noble Lords, apart from the noble Baroness, Lady Gill, supported the argument against these sanctions being available to every governor to punish any prisoner for any crime. It is all very well to say that there will be advice and that governors will take things into account, but we have 110 prisons with 110 different prison governors. If the sanctions are there, they will be used differently. I hear the Minister when she says that she will be happy to talk to me. However, I am aware of the mood of the House. I am also aware of the whipping on the other side and my own party’s lack of support, but this is, frankly, a very important matter and I wish to test the opinion of the House.
