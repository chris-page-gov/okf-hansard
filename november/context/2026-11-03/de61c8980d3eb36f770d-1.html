Turn 7 · Lord Stewart of Dirleton (Con)

My Lords, the Prison Rules 1999 already provide prison governors with an extensive suite of disciplinary measures. Prisoners who offend against prison discipline may be cautioned, suffer forfeiture of privileges, be excluded from work that they carry out in the prison estate, suffer a deduction or loss of earnings, be confined to their cells, suffer removal from the wing upon which they are located or experience payback punishment. What do these existing measures have in common? They are all directed against the offender himself—his privileges, earnings, freedom of movement on the wing or opportunity to work.
