Turn 113 · Blake Stephenson

New clauses 51 and 52 concern a particular route; 58,000 family visas were granted in the year to June, and it remains the one major route to this country with no limit at all. New clause 51 would introduce an annual cap on spouse and partner visas, set after consultation, with no more than 5% from any one country. New clause 52 would set four requirements: two years of marriage before settlement; a sponsor income of £38,700; both parties to be at least 23; and no first cousin marriages. I anticipate that Committee members may raise a number of objections to the new clauses, so let me pre-empt some of those directly. Turn 116 · Anna Turley

The Government’s position is clear: net migration must come down. We support the right to family life and value the contribution that migrants make to our society. However, that must be balanced with a properly controlled and managed immigration system. British citizens and those settled in the UK are free to enter into a genuine relationship with whomever they choose.
