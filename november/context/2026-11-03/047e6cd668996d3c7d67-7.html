Turn 55 · Speaker unlabelled

26: Clause 6, page 4, line 20, at end insert— “(5) The Secretary of State must establish a transformational fund, within a year of the date on which the Health Act 2026 is passed, to enable improvements in access to specialist palliative care and its support to generalist services.”Member's explanatory statement This amendment would require the Secretary of State to create a dedicated palliative and end of life care transformation fund to support service redesign by scaling proven models of care that help deliver the government’s intended shift from hospital to community care for people at the end of life.
