Turn 1 · The Minister for Intergovernmental Relations and European Relations (Mr Hamish Falconer)

The UK Government’s commitment to working in partnership with the devolved Governments continues to be reflected in the depth and consistency of engagement. This reporting period, particularly April to June, directly covers the pre and post-election periods for the Senedd and Scottish Parliament. While opportunities for meetings between Governments were therefore lower than in 2025, the level of engagement remained high, demonstrating the steady and frequent nature of UK Government ministerial engagement with devolved Government counterparts. Beyond formal forums such as the Interministerial Standing Committee, Ministers and their devolved Government counterparts met across a wide range of policy areas—from industrial strategy and trade agreements to welfare reform and getting people back into work—demonstrating that collaborative working is embedded across Governments.
