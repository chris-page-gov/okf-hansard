Turn 6 · Speaker unlabelled

Amendment 258 is at the heart of the group. It would require integrated care boards and NHS trusts to have regard to the need to prevent antisemitism and all other forms of racism affecting patients and staff. It would also act to prevent staff conduct that amounts to support for proscribed organisations and respond effectively when racist conduct occurs, including by supporting those affected. The Secretary of State would report to Parliament every year on progress. I appreciate that the Minister might explain that the public sector equality duty already covers this ground. With respect, it does not. While it requires public bodies to have due regard to eliminating harassment and discrimination, it imposes no specific duty to respond to incidents and to support those affected, nor does it require any reporting to Parliament.

Turn 7 · Speaker unlabelled

I recognise that the NHS’s existing equality frameworks are important, but they do not adequately address the particular challenges facing Jewish staff and patients. The workforce race equality standard compares the experiences of white staff with those of ethnic minority staff. As most Jewish staff identify their ethnicity as white, antisemitism is largely invisible in this data. Indeed, the finding I quoted earlier came from the NHS staff survey breakdown by religion, not from the standard.

Turn 8 · Speaker unlabelled

The position for patients is even more concerning. The patient and carer race equality framework applies only to mental health services, while the health inequalities impact assessments carried out by NHS bodies depend on the quality of available data. Yet the NHS does not routinely record patients’ religion in any national patient dataset. Where this information is collected, it is generally done locally and inconsistently, whether on hospital admission for chaplaincy purposes or in certain GP and mental health records. We therefore have no means of identifying discrimination against Jewish staff other than through the staff survey and electronic staff record.
