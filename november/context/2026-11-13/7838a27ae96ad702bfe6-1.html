Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport (Vicky Foxcroft)

I am today updating the House on a package of measures to expand opportunities for young people across England, enabling far more access to activities such as youth clubs, community sports and performing arts. These measures are important further steps to deliver “Youth Matters”, which is the Government strategy to support the next generation to thrive by increasing the number of places to go, things to do and adults who care. The Government are announcing the 60 local areas in England which will receive support through the new youth matters fund. These areas will benefit from over £20 million immediately and over £400 million of investment over the coming three years. Young people will help determine the new activities and youth facilities they most want in these areas. Funding has been targeted towards areas facing the greatest challenges.
