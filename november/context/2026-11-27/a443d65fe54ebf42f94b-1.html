Turn 1 · Greg Smith (Mid Buckinghamshire) (Con) [R]

I refer Members to my entry in the Register of Members’ Financial Interests. Sovereignty is not divisible. UK sovereignty was hard won by instruction of those we serve in this House in 2016. Sovereignty is now, in live legislation before this House, under threat, re-subjugating the people of the United Kingdom to EU lawmaking, with no veto or effective influence, given the majority voting system in the European Union Council of Ministers. The Conservatives are clear that we oppose this legislation, as is proven by the colleagues from across the Conservative Front Bench whose names appear on this petition, and by the confirmation issued by my right hon. Friend the Leader of the Opposition in her statement yesterday.
