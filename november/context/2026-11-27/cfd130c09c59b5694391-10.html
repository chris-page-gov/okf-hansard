It is very much my hope that, if we introduce better legislation, better laws and better regulations today, we may prevent these things from happening again in the future. While we cannot change the past, ultimately, if we can prevent such things from happening again, it will be a victory for all of us. The courts are being used as a tool to continue to abuse women. That is the most horrific abuse, using our legal system against an individual.
