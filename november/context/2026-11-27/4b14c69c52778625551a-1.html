Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Blair McDougall)

On 26 and 27 November, the Minister for Trade, Lord Sarwar, attended the 11th meeting of the Commission of the comprehensive and progressive agreement for trans-Pacific partnership in Hanoi, Vietnam. CPTPP brings together a diverse group of economies under a high-standard agreement, united in their commitment to strengthening collective resilience and upholding the rules-based system, amid global uncertainty. It is one of the largest free trade areas in the world, accounting for 14% of global GDP, or £13 trillion in 2025. The UK’s accession is estimated to add £2 billion to UK GDP and £1 billion to UK wages in the long run, with every UK region and nation set to benefit.
