Turn 30 · John Cooper

It was a sobering experience. Journalists take their responsibilities very, very seriously. With SLAPPs, we see a perversion of that time-honoured system. There is a lot of case law. Scots law is, in my opinion, excellent in this field, and there is a lot to be learned from it, but this SLAPP system is a perversion of the basics of that. Free speech is a beacon in the night, but when we get closer to it, we realise that it is less of an arc light and more of a candle, and candles can be snuffed out. That is what these SLAPPs do: they snuff out freedom of speech. They are very, very dangerous, and we must change the law. Yes, individual citizens should have protections—of course they should—but the pendulum has swung too far in favour of people with deep pockets and not necessarily a good and righteous position.
