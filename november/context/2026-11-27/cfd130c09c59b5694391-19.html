Turn 145 · Alicia Kearns

I acknowledge that one difficulty for the Government is that most of these threats are made in the dark, so we do not know their volume. However, the Government could open an online consultation—I do not want to suggest too onerous a requirement—in which people can submit, with restrictions, the sort of things that they have faced, to give us an idea of their volume. There could also be some sort of stakeholder engagement more appropriate for lawyers. Then, a year after the legislation comes into force, the Government could review the data again by reaching out to those stakeholders and lawyers and asking, “Do you think there has been a reduction? If not, why not, and how can we go further?” That sort of commitment to stakeholder engagement does not need to be included on the face of the Bill, but it could ensure that the legislation delivers action on such threats.
