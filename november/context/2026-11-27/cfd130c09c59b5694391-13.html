Turn 104 · Lloyd Hatton

My hon. Friend speaks a great deal of truth. We have looked in horror at the reporting around the Russell Brand case, and watched documentaries and read newspaper investigations. The phrase “hiding in plain sight” comes up again and again—it is used so often—and one cannot help but feel that such people are able to lean on particular legal tactics to hide in plain sight. That is part of the operation that they undertake. They use legal tactics to maintain silence so that they can continue to offend without any material challenge. The victims do not feel able or sufficiently empowered to speak up and speak out. As I was saying, my constituents and the public at large pay the price for SLAPPs, even though they do not have the means or motive to ever pursue such legal tactics.
