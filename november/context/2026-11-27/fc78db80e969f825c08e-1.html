Turn 1 · The Minister for Homelessness, Democracy, Communities and Faith (Florence Eshalomi)

The Prime Minister has launched an urgent national drive to end rough sleeping, and the Government are working to ensure that all people sleeping rough have an offer of a route off the streets this Christmas. The Government have announced £874 million for the rough sleeping programme to date, as part of more than £4.4 billion being invested in homelessness and rough sleeping services over the next three years. The rough sleeping programme supports local areas to ensure that people sleeping rough have a route off the streets this winter and beyond. But we know that an offer of immediate support will not be enough to end rough sleeping, which is why we are also providing funding for settled accommodation and intensive support for people with the most complex and long-term experiences of rough sleeping.
