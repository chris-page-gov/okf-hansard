Water Customer Support and Advocacy

Turn 1 · The Minister of State, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Emma Hardy)

I am updating the House on the steps this Government are taking to give water customers a stronger voice and help low-income households with high essential water needs with their bills. Customers in receipt of benefits such as pension credit and who cannot cut their water use due to their circumstances, such as medical conditions, are eligible for support but many simply are not getting it. For too long, water companies have let people down. Bills are rising, performance is falling short and trust in water companies has reached a record low. Customers deserve a reliable service, fair treatment, value for money and greater support for those who need it. Our programme of reform will put these priorities at the heart of a fairer, more efficient and more affordable water system, delivering lasting improvements in service while minimising the impact on bills.
