Turn 27 · Lucy Rigby

These two instruments serve a common purpose. They make targeted practical changes to relevant regulatory frameworks and support innovation in our financial services sector while, very importantly, maintaining appropriate protections for consumers and markets. Members know, we legislated to establish a financial services regulatory framework for cryptoassets, including stablecoins, with the aim of giving firms greater certainty to invest and innovate in the UK and, importantly, protecting consumers.
