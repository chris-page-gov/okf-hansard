I will finish with the amendments from the noble Lord, Lord Tarassenko. But we have to remember that data that was collected during Covid, with specific permission to be used only during Covid, was reused after Covid for something that it should not have been used for, even though it was used for a good thing. NHS England and the DHSC do not exactly have a good record in this.

Turn 10 · Speaker unlabelled
