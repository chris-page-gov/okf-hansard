Turn 84 · Speaker unlabelled

On Amendment 80, I can assure the noble Baronesses, Lady Grey-Thompson and Lady Chisholm, and the noble Lord, Lord Sharkey, that Clause 21 does require Ministers to publish ICBs’ performance assessments, and this will include how they discharge their commissioning functions, including in relation to the more specialised services, which we have been seeking. There were questions about accountability. Certainly, in future the NHS oversight framework will include an assessment of ICB capability and ICBs will be subject to statutory performance and accountability arrangements, including a requirement to publish annual reports and accounts.
