Turn 91 · Speaker unlabelled

The Government’s newly published 10 Year Capital Plan for Health and Social Care commits to improving climate resilience but, sadly, falls short of identifying the resources allocated to climate agitation, and it does not have a specific requirement yet in legislation. Where is the impetus to try to find this funding? My amendment seeks to address this omission by placing current guidance and recommendations on a statutory footing and strengthening the levels of oversight to ensure that each NHS trust and foundation can set out the specific risks it faces with its estate, workforce and patients.
