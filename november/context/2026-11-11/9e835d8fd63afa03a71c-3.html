It is an honour to serve under your chairmanship, Mr Twigg. I thank the hon. Member for Portsmouth North (Amanda Martin) for securing the debate. Our emergency services repeatedly put themselves in harm’s way to protect, serve and save our communities. They should never have to accept threats, harassment or physical violence as simply part of the job. It is not what they signed up for and it is criminality. Turn 19 · Amanda Martin

I thank the Minister for her work and her words. I am glad that we have had cross-party support in this debate today. I welcome the Opposition spokesperson’s standing firm against abuse and violence towards our emergency workers. My dad was a police officer, so this is very important to me. We have come a very long way from clapping our emergency workers during covid to the state that we are in now, but I do have hope and I want to work across Government to make sure that our emergency workers go to work every day feeling safe and protected as they protect our communities.
