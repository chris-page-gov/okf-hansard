Turn 17 · The Minister of State, Department for Education and Department for Work and Pensions (Baroness Smith of Malvern) (Lab)

My Lords, in January 2026 the Government strengthened their guidance on mobile phones in schools, making it clear that all schools should have mobile phones policy that prevents pupils having access to their phone throughout the school day, including during lessons, the time between lessons, break times and lunchtimes. This guidance was put on a statutory footing during the passage of what became the Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act, and schools have been required to implement it since September 2026.
