Turn 33 · Speaker unlabelled

Dr Danny Chambers made this point in the Commons. He argued that the responsibility for public health cannot sit with the Department of Health and Social Care alone when decisions across government have impacts on people’s health. I note that the Minister in the other place expressed considerable sympathy with the principle and pointed to a number of cross-government amendments, such as the warm homes plan, rented sector reform and the Keep Britain Working review. But I argue that there is a difference between saying that departments should work together and having a structure that makes them accountable for doing so. We hear repeated commitments to prevention and tackling health inequalities, yet our healthy life expectancy keeps declining.

Turn 34 · Speaker unlabelled

Research published by the Health Equity Evidence Centre found that health impact assessments were sporadic across government, with little central documentation or consistent policy. The authors specifically identified the need for stronger leadership and dedicated resources. The World Health Organization has identified governance, accountability, leadership and dedicated resources as essential to making cross-government public health action work. The NHS is too often left to deal with the consequences of decisions made by other government departments. We need the Prime Minister and the whole Cabinet to take responsibility for improving the nation’s health.

Turn 40 · Speaker unlabelled

I understand that vaccination coverage data is currently captured and published through different reporting mechanisms, and not all eligible cohorts are reported consistently or publicly. Inequalities reporting is improving but is not standardised across programmes. This therefore makes it difficult to compare performance across programmes, geographies and over time, limiting scrutiny and the ability to identify where additional action is needed. Having to report on what is getting done is a good incentive for Secretaries of State to make sure that things are being done.
