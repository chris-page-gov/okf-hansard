Turn 61 · Speaker unlabelled

54: Clause 12, page 9, line 8 at end insert— “(za) in subsection (1), after paragraph (j) insert—“(k) such other services and facilities as are required for persons with a primary health need arising from a progressive or deteriorating disease which is reasonably expected to continue for the remainder of the person's life, including where the person's condition is intermittent or fluctuating, and without regard to the rate of such progression or deterioration or whether a person is entering a terminal phase of their life.””Member’s explanatory statement This amendment would ensure that a wider range of serious chronic diseases, such as severe dementia, would qualify for NHS Continuing Care and not have eligibility restricted on grounds that the condition is fluctuating or is not deemed to be in a terminal phase.
