Turn 4 · The Parliamentary Secretary, HM Treasury (Lord Pitt-Watson) (Lab)

My Lords, Ministers continue to engage across government on this issue. The Government have provided a clear legal route for the proceeds to be transferred for humanitarian purposes in Ukraine. Our priority is ensuring that they reach Ukraine as soon as possible. The door remains open for Mr Abramovich to act, but the Government are fully prepared to go to court if necessary to enforce the commitment he made when Chelsea Football Club was sold.

Turn 7 · Lord Pitt-Watson (Lab)

Let me be clear about this. On 17 December last year, the Government issued a licence creating a clear legal route for the transfer of £2.5 billion of Chelsea FC sale proceeds. Those funds have not yet been released. I imagine the noble Baroness is referring to the Foundation for the Victims of Conflict? I see she is nodding. It is for the Charity Commission to review that application. We have written to the application sponsor, Mike Penrose, and are awaiting his reply. If that charity is approved, the Government’s licence is clear that any money from the sale must go to humanitarian purposes in Ukraine. However, noble Lords will understand that because of the legal procedures that might take place, I am limited in what I should say publicly to your Lordships’ House.
