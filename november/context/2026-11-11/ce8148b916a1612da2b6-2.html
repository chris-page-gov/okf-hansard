Turn 9 · The Earl of Clancarty (CB)

My Lords, following the question asked by the noble Baroness, Lady Kramer, does the Minister agree that, for arts organisations, which are often in a financially precarious position, it is their categorisation for business rates that urgently needs to be looked at? Does he agree with the Campaign for the Arts that regional theatres and cinemas, for example, should not be taxed at the same level as, say, an Amazon warehouse, as this ignores the value that these venues bring to community infrastructure, as much as high street businesses?

Turn 11 · Lord Swire (Con)

My Lords, is it not time to accept that our high streets will never look the same again, from a combination of factors including online shopping, terrible car charges for anyone parking for more than a short time and out-of-town shopping centres? Surely the only way that we can regenerate our town centres is by encouraging younger people and families back into them, which, in turn, will engender a night-time economy and lead to a plethora of new businesses and shops. We need to think this from the start and stop fiddling around the edges.
