Turn 20 · Speaker unlabelled

Amendments 168, 169 and 268 from the noble Baroness, Lady Bennett, are about commercial access and public control. Commercial suppliers may require tightly controlled access to identifiable data to maintain and support NHS IT systems, as well as to deliver direct care. The appropriate response is to ensure that any access is necessary, role-based, supervised and auditable, rather than to prohibit it outright. There are already substantial statutory safeguards against conflicts of interest, including through the provider selection regime and the Procurement Act. Ministers and civil servants are also required to identify, declare and appropriately manage actual, potential and perceived conflicts of interest.
