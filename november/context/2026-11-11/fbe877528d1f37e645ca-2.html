Turn 12 · Baroness Kramer (LD)

My Lords, do the Government understand that this legal warfare and paralysis is playing right into Putin’s hands by failing to deliver moneys that should be going to humanitarian causes in Ukraine because of constant delays. I am very concerned that £1.4 billion is not transferred to companies owned by Abramovich that claim they made loans to the entity and therefore have first rights. Can the Minister give us the Government’s assurance on that issue? This is turning into Jarndyce v Jarndyce. How much is being consumed in legal fees and how much will that deplete the money that goes to the very worthy causes for which it is intended?
