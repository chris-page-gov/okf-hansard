Employers in my constituency include many farmers and growers and related food firms and hauliers, yet much of the land in my constituency is threatened with immense solar developments, which will take that land out of production. Will he take a look at that, discuss it with colleagues across Government and see how we can reverse that trend? It puts our national economic resilience at risk.
