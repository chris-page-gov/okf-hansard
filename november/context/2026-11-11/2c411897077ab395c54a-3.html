Turn 20 · Baroness Curran (Lab)

I congratulate my noble friend on getting in in the last three seconds there. I recognise what he has said, and that is certainly not our intention. There is now much greater coherence to our civil nuclear policy. That is something that we want to argue for and proselytise about. Particularly as I am Scottish and live in Scotland, I see that some other Governments do not understand the importance of this industry, but that is something that I will certainly be advocating, as will my colleagues across the ministerial offices. We will make the point, if it is not clear to people, that there is coherence behind this. We see it as an answer to energy security and clean energy, so we need to address that. Noble Lords should believe me that the commitment is there.
