Turn 114 · Speaker unlabelled

I start with Amendment 172E, which would insert a new clause titled “Sovereign data capability for the NHS Federated Data Platform”. This clause would inject urgency into building a genuine UK sovereign data capability to operate the federated data platform before the current contract expires or within three years of Royal Assent. It would place a statutory duty on the Secretary of State to lay before Parliament within six months a detailed transition plan, including costings, timetables and transfer arrangements, subject to six-monthly progress reports and scrutiny by the National Audit Office.

Turn 115 · Speaker unlabelled

Crucially, we have incorporated two essential safeguards into the proposed new clause. The definition of “sovereign data capability” references the Companies Act 2006, in the same way that the amendments from the noble Lord, Lord Tarassenko, do. Under standard company law, a foreign multinational can bypass simple UK establishment definitions merely by operating through a London-registered subsidiary. This text would ensure that a UK supplier must have its ultimate parent undertaking incorporated and headquartered in the UK, shutting the door on foreign parent control. We have also specified that sovereign capability must be built on open data standards and NHS-owned software connectors. This ensures that we do not simply replace a foreign proprietary vendor with a domestic proprietary monopoly.

Turn 120 · Speaker unlabelled

Finally, my Liberal Democrat colleagues and I strongly support Amendments 53, 171 and 289, tabled, and so well spoken to, by the noble Lord, Lord Tarassenko. He described the current procurement situation in the NHS extremely well. As he said, Amendment 53 would create a statutory duty requiring Ministers, when procuring national systems handling NHS patient data, to evaluate UK jurisdiction, foreign compelled access risks and domestic technical capability, tested against the Companies Act 2006 parent undertaking rule. Amendment 171 would establish a comprehensive NHS digital sovereignty strategy in the way that he describes, and Amendment 289 would ensure that these provisions came into force immediately on Royal Assent.
