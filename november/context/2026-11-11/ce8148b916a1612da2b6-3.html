Turn 20 · Lord Pitt-Watson (Lab)

The answer is yes, is it not? I have talked about the rates, the £210 million for the community assets programme, and empowering the business improvement districts—did noble Lords know that they raise £185 million in voluntary rates to improve the high streets? That is fantastic. There are also the model designs for high streets, hospitality zones, and putting the high street first when thinking about new public buildings. This is a programme to revive the high street, as my noble friend says, after many years of neglect.
