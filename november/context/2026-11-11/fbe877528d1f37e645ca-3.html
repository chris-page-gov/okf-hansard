Turn 18 · Lord Foulkes of Cumnock (Lab Co-op)

My Lords, the frozen Russian assets in the UK are well beyond the £2.5 billion of the Abramovich money. Each nation seems to be unwilling to take the initiative to unfreeze the assets, whether here, in Europe, in Canada or elsewhere, because they are worried about the reputation of their financial sector. The Canadians have now suggested an initiative in which they would lead, along with the United Kingdom, and then others would join in, so that all the frozen assets could be unfrozen together. Will the United Kingdom Government join with the Canadians in taking this initiative?

Turn 20 · Lord Alton of Liverpool (CB)

My Lords, the Minister has referred to the Treasury licence and to the subsequent deed of undertaking. It would help your Lordships if he would agree to place copies of those in the Library for us to see. On the wider £185 billion of frozen Russian assets held in Belgium by Euroclear, referred to by the noble Lord, Lord Foulkes, a few moments ago, will the Government support the derisking and removal of those funds to a special purpose fund, removing Belgium and Euroclear from the crosshairs and enabling interest of around £5 billion annually to be urgently repurposed into the defence of the people of Ukraine, and indeed the defence of the wider community of Europe as well?
