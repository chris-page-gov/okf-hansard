Turn 25 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for the Home Department (Jo White)

I beg to move, That the Committee has considered the draft Licensing Act 2003 (Permitted Temporary Activities) (Amendment) Order 2026. It is a pleasure to serve with you in the Chair, Ms Jardine. The purpose of this instrument, laid before the House on 10 September, is simple. It will increase the number of temporary event notices that may be given in respect of a single premises from 15 to 20 in each calendar year. It will also increase the maximum number of days on which temporary events may take place at those premises from 21 to 26. Subject to Parliament’s approval, the new limits will come into force on 1usb January 2027, when the annual entitlements are reset.
