Turn 125 · Speaker unlabelled

It backs up that duty by asking for greater transparency and accountability. It would require an annual report to Parliament setting out the interactions between the Department of Health and Social Care, relevant NHS bodies and commercial entities when developing health policy, what steps have been taken to identify and manage conflicts of interest and disclose any breaches, and the actions taken in response. Secondly, it would strengthen the requirements on integrated care boards and other relevant NHS bodies to maintain public registers of interest, manage conflicts and ensure that commissioning and procurement decisions are transparent and based on the interests of patients and the public. Finally, it would require guidance on identifying the commercial forces that shape people’s health, making sure that public policy is not driven by the interests of companies.
