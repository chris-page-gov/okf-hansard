Turn 49 · Speaker unlabelled

On Amendment 279QA, the Government are committed to ensuring every smoker can access the support they need to quit. We have already made significant progress. As the noble Lord mentioned, for the first time, fewer than one in 10 adults in England now smoke, with smoking prevalence falling to a record low of 9.5% last year. We know there is more to do, which is why we are providing local authorities with at least £153 million a year for stop smoking services over the next three years, protected through the public health grant.

Turn 51 · Speaker unlabelled

As the noble Lord mentioned, the UK has some of the highest tobacco taxes in the world, but we are unclear on how an additional levy on industry profits would be implemented without the cost being passed on to consumers or without regulating pricing. Regulating pricing in that area is very complicated in design and implementation, and it would be very difficult to shield it from the challenge from the global tobacco industry, so our preference is to continue increasing tobacco duties, which are a proven mechanism to incentivise smokers to quit while raising revenue for public services, including public health and the NHS.
