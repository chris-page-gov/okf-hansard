Turn 145 · Blair McDougall

I will make some progress. We agree that young people should be supported into work. However, we disagree fundamentally on how to go about that. The hon. Member for South West Devon (Rebecca Smith) spoke about the minimum wage, but told the House that she did not want to talk about what might happen in the future. I think I know why that is. It is because in a report from the Prosperity Institute, the shadow Business Minister in the other place committed to freezing the minimum wage for five years. That is a real-terms cut of 12% for the poorest workers that will not just take £8,900 out of the pockets of the poorest workers in the country, but take that money out of the tills of businesses in their communities.
