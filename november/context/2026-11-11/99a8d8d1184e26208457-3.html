Turn 18 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

I have to say that I agree with the noble Lord that it is not low-level crime. The front-end of drugs being dealt on the street has an upstream effect on criminal gangs who are importing drugs and doing things which cause death and harm and lack of social cohesion. One of the things we are doing is ensuring that we have better enforcement. We are putting some 10,000 extra police officers on the beat during the course of this Parliament to look at neighbourhood policing. Neighbourhood policing leads to local policing and intelligence, and I would hope it will lead to better community involvement in reducing drug use on the streets.

Turn 20 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

Turn 22 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

The drugs trade is an evil trade; that is why the Government are committed to investing to ensure that we can reduce it. The 148 tonnes of illicit drugs seized at the border is the largest quantity seized since 1973. The drug barons need to know that the criminal justice agencies, the National Crime Agency and all local police forces will be after them to ensure that we hold them to account for this evil trade. I welcome the noble Lord’s support in doing that.
