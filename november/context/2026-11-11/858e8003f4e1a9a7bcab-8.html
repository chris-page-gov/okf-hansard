My Lords, Amendment 72, in my name, addresses a straightforward concern: patients being made to wait for assessment or treatment because of an administrative or financial rule when clinically appropriate care could be provided sooner. My amendment would prohibit minimum waiting times imposed across the NHS in England for administrative, financial or capacity-management reasons. It would apply to both commissioners and providers, including through contracts and activity plans. It would protect clinical decisions about when an assessment, investigation or treatment should take place. Turn 64 · Speaker unlabelled

My amendment would stop commissioners imposing a minimum wait through a contract or activity plan. It would not prevent normal appointment scheduling or prioritising patients according to clinical need, nor would it prevent a clinician deciding that, for clinical reasons, an assessment or treatment should take place later. There is a clear difference between having to wait because care is unavailable and being made to wait when it can be provided sooner.
