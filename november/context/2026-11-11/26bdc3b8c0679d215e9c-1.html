Turn 2 · Leigh Ingham (Stafford) (Lab)

I beg to move, That leave be given to bring in a Bill to make provision about excluding legal and professional fees from compensation payable by local authorities to persons whose properties are, in certain circumstances, subject to a compulsory purchase order; and for connected purposes. That is a mouthful, so let me explain what I mean. Across our country, in towns, villages and city centres, there are buildings that tell a story. Some tell a story of success—an old department store brought back into use as housing, a former bank transformed into a community hub or an empty shop becoming a thriving new local business—but too many tell a different story. There are buildings with boarded-up windows, leaking roofs and even trees growing out of them, in a famous case in Stafford town centre. There are buildings left to deteriorate year after year while local people look on in frustration. …
