Turn 66 · Lord Harper (Con)

The Minister has provoked me slightly. He said that he was not going to make political points, then went and made them all. He quoted George Osborne very selectively, referring to the period between 2019 and 2024, when—if I may just remind the Minister—there was a once-in-a-generation global pandemic that crashed the economy by about 10%, and there was the war in Ukraine. During the whole of the rest of the period of the Conservative Government, we were actually successful in reducing working-age welfare, in real terms. So I argue that we can be very proud of our record on welfare reform and can demonstrate that we could do it again.
