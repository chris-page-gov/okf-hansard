Turn 13 · Jenny Riddell-Carpenter

The hon. Member has put her comments on the record. I will ask the Home Office to write to her directly on those points. I am aware that there is support for the creation of a specific animal protection disclosure scheme more broadly. The Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs is already working with the Home Office to support the effective use of the existing domestic violence disclosure scheme for animal cruelty offences. The scheme provides guidance for police to use existing powers to disclose information about an individual’s history of violent or abusive behaviour where that information is relevant to protecting a victim or potential victim from domestic abuse. Police can also make a disclosure proactively where they believe someone may be at risk.
