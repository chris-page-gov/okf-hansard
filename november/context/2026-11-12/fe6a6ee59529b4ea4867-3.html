Turn 21 · Baroness Hyde of Bemerton (Lab)

There are many reasons for that decline, as I am sure the noble Baroness is aware. As we know, the Government are very committed to the devolution agenda and many of these services have long been commissioned through local authorities. It is about encouraging local authorities to focus on the granular detail, given the variation across the country, because they know their populations best and how to commission grass-roots services. Of course, the voluntary and community sector has a key role to play in ensuring that all the people who need to be reached are reached. We will continue with our devolution agenda and the ring-fenced public health fund for local authorities to ensure that every person, in every part of the country, is not left out from the possibility of diagnosis and treatment.
