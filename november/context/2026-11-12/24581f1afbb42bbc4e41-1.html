Turn 1 · Vikki Slade (Mid Dorset and North Poole) (LD)

I rise to present a petition on NHS dentistry on behalf of 1,060 residents in Mid Dorset and North Poole. In the past two years, £17 million has been lost from our dental budget in Dorset, while the population increases, demand grows, and the gaps in NHS dental services get bigger. Nearly £10 million was provided to dentists but not spent in the first year, and it was not transferred to NHS dentists with existing waiting lists. This year the budget appears to have been reduced by nearly £7 million, and the integrated care board has failed to explain how it has used the money and how it is serving people.
