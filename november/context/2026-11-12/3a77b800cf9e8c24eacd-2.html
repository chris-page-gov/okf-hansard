Turn 10 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

The Government do have a consistent China policy across the board. We engage with China on security issues when we have to. We are very cognisant of the fact that China poses significant threats to the United Kingdom. But again, as my noble friend Lady Winterton mentioned during Question Time today, we are a major trading partner with China, with both inward and outward investment. It is important that we continue to engage with the Chinese Government and with Chinese businesses on those matters, but we are cognisant of the fact that we have to be aware of potential threats and challenges. That is not an inconsistent policy and I hope it will be reflected across government.

Turn 14 · Lord Hanson of Flint (Lab)

Again, I say that I take very seriously the potential impact on any Member of Parliament, either in the Commons or the Lords, of the activities that are under current FBI investigation. I also say to the noble Lord that on 4 November the Government announced the Great Britain due diligence regime. This forthcoming legislation, which my noble friend Lady Winterton will be dealing with, strengthens the UK’s approach to forced labour in supply chains, mandatory human rights, due diligence and legal powers. We will be working closely with businesses and other stakeholders to look at how those measures are enforced. At all times, we keep under general review the security implications of any activity and purchase from UK sources of a range of foreign Government-produced materials.
