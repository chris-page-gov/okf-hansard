Turn 74 · Greg Smith

Let me have a last bite of the cherry on this point. The ORR would not be the body making a profit. Those funds would never be held by the ORR. It might be the one that levies the penalties and takes the funds in in the first instance, but there is no question of the ORR then spending them. Amendment 86—and I have suggested how the Minister might take the spirit of the amendment and improve it—is more a question of where that money goes once it leaves the ORR. The ORR has no interest in how it is ultimately spent; it is simply judging a licensee against the terms of its licence and, where a licensee fails, levying a fine. It is the Government who receive it and spend it, and how they do so is a political choice for the Minister and his ministerial colleagues.
