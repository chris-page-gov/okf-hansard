Turn 89 · Olly Glover

How generous of the shadow Minister to give way. He is usually quite a mature, thoughtful fellow—I put it to him that he might be being slightly juvenile in his comments now. He will know full well that my remarks earlier were not criticising his amendments; they were asking for his thoughts on the principles he was using in relation to use of the affirmative procedure. To take his argument to the extreme, we could have literally everything subject to the affirmative procedure. Without doubt, we would then spend our entire life having Divisions in the House of Commons, which we all love, but there is a balance. As for changes to the nature of party names, I suggest to him that abandoning the commitment to climate change and human rights is not very conservative either.
