Turn 106 · Speaker unlabelled

Clause 70 applies the company insolvency rule-making power in section 411 of the Insolvency Act 1986 for the purpose of giving effect to the highways SAR in part 5 of the Bill. It provides that section 411 applies for the purpose of giving effect to part 5 of the Bill as it applies for the purpose of giving effect to parts A1 to A7 of the Insolvency Act, and as if the references in section 411 to parts A1 to A7 includes references to part 5 of the Bill. That enables highways administration rules to supply the detailed procedure needed for the regime, which is intended to be supplementary to what is already covered in part 5 for the SAR.
