Turn 4 · The Lord Privy Seal (Baroness Smith of Basildon) (Lab)

My Lords, I extend my thanks for the constructive discussions across the usual channels about the plan for scrutiny of the Health Bill. There is clearly wide-ranging interest, as always, in this Bill, and a number of important issues that noble Lords want to discuss and debate. The plan for these two early sittings represents a proportionate and sensible plan to provide additional time for consideration of the Bill without taking time away from other planned legislation. In proposing the early sittings, the Chief Whip is aiming to avoid sitting very late as often as possible, which I know can be a struggle for many noble Lords, especially when those sittings are extended into the early hours. I beg to move.
