Turn 14 · Baroness Lloyd of Effra (Lab)

Ensuring that British Steel and, in fact, the whole of our UK-based industry remain internationally competitive is a key facet of the UK steel strategy. That is why, as we set out in March, it covers very many aspects—everything from skills to procurement and trade tariffs. Obviously, each individual company needs to look at what will make it competitive in respect of its own markets and supplies. That is exactly the question we have asked the board to answer in our transformation plan.
