Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Blair McDougall)

My hon. Friend the Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Baroness Lloyd of Effra CBE) has today made the following statement. A departmental minute is being laid before Parliament today setting out updated contingent liability arrangements arising from new liability and insurance measures announced alongside the space strategy on 8 September.
