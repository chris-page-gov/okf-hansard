Turn 2 · The Minister for Defence Readiness and Industry (Luke Pollard)

The security of the United Kingdom’s military installations, the people who work on them and the communities around them is a core responsibility of this Government. Defence faces an evolving range of threats, including hostile reconnaissance, sabotage, espionage, terrorism, organised crime, unauthorised access and the malicious use of drones. We therefore keep the protection of our sites under constant review against the intelligence and threat picture. Our approach is layered and intelligence-led; it combines physical protection, access controls, surveillance, policing, guarding, aerial monitoring and counter-drone capabilities. We do not rely on any one single measure, and I hope the House will understand that it would be irresponsible to disclose details of vulnerabilities or protective arrangements at individual sites.
