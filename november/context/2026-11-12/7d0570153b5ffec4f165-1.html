Turn 1 · The Minister for Housing and Planning (Matthew Pennycook)

This Government made a clear and unambiguous commitment in our manifesto to act where previous Governments had failed and finally bring the feudal leasehold system to an end. To prevent the renewal of that system, we will legislate through our forthcoming Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill to make commonhold the default tenure. In the commonhold future that is on the horizon, existing leaseholders will not be left behind. We will legislate to ensure that more of them are able to take control of their buildings, and more easily convert to commonhold as and when they judge the time is right for them. We will also continue to strengthen protections for existing leaseholders in the here and now.
