Turn 1 · The Minister of State, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Emma Hardy)

Today, I welcome the opportunity to update the House on the Government approach to supporting business—particularly glass manufacturing and hospitality—within the extended producer responsibility for packaging scheme. This Government recognise the contribution that glass manufacturing and hospitality businesses makes to the British economy, and the Prime Minister’s ongoing commitment to champion UK industry. That support reflects the fact that these industries are a source of pride for many communities and continue to play an important role in supporting jobs, investment and local prosperity across the country. The Government are already committed to reducing energy costs and creating a level playing field for British glass manufacturers.
