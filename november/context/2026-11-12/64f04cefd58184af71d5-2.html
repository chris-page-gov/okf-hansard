Iran: Execution of Dissidents and Opposition Activists

Turn 9 · Lord Bruce of Bennachie (LD)

My Lords, later today the Global Magnitsky Justice Campaign will present its 2026 outstanding human rights award to Mohammad Javad Vafaei Sani, the Iranian boxing champion and political prisoner. He will not be able to accept it in London tonight because he remains in prison in Iran under a sentence of death on proceedings that have been condemned as grossly unfair. I thank the Minister for her comments so far. Will the Government raise his case directly and at the highest level with the Iranian authorities and work with our international partners to make it clear that executing him would carry serious diplomatic and political consequences? Finally, given the threat facing women and others on death row, what can we do to make it clear to the Iranian regime that criticism or opposition should not end up on the gallows?

Turn 16 · The Lord Bishop of Manchester

My Lords, in Manchester, we have not only a large Iranian diaspora but a large Iranian Christian diaspora. Indeed, half a dozen are now priests serving churches in my diocese. Many of them have close family relatives in Iran who are under significant risk if it is known that their relatives in the UK have become Christians and have taken on Christian ministry. Can the Minister tell us what the Government are doing to help provide safe and legal routes for people who are under such threat in Iran to come to safety in the UK?
