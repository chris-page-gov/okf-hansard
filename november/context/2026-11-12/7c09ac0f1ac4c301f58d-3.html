Turn 17 · Lord Gadhia (Non-Afl)

My Lords, I note my interest as a board member of the Bank of England but will speak in a personal capacity. In my first Budget debate in this Chamber in 2017, I highlighted the Augustinian challenge of the repeated deferral of fiscal discipline: the desire to become virtuous but not quite yet. Almost a decade and several Chancellors later, I fear little has changed. The Chancellor’s “do no harm” Budget is understandable after recent turbulence, but a Budget which postpones difficult decisions today while leaving Britain more exposed to tomorrow’s shocks is not necessarily a harmless one. The fiscal numbers should concern us. According to the OBR, Britain’s debt to GDP ratio has increased more than that of any other G7 economy since 2006.
