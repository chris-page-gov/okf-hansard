Turn 4 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (Baroness Winterton of Doncaster) (Lab)

My Lords, the UK Government have extensive China experience at senior levels at home and overseas. We continue to strengthen that expertise through the cross-government China capability programme, which has trained more than 1,000 people from government departments, agencies and public bodies in the past year to build knowledge and language skills. We launched the China cadre, with more than 800 members across government, to support sustained career development. These initiatives support the development and retention of China expertise across government.

Turn 5 · Lord Davidson of Glen Clova (Lab)

I thank my noble friend the Minister for her very full and encouraging response. One is conscious that there are a number of exceptionally talented individual experts within government in relation to China matters. One is also conscious that expertise in Chinese language, and, importantly, understanding its political context, takes years to develop. That is an expertise that cannot be bought. Turn 6 · Baroness Winterton of Doncaster (Lab)

I thank my noble and learned friend for that question. He raises an incredibly important point. I hope I can reassure him by saying that the FCDO has an extensive and integrated pool of China expertise, both in the UK and, crucially, across the world. He talked about political analysis. This includes a cadre of research analysts covering China who provide timely, high-quality, evidence-based research and analysis. In the last financial year, we have trained over 1,000 people from government departments, agencies and public bodies to build knowledge and language skills, working closely with the Great Britain-China Centre and other leading implementing partners. As I say, my noble and learned friend raised an important point, and I hope that that gives him some confidence that we are addressing the point that he made.

Turn 8 · Baroness Winterton of Doncaster (Lab)

I do not have the figures on recruitment, but I assure the noble Lord that, as I said, we have Mandarin speakers working across our overseas network and we continue to invest in Mandarin skills. Some 58 FCDO staff achieved qualifications in advanced Mandarin at C1 or C2 level during the five years to September 2026. We also introduced a China pathway as part of the diplomatic Fast Stream, which reflects the need to develop future leaders. I hope that that addresses the point the noble Lord made about future leaders. We want to see them have a strong understanding of China’s political system, economy, language, culture and global influence.
