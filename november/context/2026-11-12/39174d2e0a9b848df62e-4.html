Turn 27 · The Chair

With this it will be convenient to discuss the following: Government amendment 5. Amendment 42, in clause 25, page 18, line 26, at end insert— “(1A) A tolling scheme must provide for lower tolls to be charged for residents living within an area in close proximity to the chargeable highway. (1B) ‘Close proximity’ under subsection (1A) is to be determined by the Secretary of State in consultation with the highest tier local authority in the areas through which the highway passes in regulations subject to the affirmative procedure.” This amendment gives local residents a right to discounted tolls on toll roads that they would expect to use regularly due to geographic proximity. Clause stand part.
