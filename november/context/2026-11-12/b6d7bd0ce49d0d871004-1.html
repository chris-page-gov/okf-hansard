Reforming Childhood Criminal Records Disclosure

Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Justice (Jake Richards)

I am today launching the Government’s consultation “Reforming childhood criminal records disclosure”. This consultation takes forward an important element of the once-in-a-generation reforms set out in the May 2026 White Paper “Cutting youth crime, changing young lives”. It seeks views on targeted and proportionate reforms to the criminal records disclosure regime for childhood offending. The proposed reforms seek to update the regime so that it strikes a better balance between rehabilitation and public protection—between enabling ex-offenders to move on with their lives, while safeguarding the public. For some individuals, a childhood conviction can continue to have consequences long after the behaviour that led to it has ceased, affecting opportunities well into adulthood.
