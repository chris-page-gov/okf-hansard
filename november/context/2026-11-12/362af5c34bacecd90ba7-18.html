Turn 144 · Peter Fortune

Q We heard from previous witnesses that there was a shift away from an “adequate” level to a “tolerable” level, so there must have been a decision to shift from one to the other. What purpose does that serve? Dame Judith Hackitt: Personally, I think the language is unfortunate. We need to be talking about life safety here. It must be an acceptable level of risk that does not put people’s lives at risk. Part of the debate is property loss versus life safety—what matters is life safety.
