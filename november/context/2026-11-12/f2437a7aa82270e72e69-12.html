Turn 93 · Sarah Gibson

Q Will that not be an issue where our current control or regulation of fire assessors varies? Previous witnesses have mentioned that different fire assessors come to different conclusions. The insurance industry will therefore not have confidence that the assessment made is robust, on the grounds that the word “tolerable” is not easy to measure. Kate Green: I share that concern. The Government will need to ensure that the fire risk assessments and the decisions of fire risk assessors are robust and consistent. Otherwise, there are avenues open for people to game the system or to obtain an assessment that is more favourable to their own interests. There is a risk there, and I hope that in the course of the passage of the Bill the Government will think about how they can ensure that that fire risk assessment PAS 9980 framework is fully robust and can give full assurance to residents.
