Turn 83 · Gareth Bacon

Q I want to follow on from Ms Blake’s question, which mirrors a concern of mine. In the event that regulators do not agree, who has the coercive power to make things happen? Turn 88 · Gareth Bacon

Q Good morning, Ms Green. It is nice to see you. Do you think that the Bill is sufficiently clear as to which organisation holds responsibility for enforcing remediation on buildings? Kate Green: I think it is true to say, Mr Bacon, that one of the things that residents are looking for is greater certainty, and this is a very complex regulatory and enforcement landscape. We would certainly like to see greater clarity, not necessarily in the Bill, but perhaps in supporting documentation and guidance on how different regulators and enforcers interact with one another, on the escalation processes, and on who is responsible for taking enforcement action in specific circumstances, so that residents can be confident in how the processes work and that they work smoothly and seamlessly.
