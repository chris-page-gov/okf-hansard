Turn 1 · Andrew George (St Ives) (LD) [R]

I rise to present a petition on behalf of residents of the west Cornwall and Isles of Scilly constituency of St Ives, including 1,752 who have signed forms online and in the street. I should declare an interest, though not a pecuniary one, as a volunteer member of two local housing charities: Cornwall Rural Housing Association and Cornwall Community Land Trust. The petitions were collected by the local Labour party. Among other things, the petition is in support of my Homes and Planning Bill, which will come to the House tomorrow for its Second Reading. Unfortunately, the Government will attempt to block it. The purpose of the petition is to support the supply of secure and affordable homes for locals and to control the loss of housing stock to second and holiday homes.
