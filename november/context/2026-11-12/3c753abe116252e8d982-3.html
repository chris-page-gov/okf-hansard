Turn 22 · Chris Webb

The Great British summer savings scheme, which temporarily reduced VAT on attractions this summer, is a good case study of the potential impact of policy changes on local economies, employment and tourism. The scheme recognised that targeted VAT reductions can support both consumers and businesses, as can free bus travel. Take the award-winning Blackpool pleasure beach in my constituency. During the pandemic, when VAT was cut to 5%, the park’s turnover rose, and it recorded an exceptional profit of £10.5 million—the biggest in years. As a consequence, it paid £1.9 million more in tax to His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs. Over the summer, businesses in my constituency were saved because of the summer savings scheme.
