Turn 30 · Olly Glover (Didcot and Wantage) (LD)

It is a pleasure to serve under your chairship, Mr Dowd. Our amendment 43 would increase the statutory guardrails around private enforcement by requiring a minimum debt threshold and a formal 28-day written notice period before a vehicle can be clamped, impounded or destroyed. We feel that the clause grants excessive powers to clamp, impound, sell or destroy vehicles, without any primary statutory minimum debt levels or mandatory warning stages. Drivers must be protected from having their vehicles clamped or impounded with excessive enthusiasm over minor, accidental or first-time toll non-payment. We do not imagine a driver having their vehicle impounded and destroyed as a result of forgetting to pay the toll once.
