Turn 89 · Olly Glover

Gosh, I am struggling to contain my joy at having the shadow Minister’s approval—I am sure it will not last. I can be pretty concise, because he has explained the logic. Were I to push it to a Division, I doubt that it would lead to some sort of Mississippi Senate race nail-biter, but perhaps the Minister could share how else he thinks the issue could be addressed, if not through the amendment.

Turn 96 · Speaker unlabelled

The schedule allows a scheme to transfer the property, rights and liabilities needed to give practical effect to the transfer, and make supplementary provision to ensure that existing arrangements and legal proceedings continue appropriately. A licence, or part of a licence, may be transferred under a scheme with the ORR’s consent and subject to any terms of the licence. The Secretary of State may direct the ORR not to consent where that is necessary in the interests of national security. Subject to that safeguard, the ORR may refuse consent only if it considers that the transferee is not a fit and proper person to hold the licence or the relevant part of it proposed to be transferred.
