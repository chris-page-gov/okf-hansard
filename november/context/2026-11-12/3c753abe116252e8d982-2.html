Turn 13 · George Freeman (Mid Norfolk) (Con)

The hon. Gentleman is making a powerful point about Blackpool. I have many happy memoires of large party conferences there back in the day. He is talking about urban hospitality, but I want to make a plea for rural hospitality. My Mid Norfork constituency has 135 villages and 50 pubs, which are the heartbeat of the community; many businesses meet in them, and they are warm places in the winter. They are being hammered by high energy costs and the jobs tax, the tourism tax and the other taxes the Government have introduced. Rather than a £10 billion VAT cut for everyone, can we have targeted relief to rural hospitality venues, which are dying, along with the rural economy?
