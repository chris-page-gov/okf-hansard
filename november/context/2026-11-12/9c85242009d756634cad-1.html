Illegal use of e-bikes and motorbikes

Commons · 2026-11-12 · Turns 1–1 of 1 in this section.

Turn 1 · Jess Asato (Lowestoft) (Lab)

I am grateful to present a petition on behalf of my constituents regarding the dangerous and antisocial use of illegal e-bikes. Through this petition, and a corresponding online petition, 1,135 local residents have called for action. One constituent told me: “I was walking along Yarmouth Road when a man on an e-bike came towards me at speed on the path. As he went by he turned the handlebars at me in a threatening way. I had to push into a hedge to be safe.” The petitioners therefore request “that the House of Commons urge the Government to tighten the law around the sale of powerful e-bikes and conversion kits and support the work of local police forces to tackle the misuse of these modes of transport.” Following is the full text of the petition: [The petition of residents of the constituency of Lowestoft, Declares that the dangerous and anti-social use of illegal e-bikes and motorbikes …
