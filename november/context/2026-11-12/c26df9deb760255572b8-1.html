Children not in School: Statutory Guidance

Commons · 2026-11-12 · Turns 1–1 of 1 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Education (Paul Waugh)

All children deserve to receive a suitable education that enables them to thrive and achieve, and the Government are committed to ensuring that no child misses out. The Government have today launched a consultation on “Children Not in School: Statutory Guidance and Secondary Legislation”. This consultation sets out our proposals to help ensure that every child not in school is receiving a safe and suitable education. Parents have a right to home educate, and we know many work hard to provide a suitable education for their children. Where parents choose to home educate, they must provide an efficient, full-time education suitable to the child’s age, ability, aptitude and any special educational needs.
