Turn 98 · Speaker unlabelled

I now turn to Government amendment 32. When schedule 3 was written, it was thought it should be the same as schedule 1 in respect of the transfer of property, rights and liabilities, including the transfer of criminal liabilities. However, on review, providing for the transfer of criminal liabilities under the SAR transfer scheme was not conducive to the special administrator being able to achieve the aims of the SAR, bearing in mind that it would make the company an even less attractive proposition than it might already be to a potential new licence holder. It was therefore concluded that this paragraph should be removed from schedule 3. The reference has been retained in schedule 1 as it provides for a wider set of circumstances such as transfers between public sector entities. I hope this amendment is uncontroversial.

Turn 101 · Speaker unlabelled

Government amendment 35 also corresponds with a similar amendment to schedule 1 to allow for the transfer of part of a licence. In the SAR, the special administrator is permitted to transfer different parts of the licence holder’s undertaking to two or more persons to achieve the SAR objective. This may happen where one or more different undertakings are taking over the licensed activities. To best equip the special administrator to achieve the objectives of the SAR, it was concluded that schedule 3 should make provision allowing for part of the licence to be transferred to account for this scenario. I hope this amendment, too, is uncontroversial.

Turn 102 · Speaker unlabelled

I come now to schedule 3. Transfer schemes may be used where the court has made a highways administration order in relation to a company and the special administrator proposes to transfer property, rights or liabilities from the company in highways administration. The scheme may transfer those assets and liabilities to a person who holds, or is to hold, a licence; a strategic highways company or their subsidiary; the Secretary of State, or any other person whom the special administrator considers appropriate for the property, rights or liabilities to be transferred. The mechanism supports the objective of highways administration. It enables the activities required under the licence to be transferred as a going concern where that is an appropriate means of securing their efficient and economical continuation.

Turn 104 · Speaker unlabelled

The schedule allows a scheme to transfer the property, rights and liabilities needed to give practical effect to the transfer, and make supplementary provision to ensure that existing arrangements and legal proceedings continue appropriately. A licence, or part of a licence, may be transferred under a scheme with the ORR’s consent and subject to any terms of the licence. The Secretary of State may direct the ORR not to consent where that is necessary in the interests of national security. Subject to that safeguard, the ORR may refuse consent only if it considers that the transferee is not a fit and proper person to hold the licence or the relevant part of it proposed to be transferred.
