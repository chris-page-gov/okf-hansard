Turn 58 · Justin Madders

Clause 42 is really about the process by which the ORR gets involved and issues contravention notices. It sets out a clear process for notices to be given, for responses to be given back, and for potential remedies to be found. It is not, in effect, adding extra layers of bureaucracy; it is setting out a process. It is important that potential investors are assured that there will be a clear and transparent process to deal with some of those issues. I hope the hon. Gentleman can understand the necessity of our having that within the Bill. Question put and agreed to. Clause 42 accordingly ordered to stand part of the Bill. Clauses 50 to 52 are about penalties in particular. They set out how the ORR can issue penalties to the licence holder where appropriate, the process by which that penalty should be issued and what should be done after the imposition of a penalty. Clause 50 enables the ORR to impose financial penalties for breaches of licence conditions and binding enforcement requirements. For a breach of a licence condition, the ORR must first have given a contravention notice; the representation period must have ended; and the ORR must remain satisfied that the person is contravening, or has contravened, the condition in a way specified in that notice. Turn 64 · Justin Madders

The shadow Minister displayed powers of prediction this morning, but they have sadly left him. I think it is clear that we do not envisage any particular circumstances in which this scenario might happen, but if the Committee thinks about it from the other perspective, it will see that we do not want to leave the door open and create a loophole whereby someone who is perhaps less community spirited than everyone in this room thinks that a clever way to avoid liability is to transfer the licence to an individual, as opposed to a company. These provisions would close off that risk. There could at some future point be a circumstance in which it occurred; I cannot think of any, but it is prudent for us to ensure that the door is not left open.
