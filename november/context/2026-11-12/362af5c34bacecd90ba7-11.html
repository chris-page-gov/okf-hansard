Turn 85 · Sean Woodcock

Q I want to come back to something that you said in response to Gareth Bacon. You said that we have made “good progress” on removing cladding. Ben Hopkinson: The fact is that there has been good progress—although it is not perfect and it is not done yet. I think that the Bill is useful in contributing to the remaining cases that are outstanding. Certainly, having 40% or 45% of homes completely remediated already is, I think, good progress being made. I did not say that it was done.
