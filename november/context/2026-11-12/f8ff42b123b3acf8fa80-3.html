Turn 19 · Lord Hunt of Wirral (Con)

My Lords, we all want British Steel to succeed, but it is vital that we attract private investment, as the Minister just pointed out. Why have the Government been unable to attract it? I ask the Minister to consider for a moment whether there is anything she can do to persuade her colleagues that the cumulative impact of carbon taxes, high industrial energy costs, the Employment Rights Act and the increases in national insurance contributions are all policies within their control. Can we not reverse them and attract the private investment that our domestic steel industry so desperately wants and deserves?

Turn 20 · Baroness Lloyd of Effra (Lab)

I am delighted to have the opportunity to talk about the business environment: today, despite the global headwinds that all countries face, we saw GDP figures in the UK that show a resilience that compares very well among peer countries. Just to emphasise, the UK has had the joint fastest-growing economy in the G7 in the first half of this year. That is the business environment that we are operating in. We are attracting further investment into energy supply: only yesterday, there was an additional £12 billion of investment into offshore wind to secure affordable, secure, clean energy for the UK. The Government are taking action in the UK steel strategy and outside to create the right business environment to attract private investment into steel.
