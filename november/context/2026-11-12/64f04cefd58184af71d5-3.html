Iran: Execution of Dissidents and Opposition Activists

Turn 17 · Baroness Winterton of Doncaster (Lab)

The right reverend Prelate is right to say that there is deep concern that Iran’s religious minorities, particularly Baha’is and Christians, continue to face significant repression and scapegoating. This has intensified during the ongoing conflict, and there are increasing and disturbing reports, as he says, of the use of asset seizure against religious minorities as a tool of oppression. That is why we were integral to the delivery of an Iran human rights resolution adopted by the UN Human Rights Council in January, which renewed the mandate of the special rapporteur and the fact-finding mission to establish the facts. I will take away the point the right reverend Prelate makes about safe and legal routes.

Turn 18 · Lord Austin of Dudley (Non-Afl)

My Lords, I understand that the traditional position has been for the UK not to set out how it votes at the UN, but right now Iran is organising terror attacks on the Jewish community in the UK and on defence sites, so surely it is time to look at this position again. Australia and Canada have confirmed that they voted against Iran yesterday. Can the Minister tell us why we did not do more to oppose the nomination in April? Will she confirm on the record that we voted against it yesterday? If not, what message does she think this sends to the Jewish community in the UK, our allies in Israel and brave Iranians who are risking their lives to campaign for freedom and democracy?

Turn 19 · Baroness Winterton of Doncaster (Lab)

The noble Lord is right to draw attention to the attacks on Jewish communities. Between March and May, across Europe and in the UK, there was a series of attacks against Jewish communities, journalists and Israeli interests. On 13 July, the Foreign Secretary publicly attributed to the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps a role in almost certainly directing the Islamic Movement of Companions of the Right to carry out attacks across Europe. I assure the noble Lord that we will use every power available to us to tackle the threat posed by the Iranian state, which we know is targeting our Jewish community. As I have said, we do not comment on how we vote, but I think I have made it very clear that we strongly regret that Iran was elected to the UN Committee for Programme and Coordination. I am not sure that I can be much stronger than that, as I have repeated that three or four times here.
