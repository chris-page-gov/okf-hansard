Turn 99 · Bob Blackman

Q You mentioned 129 buildings, I think, that are still to be remediated. Do you think the Bill, with the backstop, will enable that to happen, or are further measures needed? Kate Green: There are two things: one is that we would like to see the Bill extended to cover defects that are not related to cladding or external walls; the second is workforce capacity. By that I mean capacity both in the regulatory sector—fire authorities, fire services, local authorities and other regulators—and in the construction sector. The tension that the Government are managing between meeting their house building targets, which we support, and getting existing buildings remediated in an industrial sector that is facing significant workforce pressures is a real concern to us.
