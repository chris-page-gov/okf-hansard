Turn 109 · Peter Fortune

Q How do you think this seemingly interminable planning crisis has affected the wider market, in terms of building costs, delivery or stuffing up the pipeline? Alexander Cook: I cannot comment widely on the market—that is not my speciality area—but I can say that we have seen that safety is a factor in residents’ requirements, which is completely justified. We have also had experiences in London, as I said earlier, where delays to projects have then affected the supplier demand, in terms of where people are available—what flats, what height, and so on—across the UK. Ultimately, the market has slowed in London, particularly due to the regulator’s decisions that have not allowed us to get on site. With our dedicated team on the remediation side, it has not affected the delivery, as such; if we were able to proceed, we would have.
