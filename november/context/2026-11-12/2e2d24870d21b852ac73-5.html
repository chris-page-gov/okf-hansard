Turn 38 · Speaker unlabelled

Clause 33 deals with existing charging arrangements where a road moves into the new licensing regime. It allows the Secretary of State, through regulations, to treat an existing qualifying charging scheme as a licence holder’s tolling scheme. This can apply to certain trunk road charging schemes and charging arrangements authorised through a development consent order. The regulations can also deal with practical matters arising from the transition, including unpaid charges or penalties, ongoing recovery action and vehicles that have already been immobilised. Therefore, clause 33 allows existing charging arrangements to move into the new framework without unnecessarily disrupting their operation. I commend it to the Committee.

Turn 39 · Speaker unlabelled

Clause 34 provides continuity where a licence expires or is revoked. Where responsibility for previously licensed highways passes back to the relevant strategic highways company, that company can continue to charge tolls on the road. The existing tolling rules in part 3 will continue to apply to the strategic highways company, with the company being treated as if it were a licence holder. The tolling scheme that was in force immediately before the licence ended will also continue to operate. That avoids an unnecessary gap in the tolling arrangements simply because responsibility for the highway has changed. Therefore, clause 34 supports a smooth transition when a licence comes to an end, and I commend it to the Committee.
