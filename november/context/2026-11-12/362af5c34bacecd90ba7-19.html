Turn 149 · Noah Law

Q If so, are there no other means through which it could be enabled to be more flexible, more dynamic and more up to date while still providing the coherence and clarity that you seek? Dame Judith Hackitt: One of your colleagues spoke a moment ago about the need for trade-offs. I would not advocate prescription and fixed, inflexible definitions in relation to new build, where there is the opportunity to be creative and to innovate in the way we build, and where new things will come along that will enable us to do things in different ways. In the case of existing stock, where speed and the reassurance of residents are of paramount importance, having a very clear, non-disputable standard is really important.
