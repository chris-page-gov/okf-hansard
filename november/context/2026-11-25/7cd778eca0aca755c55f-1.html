Turn 2 · The Secretary of State for Northern Ireland (Chris Bryant)

Northern Ireland has seen faster economic growth than the rest of the UK over the past decade, and I want to build on that success with the Northern Ireland Executive. That is why we awarded Northern Ireland the largest block grant ever and offered an extra £1.5 billion across three years. However, the Northern Ireland Executive are hurtling towards a cliff edge unless they set a budget now. There is an extra £631 million on the table this year if a budget is agreed. They cannot let that go to waste.

Turn 4 · Chris Bryant

My hon. Friend is absolutely right. In her constituency and across the whole of Northern Ireland, we want to ensure that everybody has the chance of prosperity. That means building on the economic successes in Northern Ireland—for instance, with Bank of America, Thales, Shorts Missile Systems and the work we are doing in cyber-security. However, to give an example of the problem that we have today, Ulster University had to say that it was going to cancel its cyber-security courses. Institutions need to know what their budget will be this year, next year and the year after, if we are to build on economic success in Northern Ireland.
