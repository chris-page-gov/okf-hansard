Turn 37 · Speaker unlabelled

On the matter of liability for data shared, which was raised by the noble Baroness and other noble Lords, let me state the obvious about liability being a complex issue. It will depend on the circumstances of each case, and it may ultimately be for a court to determine. More broadly, health and care providers who are data controllers for their own records have a duty to ensure that those records are accurate. They could be held liable if harm is caused through sharing inaccurate information. Again, I emphasise that that will always depend on the specifics of the case.
