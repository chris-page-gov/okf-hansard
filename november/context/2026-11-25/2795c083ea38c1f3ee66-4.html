Turn 28 · Baroness Hyde of Bemerton (Lab)

My Lords, on behalf of my noble friend Lady Merron, I beg to move Amendment 181. Health is largely a devolved matter, and decisions relating to health for Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland should, and will, remain for their Ministers and legislatures. As such, the Bill primarily concerns legislation for England. However, it recognises existing long-standing relationships and cross-border arrangements between NHS England and the devolved Governments and enables these to continue. The purpose of these provisions is to ensure continuity: responsibilities currently exercised by NHS England in this area will transfer to the Secretary of State and, where appropriate, may be delivered operationally by ICBs.
