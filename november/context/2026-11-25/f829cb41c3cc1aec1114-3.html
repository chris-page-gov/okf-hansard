Turn 24 · The Prime Minister

I recognise that the energy price cap announced this morning by Ofgem will cause concern to many people up and down the country. I do accept that longer term reform of the energy market is needed, and we will set that out in the plan for Britain, to be published next month. The right hon. Gentleman calls for action. Obviously, we took a different form of action in the Budget, which was to put a levy on banks to pay for £100 support for 20 million households across England, starting in early January. What Ofgem has announced is that, for the first three months of the year, families will see about £110 extra on their bills. The support we have provided, alongside the removal of VAT from electricity bills, will give people some breathing space, but I accept there is more to do.
