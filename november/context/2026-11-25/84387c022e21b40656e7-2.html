Turn 10 · Baroness Merron (Lab)

I felt I did address that, but I understand that the noble Lord feels that I did not. I spoke about progressing the recommendations that would make the most immediate difference, bearing in mind where we are starting from. As I said, there was no national work by NHS England on ADHD prior to 2024. That is why we are focusing on improving national and local data publication, strengthening the commissioning and payment guidance, and workforce development. I have mentioned some of the things that the taskforce has rightly informed us on as we have moved forward, but it also informed NHS England’s service delivery advice and the 2026-27 payment guidance. All those things will make the most immediate difference.
