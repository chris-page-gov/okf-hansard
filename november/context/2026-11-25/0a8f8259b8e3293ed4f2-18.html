Turn 139 · John Lamont

I am going to make some progress, if I may. Anyone who has ever embarked on a renovation of their own home could have told us that this was never going to be easy. The scale of the works is clearly different, but the emotions of a home renovation are similar. The excitement and enthusiasm soon give way to the frustration of delays, the irritation of cost overruns and the mounting desire just to get it over with. Most home owners get through it, because they see progress and know that the end is in sight. We do not have that advantage. The renovation of Westminster has achieved little to date, with many billions and decades to go, so we must get this right from now—from this moment. We cannot allow this project to become a symbol of the failure of the political class to get things done, which it will if it continues on this path.

Turn 142 · George Freeman (Mid Norfolk) (Con)
