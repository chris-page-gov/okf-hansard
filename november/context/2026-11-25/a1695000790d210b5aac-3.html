Turn 17 · Lord Collins of Highbury (Lab)

I am very fortunate to have my noble friend Lady Smith sitting next to me—I was actually talking to her about the availability of English as a second language courses. Residents, depending on their status, can access courses which are funded through the adult skills fund and subject to local authorities and devolution authorities. Let me reassure the noble Lord: we are determined to ensure that access to the English language is available, and the Home Office is in regular contact with the education department about how that is working and how it can be extended.

Turn 19 · Lord Collins of Highbury (Lab)

I am not sure what independent auditing process took place over the last 14 years of the existing secure English language testing service. I reassure the noble Viscount that, as I keep repeating, we are in a procurement process, and that process did not start without us first looking at the market and speaking to our allies and other people who are doing this. As I said to the noble Baroness earlier, even if this process succeeds, we will keep it under constant review to ensure it represents value for money and is secure.
