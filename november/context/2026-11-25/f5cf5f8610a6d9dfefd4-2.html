Turn 10 · Sarah Edwards

The hon. Member raises such an important point. This issue requires talking to communities and identifying what is needed. I similarly have areas that flood within minutes—it could be one hour; they become cut off very quickly. That is where identifying small interventions that make a massive difference, particularly for safety, will make real improvements. The annual cost to the Government of flooding already exceeds £2.3 billion. The Government have begun to recognise the urgency of investing in flood defences. They have committed to a record £2.65 billion between 2024 and 2026, alongside the £10.5 billion that takes us through to 2036. However, the Government’s approach to flooding resilience marginalises our rural and remote areas.
