The Collective Old Oak

Commons · 2026-11-25 · Turns 1–1 of 1 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 1 · Dr Rupa Huq (Ealing Central and Acton) (Lab)

I am here for my constituents residing in the 500 units of the Collective, a co-living block in NW10, who face a potentially illegal bout of evictions—I have checked this—in this cold, by an absentee overseas landlord who claims that they have zero tenancy rights because they are members, not tenants. This has been in The Times and in the Metro. The petitioners therefore request that the House of Commons urge the Government to ensure that The Collective Old Oak, in matters relating to the management of their homes, engage meaningfully with residents over concerns raised by residents about the building, and that decisions are made in a fair and transparent manner, providing sufficient support to residents and avoiding evicting them over the Christmas period.
