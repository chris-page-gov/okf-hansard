Turn 25 · Lord Petterson (LD)

My Lords, I declare my interests of a 45-year career in the energy sector in both oil and gas and renewables, and that I am a director of Warwick Energy Ltd. I often share the frustration of my colleagues across the energy industry at the quality of debate in this crucial area of energy. Does the Minister agree that energy policy should be led by facts and evidence rather than the anti-renewables propaganda from the Kremlin that is often delivered through stooges such as the ludicrously named Global Warming Policy Foundation?

Turn 26 · Baroness Curran (Lab)

I am going to go back through all this, extract all the quotes and use them again and again. As the Secretary-General of the UN said, the failure of the COP negotiations is disappointing but the facts and reality do not change. We are still living with profound climate change. We need to develop a strategy of homegrown clean energy. That is where all informed opinion is driving us. People may want to cling to the past and to old prejudices and hypocrisies, but they are quite wrong. They will let people down if they do not address the fundamental challenges that we as a people face.
