Speed limits in Torwood Village

Commons · 2026-11-25 · Turns 9–12 of 12 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 9 · Speaker unlabelled

Transport Scotland, acting on behalf of the Scottish Government, is responsible for Scotland’s trunk road network, and works with relevant local authorities and stakeholders in relation to road safety matters. Decisions regarding speed limits and any associated enforcement or traffic-calming measures are therefore for the appropriate Scottish authorities to consider. In this case, Falkirk council, as the roads authority for Torwood village, is responsible for assessing and determining appropriate speed limits.
