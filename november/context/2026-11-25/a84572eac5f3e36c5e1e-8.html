Turn 57 · Lord Clement-Jones (LD)

My Lords, I rise briefly to speak to Amendment 156, which for some reason seems to have been inserted into this group. It raises a straightforward question. With the abolition of NHS England and the transfer of its statutory data functions to the Secretary of State, there must be no dilution of, or gap in, the statutory requirement for published guidance governing health and social care data functions. As the BMA and the National Data Guardian have highlighted in their joint briefing, the standards of information governance, transparency and accountability that apply to NHS England must continue seamlessly under the Secretary of State. Will the Minister confirm how the Government will maintain clear, binding accountability for these data functions and for social care data standards as responsibilities shift across departments and arm’s-length bodies?

Turn 60 · Speaker unlabelled
