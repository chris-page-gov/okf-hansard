Turn 4 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (Baroness Curran) (Lab)

This Government recognise that this is an extremely worrying time for families across the United Kingdom. Energy is an essential of everyday life and prices are too high. As a Government, we are clear that both the causes and the consequences of such high prices must be addressed. Policy and network costs have not contributed to today’s price cap increase. As is widely acknowledged, the rise is driven by increasing wholesale costs from global conflict. This Government have taken a range of interventions to address the immediate challenges people face but also to attempt to deal with the middle-term and longer-term challenges.
