Turn 50 · Steve Darling (Torbay) (LD)

Earlier this year, I visited Torbay Ladies Lounge, a group of women who have survived domestic violence. They shared with me how some of them had been failed by Devon and Cornwall police. Only last month, nine women received compensation from Devon and Cornwall police due to failings around domestic violence and abuse investigations. While Devon and Cornwall police made some assurances to myself and the community within its region, what assurances can the Minister give that she will be driving cultural change throughout the police services in the United Kingdom, so that these crimes can be investigated in a timely and appropriate way?

Turn 51 · Bridget Phillipson

The hon. Gentleman is right about the wider cultural change that we need to see. We know that it is very difficult for women to come forward and report crimes that are often highly personal around relationships, domestic abuse and sexual violence. We need to make it as easy as possible and ensure that they have experienced support when they do so. I have seen some pioneering work that has gone on in other police forces, such as my own in Northumbria, which is using technology to make it easier for women to report crimes and further explore what action will be taken. There are lessons that can be taken forward from that. I will ensure that my colleagues in the Home Office consider the wider point that the hon. Gentleman raises and respond to him in detail.
