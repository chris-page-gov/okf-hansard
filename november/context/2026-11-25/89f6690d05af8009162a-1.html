Local Council Improvements: Update

Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government (Jim McMahon)

My noble Friend the Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Housing and Local Government (Baroness Taylor of Stevenage) has today made the following statement: This Government are committed to shifting power closer to communities, strengthening the role of local and regional government and giving places greater control over the decisions that affect them. This will enable councils and communities to shape better public services, unlock growth, and deliver better outcomes for residents. Strong and accountable local institutions are fundamental to meeting this ambition. That means ensuring councils have the capacity and capability they need to deliver for local people, with this Government taking action to support improvement where necessary.
