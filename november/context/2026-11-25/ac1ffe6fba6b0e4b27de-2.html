Turn 11 · Speaker unlabelled

Local authorities are also free to make their own decisions about the design of the streets under their care, provided they take account of the relevant legislation. They have a range of traffic management tools available to them to improve safety near schools, including traffic calming, pedestrian crossings and school streets. The Government expect local authorities like Staffordshire county council to work with the local community in Werrington to address their concerns about road safety in their local area.
