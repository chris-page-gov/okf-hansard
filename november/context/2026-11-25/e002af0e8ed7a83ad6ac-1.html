Turn 2 · The Secretary of State for Northern Ireland (Chris Bryant)

Most recently, on 5 November, I chaired discussions at Hillsborough and reaffirmed the Government’s significant offer of £1.5 billion over the next three years to help the Executive stabilise their finances.

Turn 4 · Chris Bryant

I think all Members agree that Northern Ireland deserves financial and political stability, but that can only be achieved if the Northern Ireland Executive set a budget. If they continue on the course they are on, they will end up running out of expenditure around the beginning of February. They will not be able to spend a single penny in the next financial year. That will hurt every single nurse, every single doctor, every single teacher and every single part of the public sector in Northern Ireland. That is why I strongly urge them to take the responsible decisions and set a budget now.

Turn 6 · Chris Bryant

I will work with all political parties to try to ensure we have the fiscal responsibility and stability that we need in Northern Ireland. I warmly commend those who, despite the difficulties of working in the Stormont system, stick with it and try to ensure that power sharing really works. It would have been possible at the beginning of the financial year to make pay awards and to factor them in for each Department in Government. That has not happened. Every single time that somebody refuses to cut expenditure or adds to the bill, it makes it more difficult to make those pay awards. It is time that the Northern Ireland Executive settled the budget.
