Amendment 279Q looks ahead. Brain-computer interfaces and neural monitoring devices are moving faster than the law. Turn 98 · Speaker unlabelled

I therefore have four questions for the Minister. First, what assurance can she give the Committee about where the single patient record will be hosted and who will control access to it? Secondly, how will the Government make sure that the NHS, and the taxpayer, receive fair value from the commercial use of NHS data? Thirdly, do the Government intend to set out a national framework for adopting and monitoring AI in the health service, and if so, how quickly could that be done? Fourthly, are they satisfied that existing data protection law adequately protects neural data?
