Turn 4 · The Secretary of State for Northern Ireland (Chris Bryant)

We Government Members made a manifesto commitment that we would repeal the legacy Act because it was legally flawed, did not have the support of any political party in Northern Ireland, and gave the same immunity to terrorists as to British service personnel. We have made good progress; the Northern Ireland Troubles Bill passed its final stages in the Commons over a month ago, and it will have its Second Reading in the Lords on 2 December.
