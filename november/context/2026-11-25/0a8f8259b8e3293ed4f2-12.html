Turn 90 · Sir Edward Leigh

I would repair the building! I would make it safe, which is what we should have done 10 years ago, and make sure that it does not burn down. That is what I would do. We do not need a new visitors’ entrance, a Nelson Mandela room or anything else. The building works. Let us just get on with it. I have served on the R&R boards, and it is staggering. There are 25 members on the client board, and 15 members on the programme board. The delivery authority has been working on this since 2019, and has been endlessly developing plans but not doing anything. My final words: just get on with it.
