Turn 4 · The Minister of State, Department for Education and Department for Work and Pensions (Baroness Smith of Malvern) (Lab)

My Lords, we want technical and vocational education to be valued as highly as academic study. We are co-designing new technical education routes from 14 to 16 with mayoral and strategic authorities to ensure that local skills needs are reflected, and we will work closely with schools, colleges and employers as these are developed. Schools must provide independent careers guidance from year 7 and give pupils at least six opportunities during years 8 to 13 to hear directly from apprenticeship and technical education providers.

Turn 5 · Lord McNicol of West Kilbride (Lab)

I thank my noble friend the Minister for her response. When polytechnics and technical colleges became universities in the early 1990s, something was lost. What was lost was a clear and respected education path whose value was understood by students, employers and parents alike. I benefited from that route at Dundee Institute of Technology, where my HND in building management laid the foundations for a career that I am proud of. Today, that pathway is less visible in our schools. I welcome the comments of my noble friend the Minister, but can she go further? Does she agree with me that careers advice should present students with the option of technical qualifications with the same weight as university degrees? If yes, what more can the Government do?

Turn 8 · Baroness Smith of Malvern (Lab)

The honest answer is no, I do not think there is enough yet—which is why we want to double down particularly on our efforts to increase the numbers of young people who are able to benefit from apprenticeships. It is why we have invested an additional £1 billion into the apprenticeship budget, why we are providing new incentives for employers to take on young people in particular as apprentices, and why we are developing a local apprenticeship service—so that, at a local level, businesses can be brought into direct contact with the benefits of offering apprenticeships, and with the young people who can benefit from them.
