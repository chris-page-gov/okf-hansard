Turn 39 · Mary Creagh

Allow me to reminisce if I may, Mr Efford. There is a political point at the end of this. We sacked some of our scheme managers and kept the administration of that pension scheme in-house, when we could have been seduced as trustees by outsourcing it or lumping it in with others. I believe that pensions are administered best when they are administered close to home by people who will one day themselves be pensioners. Clearly, the outsourcing moment breaks that link between the beneficiaries and the administrators. In the case of Capita, that is a matter of regret, but we must do the best with what we have. We should learn from that experience of AWE as we take those pensions into the Crown. I thank you for your indulgence, Mr Efford.
