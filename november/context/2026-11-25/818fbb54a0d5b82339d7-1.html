Turn 4 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Baroness Hayman of Ullock) (Lab)

My Lords, this statutory instrument was laid before this House on 22 October 2026. It forms part of a package of three instruments designed to make changes to the marine licensing regime under the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009. This instrument is needed as a consequence of the other two instruments, which have already been made, and is very technical in nature. The purpose of the package as a whole is to improve the Marine Management Organisation’s cost recovery to reduce reliance on grant-in-aid funding. It will also make the marine licensing process more streamlined and proportionate to support economic growth while promoting nature recovery. I begin by setting out what these three instruments are and how they interrelate.
