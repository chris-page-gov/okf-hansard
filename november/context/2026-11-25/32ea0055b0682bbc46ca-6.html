Turn 42 · Speaker unlabelled

The noble Baroness, Lady Hollins, asked why people with learning disabilities are left without a framework. When I look at a number of the frameworks, they are built not around groups of people but around conditions. We are building the needs of people with a learning disability into care pathways—for example, in the mental health personalised care framework and, as the noble Baroness referred to, the MSF being developed for dementia and frailty. We are coming from that direction.
