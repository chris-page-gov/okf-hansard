Turn 3 · Rachael Maskell (York Central) (Lab/Co-op)

I am really grateful to my hon. Friend for securing this debate. Kevin Young was a constituent of mine, and he retold to me the horrific abuse that he experienced at Medomsley in 1977. That trauma stayed with him until his premature death in 2021, and he never received the apology that he was due. One of the things he told me was that when he left the gates of Medomsley, he went and reported the crimes to Durham police; they told him to go home because he should not report on prison officers. The injustices in Durham police need investigating. Does my hon. Friend agree?
