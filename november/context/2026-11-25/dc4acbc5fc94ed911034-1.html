My Lords, I am pleased to be speaking to this draft code of practice, which was laid before the House on 21 October 2026. Digital verification services, or DVS, are a vital layer of the UK’s modern digital economy. More and more services are now provided digitally, saving time and money and reducing paperwork. There will always be times when people need to prove who they are or their eligibility to do something as part of a transaction. DVS are, for people who choose to use them, a way to do this securely and with trust. That is why Part 2 of the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 established, among other things, a statutory trust framework of rules for digital verification services and a statutory register of trustworthy services assessed as meeting those rules.
