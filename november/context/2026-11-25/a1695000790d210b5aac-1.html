Turn 4 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Home Office, and Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government (Lord Collins of Highbury) (Lab)

My Lords, the department recognises the particular risks associated with remote testing and has carefully considered identity assurance, impersonation, fraud, and attempts to circumvent test controls. These considerations are fundamental to procurement. Home Office English language testing is currently in live procurement, and we will adopt only a solution that demonstrates it can meet the Home Office’s robust security and integrity requirements.

Turn 5 · Lord Davies of Gower (Con)

My Lords, Cambridge University Press is so concerned about fully remote delivery of English language testing that it has withdrawn from the bidding process to deliver the new test, because “no responsible provider could deliver the tender’s specification with sufficient security for a test of this importance”. It is explicitly warning that the new fully remote test is going to be rife with cheating, meaning the integrity of our entire immigration system is at risk. Why are the Government burying their heads in the sand and continuing with fully remote delivery when everyone else is warning them not to?

Turn 6 · Lord Collins of Highbury (Lab)

The main point is that all systems have risks, not least the current one, which has many remote locations and is sometimes difficult to assess. We are striving to be more efficient. The world has moved on substantially on digital ID and how that can work. I assure the noble Lord that the department is absolutely concerned about security and ensuring that we do not have fraud in any system. This new system is about greater efficiency in addressing the numbers that will apply. The systems that we now know are available include remote-testing technologies that banks use. In banking, there are much more secure identity systems. I assure the noble Lord that we will not move ahead without being absolutely satisfied about the security of the system we ultimately will adopt.

Turn 8 · Lord Collins of Highbury (Lab)

I reassure the noble Lord that, even before we started the procurement process for this online testing facility, we spoke to a wide range of suppliers. Consultation took place widely before even starting the procurement process, so let me reassure the noble Lord of that.
