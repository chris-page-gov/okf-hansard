This is the problem that we have today. We are assessing these drugs as Schedule 1 drugs. This makes it extremely expensive to use them in clinical trials and adds massively to the costs. There have been clinical trials on MDMA to deal with complex PTSD. It has had miraculous effects on servicemen in America, Canada and Israel, where those trials were carried out. Complex PTSD is a terrible affliction. It makes it very difficult for ex-servicemen to live at all and certainly to work. Many resort to suicide. It scrambles the brain in every conceivable way. Somebody was talking to a veteran when a helicopter from Battersea heliport flew overhead. The veteran dived under the table as a reaction.
