Turn 25 · Lord Clement-Jones (LD)

My Lords, the Minister understands and has tried to address the perishable goods point, but it is still extremely uncertain how those provisions are going to operate and whether they are going to be fair for those suppliers. No doubt the Minister has had representations, as we have, from people such as HelloFresh and so on. He did not address the issue of the review period. At the very least, does he not think that, because these perishable goods provisions are so tricky, they should be reviewed at a much earlier point than the five years that are suggested?
