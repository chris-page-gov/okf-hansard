I have spent the best part of the last 20 years caring for doctors with mental health problems—doctors who have suffered, and I do not say this lightly. These doctors in Malta are mainly young people. The university has worked hard to support those affected, including through opportunities for further study and welfare support. However, these measures cannot replace the clinical training pathway that these students have spent years preparing for. This is not a one-off; unless we act now, future cohorts who enrolled under the previous arrangements will face the same uncertainty as this group.
