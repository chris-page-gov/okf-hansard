Turn 160 · Speaker unlabelled

Given the present situation and the potential for deep distress for family members, perhaps we should adopt the Scottish situation. The Burial and Cremation (Scotland) Act 2016 set a new legal framework for burial, inspection and cremation, with a statutory funeral director code of practice created in March 2025 which had legally binding standards for premises, care of the deceased, transparency and funeral delivery. Funeral directors must be registered, hold accurate and confidential records and carefully follow handling, tracking and storage rules for cremated remains, which includes reporting lost or damaged ashes to inspectors within 48 hours. The Scottish Government, alongside Police Scotland and the Crown Office, also published standards guidance for all organisations providing mortuary services, to ensure high standards for all services. We must follow Scotland’s lead.
