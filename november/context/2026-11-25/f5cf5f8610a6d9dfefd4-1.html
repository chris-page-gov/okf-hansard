Turn 2 · Sarah Edwards (Tamworth) (Lab)

I beg to move, That this House has considered access to remote locations during floods and emergencies. It is an honour to serve under your chairship, Sir Desmond. Flooding is the most common natural emergency in this country, and the frequency and severity of these events are set to increase. Now, nearly 7 million properties across the UK are in flood-risk areas; by 2050, that could increase by 40%, while the cost of annual flood damage could rise from £3.3 billion to £4.5 billion. Already, villages in my constituency of Tamworth are facing the effects of increasingly extreme weather. Elford, Edingale, Harlaston, Whittington, Haunton, Comberford and Fisherwick are all communities that battle the elements each winter.
