Turn 2 · Lola McEvoy (Darlington) (Lab)

I beg to move, That leave be given to bring in a Bill to create offences in relation to the sale of certain electric personal vehicles; to create penalties in relation to those offences; and for connected purposes. The laws on licensing and selling battery powered two-wheel vehicles are being abused by those who do not share our values. They are being abused by those who are exploiting them to further criminal behaviour, and in doing so, these people are not only committing crimes but endangering and taking the lives of innocent people, making our communities feel unsafe and draining the resources of our police, fire brigades and NHS hospitals. The laws must be tightened, and new offences brought in and enforced, and this Bill is another attempt by Members of this House who stand against this behaviour to do just that.
