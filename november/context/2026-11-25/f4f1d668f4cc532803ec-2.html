Drink driving, drug driving and dangerous driving laws

Commons · 2026-11-25 · Turns 9–10 of 10 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 9 · Speaker unlabelled

The courts already have powers to disqualify repeat offenders and high-risk drivers, including for such period as they consider appropriate depending on the offence and the circumstances. Existing measures also include the high-risk offender scheme for drink-drivers, under which those who present the greatest risk must satisfy the DVLA that they are medically fit before a licence can be restored. The Government will also publish their response to the 2022 call for evidence on protecting the public from repeat drug-driving offenders alongside the response to the motoring offences consultation.
