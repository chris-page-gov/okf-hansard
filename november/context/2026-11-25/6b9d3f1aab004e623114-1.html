Turn 7 · Speaker unlabelled

However, I am concerned that there is no provision for the monitoring of the impact of this order on attendance at sentencing hearings. This issue was raised by the Secondary Legislation Scrutiny Committee, and I echo its concerns on the lack of publication of regular information on these measures. Given that the purpose of introducing these sanctions is to increase the attendance of offenders at their sentencing hearing, it would make sense for their effect on this to be monitored. With no consideration as to the ongoing effectiveness, it will be difficult to assess whether further measures are needed. Will the Minister consider including a provision to monitor effectiveness? How do the Government currently plan to assess the effectiveness of introducing these sanctions?

Turn 8 · Speaker unlabelled

This leads me to a further concern, which is that these sanctions will do little to deter the most hardened criminals from continuing to behave in the manner this sets out to prevent. The sanctions available may not affect the behaviour of offenders likely to face the longest sentences for the most serious crimes. Again, this is why publishing the data on the effectiveness of the measure is so important. If the Government will not commit to publishing such data, will the Minister agree to review these measures in future if these sanctions are not effective in compelling the most serious offenders to attend their sentencing hearings? I look forward to the Minister’s response.
