Turn 34 · Lincoln Jopp

Conspiracy theories will always be there, but I seek the Minister’s reassurance. There are two pieces of work going on here: there is trying to extract the €200 billion of frozen assets, and there is trying to find the money for reparations and compensation. There will be a suspicion that Governments across Europe are slow-rolling the first, in the hope that it will be available to fund the second. Can the Government reassure us that, as we have heard many times in the Chamber and in other places, they are doing all they can to release the more than €200 billion in frozen assets across Europe to support the Ukrainian war effort today?
