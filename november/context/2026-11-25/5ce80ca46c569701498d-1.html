Turn 1 · James Naish (Rushcliffe) (Lab)

On a point of order, Madam Deputy Speaker. I wish to correct the record about two references I have made about my constituent’s daughter, Amanda Walker, on 15 September 2025 and 27 October 2026. In those remarks, I stated that Amanda took her own life. While it is correct to say that the cladding crisis consumed Amanda towards the end of her life, following the inquest into her death, the coroner recorded a conclusion of misadventure. It is important that the official record reflects that, so I make this contribution to correct the record. I also look forward to continuing to work with the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government to ensure that Amanda’s legacy is reflected in forthcoming legislation.—[Official Report, First Delegated Legislation Committee Delegated Legislation Committee, 25 September 2025; c. 6.] and [Official Report, 27 October 2026; Vol. 792, c. 219.]
