Turn 66 · Speaker unlabelled

Turning to Amendment 165, we have to be very careful. This is really interesting. The proposers of this amendment are asking to use the census data to record somebody’s ethnicity. In the census, there is no “I shall not tell you” option; it is a mandatory question. Are we saying that an individual who does not have their ethnicity on a GP’s record—this is the importance of understanding what is being asked—is then mandated to tell the GP about their ethnicity? That breaks down patient-doctor trust and puts the doctor in a position where they are forcing a patient to give information that they do not wish to give, which creates a problem.
