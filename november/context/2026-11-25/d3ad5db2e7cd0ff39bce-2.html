Violence against Women and Girls

Turn 9 · Sarah Owen

Sport has a brilliant role to play not just in tackling violence against women and girls, but in supporting women and girls into secure futures and opening opportunities for them. I know that many in Northern Ireland will be excited about the news. The hon. Lady is right that we have to tackle violence against women and girls across all sectors of our society, whether in the entertainment industry, policing or politics. I am looking forward to welcoming my hon. Friend the Minister for victims and tackling violence against women and girls to Northern Ireland this week, and I will work with her and Northern Ireland Executive members to do just that.
