Turn 2 · The Secretary of State for Northern Ireland (Chris Bryant)

I had meetings with a wide range of people about the stand-off at Drumcree, including several rounds of talks with residents of the Garvaghy Road and with the Portadown Orange lodge, before I reluctantly used my powers under the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998. I hope to publish the findings of Justice O’Hara’s review of the Parades Commission shortly, but I am pleased that events passed off peacefully and that the vast majority of parades in Northern Ireland proceed without controversy.

Turn 4 · Chris Bryant

I honestly could not hear the hon. Gentleman, but I think he was asking me what steps I will take to ensure court orders are properly adhered to. First of all, it is for the courts to ensure that court orders are enforced, and any breach is a matter for the prosecuting authorities. It is not for me to judge the individual actions of politicians, but I say clearly that every elected representative, whether in this House or in the Northern Ireland Assembly, should abide fully by the law.
