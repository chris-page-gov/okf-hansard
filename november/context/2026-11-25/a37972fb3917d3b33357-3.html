Turn 23 · Baroness Winterton of Doncaster (Lab)

The right reverend Prelate raises an incredibly important point. She will know it was referred to in the judgment. The UK’s response to the 2025 earthquake in central Myanmar prioritised support to women and girls, including access to sexual and reproductive healthcare and establishing women and girls centres in affected areas to provide holistic support. In 2023, UK education support through the Myanmar Education Consortium trained more than 15,000 female teachers and reached more than 135,000 girls. From 2022 to 2026, the UK contributed over £1.3 million to support grass-roots women’s organisations to build capacity, strengthen feminist movements and creatively respond to crises. I hope that reassures the right reverend Prelate that we are taking practical actions to support women and girls.
