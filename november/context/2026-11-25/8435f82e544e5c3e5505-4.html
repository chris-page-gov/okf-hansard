The testimony we heard today and in response to the Foreign Secretary’s statement has been incredibly powerful. This debate comes at an important moment for the Rohingya, but also at a moment of choice for all of humanity, as the right hon. Member for Godalming and Ash (Sir Jeremy Hunt) said.
