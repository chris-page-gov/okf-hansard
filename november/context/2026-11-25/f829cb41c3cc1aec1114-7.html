Turn 49 · The Prime Minister

I do agree, and I pay tribute to my hon. Friend for her dedicated campaigning on this issue. We are launching a national conversation covering all the issues that she rightly raises. Joanne cannot speak in this House, so I want to repeat from the Dispatch Box some of her words: “Consent has to come from the person themselves. Joanne is absolutely right, and we praise her courage today in this House. More victims will be able to speak out because of Joanne.

Turn 50 · David Davis (Goole and Pocklington) (Con) · Question 902266

Q11. Twenty thousand children were harmed by the sodium valproate scandal, including in my constituency. Children were born with lifelong conditions, including heart defects, limb abnormalities, spinal deformities and severe learning disabilities. In 2024, the Hughes report recommended interim compensation to start last year. A year beyond that, the Government have failed even to publish a formal response, let alone make payments. All the while, victims suffer and die. From the Post Office to the infected blood scandal, it is the same story: we have a recommendation, and then Whitehall drags its feet. Will the Prime Minister end this delay and commit today to delivering compensation for the victims of this atrocious scandal?

Turn 51 · The Prime Minister

I have campaigned on this issue in the past, and I am not going to take a different line as Prime Minister. I am happy to work with the right hon. Gentleman on this issue. We need to see full justice for those affected by the sodium valproate scandal, and I will work with him to that end.
