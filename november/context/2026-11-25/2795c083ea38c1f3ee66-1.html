177: After Clause 57, insert the following new Clause— “Cap on care costs(1) Sections 15 (cap on care costs) and 16 (cap on care costs: annual adjustment) of the Care Act 2014 come into force at the end of the period of two months beginning with the day on which this Act is passed.(2) The Secretary of State must make regulations under section 15(4) of the Care Act 2014 to take effect no later than 12 months after the entry into force of sections 15 and 16 of the Care Act 2014.”Member’s explanatory statement This amendment would bring into force the cap on care costs which was recommended by the Dilnot Commission in 2011 and passed into law in the Care Act 2014, but which has not been brought into force.
