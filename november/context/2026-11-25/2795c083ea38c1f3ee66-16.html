Turn 121 · Speaker unlabelled

I give a friendly warning to the Minister; I know she has heard reference to this from others who have spoken. During the passage of the Mental Health Bill, these Benches supported the Government in resisting a separate bureaucracy and a mental health commissioner. We felt the CQC’s competence in mental health could be improved. However, the Government have now proposed a separate maternity and neonatal commissioner, and I am sure the Minister will be aware that that has raised eyebrows about whether it sets a precedent. Noble Lords who asked for a separate mental health commissioner might feel encouraged and motivated to try again—I see a few nods around the Chamber. This is not to belittle or diminish the importance of a maternity and neonatal commissioner, which is really important. I think all noble Lords see that, but still, that is a friendly warning.
