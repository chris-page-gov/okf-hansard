Turn 33 · Louise Sandher-Jones

That is a question that we are looking into and my right hon. Friend is absolutely right to raise what appears to be a discrepancy. I am afraid that I do not have an answer here today. We are looking into it and I am committed to updating her when we have the answer. Regarding the freedom of information requests that have been made, I urge those not happy with the response to appeal and go through the independent process. It is absolutely right that the MOD should be questioned and held to account on whether decisions have been made in line with either national security or protecting personal information. I urge people to use that process, which is designed for situations exactly like this, and to make sure that the MOD is applying the Freedom of Information Act correctly.
