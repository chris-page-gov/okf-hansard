Turn 58 · Rachel Gilmour (Tiverton and Minehead) (LD) · Question 902268

Q13. Tiverton and Minehead is a coastal constituency with a rural backdrop, so I know that it will be of particular interest to the Prime Minister. We face myriad challenges, including poor transport connectivity and health outcomes. At the last general election, Labour pledged to double the number of CT and MRI scanners. Unfortunately, Minehead community hospital saw its scanner taken away after the general election. The Prime Minister’s signature maxim is about delivering for every postcode, so will he arrange a meeting for me with the Health Secretary to discuss reinstating Minehead’s scanner and provision in rural healthcare settings? In case he is wondering which postcode would otherwise be left behind, it is TA24 6DF.

Turn 59 · The Prime Minister

Let me just write that down—say it again? [Laughter.] No, I fully understand the importance of what the hon. Member is saying. Diagnostic capacity should be close to the community, and that is indeed the programme that my right hon. Friend the Health Secretary is leading. I cannot promise a meeting with her, but we can promise the hon. Member a meeting with a Minister to discuss the issue, to ensure that her constituency and all others in the area have the diagnostic capacity that they need.
