Licensing Knife Sales: Government Response to Consultation

Commons · 2026-11-25 · Turns 1–1 of 1 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 1 · The Minister for Policing and Crime (Sarah Jones)

The consultation paper “Licensing for the Sale of Knives” was published on 16 December 2025. It invited comments on Government proposals to introduce a licensing system for sellers and importers of knives and bladed articles. The consultation ran for a 10-weeks period, closing on 24 February 2026. In the consultation, we proposed introducing registration through a licensing scheme for sales of knives and bladed articles. We sought views on whether businesses that sell knives and bladed articles should be required to hold a licence and whether this should also apply to private sellers. Alongside the licensing for sellers of knives, we also proposed introducing licences for the importation of knives and bladed articles. It is recognised that stronger regulation of the UK market could encourage people to look to buy from sellers based abroad and therefore import licensing will be important …
