Turn 17 · Speaker unlabelled

On the impact of this SI, I will try not to repeat any of the questions that the noble Baroness, Lady Grender, asked. The impact assessment recognises that businesses will face transition costs: £1.9 million for permit applications, £2.4 million in technical competence and operational costs of £112.9 million, to name a few. What assessment have the Government made of the ability of operators to take on these costs, in particular the ability of small-scale metal recyclers to adapt now that this SI removes the T9 waste exemption for scrap metal? I hope the Minister can respond to these questions and explain her Government’s direction in adopting certain of my party’s policies.
