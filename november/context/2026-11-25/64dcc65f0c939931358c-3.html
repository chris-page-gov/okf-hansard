Turn 17 · Lord Mackinlay of Richborough (Con)

My Lords, the House will note that I am the director of the Global Warming Policy Foundation. I looked at some figures before coming into the Chamber. The price of gas in the United States is one-ninth of the price that we pay in the UK because the US has decided on domestic production. Trying to get the Government to agree Jackdaw was like pulling teeth. No matter what your view is on the net-zero pathway, we will need oil and gas for many decades to come. When will this Government agree that domestic supply is good for security, good for price, good for tax revenues, good for investment and good for jobs?

Turn 20 · Baroness Curran (Lab)

I thank the noble Baroness for the range of questions. We are on the journey to delinking. I do not think it will be one fundamental daybreak that will change everything, but that journey is important. We are absolutely on that journey and we need to keep developing our industries on it. I very much reinforce the point on hypocrisy. It is sometimes frustrating for ordinary people to see government spending in infrastructure because they do not see the immediate results of it, particularly when they are struggling with such financial difficulty, but it is absolutely required. We have to invest in the infrastructure in the grid. We will not solve the long-term problems unless we do it. I think that is something on which, with the experience that is in this House, we could have had some consensus.

Turn 24 · Baroness Curran (Lab)

I have a fundamental disagreement with the noble Lord because the policies he is advocating would expose us even more to the volatile international gas markets. I am astonished to see shakes of the head on the opposite side. It is absolutely unequivocal and widely acknowledged that the conflicts in Iran, the Middle East and Ukraine have set those prices. I am even more astonished because I remember former Conservative Prime Ministers talking about the conflict in Iran and the impact that it had on energy prices. I do not understand the inconsistency that I keep hearing from the other side.
