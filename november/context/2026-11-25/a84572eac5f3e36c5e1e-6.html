Turn 41 · Baroness Finlay of Llandaff (CB)

On access and a log of who has accessed the data, can the noble Baroness provide some assurance that, if the data has been accessed as part of a quality-improvement audit process or as part of a research or educational process—for example, for a doctor in training—that will be able to be marked as the reason why it was accessed when it would not have been for direct clinical care? I am thinking of, for example, a radiology trainee who has to review a certain number of CT scans. They may not have seen that number in direct patient care and may wish to go back and review a large number from the bank of CTs in the department, but of course, each one will be associated with an individual patient.
