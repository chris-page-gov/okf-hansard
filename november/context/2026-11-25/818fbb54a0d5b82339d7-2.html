Marine Licensing (Notices Appeals) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Lords · 2026-11-25 · Turns 9–14 of 14 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 9 · Speaker unlabelled

On monitoring and costs to ports, which the noble Lord mentioned, Cefas and the MMO are working with ports to develop a dredge and disposal monitoring plan that considers any industry data provided and improves monitoring quality. The monitoring plan will be reviewed after three years to determine whether it is achieving its intended objectives. Fees will also be reviewed annually post-implementation to ensure that they are raising what is required for an evidence-based monitoring plan, while still supporting growth.

Turn 10 · Speaker unlabelled

On whether the cost will be damaging to ports, we think that the cost increases should be relatively low for most ports. More significant increases will be limited to major ports that dredge and dispose of larger volumes of material. Also, bringing in the cap at £60,000 protects ports that may dredge a lot in one year from extremely high and disproportionate costs. We are prepared to work with ports to explore further support with alternative options for dredged material, in line with the waste hierarchy. A beneficial use working group, which includes port representatives, is looking at different options for the reuse of dredged material.

The wider monitoring plan still benefits ports in low-risk areas because it informs the overall policy approach and provides evidence that the dredged material disposal is not causing any harm.

Turn 12 · Speaker unlabelled

It is also important to point out that the MMO has robust processes in place to ensure that dredging and disposal activities are managed sustainably and do not cause unacceptable harm to the marine environment. The sites take account of seabed characteristics, hydrodynamic conditions, existing environmental sensitivities and other marine users. Ongoing monitoring will help us to pick up any adverse effects as soon as we can.
