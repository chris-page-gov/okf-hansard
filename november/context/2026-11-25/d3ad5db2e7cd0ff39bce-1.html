Turn 3 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Northern Ireland (Sarah Owen)

Today is the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women. Such violence is a scourge on society that we must do all we can to eradicate, and we must challenge the patriarchal structures that enable perpetrators. Ahead of the 16 days of action, I look forward to welcoming the Minister for victims and tackling violence against women and girls, my hon. Friend the Member for Pontypridd (Alex Davies-Jones), to Northern Ireland later this week to work on this very issue. This Government will continue to support brilliant organisations like Foyle Women’s Aid in order to deliver on our commitment to ending violence against women and girls.

Turn 5 · Sarah Owen

I thank my hon. Friend for the fantastic work that she has undertaken to progress this serious matter, and to ensure that women and girls are safe, no matter where they are. I welcome the progress made by the Department of Justice on piloting domestic abuse protection notices and protection orders. That will start with the Greater Belfast police district early next year. While tailored specifically to the unique needs of Northern Ireland, the pilot will draw on insights gained from England and Wales, and will strengthen safeguards for vulnerable children.

Turn 7 · Sarah Owen

My hon. Friend’s hard work highlights the importance of a whole-society approach to improving outcomes for victims and survivors of abuse. While housing is devolved to Northern Ireland, initiatives like free travel and the sanctuary scheme have been essential to support victims and survivors of abuse and, crucially, to help them and their families stay safe in their home. Today, White Ribbon Day, would be as good a day as any for the Northern Ireland Executive to agree a budget and give service providers who are tackling violence against women and girls the certainty that they need for the future.
