Turn 17 · Lord Wallace of Saltaire (LD)

My Lords, what is the role of FE colleges in all this? I declare an interest: Shipley College is based in Saltaire. I am very conscious that a lot of 16 to 18 year-olds who would otherwise drop out of education do technical courses in FE colleges. How are the Government going to make sure that these play a very worthwhile role in developing this further?

Turn 18 · Baroness Smith of Malvern (Lab)

The noble Lord probably shares my view that FE is the sector that has too often been overlooked in the education system. But not by this Government, which is why we have increased investment, we have enabled pay awards to be offered on the same basis as they are being offered in schools, we have invested in technical excellence colleges and we will continue to work with FE colleges to ensure that the excellent partnerships they have with employers, and the technical education in which they often excel, are not only supported but developed in the years to come.
