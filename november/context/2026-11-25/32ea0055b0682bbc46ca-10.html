Turn 73 · Speaker unlabelled

Of course, complex PTSD is not limited to ex-servicemen. Many other people suffer from it as well. This is a big problem for people who have been involved in accidents and many other traumas of one sort or another during their lives. Any treatment that we can achieve on this will be of enormous benefit to people in this country. However, to get on top of this we must start to reassess the whole business of these drugs being in Schedule 1, which makes it very difficult to use them for clinical trials. We want to look at all this, because we have a very big problem, which the noble Baroness, Lady Finlay, referred to. We are falling behind on clinical trials. Many companies that are producing new drugs are looking to other countries where they can get much better service. The National Health Service is letting them down very badly on this. We should look at all this again.
