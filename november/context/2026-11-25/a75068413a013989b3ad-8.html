Turn 57 · Fleur Anderson (Putney) (Lab)

I welcome today’s statement, and congratulate the campaigners on campaigning for so long when they suffer from ill health themselves—that is so hard. I would like to raise the issue of the Poly Implant Prothèse breast implant scandal, which the Prime Minister has mentioned in this place before. Those campaigners are also looking for answers, and next week, they will be coming to Parliament to lobby. Will the Prime Minister meet Jan and Louise, two of the 47,000 women affected by toxic implants? So many of them suffer ill health, but doctors said to them, “It’s all in your heads.” They are looking for truth and justice, so will the Prime Minister talk to them about the possibility of setting up a panel for them, similar to this one?

Turn 59 · Claire Young (Thornbury and Yate) (LD)

I welcome the Prime Minister’s response to the question from my hon. Friend the Member for North Norfolk (Steff Aquarone) about the scope of the inquiry. My constituent, Colin, served with RAF Wyton squadrons that flew through French and Chinese nuclear test clouds. The ground crews who decontaminated those aircraft without protective clothing are still denied the nuclear test medal and access to their records. Many have since died of cancer. Will the Prime Minister commit to righting those wrongs?

Turn 61 · Amanda Martin (Portsmouth North) (Lab)

The memorial stone to British nuclear test veterans in Portsmouth cathedral bears a simple, but powerful inscription: “All we seek is justice.” I sincerely thank the Prime Minister for launching today’s long-awaited inquiry into the truth. First, will he join me in recognising the extraordinary efforts of veterans and campaigners who brought us to this point? Will he honour those who sadly did not live to see this day? Secondly, given Portsmouth’s proud naval heritage, will he ensure that, through medical record retrieval, we establish how many local veterans in my city took part in the tests and the lasting impact on them and their families, so that we can finally deliver the justice that they all seek?
