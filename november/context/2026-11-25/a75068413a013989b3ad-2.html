Turn 12 · The Prime Minister

My hon. Friend puts it better than I ever could, and that was a fantastic contribution. She was right to name all those people, but I will turn the compliment around. I was first asked to help in late 2021, when the Mayor of the Liverpool City Region, Steve Rotheram, and I were asked to apply our Hillsborough experience. I remember going to a meeting in January 2022 in a windswept Manchester very early in the new year, and my hon. Friend was there. The care that she had for the veterans and the confidence they had in her were clear, and she made a massive difference. I thank her from this Dispatch Box. She has played a huge role in bringing about this day, and she should be very proud.
