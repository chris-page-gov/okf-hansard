Turn 98 · Speaker unlabelled

187: After Clause 71, insert the following new Clause— “Maternity and Neonatal Commissioner for England(1) The Secretary of State must appoint a person as the Maternity and Neonatal Commissioner for England (“the Commissioner”).(2) Schedule (Maternity and Neonatal Commissioner for England) makes provision about the Commissioner.”Member’s explanatory statement This amendment would require the Secretary of State to appoint a Maternity and Neonatal Commissioner for England, and would also introduce a new schedule (after Schedule 11) setting out how the Commissioner will operate.

Turn 102 · Speaker unlabelled

I would be grateful if the Minister could answer the following two questions. First, what statutory powers, independence and resources will the maternity and neonatal commissioner have to ensure that NHS organisations act on recommendations and are held accountable for repeated failures in maternity and neonatal care? It is very important to have this power. Secondly, how will the commissioner ensure that women, bereaved parents and families, particularly those from Black, Asian and disadvantaged communities, have a meaningful voice in decision-making and that their experience leads to measurable improvements in safety and outcomes?
