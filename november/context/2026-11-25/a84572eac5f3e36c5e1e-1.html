Turn 8 · Lord Scriven (LD)

My Lords, I will speak quickly in this debate but not generally about the single patient record—my noble friend Lord Clement-Jones did that admirably. I wish to speak to Amendment 137A, specifically proposed new paragraph (h). Noble Lords will know that I have a long-standing and personal interest in learning disabilities, and I have a very specific question for the Minister. Many people will request access to the single patient record, so has a disability impact assessment been done for groups that will need the SPR? In addition, importantly, what tends to happen with such government computer systems is that reasonable adjustments are an afterthought rather than built in by design. In contracting and design, are issues for people with learning disabilities and others who will need reasonable adjustments being built in by design and in contractual arrangements?
