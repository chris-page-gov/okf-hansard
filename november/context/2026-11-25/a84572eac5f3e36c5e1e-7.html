Turn 50 · Speaker unlabelled

Amendment 167 concerns the diagnosis of ADHD and autism. When a patient receives one of these diagnoses, the record should show how it was reached, because there are many different diagnostic criteria that can be applied—there are noble Lords present who will speak more expertly than me on that. We should know which assessment was used and who carried it out. At present, the record often shows only the label—the diagnosis.

Turn 53 · Speaker unlabelled

These amendments build on reviews that the Government themselves have commissioned: Sullivan, Whitehead, Fonagy and Milburn. They treat the patient record as a patient safety tool. Although the Government have already given guidance on recording of sex data, which has been welcomed, this is the time—the introduction of the single patient record—to ensure that all these categories of data are made mandatory and patient safety is prioritised.
