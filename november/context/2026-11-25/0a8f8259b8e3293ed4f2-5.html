Turn 34 · John McDonnell (Hayes and Harlington) (Lab)

Members need to take into account the fact that this is a workplace. There are 3,000 workers who work here. They have had to endure asbestos. We have a responsibility to those workers, and every single trade union representing them has made it clear that they do not wish any further delay in the refurbishment and restoration of this building.

Turn 38 · Jerome Mayhew

One of the key reasons why the costs are so exorbitant is the enormous contingency increases, ranging from the lowest at 32% to the highest, which goes up to 135% of the core base price. The primary reason, in addition to the fact that this is an old building, is that, collectively, we are terrible clients: we do not know what we want, we have not decided what the scope is, we change our minds the whole time and we have labyrinthine governance that includes both Houses, the Commons Commission, the Lords Commission, the client board, the programme board and its sub-board, the Commons Clerk, the Lords Clerk, the client team, Strategic Estates and the delivery authority. No one is in charge. If the Leader of the House is serious about our progressing this, he first has to get simple, accountable governance that we can all believe in.
