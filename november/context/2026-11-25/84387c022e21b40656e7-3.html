Turn 22 · Baroness Merron (Lab)

Perhaps it would be helpful to clarify that the Fonagy review was into the prevalence of and support for mental health conditions, ADHD and autism. So I politely disagree with the point from the noble Baroness, because it is a different approach. We also have to take in the mental health strategy, which is about how we move from a diagnosis-led system, which is key for ADHD, to a needs-led system around the patient. That is a major change. The chair of the taskforce recently met the Minister responsible for this area, Alison McGovern. They discussed the taskforce’s work, including its relationship with the independent review and the mental health strategy. I am happy to share that they also talked about engagement with former taskforce members and, crucially, people with lived experience. I would say that that is pretty comprehensive in terms of working with the taskforce.
