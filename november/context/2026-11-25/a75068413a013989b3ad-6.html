Turn 43 · Christine Jardine (Edinburgh West) (LD)

Like others in the House today, I welcome the Prime Minister’s statement. I know that it will be particularly welcomed by my constituent, as three generations of her family have suffered as a result of her father being a nuclear test veteran. One difficulty they have in establishing the root cause of the medical implications referred to, is the fact that not only are her father’s records missing, but so are her own medical records for the first 20 years of her life. Can the Prime Minister ensure that not only veterans, but their families, get access to their missing records through this inquiry?
