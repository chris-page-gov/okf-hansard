Turn 26 · Baroness Helic (Con)

My Lords, I welcome the long-awaited judgment of the International Court of Justice. Turn 27 · Baroness Winterton of Doncaster (Lab)

The noble Baroness is quite right that we must ensure that action is taken against those who have perpetrated violence so that justice is seen to be done. What we must do, first of all, is ensure that Myanmar complies with the judgment. As I said in response to another question, the ICC has arrest warrants live and we will do what we can to support that. Obviously we respect the independence of the courts, but if further actions need to be taken then we will do what we can to offer support. In terms of women and girls, I have outlined some practical steps that we are taking. We have also put additional funding aside to give further support to the women and girls facing this appalling situation.
