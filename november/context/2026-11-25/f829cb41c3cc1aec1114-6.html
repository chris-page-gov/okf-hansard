Turn 43 · The Prime Minister

The question is, how much have we also lost in our economy from leaving the EU? I did not agree the deal that the hon. Gentleman and his colleagues signed up to—indeed, that many of them forced previous Prime Ministers into when there was a better deal on the table. That is the challenge that the country has been facing: low growth and, because of that, low ability to finance our priorities. I am doing what I can to repair that flawed deal, to bring more growth to the economy and therefore more resources to the Exchequer.
