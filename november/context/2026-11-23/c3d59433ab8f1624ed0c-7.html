Turn 52 · Caroline Voaden

I absolutely agree that if we could wrap it up in the Tell Us Once service, it would be extremely helpful. We must also look closely at the long-term impact on the children left behind. Around 120 children lose a parent every single day in the UK. Research shows that serious emotional or behavioural difficulties often emerge two or three years after a death, and that children revisit their grief at every milestone as they grow. They really do. One study even found that 17% of persistent offenders reported a parental bereavement. There is currently no data collected on bereaved children. They are in every school in the land, but they are all but invisible to the Government. A financial payment cannot prevent all the heartbreak, but targeted support that allows a surviving parent to work fewer hours, keep their children safe and stable, and be reliably there for them is surely far cheaper …
