Turn 33 · Jeremy Corbyn (Islington North) (Your Party)

I thank the Foreign Secretary for the way he delivered his statement and for British support for the case that was taken to the Court. That is absolutely important. The fundamental question, of course, is what happens if the Government of Myanmar carry on with their murderous regime against the Rohingya people? I absolutely welcome the Foreign Secretary’s commitment to the following of international law. While this is a definitive judgment from the ICJ concerning Myanmar, there is also the question of advisory judgments and the duty of member nations that support the ICJ to prevent genocide from taking place in any circumstance around the world. That includes Sudan, Congo, and of course, the Palestinian people in Gaza. We need to have a rethink, because I welcome the Government’s support for the principle of total adherence to international law.

Turn 39 · Seema Malhotra (Feltham and Heston) (Lab/Co-op)

I too welcome the ICJ ruling, and thank the Foreign Secretary for the swift action, at home and on the international stage; it must be the start of the military regime being held to account for their horrors in Myanmar. It is important that this should be the renewal of a strong and determined international coalition that stays the course on the path to a sustained peace. Can the Foreign Secretary confirm that he will—as I think he has intimated he will—continue to work closely with the Bangladesh Government, who have taken so much of the burden of the needs of the Rohingya refugees? Will he also work to keep on the international stage the situation facing children—those who are malnourished, and the millions who are out of education, due to displacement and armed conflict, including the quarter of a million Rohingya children with no access to education?
