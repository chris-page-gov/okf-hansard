Turn 4 · Sir Stephen Timms

My hon. Friend raises some important points. When the review is published, the review will call for a fundamental overhaul to rehumanise PIP. He is right about the co-produced nature of the report, and that approach will be vindicated when the House sees it. We will be taking forward recommendations arising from the review to look at how the assessment should be carried out. We will need to engage very closely with disabled people’s organisations and disabled people in the course of that work. He is absolutely right to highlight the important contribution that disabled people’s charities can make to supporting people into work. We have had a stubbornly large disability employment gap for far too long. We want to get that gap down and the contribution of disabled people’s charities will be important to bring that change about.
