Turn 135 · Speaker unlabelled

Although there has been lots of talk in the debate about how Healthwatch is not delivering, it is delivering in parts of the country. Some local Healthwatch organisations have achieved a lot of great work. In Southwark, where I live, in-depth work on the health needs of Latin American communities and around Black men’s mental health has helped to shift services at Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust and South London and Maudsley NHS Foundation Trust. That is an example of grass-roots work making a real difference for patients.
