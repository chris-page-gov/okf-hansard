Turn 153 · Speaker unlabelled

I am grateful to the noble Lord, Lord Lansley, and the noble Baroness, Lady Campbell of Surbiton, for their Amendments 178 and 179 on personal health budgets. Fortunately, legislation provides a basis for bringing health and social care funding together, including healthcare funding, within an individual’s personal budget. The challenge is rather more practical than simply a gap in legislation. I assure noble Lords that, on the point raised by the noble Baroness, Lady Campbell, individuals should not be forced into services simply for the commissioning convenience of ICBs. Decisions should reflect a range of factors, including assessed needs and individual preferences. I assure the noble Lord, Lord Lansley, that where a personal health budget is refused, the individual should get a clear explanation of the reasons and information on how to request a review of the decision.
