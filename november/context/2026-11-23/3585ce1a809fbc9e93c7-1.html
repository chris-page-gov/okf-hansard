Turn 2 · The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions (Pat McFadden)

We are working across Government to ensure that every young person has the support and opportunity they need to succeed. The hon. Lady asks about Cabinet discussions. The Cabinet discussed the Milburn report last week, and I announced that the jobs guarantee for young people will be available after 12 months on universal credit. She is right that other Cabinet members are involved, and I welcome the announcement from my right hon. Friend the Secretary of State for Education, for example, that colleges would be guaranteed funding for 16 to 18-year-olds in the next academic year.

Turn 6 · Pat McFadden

My hon. Friend is right to say that transport costs are often raised in this regard. We have announced a cap on bus fares, and the Secretary of State for Transport and I have also announced a lifting of the hours restrictions for bus passes for disabled people. These are small measures that will hopefully make it a bit easier for people to get to work if they want to.
