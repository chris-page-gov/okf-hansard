Turn 18 · Baroness Sherlock (Lab)

My Lords, when the Prime Minister made this announcement, he made it clear that he was not simply changing the triple lock but creating a new settlement for older people, adjusting the triple lock to make the pension sustainable, and committing the Labour Government to creating a national care service. That is what will make the difference. He has made it clear that the costings for that will be published when the noble Baroness, Lady Casey, has produced her report. The Government have made it clear that we will make the state pension sustainable and we are committed to a national care service. I invite the noble Baroness to make the same commitments.
