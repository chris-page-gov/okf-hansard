Turn 12 · Ed Miliband

My hon. Friend characteristically gets to the absolute nub of the issues we face. As I said in response to the Liberal Democrats, we want to use all the means at our disposal to seek to create pressure on the regime. I think it is worth saying that a resolution 2669 was passed by the Security Council in December 2022, and that was an important resolution on Myanmar. We will be saying to all countries across the world, including our partners and others, that with this finding of genocide—the first ever finding against a state of the crime of genocide—the UN has got to act. As I said in my statement, at a time when international law and multilateralism are under threat and under duress, this is a test. This is a test of the international community, and in my view the international community must not fail.

Turn 16 · Ed Miliband

I thank my hon. Friend for the great work she has done on this issue. The first part of her question is so right. When we read the pages of the judgment, it is a frankly horrifying account—a compelling and horrifying account—of what happened, and she is right that the world has taken far too long to wake up to this. It is true to say that in the UK, we have had 19 rounds of sanctions since the coup of 2021. We will definitely look at the specific points she raises about telecoms, and oil and gas; we undertake to do so. I was very clear in my statement that this is simply the first part of what we intend to do. We will keep looking at how we can put pressure on the regime through both our domestic action and working with partners.
