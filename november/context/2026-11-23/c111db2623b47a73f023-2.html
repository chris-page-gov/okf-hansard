Turn 13 · Speaker unlabelled

My concern is that a trust that has struggled to make its existing arrangements work may do no better when it is free to design its own. Removing the statutory requirement could leave it under less pressure to listen and respond. Meanwhile, trusts with effective governors risk losing committed people, local knowledge and relationships built over many years. Will the Minister explain what the proposed arrangements will provide that cannot be achieved simply by improving the existing model? How will they replace governors’ powers and how will the CQC establish whether patients, staff and communities have real influence over decisions?
