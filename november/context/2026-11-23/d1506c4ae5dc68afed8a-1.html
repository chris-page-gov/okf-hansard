Turn 4 · Lilian Greenwood

As I have already said, these reforms are fair. They will bring down the welfare bill by ensuring that those who contribute and gain access to the considerable rights and benefits that come with settlement have to earn them. In the normal way, when we bring forward the rule changes, we will bring forward the full details in the accompanying assessment.
