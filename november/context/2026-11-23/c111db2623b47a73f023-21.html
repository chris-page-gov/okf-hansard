Turn 161 · Speaker unlabelled

106B: Schedule 6, page 110, line 13, leave out sub-paragraph (3) Member's explanatory statement This amendment would reinstate the requirement that a trust special administrator should, when consulting on their draft report, seek to hold a meeting with any relevant Local Healthwatch organisation. That requirement is currently found in section 65H(9)(c) of the National Health Service Act 2006.
