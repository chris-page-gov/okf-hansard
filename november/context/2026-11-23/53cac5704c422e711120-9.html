Turn 65 · Daisy Cooper

We believe there is a growth dividend of £29 billion by year 5, of which £2 billion will be paying for the membership, so there will be a £27 billion net growth dividend to the UK from joining the single market and the customs union, reducing red tape and making it cheaper for businesses to do business here in the UK. Finally, we can all agree that stronger economic growth remains the only durable route to improving living standards. A new partnership with the EU could secure the growth dividend that I have just described, but unfortunately the Budget before us is not a growth Budget. That is why in our amendment we have set out all the reasons we cannot support the Finance Bill this evening.
