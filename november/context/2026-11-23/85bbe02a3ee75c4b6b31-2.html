Turn 9 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Education (Josh MacAlister)

I thank the hon. Member for West Dorset (Edward Morello) for raising this important issue, for securing the debate, for highlighting the issues around the closure of Leweston school and for touching on the Government’s decision to apply VAT to private school fees—although I appreciate the debate is focused primarily on the school in question. As a former teacher, I know the central role that schools play in their communities. When they close, the impact on pupils, their families, school staff and wider communities can be significant. However, private schools are independent institutions and decisions about ownership, financial sustainability and closures are all currently matters for their proprietors.
