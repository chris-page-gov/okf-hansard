Turn 25 · Bob Blackman (Harrow East) (Con)

The refugees from Myanmar fled to Bangladesh in their hundreds of thousands and that community has grown substantially over the past nine years. The Bangladesh Government have struggled with what to do with that huge influx of refugees. There have been a lot of problems in the camps, as we know. What discussions has the Foreign Secretary had with the Government of Bangladesh? Clearly, the Rohingya refugees will not return any time soon, until there is a clear change of Administration and Government in Myanmar. What discussions has he had about what support the UK Government can give to alleviate the suffering of the refugees in the camps?

Turn 26 · Ed Miliband

The hon. Gentleman raises a very important question. Governments of both parties have contributed, I think over the last 10 years, hundreds of millions of pounds to Bangladesh, but he is right to say that this is a very difficult situation. I do not have an easy answer for him. We are in touch with the Government of Bangladesh—I met the Foreign Minister recently. He is also right to caution that, given what is happening in Rakhine state at the moment, the refugees will not be able to return quickly. Therefore, making sure that they lead their lives with as much dignity as possible is part of our collective task.
