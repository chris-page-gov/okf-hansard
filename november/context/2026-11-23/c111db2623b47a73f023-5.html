Turn 33 · Lord Patel of Bradford (Non-Afl)

I will add to the issue about interviews. I have been on many panels, over probably the last 10 years, where several things have impacted the interview. Officials have been very clear about what they needed to do, in terms of the job specification. The independent person on the panel played a really clear role for me. However, 90% of the time we were asked to choose not the best candidate but three or maybe four people we could put forward, and 70% of the time the person we all thought was the best was not appointed. I do not know why; the independent person agreed with the panel, but the person appointed was certainly not the best person. I leave it to noble Lords to think about why that is the case.

Turn 34 · Baroness Coffey (Con)

My Lords, this has been a useful debate. Clearly, there is still a difference in views on how local accountability can be delivered for ongoing foundation trusts. My broader point is that I have reached a stage, recognising how underperforming quite a lot of foundation trusts are, of asking whether the status means anything any more. However, I look forward with interest to the letter the Minister said she will put in the Library. I am conscious that, given the Carltona principle, civil servants often do things on behalf of the Secretary of State; indeed, the deemed appointable process is key. I think we would still expect that to be ongoing. Without further ado, I beg leave to withdraw my amendment.
