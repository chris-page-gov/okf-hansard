Turn 45 · The Prime Minister

Obviously, the SAFE talks concluded last year, and we will only ever sign agreements if they are in our national interest, as I have said today. British firms have access to SAFE on standard third-country terms, and that will remain the case—this agreement does not affect that. More broadly, though, we are seeking deeper co-operation on defence, particularly defence procurement. Through the multilateral defence mechanism, the Chancellor is working hard to build partnerships with EU countries and others to procure jointly and get those costs down.
