Turn 193 · The Prime Minister

I distinctly remember Boris Johnson saying there would be no checks in the Irish sea, but the hon. Gentleman knows and Northern Ireland knows that that has not been the reality. This deal removes 90% of those checks on agrifood coming into Northern Ireland. How do the Conservatives defend the status quo that would have trapped Northern Ireland in that position? We are trying to help to remove 90% of those checks and potentially get significant reductions in energy costs in Northern Ireland. We will keep working at that because we believe that his constituents deserve those benefits.
