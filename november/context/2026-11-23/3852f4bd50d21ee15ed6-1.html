Improving Education in Young Offender Institutions

Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Justice (Jake Richards)

The primary mission of youth custody is to turn around the lives of those children who arrive in our care, while protecting the public. This means a focus on rehabilitative services that really work, leading to reduced reoffending upon release and better outcomes for our communities. High-quality education in custody is central to that mission. It gives children the skills, qualifications and confidence to progress into training or employment. These are the foundations of successful integration back into the community, and each child has a right to high-quality education that sets them up for life, whether in the community or in youth custody.
