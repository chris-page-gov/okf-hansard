Turn 12 · Sir Stephen Timms

I am grateful to the hon. Member for his interest in the review. I know he will be looking at it with great interest after its publication. Tomorrow, we will set out the view that in addition to a cash benefit from PIP, which we are recommending should remain a cash-first benefit, there may well be services that would be helpful to people as part of their PIP award. The Motability scheme already enables many disabled people to enjoy the mobility that they would not otherwise be able to enjoy, and they choose to forgo part of their cash benefit in return for access to the scheme. We think that principle could be applied more widely with the PIP scheme.
