This is an arrangement with potentially enormous consequences for the NHS, yet we still have no published impact assessment setting out what those consequences are likely to be. The agreement commits the UK to a substantial increase in spending on new medicines, from 0.3% to at least 0.6% of GDP by 2036. It also sets an expectation that medicine spending will rise from around 10% to 12% of the NHS budget. These are very significant changes to how public money is allocated, and they raise a fairly straightforward question: where is that money to come from?
