Turn 134 · Speaker unlabelled

We have already debated the health problems for adopted children, but a record which holds vast quantities of information may become unworkable in the different scenarios in which it will be used. There are those with chronic conditions whose diagnosis, investigations and medication—current and previous, and including adverse reactions—should be recorded, and there may be key points in their social history, which the patient agrees can be shared, that could be included when they are undergoing intensive treatment for a complex condition where potential red flags, all current investigations and advanced care plans should be included.
