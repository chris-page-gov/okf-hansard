Turn 233 · The Prime Minister

I have been very clear that the red lines of our manifesto mean something. We will honour them, but we will do so while getting the best possible arrangement we can in this Parliament. This is a good deal, and I look forward to the hon. Lady helping to get it through the House as quickly as possible.

Turn 235 · The Prime Minister

It was interesting being in Brussels and the Netherlands over the weekend, where people were saying, “This is better. We can work with Britain again. We feel more positive about Britain again.” The Conservatives created a lot of negativity about Britain. People thought we had changed and had become a different, unwelcoming country. Let us be honest: the Conservatives did a lot of damage to the national interest through how they conducted those Brexit negotiations. The new approach that we are taking—the constructive approach pioneered by my right hon. Friend the Europe Minister—will be good for Bournemouth and for Britain.
