Turn 31 · Earl Howe (Con)

My Lords, I have a quick question. I heard what the Minister said about chairs of foundation trusts being, in practice, appointed by officials on behalf of the Secretary of State. I completely recognise that officials are very conscientious when given that role and that they do, as a matter of routine, follow a fair process. Nevertheless, in the final analysis, it is the duty of officials to follow government policy, and I am uneasy about simply saying that there will be a separation between the process adopted by officials and their ultimate accountability to the Secretary of State. I wonder whether, in practice, that opens up the scope for political influence over appointments.
