Turn 89 · Taiwo Owatemi (Coventry North West) (Lab)

Penicillin was Britain’s gift to the world. It transformed modern medicine and it remains vital today, yet Europe’s last major penicillin manufacturing plant is reportedly at risk of closure. I welcome the Prime Minister’s progress on health corporations with the EU, including plans for earlier warnings of medicine shortages, but warnings alone will not solve the problem if we lose the factories that make our medicines. As I listen to Opposition Members reject the Prime Minister’s closer engagement with Europe, can he explain how facing alone a risk such as that would make our medicine supply safer? Will he commit to making pharmaceutical manufacturing a priority in the next stages of the UK-EU reset?
