Turn 11 · Irene Campbell

Again, I agree with everything my hon. Friend said. I think the benefit has been in existence since 2017. That is nine years now, so there is definitely merit in reviewing why we do not uprate it. The first few months of dealing with partner bereavement are very busy, potentially involving administrative work, funeral organisation, dealing with financial matters and insurance companies, updating banking information and much more. People are not always aware that bereavement support payment exists. It is a very stressful time. People are often dealing with bereaved children, which complicates things and puts a lot of emotional strain on everyone. Sometimes, people find out about the benefit after the three-month period has ended. The petition requests that the Department for Work and Pensions reviews and considers extending that limit to 12 months or more.
