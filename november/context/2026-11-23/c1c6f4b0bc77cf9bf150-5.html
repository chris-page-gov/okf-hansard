Turn 40 · Speaker unlabelled

Amendment 172D is designed to bring pace and accountability to the process. Within six months of Royal Assent, the Secretary of State would have to lay before Parliament a Statement setting out how the recommendations will be implemented. It would say which steps need regulations under the Medicines and Medical Devices Act 2021 and which will need other legislation, and when each will happen, including dates for the statutory consultation and laying draft regulations.
