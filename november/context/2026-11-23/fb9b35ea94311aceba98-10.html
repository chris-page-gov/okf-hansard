Why are the Opposition unable to welcome something that is so obviously in the interests of their constituents and ours?

Turn 77 · The Prime Minister

I very much believe that. Let me come back to something I said earlier. I think 10 or 15 years ago our enemies had a clear plan to invade neighbouring countries—that is, Ukraine—and at the same time weaken the cohesion of Europe and the ability of Europe to fight what they were doing. Sadly, they succeeded on the first, but I do not believe they have succeeded on the second. As I said at the start of this statement, the coalition of the willing in Berlin on Thursday was magnificent in the solidarity among all the different countries and in the strength of the resolve expressed by all the leaders. Russia tried to break the resolve of the European Union and it tried to break its cohesion; it has failed.
