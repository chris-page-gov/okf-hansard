Turn 33 · Dr Ellie Chowns (North Herefordshire) (Green)

My constituent Mary’s adult son Peter is profoundly autistic and has very severe learning difficulties. He cannot talk or play games, and Mary explains that his main pleasure in life is going out for visits in his Motability car. However, the mileage limit on Motability, introduced earlier this year, means that his ability to do that has effectively been halved, and he risks becoming extremely anxious, potentially harming himself or his carers. Will the Secretary of State work with Motability to introduce an exception for journeys necessary for mental health or behavioural regulation, to help people such as Peter?

Turn 40 · Lilian Greenwood

I share my hon. Friend’s concern on behalf of her constituent. The Child Maintenance Service has a range of robust enforcement powers to pursue unpaid maintenance and ensure that children get the financial support they deserve. Where payments are not made on time or in full, the service can deduct directly from earnings or bank accounts and, where necessary, take stronger enforcement action, including taking control of goods, forcing the sale of property, and seeking disqualification from driving or from holding a passport.
