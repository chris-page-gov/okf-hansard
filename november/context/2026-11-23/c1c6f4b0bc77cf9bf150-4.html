My Lords, I will speak to Amendment 203, in my name and that of the noble Baronesses, Lady Goudie and Lady Gerada. This addresses the reclassification of injectable medicinal products. Under pharmacy status, medicines can be supplied under pharmacist supervision without a prescription. Under the Human Medicines Regulations 2012, medicines intended for administration by injection must be classified as prescription-only. Turn 31 · Lord Tarassenko (CB)

My Lords, I will speak to Amendment 172D in my name, to which the noble Baroness, Lady Harding of Winscombe, and the noble Lord, Lord Clement-Jones, have added their names. It concerns AI regulation. I declare my interests as senior adviser to the Alan Turing Institute, director of LIO Health, and the holder of an honorary contract with Oxford University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust. When an AI algorithm has a medical purpose, such as diagnosing, monitoring or guiding treatment, it is regulated as a medical device under the UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002, and the regulator is the MHRA. So how quickly AI can be safely adopted in the NHS depends largely on MHRA and regulations written more than 20 years ago.
