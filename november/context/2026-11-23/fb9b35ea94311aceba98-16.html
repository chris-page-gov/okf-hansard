Turn 125 · The Prime Minister

We owe it to the children of Hazel Grove, their families and the hon. Lady to put before the House the assessment of what we could do through the linking of electricity trading in Europe, and we will do that. To further my answer, the chief executive of National Grid, who was with us in Brussels, pointed very much to the opportunity to bring down bills if we were to pursue that route. There are wider considerations, but, as a Government, we will look at anything that brings down the cost of electricity bills.
