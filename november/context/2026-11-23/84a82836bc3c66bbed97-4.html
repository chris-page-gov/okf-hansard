Turn 27 · Kate Dearden

It is a pleasure to serve under your chairship this evening, Mrs Jardine. These regulations, which were laid in draft before this House on 19 October, implement the subscriptions chapter in the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, known as the DMCCA. For consumers who enter subscription contracts, they offer new protections against being stuck in unwanted contracts. We estimate that there are around 155 million active subscriptions in the United Kingdom. However, although they bring convenience for both businesses and consumers, if they trick or trap consumers into subscriptions they do not want, they can be a source of consumer harm.
