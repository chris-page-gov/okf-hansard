Turn 2 · The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions (Pat McFadden)

We are delivering the youth guarantee to support young people across Great Britain. We have introduced more intensive, earlier support in jobcentres, expanded the network of youth hubs—more than 175 are now open—and introduced a youth jobs grant and the jobs guarantee nationally. I announced last week that, from next April, we will bring forward eligibility for the jobs guarantee so that it is available for young people who have been on universal credit for 12 months rather than 18 months.

Turn 4 · Pat McFadden

I commend my hon. Friend for working with his jobcentre and local employers. The truth is that we need to ensure that offers such as this are as simple as possible for employers to use. It is a challenge to ensure that the information is as user friendly and as simple to access as possible, especially for small and medium-sized businesses that do not have big human resources departments and so on. However, employers are signing up: earlier this month the waste management company Biffa announced that it would be offering up to 2,000 jobs to young people through the youth guarantee. I welcomed that announcement on a recent visit to one of its sites.
