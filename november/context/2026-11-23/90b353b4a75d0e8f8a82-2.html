Turn 11 · The Minister for Employment (Andrew Western)

I am grateful to my hon. Friend for raising his constituent’s concern. He will know that the Milburn review contained a number of recommendations on how we deliver a new settlement for young people, including proposals for a higher payment to those with the most severe conditions and for those with additional costs to continue to have those costs met. Although I appreciate that his constituent wants certainty about his support, the Government require a little time to consider the recommendations in full. We will respond to all the proposals in the review early in the new year.
