Turn 34 · Bradley Thomas

I wholeheartedly agree with my right hon. Friend; we must be very careful of unintended consequences. I implore the Government to review the implementation of the regulations on an ongoing basis to ensure that they do not put an unfair and unnecessary burden on businesses. The Opposition will not oppose this draft legislation. We know that unwanted subscriptions can cause significant financial trouble for households, and we want consumers to be protected from deceptive subscription providers. However, a careful balancing act is required to ensure that consumers get the protection that they need while ensuring that legitimate providers do not become unintentional collateral damage. I would be interested to hear from the Minister how the Government will ensure that that is achieved.
