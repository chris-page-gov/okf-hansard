Turn 3 · Henry Tufnell

In Mid and South Pembrokeshire, the number of 18 to 24-year-olds claiming unemployment-related benefits increased by 29% between August 2025 and August 2026. These young people now account for a quarter of all unemployment-related benefits claimants in my constituency, and 88% of new claimants. I am sure that the Minister shares my concern about those figures, but will he set out what the Government are doing to ensure that young people in Pembrokeshire have access to the support and opportunities they need to build their careers?
