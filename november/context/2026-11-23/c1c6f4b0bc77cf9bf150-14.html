My Lords, I can deal with this amendment quite quickly. Its purpose is to bring to the fore a matter of principle as regards the regulation of NHS organisations. As we have discussed, the Government’s main purpose in bringing forward the Bill is to transfer NHS England’s functions into the Department of Health and Social Care. The stated objective is to simplify accountability and eliminate duplication. Those are aims that I believe we can all understand.
