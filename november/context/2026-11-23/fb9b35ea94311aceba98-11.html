Turn 84 · Ms Stella Creasy (Walthamstow) (Lab/Co-op)

I very much welcome the deal. Frankly, being lectured by those on the Opposition Benches—they oversaw a deal in which up to 20,000 British businesses gave up trading with Europe altogether and our food exports were cut by a fifth, and food prices are rising—is a bit like being lectured by Henry VIII on marriage or by Hannibal Lector on veganism. Now, they want to make things even worse, not just by rejecting any progress at all and defending the status quo, but by ripping up our membership of the ECHR, which underpins international trade treaties such as this and gives other nations confidence in debating and negotiating with us. Will the Prime Minister promise my constituents and all businesses who have been affected that, as he sorts out the mess that Brexit has made in our economy and our society, he will not make the same mistakes as them?
