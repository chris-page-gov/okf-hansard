Turn 116 · Speaker unlabelled

As the noble Baroness, Lady Campbell of Surbiton, said, Ravi is 37 and lives with Duchenne muscular dystrophy. He relies on a ventilator and needs two carers with him around the clock. For 15 years, he lived in Lambeth with carers he chose and employed through his personal health budget. He worked part-time for a charity, went to football and saw his friends. In September last year, he went into the Royal Brompton for what should have been a three-day ventilator check and has been medically fit to go home ever since, but he has been stuck in a hospital for more than 14 months, waiting for his right to a personal health budget.
