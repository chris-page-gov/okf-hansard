Turn 4 · Edward Morello

I thank the hon. Member for his intervention. I shall be listing some recommendations that I would like the Government to look at in order to prevent such circumstances from arising again, not least the introduction of a minimum notice period. I am not a lawyer or an accountant, and I am not alleging that every failure was deliberate or malicious or attempting to prejudge any investigation. Whether these failures arose from negligence, incompetence or something more serious, they cannot be ignored. What is clear is that 550 students were left with no school place at no notice, 54 staff were left unemployed, and parents were left tens of thousands and, in some cases, hundreds of thousands of pounds out of pocket. The necessary regulators and the police must look at the evidence and use their best judgment, but when wrongdoing is established, those responsible must be held to account.
