Turn 60 · Lilian Greenwood

I will come to that point shortly, but it was built into the system when it changed in 2017. The bereavement support payment has been compared with widowed parent’s allowance, which would continue while child benefit remained payable—potentially for many years. It evolved from an older model of widowhood provision, developed when married women were much less likely to be in sustained paid work and surviving parents could face significant barriers to entering or returning to work. By the time of the “Bereavement Benefit for the 21st Century” consultation in 2011, women’s participation in the labour market had increased, and the introduction of universal credit was changing the wider welfare system.
