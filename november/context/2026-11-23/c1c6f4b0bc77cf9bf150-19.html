Turn 148 · Speaker unlabelled

Finally, Amendment 137 in my name and that of my noble friend would ensure that relevant patient information for both the NHS and independent care settings is made available through a record. Providers in each setting should see it when it is relevant. Many patients already move between the NHS and independent hospitals and care. They pay for it, or their employer pays it through insurance. We cannot have a record that stops at the door. The Government have told us that one of the reasons for this Bill, which we agree with, is to stop duplication. If it is not readable between the independent sector and the NHS, we are creating new duplication. Let us not forget what happened with the Paterson inquiry; one of the recommendations was that information on hospital care should cover both the NHS and the independent sector.
