Turn 6 · The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions (Pat McFadden)

The Government are already investing an additional £2.5 billion through the youth guarantee and the growth and skills levy to support almost 1 million young people to earn or learn. We have recently reached agreements with the retail, hospitality and tourism sectors, which will take on 250,000 young people who are not in education, employment or training over the next few years. In addition, we are supporting employers through the youth jobs grant and the youth guarantee.

What steps is the Secretary of State taking with the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero to create more apprenticeships? Will he visit the Energy Academy to see how the green transition can be used to tackle youth unemployment?

Turn 8 · Pat McFadden

I often have quite a full diary after these monthly question sessions, so I hope that a Minister will visit the facility that my hon. Friend spoke about, but I thank her for her question. She is right that the transition to clean energy can potentially create great jobs for young people, and the Energy Academy is an excellent example of the role that colleges can play. I assure her that I am working with the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero to ensure that as many of those opportunities are available as possible.
