Supporting Young People into Work: Cabinet Discussions

Commons · 2026-11-23 · Turns 9–10 of 10 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 9 · Deirdre Costigan (Ealing Southall) (Lab)

I recently visited the Ealing youth hub at Ealing Green college in my constituency, where young people are already getting tailored one-to-one advice and guidance to help them get a job under this Government. Now, in the next step, Ealing council will be taking the lead on the jobs guarantee scheme as part of the West London Alliance, and this will see 2,500 young people on universal credit being helped into a job across west London, with 562 in Ealing. Will the Secretary of State congratulate Ealing council on taking the lead on this initiative, and will he also wish the best of luck to those young people who will be helped to get a job?
