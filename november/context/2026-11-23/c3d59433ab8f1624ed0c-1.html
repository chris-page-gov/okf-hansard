Turn 3 · Irene Campbell (North Ayrshire and Arran) (Lab)

I beg to move, That this House has considered e-petition 752501 relating to the Bereavement Support Payment. It is a privilege to serve under your chairmanship, Mr Pritchard. The petition is titled “Raise Bereavement Support Payment and extend beyond 18 months”. It goes on to say: “Reform Bereavement Support Payment so families with and without dependent children receive help beyond 18 months, with annual uprating to reflect cost of living. BSP ends after 18 months and is not uprated, which can leave widowed families in financial hardship for years. I lost my husband in May 2025, gave up my career to care for him, and now earn less than 25% of our previous income while raising two young children. Previous Widowed Parent’s Allowance lasted until Child Benefit ended, offering stability. We feel that BSP ignores long-term income loss and emotional strain.” The Government’s written response …
