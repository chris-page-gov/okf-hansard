My Lords, in this amendment I suggest that the power to suspend and remove directors, which is in Schedule 3, should be put directly into Clause 30. Turn 8 · Speaker unlabelled

We read today that six NHS trusts, five of which I think are foundation trusts, have put at the top of their risk register the highest score possible for financing and whether they can cope. The best-performing NHS trusts are not at that level but, sadly, five of those six are foundation trusts. My call to the Committee is to think carefully when we try to defend this independence—independence on behalf of whom? It is not on behalf of the patient, when these trusts are performing so badly.
