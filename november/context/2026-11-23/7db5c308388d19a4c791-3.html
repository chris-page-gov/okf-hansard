Women Experiencing Rough Sleeping

Lords · 2026-11-23 · Turns 17–20 of 20 in this section.

Turn 17 · Lord Young of Cookham (Con)

My Lords, I welcome the recently announced rough sleeping programme, which builds on earlier initiatives, but as the noble Baroness, Lady Thornhill, said, most rough sleepers are men. Night shelters and hostels are dominated by men, many of whom have alcohol and drug issues and some of whom have mental health problems and are prone to aggression. For that reason, many women refuse the accommodation that is available. Will the programme have suitable alternative accommodation for women rough sleepers?

Turn 18 · Baroness Taylor of Stevenage (Lab)

It is very important that a range of accommodation is offered. I totally accept the noble Lord’s point: it is vital that we do not place women in further danger in the temporary accommodation that they are placed into. Our local councils do great work in trying to ensure that housing is safe and, particularly for someone who may already be traumatised by rough sleeping, that they are not then put into further danger when they go into emergency accommodation.

Turn 19 · Baroness Lister of Burtersett (Lab)

My Lords, perhaps I may take us back to my noble friend’s supplementary question. According to the Single Homeless Project, the women’s rough sleeping census identified 10 times more women sleeping rough than the official count. Everything my noble friend the Minister has said reinforces the case for the census that my noble friend Lady Nye has asked for. Will she look again at it, because we are failing by leaving the census on a voluntary basis?
