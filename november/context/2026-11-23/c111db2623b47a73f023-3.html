Turn 17 · Lord Blunkett (Lab)

My Lords, I appended my name to my good and noble friend Lord Hunt’s amendment and commend the contributions already made by him and the noble Lord, Lord Patel, on the value of what I describe as “citizen engagement”. It is self-evident that the contribution, and therefore the effectiveness, of elected governors is so variable across the country that you can pick out individual cases where abolition would have no impact whatever. But that is not a reason for abolishing; it is a reason for spreading best practice, linking those who are working well with those who do not, and putting a small amount of resource into ensuring that people understand the process and engage with it in voting people in, their report back and their contribution. The system does not work well. Normally, I agree entirely with the noble Baroness, Lady Pidgeon, on just about everything she has ever said in this House …
