Turn 2 · The Minister for Employment (Andrew Western)

We have committed to give mayors the powers, flexibility and accountability to bring together education, employment, health and local support into a clear route for young people, and we will devolve employment support to mayors, including tailored and specialised support for the long-term unemployed. The “Rewiring the State” delivery plan will provide more detail on planned reforms, including employment support devolution.

Turn 4 · Andrew Western

I thank my hon. Friend for her question. I was with her in Heywood just last weekend, and as ever, she is championing not just her town but her entire constituency. It is encouraging to hear about the Sandon House work and skills youth hub, and I thank its people for their efforts—it is a good example of properly joined up work to support young people. The work we are doing on careers support forms part of that wider approach, working with local leaders and services to design support that responds to the needs of local people and places. Our immediate priority is continuity of careers support, following in-housing of that service into the DWP last month, but we will set out more detail about long-term unemployment and the jobs guarantee in the “Rewiring the State” delivery plan.
