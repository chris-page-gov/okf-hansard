Turn 117 · The Prime Minister

Neither. We had a good, positive discussion throughout. Hence, we have a constructive deal that has responded on many of the things that we wanted to see. At the scheme’s steady state, the Home Office estimates that around 14,000 young people will come in from Europe every year. The point that the right hon. Gentleman and his Opposition colleagues are missing is that this is within the context of a bigger economy where there are more opportunities to get the kind of jobs that people will seek, such as those in retail and hospitality. He needs to broaden his perspective and think of a bigger British economy that has more positivity and is more open to Europe. I think that will be a good thing for young people in this country.
