Small Businesses: Access to Finance

Lords · 2026-11-23 · Turns 17–20 of 20 in this section.

Hansard speaking order. A reviewed question or reply has its own link. Open Hansard for the full wording of any excerpt.

Turn 17 · Lord Londesborough (CB)

My Lords, we need to see more of our small business grow to become scale-ups. Only 1% make that journey, yet those that do account for more than 50% of all SME output. Despite some welcome but modest measures in the Budget, scale-ups continue to say that lack of access to finance will be their biggest growth blocker. Can the Minister—a former entrepreneur himself—tell us whether further measures are in the pipeline?

Turn 18 · Lord Leong (Lab)

My Lords, we are building a funding escalator that supports British businesses throughout their journey, from start-up loans and SEIS for start-ups through to EIS—which the noble Lords knows very well—VCTs and the British Business Bank investment in series A and series B to patient growth capital and, ultimately, IPO. With the British Business Bank’s capacity now increased to £30.6 billion, our ambition is very clear: Britain must be not simply the best place to start a business but the best place to scale, list and sustain it.

Turn 20 · Lord Leong (Lab)

The noble Lord makes an important point. Tax reliefs are available for all kinds of investment. I mentioned SEIS and EIS, which noble Lords know. Tax relief is good, as it encourages entrepreneurs to invest in start-up businesses. We will continue to encourage that as the environment allows. Importantly, we have the best challenger banks and banks lending money, but we also need to have private capital, and these schemes allow the capital to be funding our SMEs and start-ups as well.
