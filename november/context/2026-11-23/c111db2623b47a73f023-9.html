Turn 70 · Speaker unlabelled

As mentioned by the noble Baronesses, Lady Owen and Lady Deech, the HFEA’s 2023 report on modernising fertility law was an important recognition that the existing regulatory framework is not adequately protecting patients undergoing treatment and needs urgent reform. Amendment 279R would go further, translating those broad recommendations into detailed statutory duties and procedures. I therefore ask my noble friend the Minister whether she and her officials will meet with me to discuss whether the HFEA can collect information about drugs dosages and red-rated add-ons administered to women as part of their fertility treatment—and if not, why not? Can the HFEA be given enhanced information-gathering powers to collect data without delay? Can the HFEA be given interim enforcement powers to protect patients from harm? I look forward to her reply.
