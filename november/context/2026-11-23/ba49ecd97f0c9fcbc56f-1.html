National Risk Assessment of Proliferation Financing

Turn 1 · The Economic Secretary to the Treasury (Lucy Rigby)

The proliferation of chemical, biological and nuclear weapons poses a serious and evolving threat to the United Kingdom’s national security and to the wider international community. Proliferation financing remains central to the ability of hostile states and networks to procure materials, technology and expertise in support of illicit weapons programmes. As a global financial centre and trading hub, the UK is exposed to indirect and inherent PF risks. This updated “National Risk Assessment of Proliferation Financing”, published on 23 November 2026, sets out the Government’s assessment of those threats and vulnerabilities, building on the UK’s first PF NRA published in 2021.
