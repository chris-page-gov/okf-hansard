Turn 2 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Work and Pensions (Lilian Greenwood)

The Government remain determined to combat benefit fraud. We have already delivered the Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025, which introduced the most significant new anti-fraud powers for a generation, and since the 2024 autumn Budget we have committed ourselves to delivering £21.6 billion in fraud, error and debt savings by 2031-32. In last month’s Budget we announced five new measures to strengthen welfare delivery, increase payment accuracy, and protect public money.

Turn 4 · Lilian Greenwood

My hon. Friend does a fantastic job for the people of Newcastle-under-Lyme. I absolutely agree that the welfare system must be fair, both to taxpayers and to those who genuinely need support. We are determined to tackle benefit fraud, using data and enhanced checks to identify incorrect claims and taking firm action when fraud is found. Through the Jobs and Careers Service, we are ensuring that those who can work receive the help and support that they need to get a job.
