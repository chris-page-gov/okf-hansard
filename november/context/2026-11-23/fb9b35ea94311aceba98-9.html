Turn 65 · The Prime Minister

I know how much my hon. Friend cares about these issues, and I do too. I would routinely hear prominent voices in the Manchester music industry saying how difficult it had become, to the point at which they had given up on doing it. How sad is that? British bands who were listened to all over Europe are having to withdraw from touring. I was therefore really keen for that wording to be secured in the agreement, and I am going to try hard to turn it into something positive—I hope, visa-free touring for British performers. I think that that would be a really good way of projecting the best possible image of our country around Europe, and this is one area in which I hope that perhaps not everybody, but as much of the House as possible, will get behind it, because it is what our musicians and other performers deserve.

Turn 67 · The Prime Minister

That was a long question. From my point of view, I have had no discussions about fishing as part of this deal. On the exchange I had with my hon. Either we can keep our head in the sand, or we can act when things are not working. Does the Prime Minister agree that the direction of travel that we are now taking to see closer relationships with the European Union is vital for supporting businesses and families in all our constituencies to take up more of the opportunities that that will bring, and gives a message of hope that things can be better than they are?
