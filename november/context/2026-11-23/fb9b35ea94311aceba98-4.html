Turn 28 · The Prime Minister

I agree strongly with my right hon. Friend. May I pay tribute to her in her role as Chancellor for laying the ground for this agreement that has reached, and to the former Prime Minister too? They were both acting in Britain’s best interests and they enabled us to reach the agreement that we reached on Friday. It is pragmatic, but I agree with her completely: all the evidence points to the fact that this will boost Britain’s security, boost our economy and increase our exports, all of which are good things. If the Conservatives have different ideas about how we achieve all those things in the next decade, it is time we heard them. I am really grateful to her and to the former Prime Minister for all their efforts to make Friday’s deal possible.

Turn 32 · The Prime Minister

In this Parliament we will honour the red lines in the manifesto and we will get the maximum for Britain within that context, and I believe that the deal we got achieves that. As we look to the Parliament beyond this, the work that I have set in train will help inform decisions in the Parliament beyond this one. It is important for me to say again that the world has changed and will continue to change. We need to test any options against that reality, but in my view we should always be pragmatic and choose whatever option secures the best deal for Britain.
