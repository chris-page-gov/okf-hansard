Turn 10 · Baroness Ramsey of Wall Heath (Lab)

I am very sorry to hear that the noble Lord’s pub is struggling, for whatever reason, but I resist and disagree with his description of problems for pubs. The Government are introducing permanently lower tax rates for retail, hospitality and leisure properties with a rateable value of under £500,000, worth nearly £900 million per year and benefiting over 750,000 properties. We have launched a new £10 million hospitality grant scheme, backed by funding over three years, to support growth, resilience and innovation across the hospitality sector.

Turn 16 · Baroness Ramsey of Wall Heath (Lab)

Consumer food prices are impacted by a range of factors, including agri-food import prices, domestic agricultural prices, domestic labour and manufacturing costs, and sterling exchange rates. While the Government have limited direct levers to influence food prices, we are supporting households through cost of living measures, including the suspension of import tariffs on everyday goods and the maintenance of the energy price cap.
