Turn 167 · Speaker unlabelled

128: Clause 52, page 36, line 37, at end insert— “(2A) A requirement by virtue of subsection (2)(b) may be imposed on a person providing health care or social care only if they provide health care or social care in England.”Member’s explanatory statement Regulations can require people to disclose information for the purposes of making it available through the single patient record etc. This ensures that the regulation-making power can only be used to require information from those who provide health care or social care in England.
