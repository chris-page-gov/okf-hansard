UK-EU Emission Trading Systems Linking Agreement

Turn 1 · The Financial Secretary to the Treasury and Paymaster General (James Murray)

The Government are introducing a carbon border adjustment mechanism from 1 January 2027. It will ensure that highly traded, carbon-intensive goods imported into the UK face a carbon price comparable to the price that would apply under the UK emissions trading scheme if those goods were produced in the UK. At the UK-EU summit, the UK and the EU have agreed to link their respective ETSs.
