On levers, which noble Lords raised a number of times, Healthwatch England has the power to give written notice to the local authority if it is of the opinion that local Healthwatch duties are not being carried out. Turn 150 · Speaker unlabelled

On Amendment 200E, tabled by the noble Earl, Lord Howe, NHS trusts and foundation trusts are already under a statutory duty to involve service users when planning services and considering changes. I understand the intention of the amendment, but we are not persuaded that it is necessary. As I have mentioned at a number of points, local Healthwatch can already play a role in supporting NHS bodies to involve local people where that is appropriate and makes sense. Therefore, we do not believe that legislation is needed to enable that collaboration.
