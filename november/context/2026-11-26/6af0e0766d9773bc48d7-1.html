Turn 3 · Mr Tanmanjeet Singh Dhesi (Slough) (Lab)

I thank and commend my hon. Friend for securing this important debate. Many of my constituents and I are very concerned that approximately 45% of all religious hate crimes recorded by the police this year were anti-Muslim, which marks a 15% increase on the previous year. With Islamophobia on the rise, does my hon. Friend agree that we need to tackle its root causes in our society? Given that Islamophobia Awareness Month’s theme this year is “Don’t let it slide,” as she rightly noted, it is right that we call out the divisive politics of Reform UK, Restore Britain and others on the far right of our politics who are looking to divide our society and our country.
