Turn 42 · Josh Newbury (Cannock Chase) (Lab)

The news of the significant increase in the energy price cap as a result of ongoing volatility in international wholesale prices will be of huge concern to many of my constituents. I know that the Minister will share my deep concern for vulnerable people, like pensioners and those with learning difficulties, who might not be able to easily get hold of information on shopping around and the support that might be available to them. Will she impress on energy companies, Government agencies and local authorities the importance of resharing the details of support in an accessible way, and will she continue to keep the support available for our most vulnerable constituents under review in the coming months?

Turn 45 · Ms Billington

I agree. For far too long, I have been spending my time talking about this as a long-term issue, and it is becoming increasingly pressing. We have a crisis now that is one of several that have happened since 1973. Half of global recessions since 1973 have happened as a result of global fossil fuel prices, so we cannot simply say, “Right, let’s spend some money on this and worry about it later.” We have to do something now. That is why this Government are so determined to reform the energy system in a way that will benefit my hon. Friend’s constituents and constituents across the country.
