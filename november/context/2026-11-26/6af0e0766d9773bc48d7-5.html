Turn 35 · Katie Lam

I thank the hon. Member for her intervention, but what we are talking about today is Islamophobia. Unfortunately—it is deeply regrettable—Islamophobia definition advocates themselves have gone to great pains to connect these two issues, and to paint honest discussion of the rape and grooming gangs as Islamophobic. The consequences of that have been utterly catastrophic, and I believe that must be refuted. The idea that, faced with the record of a child’s abuse, someone’s instinct was to hide the ethnicity of those responsible is not just shocking but sickening. Those who did try to tell the truth were smeared. Baroness Casey herself was put forward for the Islamic Human Rights Commission’s “Islamophobe of the Year” Award and, the following year, so was the hon. Member for Rotherham (Sarah Champion).
