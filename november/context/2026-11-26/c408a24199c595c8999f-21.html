Turn 162 · Lauren Edwards

The Minister will probably not support the new clause, but I ask her to use her influence to develop a specific strategy to tackle APP fraud—if not in the Bill, then somewhere else. We need to find a way of capturing the role of social media companies in stopping APP fraud, and of making them play their part in sharing costs with the banks, which can only do so much to tackle it. The cost to the economy is huge. Back in 2024—I cited this on Second Reading, but let me do so again—the estimated cost of APP fraud was somewhere in the region of £450 million. That is considerable, and we really need to crack down on it. I ask the Minister to consider whether another mechanism could bring forward a strategy to tackle such a significant harm.
