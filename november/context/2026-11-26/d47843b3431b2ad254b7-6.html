Turn 41 · Jonathan Reynolds

I thank my constituency neighbour for her question. We are looking at a whole range of measures on responsible business conduct, including issues like forced labour, which has a lot of interest in both Houses of Parliament. I think the depiction of everyone in business as somehow seeking to abuse human rights or destroy the planet is simplistic. This is about a balance of responsibilities and the due diligence we need in supply chains, but we all stand to benefit when businesses employ people in a borough like ours and give them good wages. It is important to strike that balance in how a question like that on a policy issue is considered.
