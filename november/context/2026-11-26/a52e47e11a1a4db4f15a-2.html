Turn 10 · Lord Hendy of Richmond Hill (Lab)

I think the noble Baroness had something to do with that report in 2021, and it is of course a very good report. There is a funding issue here. The previous Government took away TfL’s annual operating grant in 2018. London boroughs have been ineligible to apply for national programmes, but this Government have restored that eligibility, together with a doubling, in effect, of highway maintenance funding across the country from 2024-25 to 2029-30. Highway authorities will be in a much better position in the future to fund proper bridge maintenance, which stops catastrophic events being contemplated. In recognition that not enough has been done, the structures fund will consider what needs to be done. The joint work between the London Bridges Engineering Group and TfL that I previously referred to has set out the most serious structures in London for application to the structures fund.
