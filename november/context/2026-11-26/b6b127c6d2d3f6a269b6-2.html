Turn 11 · Lord Markham (Con)

My Lords, my experience as Health Minister of dealing with the cyber attack on NHS diagnostic facilities reinforced the importance of minimising the amount of identifiable patient data held across different systems. Given the increasing capabilities of autonomous AI agents, what steps are the Government taking to ensure that NHS data is anonymised or pseudonymised wherever possible so that, even if unauthorised access occurs, the risk to individual patients is significantly reduced? This is not a matter of GDPR; all GDPR regulations are followed in these cases. It is a matter of the NHS protecting our data by not making it identifiable.
