Turn 209 · Kerry McCarthy (Bristol East) (Lab)

Subsection 3(c) of the new clause refers to “the provision of affordable credit to small and medium-sized enterprises, and social enterprises”, which takes us back to what the Minister said about a question of affordability rather than access to loans. Given the structure of social enterprises and voluntary sector organisations, when the community comes together because it wants to do something like community asset transfers, it is partly about affordability, because perhaps the only way finance could be accessed at all would be from extortionate lenders, but it is also about accessibility and having institutions that want, and are geared up, to funding such projects. Will the Minister say a bit more about where CDFIs fit into that?

Turn 210 · Lucy Rigby

Absolutely. For what it is worth, my hon. Friend is absolutely right that it is about access. It is about knowledge as well. We are trying to ensure that more people and organisations throughout the country are aware of the role of CDFIs so that greater access can be achieved. That benefit goes alongside affordability. My hon. Friend the Member for North West Cambridgeshire said that his contribution might be his last for today, but I encourage him to keep his very thoughtful contributions coming. He is right that there is consensus on increasing access to affordable credit. The priority is to remove the barriers that prevent households and small businesses from accessing it. We are supporting lenders to reach more customers and the growth of the community finance sector, while keeping responsible lending at the heart of what we wish to do.
