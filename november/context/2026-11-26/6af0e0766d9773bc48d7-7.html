Turn 51 · Katie Lam

I have taken as many interventions as I can in the time available. Instead of firmly and fairly enforcing the laws that we already have, the Government have reached for a new definition, which will make no difference to the rules about and protections from physical harm, but will certainly have a chilling effect on the ability of people in this country to tell uncomfortable truths. That is a very dangerous path for Britain to take. The interests of everybody in this country are best served by the application of the rule of law.
