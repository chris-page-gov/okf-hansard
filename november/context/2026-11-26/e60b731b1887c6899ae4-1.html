Stalking Legislation Review: Government Response

Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for the Home Department (Satvir Kaur)

This Government are committed to halving violence against women and girls in a decade. A key part of that mission is transforming the response to stalking, which is a uniquely intrusive and devastating crime. Around 1.9 million people aged 16 and over experienced stalking in the year ending March 2026. For victims, the impact can be constant and life-changing, stripping away their sense of safety, freedom and control. While important progress has been made since stalking became a specific criminal offence in 2012, too many victims continue to face inconsistent experiences across the criminal justice system. Concerns about how stalking is understood, identified and investigated were highlighted by the super-complaint on the police response to stalking cases, which also raised questions about whether the law is sufficiently clear and effective.
