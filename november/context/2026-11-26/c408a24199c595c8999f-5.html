Turn 35 · Mark Garnier

From listening to the hon. Gentleman’s speech, and that of the hon. Member for North West Cambridgeshire, what we are talking about is the difference between tactics and strategy. A grand strategy is one where we know what our north star is, but tactics refer to the small bits and pieces that go with it, such as non-fungible tokens. Without knowing our grand strategy, it is not always easy to understand how our tactics fit in. This is about the importance of having a grand strategy, rather than necessarily worrying that the tactics will take us somewhere, but that we do not ultimately know where.
