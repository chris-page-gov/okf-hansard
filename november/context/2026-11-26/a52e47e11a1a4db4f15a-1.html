Turn 4 · The Minister of State, Department for Transport (Lord Hendy of Richmond Hill) (Lab)

My Lords, it is the responsibility of London’s highway authorities and bridge owners to make their individual assessment of the state of repair of each of London’s bridges. This year, the Government launched the new structures fund to help local authorities repair and/or replace ageing structures; that is, bridges, flyovers and tunnels. We are now considering all applications and funding decisions will be made soon.
