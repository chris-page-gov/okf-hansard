Turn 11 · Speaker unlabelled

It is a sad fact that the vast majority of the 22,000 people who witnessed the tests have died, some many years ago. But they passed on the torch of their campaign over the years, and it is right and fitting that we are honoured by the presence of members of the test veterans community with us today. We salute you all and we know you represent every single person you were proud to serve alongside and every single person who was caught up in this. We are also joined by families affected: wives who have fought for husbands, and children and grandchildren for fathers and grandfathers. You have been through what no British family should, but today we recognise the depth of your commitment to the loved ones you have lost.

Boris Johnson as Prime Minister was right to call for the Nuclear Test Medal in 2022, finally recognising their service and their sacrifice. Turn 13 · Speaker unlabelled

There is a very significant reason why this is an urgent issue now and not just one of the past. It is a fact that it was not just the 22,000 who experienced cancers and a range of other health complications way above the rate to be expected in the wider population. Sadly, those health issues have been passed down the generations and have led to disability and illness in descendants, including very young children today showing signs of chalk teeth. It is a moral imperative that people get the full truth and that they get it as quickly as possible, so that the health risks are fully understood and acted upon properly. I am today committing from the very top of government to do precisely that.

Turn 16 · Speaker unlabelled

I want to explain why I believe that a non-statutory, fully independent panel represents the best way forward for nuclear test veterans and their families. I hate to put it so bluntly, but time is not on our side, and it is not on their side. Our nuclear test veterans will not be with us for ever. We need to move quickly. I understood the call for a special tribunal, but primary legislation would be required to establish it. That would mean we were still some years from the truth. This approach will achieve maximum possible disclosure in an accelerated timeframe.
