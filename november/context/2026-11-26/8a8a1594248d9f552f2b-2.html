Turn 12 · Steve Barclay (North East Cambridgeshire) (Con)

My constituents are deeply worried about the prospect of energy bills going up by more than £400 in January, which will be £800 since the election. The Minister’s whole tone seems to be, “It’s nothing to do with the Government.” She even started her reply to my hon. Friend the Member for Bridgwater (Sir Ashley Fox) by saying, “This is going to be fun”, but people are deeply worried about this. Just two years ago, Labour said that it could do something about this—in its manifesto, it said it was going to cut bills by £300. Four years ago, Labour said that the Government could do something about it and called for an energy price cap freeze. Is the reality not that this Government could have done something in the Budget, but they chose not to because they want to prioritise other areas of policy, and they are leaving residents to face this £400 rise without adequate support?
