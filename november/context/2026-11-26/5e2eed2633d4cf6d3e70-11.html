Turn 82 · Chris Webb (Blackpool South) (Lab)

At the Salvation Army’s Bridge Project in Blackpool, I saw how a hot meal, a shower, and the support that people need and are entitled to, including support in finding a new home, make a real difference. Our drive to end rough sleeping is not empty words; it is backed by the Prime Minister, who is coming forward with the funding needed to offer everyone who is sleeping rough a route off the streets this Christmas. Will the Leader of the House join me in thanking all the amazing volunteers at the Bridge Project, and our outstanding outreach officers in Blackpool, and urge everyone who sees anyone sleeping rough in the cold this Christmas to immediately report it through the StreetLink website?

Turn 83 · Sir Alan Campbell

I certainly join my hon. Friend in thanking the dedicated staff and volunteers at the Salvation Army’s Bridge Project for their vital, lifesaving work. I thank the Salvation Army, not just in his constituency but in others, including mine. It does a fantastic job, and I want to put that on record. Nobody should have to spend a single night sleeping out in the freezing cold, least of all over Christmas. Services such as the Bridge Project are crucial. The Government are committed to ending homelessness and rough sleeping with real action. I also join my hon. Friend in urging everyone to use the StreetLink website to make sure that those sleeping rough get the help that they need.

Turn 86 · Sir Alan Campbell

Of course I understand that my hon. Friend is disappointed that he is not playing Bond, but he should not give up on playing for Leeds United; there may well still be time. Councillor Kay Morrison is playing an excellent and important part in that.
