Turn 51 · Sir Alan Campbell

I join my hon. Friend in his opposition to the divisive and toxic politics that he describes. The right to peaceful protest does not extend to unlawful behaviour or violent actions; he is right to call those out and he is right to condemn them, and I am happy to join him in that. Let me also say that we have an obligation to ensure the language that we use in this place does not encourage people outside to think that they have the green light to act in that way. He raises issues around the decisions made at the time; decisions on arrest and use of police powers remain operational matters, but we constantly review the safety of elected officials, including through the work of the defending democracy taskforce, and I will ensure that the chair of the taskforce, my right hon. Friend the Security Minister, has heard my hon. Friend’s concerns.
