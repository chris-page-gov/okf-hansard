Turn 2 · The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Jonathan Reynolds)

I thank the hon. Member for the opportunity to finally recognise in this House the importance of King Æthelstan uniting England 1,100 years ago. Being responsible for the designation of bank holidays is not as much fun as it sounds because Members of Parliament tend to ask for quite a lot. I have to tell the hon. Member that there are no plans to establish one on this occasion, but I am grateful for the opportunity to mark this milestone in the House.

Turn 3 · Andrew Rosindell

As you will know, Mr Speaker, 2027 marks the 1,100th anniversary of the establishment of England by King Æthelstan. It is no overstatement that this is an occasion that needs to be celebrated and marked. In other nations, there would be postage stamps, coins, bank holidays and other kinds of celebrations, but it appears that nothing is happening in England. Will the Minister treat this unique occasion with the dignity it deserves to ensure that all the people of England can celebrate their proud English heritage?

Turn 4 · Jonathan Reynolds

As I have said, I cannot promise the hon. Gentleman a bank holiday, but this is an important occasion to recognise all the achievements of Æthelstan. We might consider his work on strengthening English Government in law and on regulating our coinage, which is relevant to this Department—I think he also took quite significant measures against theft and corruption. He also had a significant set of achievements in forging stronger links with leaders in continental Europe. I am pleased that Reform is recognising the importance of that in a national leader.
