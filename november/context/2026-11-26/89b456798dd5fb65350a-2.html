Turn 9 · Daniel Francis (Bexleyheath and Crayford) (Lab)

I also thank all the officials who helped us to produce this report in record time. The report concludes that the Government should bring forward an overarching strategy for UK aviation, international trade and connectivity. For my constituency, the easiest solution to improve access to western Europe would be to return international rail services at Ebbsfleet, which would be a very quick and easy solution. Will the Chair of the Committee set out her views on the need for the Government to produce both an aviation strategy and a position on how we can have a strategy for other modes to increase international travel?

Turn 10 · Ruth Cadbury

My hon. Friend has been an active and helpful member of the Committee since he joined, as are all the Committee members present today—most of the others are very active and helpful, too. Again and again, witnesses told us that we need an overarching aviation strategy, but we also need an overarching transport strategy. We opine on individual transport policies, individual transport sectors or particular projects such as HS1—like my hon. Friend, I look forward to see significant increase in capacity on HS1 and hopefully seeing it stop at Ebbsfleet—but it is difficult to discuss these issues in isolation from each other when people make decisions on how they travel or transport their freight depending on the overarching choice around them, not on an individual mode.
