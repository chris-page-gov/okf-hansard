Turn 41 · Sir Alan Campbell

We recognise that military flying can cause disturbance to local communities, and we are particularly mindful of the sensitivities surrounding Lockerbie. The Ministry of Defence is looking at the reported activity over Lockerbie on 12 and 13 November, and at the arrangements that currently apply to military flying in that area, but I understand the sensitivity and importance of these matters. I will support the right hon. Gentleman’s request for a meeting with Ministers, because I am sure that they will need to hear what he is saying in speaking up for his constituents.
