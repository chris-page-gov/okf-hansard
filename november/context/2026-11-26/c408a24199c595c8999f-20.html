Fraud is the most commonly experienced crime in Britain today. It is a devastating crime that can leave people penniless and broken-spirited. The UK financial services industry recognises its responsibilities in this respect and makes great efforts to prevent it, and it is ultimately held liable for 100% of the costs of authorised push payment fraud, up to £85,000.
