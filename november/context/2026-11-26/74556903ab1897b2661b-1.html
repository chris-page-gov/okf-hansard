Turn 2 · The Minister for Science, Innovation and Investment (Chris McDonald)

Hopefully we can be just as excited about access to finance for women in business. [Hon. Members: “Hear, hear!”] Currently, women only receive about 2% of equity investment for their businesses, which is why at the Budget, we announced that the British Business Bank would commit a further £100 million to back women-led businesses and funds on top of its £400 million investor pathways capital and diverse angel syndicate programmes, widening participation in venture investing. The Government also back the Invest in Women Taskforce, which has raised £635 million, including £130 million from the British Business Bank. We lead the Investing in Women Code, which is driving industry action on access to finance and has over 300 signatories.

Turn 3 · Sonia Kumar

I thank the Minister for his response. Warwick Business School estimates that £310 billion is at stake because female-led businesses are being held back from scaling. Young women face additional barriers because they may have less access to networks and funding, and they may not yet have the track record that lenders require in an environment in which business support is fragmented. What is the Minister doing to encourage young female entrepreneurs to start businesses, and what is he doing to make sure that they can start their business and grow in scale?

Turn 4 · Chris McDonald

Turn 5 · Jim Shannon (Strangford) (DUP)

I thank the Minister for his answers, and the hon. Member for Dudley (Sonia Kumar) for asking the question. I think of Tracy Hamilton and her husband Martin, who started their business in their kitchen. It is called Mash Direct. They now employ almost 250 people, and it all started with their ideas for how their vegetables from the field could be put into packages and sold across the nation. They sell not only in the south of Ireland, but across the United Kingdom and further afield. Tracy is an example of an innovative lady with confidence—a lady who had a kernel of an idea. We in Strangford are blessed to have many ladies of similar quality. What discussions has the Minister had with the relevant Minister in Northern Ireland to ensure that the ladies in Strangford and Northern Ireland can have advice and finance whenever they need it?
