Turn 26 · The Economic Secretary to the Treasury (Lucy Rigby)

It is a privilege to serve under your chairship, Sir Edward. Section 19 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 established the general prohibition that prohibits the carrying on of regulated financial services activity unless a firm is authorised or exempt. Section 39 of FSMA sets out the exemption from the general prohibition that enables appointed representatives to carry on regulated activities. An appointed representative is a firm that can carry on certain regulated financial services activities without being authorised itself, under the responsibility of an authorised firm known as its principal. For example, a shop may sell insurance for the products it sells, such as a television, by acting as an appointed representative of an insurer. Turn 29 · Lucy Rigby

The senior managers and certification regime is the framework for conduct, fitness and propriety that applies to professional staff working in authorised financial services firms. When we reach clauses 34 to 39 and schedule 3, we will deal with the wider reforms to this regime. For now, I will concentrate on how this regime relates to appointed representatives. An earlier framework, the approved persons regime, currently applies to appointed representatives, rather than the SMCR. The Bill addresses this inconsistent approach by bringing appointed representatives within the scope of the SMCR. I commend the clause to the Committee. Question put and agreed to. Clause 29 accordingly ordered to stand part of the Bill. Clauses 30 and 31 ordered to stand part of the Bill. Turn 31 · Lucy Rigby

The competitive financial services sector depends on new firms being able to enter the market to test new ideas and to grow. However, early-stage firms can find it difficult to demonstrate from the outset that they are able to meet the requirements for full authorisation on an ongoing basis. Clause 32 addresses that barrier by creating a new route through which eligible firms can obtain temporary part 4A permission from the Financial Conduct Authority, known as the provisional licences regime. This is not a route for firms to operate outside of regulation, nor is it intended to lower the standards expected of firms. A firm with temporary permission will operate within the FCA’s regulatory framework, subject to the requirements and restrictions applying to that permission, and under the FCA’s close supervision. Turn 32 · The Chair

With this it will be convenient to discuss the following: Schedule 3. New clause 34—Continuity of senior manager’s approval— “(1) The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 is amended as follows. (2) After section 67ZB insert— ‘67ZC Continuity of senior manager’s approval The FCA may vary an approval under section 59 given by the FCA or the PRA for the performance of a designated senior management function in relation to the carrying on of a regulated activity by an authorised person if that person is moving to a different UK-based firm.’” This new clause would allow a senior manager who has gone through the senior managers certification regime for one firm to passport that certification if they move to another firm, so they would not be required to go through the certification regime again.
