Turn 200 · Bobby Dean

I beg to move, That the clause be read a Second time. The new clause relates to access to affordable credit, and is about tackling systemic financial exclusion. Millions of financially viable individuals, households and small businesses are currently shut out of mainstream banking. Over 3.5 million people in the UK rely on high-cost credit, while 24% UK adults have no financial resilience. The new clause also relates to the failure of mainstream bank models for small and medium-sized enterprises. High street banks have basically moved away from local relationship banking.
