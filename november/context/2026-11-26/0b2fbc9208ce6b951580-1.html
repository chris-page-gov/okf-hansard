Turn 4 · Speaker unlabelled

This day has taken far too long in coming. The only reason it has arrived is the sheer courage of thousands of ex-servicemen and their families who refused to give in. Our nuclear test veterans stand with all other veterans in displaying all the typical characteristics that we so rightly commend in the British Armed Forces: service, bravery, dignity and determination. Yet these veterans have been left to stand apart for decades, in a wilderness and with no choice but to fight their own country for recognition and answers. It is hard to know how we can even begin to put that right today, but it does mark the day when we make a start in earnest.

Turn 5 · Speaker unlabelled

Next year will mark the 75th anniversary of Britain’s first nuclear test. Operation Hurricane took place on 3 October 1952 off an island off the coast of Western Australia. It was one of many detonations that were to take place across the 1950s and 1960s in the south Pacific, involving about 22,000 British servicemen, many called up on national service.
