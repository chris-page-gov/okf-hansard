Turn 37 · Ms Billington

The issue of heating oil plays on my mind a lot because of the situation we have with off-grid households. I am talking to trade bodies in the next couple of weeks about ensuring that the voluntary codes of conduct are upheld and enforced this winter. For me, that is very important. It includes things like payment plans and minimum volumes, and making sure that the system works as well as it possibly can before we have regulation. We looked at exempting heating oil from VAT, but because of the nature of the market and fact that it is not regulated, our ability to guarantee that the cut in VAT would be passed on was limited. We have been able to take VAT off electricity, which applies to 98% of people who not on the gas grid, because we could enforce that through the regulatory system, but we cannot do that with heating oil until we have regulated the system.
