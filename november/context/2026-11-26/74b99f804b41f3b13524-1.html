Turn 2 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Blair McDougall)

The Government do not generally get involved in general disputes between businesses, except late payment disputes. The Office of the Small Business Commissioner operates a complaint scheme for payment disputes with recommendations to help to resolve them, while providing guidance on unpaid invoices, fair payment terms and contracts. The Commercial Payments Bill currently before Parliament will strengthen the Small Business Commissioner’s powers and introduce a new adjudication scheme, enabling the commissioner to resolve payment disputes outside the courts process.

Turn 3 · Sir Julian Smith

I refer colleagues to my voluntary interests. Disputes are costing British business billions of pounds. There can be all sorts of disputes: employment disputes, late payment disputes and big-ticket commercial disputes. The Government have set up a taskforce to look at how the systems around earlier settlement can be improved. That is a great idea. Technology can play an important role. I would appreciate a meeting with Ministers to discuss the taskforce further.

Turn 4 · Blair McDougall

I will make sure that the relevant Minister has a meeting with the right hon. Gentleman. He mentioned the late payments process, as I did. Businesses spend something like 130 million hours chasing late payments. That is time that could be spent chasing orders and winning new business. That makes the case very strongly that this is about saving not just time for businesses, but money.
