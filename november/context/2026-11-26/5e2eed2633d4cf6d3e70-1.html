Turn 5 · Sir Alan Campbell

Before I respond to the comments of the Opposition Deputy Chief Whip, I want to pay tribute to Wayne Jenkins, the senior Doorkeeper who is leaving us this week. Wayne served in the Irish Guards and the Metropolitan police before joining the House. He has dedicated 51 years to public service. We deeply value the excellent service that the Doorkeepers provide, and I am sure the whole House will join us in thanking Wayne for his commitment. Wayne and I have spoken on many matters over the years, not least his love for Liverpool football club, but my abiding memory of him is from that whole period—it was televised, for obvious reasons—when Her late Majesty lay in state in Westminster Hall and crowds came to pay their respects. Wayne and his colleagues did a fantastic job in making sure that went ahead with the respect it deserved. I am sure that was a proud moment for him. It makes us proud …

Turn 8 · Nick Smith (Blaenau Gwent and Rhymney) (Lab)

My constituents continue to experience a poor service from Royal Mail, despite the best efforts of our posties, and some are still missing vital health appointment correspondence. The NHS specific barcode is speeding up delivery of appointment letters in England. I raised its possible adoption with the Aneurin Bevan university health board in August, and I await a positive response. Will Health Ministers please raise the issue with their Welsh counterparts, and encourage use of the barcode on appointment correspondence, so that all our constituents benefit?
