Turn 14 · Mr Frith

I thank my hon. Friend for her point. She will see that this is a good guide—but we are not using props. I concur that it warrants further consideration and ongoing review, although, of course, the hyper-acute model developed in Greater Manchester does include rural areas. I myself represent, and my constituency is adjacent to, rural areas, and that development does support them, but I think we would all do well to pay attention to anything that the chief medical officer includes in his annual report. He makes a particularly powerful argument to Government and policy makers alike about rural areas, including on embracing technology and the need to have in place the digital infrastructure needed to support rapid deployment and adoption, so that we can assess in real time whether somebody is, in fact, having a stroke.
