Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government (Jim McMahon)

Local government is at the heart of this Government’s ambition to rewire the state, empowering local leaders to deliver good growth in every postcode. Local authorities provide the foundations on which successful devolution depends, delivering the services communities rely on and shaping their local economies. Strategic authorities can only be as strong as their constituent local authorities, making local authorities’ financial sustainability central to successful devolution. Today, the Government have published a policy statement setting out their current intentions for the 2027-28 local government finance settlement for local authorities across England. It provides early indication on key aspects of local authorities’ funding ahead of the provisional settlement in December, supporting authorities as they plan their budgets for next year.
