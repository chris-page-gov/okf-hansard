Turn 13 · Helena Dollimore (Hastings and Rye) (Lab/Co-op)

May I join your tribute, Mr Speaker, to Wayne our brilliant Doorkeeper? Twenty-eight elderly residents of Old Hastings House care home have been given the worst news in the run-up to Christmas, because the care company that owns the home is hiking fees by up to 80%, with no notice, and residents have been told that they have to leave before Christmas and find a new home. The care company recently purchased the home and, as far as I can tell from Companies House, it has at least three different guises: Adey Care, Arissa Care and Suricare, which are all run by a Mr Thind. Does the Leader of the House agree that it is callous to evict elderly residents at such short notice, especially before Christmas, and that Mr Thind must give residents more time to find new homes? Will he thank council staff who are working hard to support them in that?
