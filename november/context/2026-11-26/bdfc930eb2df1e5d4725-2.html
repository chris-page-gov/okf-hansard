Turn 9 · Caroline Voaden (South Devon) (LD)

I represent a large rural constituency, where people often have to travel for well over an hour to get to the nearest hospital. For those cancer patients having daily radiotherapy treatment, that can be exhausting. Parts of the area have no public transport whatsoever and hospital transport is almost non-existent, so it can cost hundreds of pounds in taxis to get back and forth to radiotherapy—not everybody has a car or somebody who can drive them. Does the hon. Member agree that a local radiotherapy offering is really crucial, particularly in rural areas?
