Turn 57 · Sir Alan Campbell

We all remember Bill Cash with great fondness. He once advised me, after I made some comments on the EU, that if I was not careful my career would go downhill rapidly. Gentleman has never been shy in talking about Europe. I am sure that if he catches the Speaker’s eye and contributes to that debate, he will make that point again.
