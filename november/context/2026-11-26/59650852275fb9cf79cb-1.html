Turn 2 · The Minister for Science, Innovation and Investment (Chris McDonald)

We are backing women to start, grow and scale successful businesses. Some 40% of the British Business Bank’s start-up loans have gone to female entrepreneurs, providing finance and mentoring. We are also backing the Invest in Women Taskforce’s £635 million pool, alongside British Business Bank programmes to increase capital for women founders and investors. Through the Business Growth Service and UK Export Finance, we are helping women to access the support and opportunities that they need to grow and trade internationally.

Turn 4 · Chris McDonald

The hon. Lady is right to say that we have great women-led businesses across the country in a range of sectors. Many of them can already receive support through the Business Growth Service, but also through the British Business Bank’s start-up loans ambassador scheme. They include businesses such as Sea Sisters in Cornwall, which is involved in fish canning, and in my own area, the north-east of England, the Pretty Knits Wool Shop, led by Hannah Hazledine-Herd. We have female-led businesses in retail, manufacturing and food services, and they all deserve support.
