Turn 20 · Mr Frith

My hon. Friend makes a valuable point. I will ask that question in our preparations for the workforce plan, which is due shortly. To drive improvements in stroke outcomes and reduce regional inequalities, the cardiovascular disease modern service framework, published in July, includes a priority focused on strengthening and scaling cardiovascular rehabilitation, including stroke rehab. By embedding stroke rehabilitation closer to home and linking it to cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic risk management, the MSF aims to improve recovery outcomes, reduce variation and strengthen long-term support for stroke survivors. The MSF includes priorities that aim to improve the prevention and treatment of strokes.
