Forty-four thousand people in my constituency really welcomed the Government intervention and the £100, but they are asking a simple question: is Ofgem fit for purpose? Turn 30 · Chris Webb (Blackpool South) (Lab)

I have lost count of the number of constituents who have come to me complaining about the failure of 40 years of Conservative privatisation to deliver energy security and lower bills, and yesterday’s price cap announcement is just more evidence of that. Will the Minister outline what additional support constituents can access if they are struggling to pay the bills this winter?
