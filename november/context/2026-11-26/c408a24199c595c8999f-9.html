Turn 69 · Lucy Rigby

I am grateful for that reminder. I have spoken directly to the Post Office about the importance of the framework that we have in place with it. Essentially, a commercial agreement between the Post Office and banking firms allows personal and business customers to withdraw and deposit cash, as well as to pay bills and to check balances. It applies to over 10,000 Post Office branches around the UK. The commercial agreement, banking framework 4, runs until the end of 2030. Alongside ministerial colleagues in the Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade, I co-hosted discussions between the UK’s largest banks and the Post Office at the beginning of this year. We discussed the ways in which we can continue to work together to ensure that that service, which people need, continues. Question put, That the clause be read a Second time.
