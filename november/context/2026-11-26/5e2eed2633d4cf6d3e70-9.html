Turn 68 · Dr Danny Chambers (Winchester) (LD)

For years, there has been an illegal encampment in Soberton, in my constituency. They regularly burn huge pyres of rubbish, sometimes more than once a week. This is causing misery for local residents, but it is also a public health issue; especially over the summer, when the fire service was stretched dealing with wildfires down in the New Forest, it could have caused devastation in the area. It seems that enforcement is falling between agencies—the police, the local authority and the fire service—with no one taking ownership, while the residents are left in limbo. Does the Leader of the House agree that we need clear guidance in these situations so that one agency grabs the bull by the horns? Can he advise me who I should speak to or meet with to pursue this matter further?
