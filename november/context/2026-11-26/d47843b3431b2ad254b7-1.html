Turn 7 · Jonathan Reynolds

I am pleased that the hon. Lady recognises the scale of the challenge posed to us by “Made in Europe”. She will know that it is a policy thread running through 50 different legislative areas in the European Union. As the process has not finished, there is no definitive legal advice that would give us a view on what she asks, because there is not a legal document. At the summit, we secured a political declaration, to which we must hold the European Union, in terms of whether the legislative process makes good on those promises. We have been able to find a real breakthrough and give maximum reassurance to British businesses. I think the whole House should welcome this progress.
