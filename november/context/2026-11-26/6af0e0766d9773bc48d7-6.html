Turn 42 · Katie Lam

I do not think I have time to give way. I will move on to the Government’s definition of so-called “anti-Muslim hostility”. The Government may have dropped the word “Islamophobia” but my hon. Friend the Member for West Suffolk (Nick Timothy) was right to call it “old wine in new bottles”. I pay particular tribute to my right hon. Friend the Leader of the Opposition, who warned long before the definition was drafted that the Labour party’s version would create “a blasphemy law via the back door”. There is a fundamental problem in the very concept of Islamophobia, which blurs the line between protecting people and protecting ideas. Muslims are not a race. Islam is a belief system. Nobody should ever get into trouble for saying they believe Sharia law is sexist or that Halal slaughter is cruel.
