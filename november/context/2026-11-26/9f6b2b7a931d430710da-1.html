Turn 3 · The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Jonathan Reynolds)

As official statistics show, despite considerable pressure arising from international events, British businesses continue to be resilient, and growth to the end of the year in September was back to over 2%. Small and medium-sized enterprises represent 99% of businesses in the UK, and they are the backbone of that resilience and success. The Department supports them in a number of ways, including increased access to finance, cutting regulation, increasing small business rates relief thresholds, and tackling late payments with the Commercial Payments Bill.

Turn 5 · Jonathan Reynolds

I agree wholeheartedly with my hon. Friend about the role that small business plays in Wrexham, and in every constituency, in providing opportunities for young people. The Government’s work across a number of policy areas recognises that. He is right to mention the tax system, and the Milburn report suggested a further tilting of apprenticeship funding towards opportunities for young people. As he says, business rates and high street regeneration are devolved in Wales, and we would encourage the Welsh Government to use those powers alongside UK-wide measures to help businesses invest in young people and give them those opportunities.

Turn 7 · Jonathan Reynolds

Paul sounds to me like exactly the sort of person who is playing a major role in his local community; a business like that is a pillar of so many local economies up and down the UK. I say to my hon. Friend and to Paul that our small business plan is about supporting firms with the everyday pressures they face, through measures such as tackling late payments, improving access to finance and greater business support, all of which have come out of engagement with small business representative organisations and small businesses themselves.
