Submarine Maintenance: Naval Base Support Services

Turn 1 · The Minister for Defence Readiness and Industry (Luke Pollard)

Today I am informing the House of the award of the gateway agreement with Babcock to provide essential submarine maintenance and naval base support services that help sustain the United Kingdom’s continuous at sea deterrence. The contract, awarded to Babcock International Group plc subsidiary Devonport Royal Dockyard Ltd, covers maintenance services for the Royal Navy’s nuclear submarine fleet and support services at His Majesty’s Naval Bases Devonport and Clyde as part of the Naval Support Integrated Global Network programme. The contract represents a significant investment in skilled employment and the United Kingdom’s sovereign defence industrial capability.
