Culture, Accountability and Governance at the Information Commission: Independent Review

Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport (Lisa Nandy)

Today I am announcing the launch of an independent review of culture, accountability and governance at the Information Commission. Following concerns raised about the conduct of the former Information Commissioner, the Information Commissioner’s Office commissioned an independent investigation in February 2026, which concluded in June. That investigation upheld multiple allegations against the former commissioner and he subsequently resigned. That investigation considered the conduct of one individual but did not examine the wider organisational conditions in which the relevant events occurred. This review has therefore been established to consider those wider conditions and identify any lessons for the future.
