Turn 33 · Sir Alan Campbell

I certainly encourage people to go along to the Brackley tractor run, and indeed to support Yellow Wellies. I am very much a supporter of the work that farmers do, as are the Government. On a number of occasions during these sessions, I have raised not just the importance of farmers in keeping our nation fed, but the fact that during what was probably the hottest summer on record, they stepped up—including in the hon. Lady’s area, I am sure—and supported their local communities. It is important that local communities step up at this time of year, in the run-up to Christmas, by buying good British produce and supporting our British farmers in every way they can.

Turn 35 · Sir Alan Campbell

First, I wish my hon. Friend—and indeed you, Mr Speaker, and all Lancastrians—a great Lancashire Day. At the Budget, we announced that we are in the final stages of agreeing a devolution settlement for Lancashire, with a mayoral strategic authority that will build on the existing structures. We are committed to supporting the roll-out of good growth funds across England, which, coupled with fiscal devolution, will empower local leaders to develop single local investment approaches. I will make sure that my hon. Friend’s wise words are heard by Ministers, and if he seeks a debate—either in Westminster Hall or an Adjournment debate—I am sure that other Lancastrian MPs and MPs with similar concerns in their areas will want to contribute.

Turn 39 · Sir Alan Campbell

I am sure the whole House will join me in extending our heartfelt condolences to Lee and Helen, and to all those affected by the outbreak. I understand that the inquest into Meg’s death will consider whether it was preventable or not, so I will not comment on that. Learning from these events is a vital contribution to transparency and public safety. In the light of that, we are considering advice from the Joint Committee on Vaccination and Immunisation on developing a routine programme for adolescents. I understand that my right hon. Friend is in touch with the relevant Ministers, and I will not only pass on his request but do everything I can to ensure that he gets the meeting that he seeks.
