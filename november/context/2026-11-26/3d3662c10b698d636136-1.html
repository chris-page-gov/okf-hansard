Turn 2 · The Minister for the Future of Work (Kate Dearden)

The Government are determined to restore pride in our high streets, support our local businesses and ensure that our town centres can thrive. Our new high street strategy sets out how we will revive high streets, support our local communities and restore confidence in our town centres. We want our high streets to be places where businesses can succeed, jobs can be created and communities can thrive. That is why the work we are doing to back local entrepreneurs, encourage investment and bring empty premises back into use is so important.

The owner of the Milk and Bean caff in Newbury told me that their VAT bill last year swallowed up nearly half the money in their bank account. Will the Minister work with the Chancellor to introduce a temporary 5% cut in VAT?

Turn 6 · Kate Dearden

My hon. Friend is a champion for hospitality businesses in her constituency, and I thank her for raising this issue. I meet hospitality businesses regularly, and I know their top demands in this space. She will have heard me say, in my response to the hon. Member for Newbury (Mr Dillon), that the Treasury keeps all taxes under review as part of the policymaking process. I am proud that we are backing our small businesses up and down the country. From the measures that we announced in the Budget, particularly on small business rate relief, to the work we are doing with the hospitality support fund and the work of colleagues in the Home Office to tackle dodgy shops on our high streets, we are backing businesses in constituencies up and down the country.
