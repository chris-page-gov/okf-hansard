Turn 2 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (Blair McDougall)

The 10-year modern industrial strategy sets out our long-term approach to boosting investment, productivity and growth. Through the complementary advanced manufacturing sector plan, backed by up to £4.3 billion of Government funding, we are helping manufacturers to grow and thrive. The Budget announced £1.5 billion more for DRIVE35, a £2.5 billion Rolls-Royce turbine partnership and continued support for electricity bills through BICS.

Turn 4 · Blair McDougall

Banbury and the wider Oxfordshire area have real strengths in automotive, motorsports and advanced engineering, which offer an exciting and purposeful career for young people. We are investing £182 million in engineering skills, as well as support for engineering, T-levels and new short courses in digital, AI and engineering. Building on the Milburn review, our forthcoming advanced manufacturing jobs plan will set out our wider ambition to strengthen routes into apprenticeships and make sure that more young people get the purposeful jobs that my hon. Friend spoke about.
