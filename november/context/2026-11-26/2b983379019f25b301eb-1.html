Turn 1 · The Minister for Social Security and Disability (Sir Stephen Timms)

I am pleased to announce reforms to the Disability Confident scheme, the Government’s flagship employer initiative to promote disability inclusion in the workplace. Since its launch in 2016, Disability Confident has supported thousands of employers across Great Britain to recruit, retain and develop disabled people and people with health conditions. The scheme has played an important role in encouraging employers to consider how they can remove barriers to employment and create more inclusive workplaces. As the scheme has matured, employers, disabled people, and representative bodies have identified opportunities to strengthen its impact, consistency and credibility. Through extensive engagement, stakeholders have highlighted the importance of ensuring the scheme continues to drive meaningful action by employers while remaining accessible to organisations of all sizes.
