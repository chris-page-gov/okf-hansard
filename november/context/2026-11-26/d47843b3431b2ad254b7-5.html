Turn 33 · Chris McDonald

I recognise the essential nature of the work done at Daresbury and the great uncertainty being suffered by researchers there as a result of the restructure in UK Research and Innovation and the necessary financial discipline that has been brought by the Science and Technology Facilities Council. I reassure the hon. Member that I have had regular meetings with the head of UKRI and his executive chairs and with the Prospect trade union, which is doing good work in this area. We expect to have further meetings in the coming weeks to ensure that we maintain that focus and that we are keeping those employees at the front of our minds during this restructuring process, and I thank her for her interest in that.
