Turn 9 · Blair McDougall

Unfortunately for the hon. Gentleman, I am a Minister in the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero as well as DBIS, so I have to be very careful about any commentary on the decision he mentioned. On industrial energy prices more generally, we are doing far more than the system we inherited to support businesses with energy prices through this difficult period, including stepping up support through the supercharger and the work on the British industrial competitiveness scheme. As the Prime Minister and the Chancellor have said, the 10-year plan will set out the pathway to get to European levels of competitive energy prices over that 10-year period.
