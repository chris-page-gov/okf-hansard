Turn 73 · Lucy Rigby

The Government want small businesses to be able to access the finance they need to grow, invest, innovate and hire. The commercial credit data sharing scheme supports this by making relevant credit information available to designated credit reference agencies, helping lenders to make better-informed decisions, and therefore promoting greater competition in SME lending. However, the SME finance market has evolved since the scheme was introduced in 2015, and the current legislation sets a specific definition of an SME that can create gaps in data. Business turnovers may temporarily fluctuate, which can cause them to fall outside the existing definition and therefore out of scope.
