Turn 11 · The Lord Bishop of Gloucester

My Lords, yesterday, through the Centre for Women’s Justice, I met several amazing women who had survived sexual exploitation and who described being raped by men who treated their pimp’s consent as if it were the women’s own. These women continue to live with a criminal record after escaping abuse and exploitation, and they, along with hundreds of women, are experiencing lifelong harm. Will the Minister commit to meet these survivors, and will the Government look at repealing the outdated offence of loitering or soliciting and ensure that criminal records for this offence are expunged, allowing survivors to move on with their lives?
