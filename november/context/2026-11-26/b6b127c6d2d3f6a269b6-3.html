Turn 18 · Baroness Lloyd of Effra (Lab)

The noble Lord is right about the importance of the UK developing sovereign capabilities, but we must also have access to the right global capabilities. We want the UK, the NHS and others to benefit from the advances that we are seeing. In respect of our own developments, we have £500 million in our sovereign AI fund that is continuing to make investments, the £1.1 billion AI hardware plan and the £2 billion quantum investment, all of which are building out UK capabilities which will support the public and private sectors.
