Turn 24 · Lord Hendy of Richmond Hill (Lab)

Let us talk about the funding a bit. If we refer to the bridges in London, the last Government removed the TfL annual operating grant in 2018, and London boroughs, prior to the advent of this Government, were ineligible to apply for national programmes. Long before we get lots of HGVs with batteries on the roads, the Government have put that right by restoring that eligibility and putting more funding into highway maintenance generally, so it will more than double by 2029-30. Of course, the noble Lord is right that the roads of Britain ought to be fit for the traffic that goes on them, and I am sure that the Government’s commitment will result in better road conditions in the future than in the recent past.
