Turn 9 · Chris McDonald

There is clearly a lot more than the economic environment when we look at the issues around Poundland, and it is important to be slightly cautious about how we talk about things. I understand what the hon. Lady says when she talks about Poundland not being a strategic industry, but Poundland stores are strategic stores in towns in my constituency—I am sure she would acknowledge that—and they are strategically important to the employees who work there and who are currently our greatest concern. Poundland is part of the fabric of high streets up and down the country and this will be a very worrying time, particularly for employees who are very much in the front of our mind. The Secretary of State has been in close contact with Gordon Brothers about Poundland, as well as with USDAW, the shopworkers union, which is working hard on this.
