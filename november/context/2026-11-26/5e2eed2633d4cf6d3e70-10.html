Turn 73 · Sir Alan Campbell

The hon. Lady raises a very important point, as ever. There is no doubt that GP services being online is a huge step forward for all of us, but it is sometimes difficult to access them. The Secretary of State for Health and Social Care is acutely aware of that. We are doing everything that we can to make sure that, while services are online, people have the support that they need to access them or to physically get the help that they need. These are important matters, so I will raise them with the Department of Health and Social Care. Everybody must be able to access the help that they need, whether or not they can access online services.

Turn 79 · Sir Alan Campbell

I certainly join my hon. Friend in congratulating Simon on developing Vape Guardian. Youth vaping is a serious and growing public health concern. The Government are taking decisive action to protect young people from the harms of nicotine, including by introducing a vaping duty to discourage young people from taking up vaping. I will make sure that his remarks are shared with Ministers at the Department for Education, so they can take note of that important innovation.
