Turn 11 · Steve Race (Exeter) (Lab)

I welcome my hon. Friend’s powerful statement, and the important report that the Committee has done under her chairmanship. She mentioned the important work that volunteers do around the country. We have Citizens Advice Exeter, and the University of Exeter Community Law Clinic, in which early-career students provide early-stage support and advice to people who desperately need free legal advice. Also, our constituency offices are often the first port of call for people who are looking for legal advice. Our offices cannot provide that, but they can signpost to those organisations. Will she join me in thanking all of them, across the country, for their work?
