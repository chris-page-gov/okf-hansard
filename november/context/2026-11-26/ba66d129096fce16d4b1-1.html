Turn 1 · The Lord Speaker (Lord Forsyth of Drumlean)

My Lords, before I invite the noble Lord to ask his Question, I remind the House that the Joanne Young case will remain active until such time as the guilty parties have been sentenced. In accordance with the sub judice resolution, noble Lords, as they discuss the issues raised in the Question, should not refer to those ongoing court proceedings.
