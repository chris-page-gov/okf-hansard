Turn 25 · Dan Jarvis

Those are entirely reasonable questions from the hon. Member. I hope that, through the remarks that I have made as part of the statement, I have conveyed the importance that we attach to process and looking carefully and systematically at what went wrong on this particular occasion. He will have heard the comments that I made earlier about the Cabinet Office shortly publishing a strengthened declaration and management of outside interests policy. That will apply across government and is one tangible example of how things will be done differently in the future, but I accept his basic point. We have seen behaviour that is not acceptable, and it is the job of Governments, Ministers and senior officials to put in place the right processes and the right structures to ensure that that kind of behaviour can never happen again.
