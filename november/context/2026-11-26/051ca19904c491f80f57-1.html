Turn 1 · Mr Speaker

Before we come to business questions, we pay tribute to Wayne Jenkins, Deputy Principal Doorkeeper. I am sure he is known to many colleagues. He will retire from the House service on Monday, when he will lead the procession, I believe. Following his distinguished career in the Irish Guards and the Metropolitan police, Wayne joined the House service on the senior Doorkeeper team in 2013. In 2016, he started in his current role, which he has carried out with professionalism, dedication and good humour. Throughout his time in the House, Wayne has been a valued member of the Doorkeeper team. I am grateful for the support he has provided to me in my role previously as Chair of Ways and Means and now as Speaker. I am sure that colleagues will join me in wishing Wayne all the very best in his retirement. I just say to anybody looking on that we will always welcome people from the armed forces.
