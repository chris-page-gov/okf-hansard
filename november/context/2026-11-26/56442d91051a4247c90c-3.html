Turn 17 · The Earl of Effingham (Con)

My Lords, the previous Education Secretary dismissed a legal ban on smartphones in schools as a “headline-grabbing gimmick”, which proved ill-advised. As the noble Baroness, Lady Berger, rightly said, reports show that some school iPads can reach pornography and execution videos. Frankie Thomas took her life after reading suicide stories on an unfiltered school iPad. The Government’s edtech safety consultation does not even mention screens. Surely, we need to look at maximum limits for classroom screen use before even more children are harmed. Why are screens not on the edtech consultation?

Turn 18 · Baroness Ramsey of Wall Heath (Lab)

On 10 November, the DfE launched a consultation on a safety certification framework for certain types of edtech used in education settings. The proposed approach focuses on products and features with the greatest potential to cause harm in three areas: serious data infringements, harmful content exposure and risks to cognitive development and social and emotional well-being. This includes products with certain gamification features. This will build on existing digital and technology standards and generative AI product safety standards. The consultation will help develop a proportionate and robust approach to help schools identify products meeting relevant safety expectations and to inform purchasing decisions.
