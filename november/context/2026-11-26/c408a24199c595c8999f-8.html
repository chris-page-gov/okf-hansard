Turn 63 · Speaker unlabelled

To add some more bank closure statistics to those provided earlier, around 15,000 bank branches have closed since the 1980s. Banking hubs are being promoted as an alternative, but there is an existing service that we are not making enough use of: post offices. Banks already rely on post offices to provide essential services in areas where banks have largely or completely disappeared, but their ability to do so depends on a corresponding requirement for banks to fund those services properly on terms that can keep them viable, and that is not happening at this point.

Turn 64 · Speaker unlabelled

I appreciate that the Lloyd review has only just been published, so the Government have had very little time to react, and have done so in the shape of Government new clause 17. For that reason, new clause 21 was tabled as a probing amendment to give the Minister an opportunity to lay out the Government’s intentions, particularly with regard to protecting rural areas. Specifically, will the regulations set a maximum distance or travel time, and will those will be different for rural areas? Secondly, will rural coverage be reported separately, so that the 5.6% and 62.3% figures can be tracked against performance? Finally, when a rural branch closes, is a mobile service or hub enough to meet the standard, in her view?
