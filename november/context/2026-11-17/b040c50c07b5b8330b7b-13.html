Turn 102 · Greg Smith

I understand the Minister’s arguments. I could probably list a number of things that led to our defeat in 2024, but I am not sure that this was one. However, this point continues to be, at best, a grey area. Notwithstanding the arguments that the Minister has just made, I am not certain that a circumstance could not come to pass in which a licensee, who may well be an airport operator seeking to build new surface access to their airport, would not be able to over-inflate a toll to manipulate the manner in which its customers travel to the airport. The Opposition feel that it is important to shut it down explicitly via new clause 3.
