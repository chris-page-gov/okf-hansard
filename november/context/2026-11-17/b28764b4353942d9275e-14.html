Turn 110 · Speaker unlabelled

One element of the amendment on which the Government and the hon. Member for Orpington are less aligned is the reference to “the immediate vicinity”. A lack of local capacity should not by itself justify delays where suitable assessors, contractors or materials could reasonably be obtained from further afield. Where residents’ safety is at stake, responsible entities should be expected to take reasonable steps to find a solution, rather than stopping at what is available in their immediate vicinity. For those reasons, the Government cannot accept the amendment, and I urge the hon. Member to withdraw it.
