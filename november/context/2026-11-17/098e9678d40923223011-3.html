Turn 22 · Speaker unlabelled

Also, excessive use of screen time can affect several aspects of children’s healthy development, including sleep. Screen use, particularly before bedtime, can delay sleep and disrupt sleep routines. Insignificant sleep can affect mood, concentration, memory, behaviour and physical development. Non-physical activity—children not playing outdoors or taking part in exercise and other physical activities—is harmful, which in turn can cause obesity, an increased risk of unhealthy weight and poor physical fitness. Also, prolonged device use can contribute to eye strain, headaches, neck pain and poor posture. Importantly, it can cause increased anxiety, low mood and depressive symptoms.

Turn 24 · Speaker unlabelled

Finally, I support Amendment 279P in the name of the noble Baroness, Lady Barran. We need the same protections for digital health services as we do for other medical devices, especially when it comes to the safety of children. Currently, this is not the case. We all know that digital devices, such as diabetes monitoring, can be life-saving, but they also give children unregulated access to the internet, social media and AI chatbots. Not surprisingly, children have accessed harmful and unsuitable material as a result. In fact, very young children, even some of primary school age, have done so. This is not good for their well-being.
