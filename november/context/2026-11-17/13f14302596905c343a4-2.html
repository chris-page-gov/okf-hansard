Turn 11 · Baroness Blake of Leeds (Lab)

I think the noble Baroness is referring to the requests for data from private parking operators to the DVLA. That changed, perversely, when we ended clamping. The number of parking operators and the demands for information about the keepers of cars, as she rightly says, have gone through the roof. The number of requests for information has gone up to 16.9 million in 2026. She is absolutely right that this needs to be tightened up, and that is exactly what the code is doing. We are very grateful to the CMA for the recommendations that it has made, and we look forward to the next stages of this to implement fully the resources at our disposal.

Turn 16 · Baroness Walmsley (LD)

My Lords, RCP refers to asymmetric information between parking operators and motorists. This means that motorists send information, and parking operators ignore it. I took up the case for my neighbour, Mr Lloyd Roberts, against Euro Car Parks. He paid and displayed, and I have seen the payment on his bank statement. He received a fine and appealed within 28 days, both online and in writing, showing the proof of payment. He had no reply to either, while demands for money ramped up. This morning I had a letter from Euro Car Parks claiming that he did not pay and did not appeal. Is this not proof of the need for an independent regulator?
