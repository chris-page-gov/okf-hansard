Turn 86 · Gareth Bacon

I do not agree with the hon. Gentleman’s assessment, because the amendment says that they must demonstrate that they could not “engage a qualified assessor”. We know there is a shortage of such assessors. Although we support the Bill’s overall aim of forcing people to do this work, if there are no qualified professionals—or insufficient numbers of qualified professionals—to carry it out, we are effectively setting people up to fail. What we are trying to do is show people that we care about something by legislating for it, all while knowing that there is no means of making it happen. That core point will run through many Opposition amendments.
