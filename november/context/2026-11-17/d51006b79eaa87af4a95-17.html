Turn 135 · The Chair

With this it will be convenient to discuss the following: Clause stand part. Amendment 15, in clause 23, page 13, line 25, at end insert— “(1A) The Secretary of State must provide relevant enforcement authorities with ringfenced financial resources and grant funding sufficient to meet the full administrative, technical, and legal costs incurred in exercising their functions under this Chapter, including step-in remediation works under Schedule 1.” This amendment places a statutory duty on the Secretary of State to provide ringfenced funding to local housing authorities and fire authorities so they have the resources required to enforce remediation and exercise step-in powers. Turn 136 · Gareth Bacon

At present, clause 22 allows for multiple bodies, including local authorities, local fire authorities and the Building Safety Regulator, to hold overlapping responsibilities over individual cases. This is a regulatory tangle that we cannot risk. Remediation has simply, and worryingly, been far too slow in the nearly 10 years since the tragedy that was the Grenfell Tower fire. All of us present have a duty to ensure that this lag has ended, which is why we must work to iron out any regulatory creases that appear in the Bill. That is why amendment 38 would introduce a crucial principle of administrative clarity: each building must have a single lead enforcement authority. In designating just one primary agency to have oversight and lead enforcement for every individual case, we eliminate the risk of regulatory delay and impasse.
