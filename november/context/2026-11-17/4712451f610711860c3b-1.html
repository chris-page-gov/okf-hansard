Turn 8 · Andy McDonald

The hon. Gentleman makes a valid point. The description of cases as being somehow complex masks the inability of Capita to resolve matters. Behind the figures that I recited are people who spent their working lives in public service only to face uncertainty and hardship in accessing their pensions. PCS reports that some have even been forced to postpone their retirement. There is also a cost to the taxpayer. PCS reports that more than 100 civil servants have been deployed to help to restore the pension service, at a cost of £12.5 billion. Taxpayers are paying a private contractor to deliver a service while public servants and public money are deployed to rescue it. That raises a fundamental question about whether outsourcing delivers value for money.
