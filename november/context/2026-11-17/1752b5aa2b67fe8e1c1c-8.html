Turn 58 · Pat McFadden

My hon. Friend is right to raise work experience. I spoke about mental health problems being perhaps a newer factor in this cohort compared with the situation when I was a Minister in a previous Government. Let me give her another statistic. About 60% of the 1 million young people not in education, employment or training have never had a job. This is not just youth unemployment. It is complete detachment from the labour market—from the sense of pride and purpose people get from getting up and going to work in the morning. We are providing 300,000 more work experience placements through the youth guarantee, which has a critical role to play in positively enhancing young people’s experience and giving them a better chance of getting a job.

Turn 60 · Pat McFadden

The right hon. Member makes a good point. Without going too far into my own anecdotage, when I was a teenager and I wanted a job stacking shelves in supermarkets, I could go into a supermarket and say, “Have you got any jobs?” That is harder for today’s young people, as recruitment is very often online and centralised. On the upside, the right hon. Member talked about personalised help. If he has not done so, I encourage him to go and visit a youth hub in order to see employment advice, health advice, housing advice and debt advice brought together in one place in a trusted venue in the community—often a community centre or a local sports club. That gives an insight into the kind of personalised and local support that we envisage doing much more of.
