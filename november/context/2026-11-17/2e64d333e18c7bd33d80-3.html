Turn 21 · Anna Turley

I am grateful to the hon. Gentleman for raising that issue on the Floor of the House. That is exactly what this Chamber is for, and I should be more than happy to discuss the issue with him after the debate. As I was saying, those who are in detention and fall in the late claims process may have their appeals expedited, but that is on the basis that they have not ignored the requirements of the claim notice. New clause 12 streamlines the immigration and asylum appeals process by removing the power of the first-tier tribunal to give permissions to appeal to the upper tribunal. Applications for permission can be made directly to the upper tribunal instead. As the House knows, the first-tier tribunal immigration and asylum chamber is currently facing significant pressures, with an outstanding caseload of 156,000 cases as of June 2026.
