We are working across Departments and closely with the Government in Edinburgh to support growth and secure a prosperous industrial future for Forth valley. In addition to the substantial funding already deployed at Grangemouth, the National Wealth Fund stands ready with £200 million to support new opportunities, alongside private investment, while our investment agencies are working hard to bring forward investors.

Turn 7 · Blair McDougall

For a year before the election, the Conservatives knew that Grangemouth was going to close and they had absolutely no plan for it. The technologies that we are working on—through Project Willow, for example—involve complex, often unproven technology, so this will take time. As I have just said in response to my hon. Friend the Member for Falkirk (Euan Stainbank), we are working at pace to ensure that ground remediation, for example, is done at pace so that we can begin to deploy that £200 million. This is co-investment, so it will rest on investors coming forward, but we are working hard to make sure that that happens.
