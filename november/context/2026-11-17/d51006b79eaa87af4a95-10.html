Turn 75 · Florence Eshalomi

The hon. Gentleman is right: we need to ensure that all developers and responsible entities are able to comply, and I will be happy to update him further on that issue in writing. The Bill also includes a reasonable excuse defence that provides further protection where circumstances generally prevent compliance. Whether a responsible entity has a reasonable excuse must depend on all the circumstances of the individual case, including what steps it took to overcome the barrier and what action remained available to protect residents. For those reasons, the Government again cannot accept the amendment, and I urge the hon. Member for Orpington not to press it. I commend clause 12 to the Committee.
