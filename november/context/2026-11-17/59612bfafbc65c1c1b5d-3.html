Turn 18 · Baroness Lloyd of Effra (Lab)

Obviously, we are waiting for the full report from Microsoft, to learn the full details of what caused the outage and the disruption, as well as what mitigants were effective in reducing its impact in the Government and in other services. Once we have looked at that carefully, we will see what lessons can be learned. In respect of the cyber security and resilience Bill more generally, cloud computing services are already within the scope of the NIS regulations, which would include, in respect of Microsoft, some email services if they meet the statutory definition of being scalable and elastic. The cyber security Bill is also bringing managed service providers within its scope.
