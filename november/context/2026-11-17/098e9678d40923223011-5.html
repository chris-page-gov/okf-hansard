Turn 38 · Speaker unlabelled

Just this year, we refreshed the guidance under the healthy child programme and established new high impact areas, which are the public health priorities where health visiting and school nursing can most effectively contribute. This includes guidance on screen time and screen use for children at the 12-month, two-year and two-and-a-half milestones, and digital concerns such as online bullying, screen time and exposure to harmful content. We have published screen use advice for families who have children under five, and we are preparing guidance for five to 18 year-olds and supporting the “In Real Life” trial to test whether limiting social media improves mental health and sleep, because of the many understandable concerns about that. Broadly, what children experience and what they do online can matter as much as how much time they spend online, as noble Lords have said.
