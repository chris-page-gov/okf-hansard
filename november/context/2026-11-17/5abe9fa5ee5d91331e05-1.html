Turn 2 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero (Ms Polly Billington)

The Prime Minister has committed to bring down energy costs so that they are in line Europe within 10 years. Measures announced in the Budget will give families breathing space this winter. In the short term, households in council tax bands A to D are eligible for the £100 household energy payment, and all households will benefit from the zero rate of VAT on electricity bills, which we have extended to July 2027. For constituents in Newcastle upon Tyne Central and West, around 59,000 households will benefit from the energy payment and 17,400 from the warm home discount.

Turn 4 · Ms Billington

My hon. Friend is right: there is an enormous amount of opportunity and consistency in the reliable energy that we can generate, from North sea offshore wind in particular. The National Wealth Fund last week announced two £500 million commitments for each of RWE and KKR’s Vanguard West and Vanguard East wind farms off the coast of Norfolk. That is not in Newcastle, admittedly, but it is another vote of confidence in British home-grown clean energy. The projects combined will deliver 2.8 GW of capacity, which is enough to supply the equivalent of about 3 million UK homes. Of course I am happy to have a meeting with my hon. Friend, particularly to focus on how the local power plan will increase the community benefits and improve the quality of things in her constituency.
