Turn 3 · Dr Chambers

Energy bills have been skyrocketing for the last few years across the whole country and are putting real pressure on households in Winchester. Now that the Chancellor has confirmed the £1 billion cut in capital grant funding for the warm homes plan, thousands of people will struggle to make the adaptations they need—for example, installing heat pumps, solar panels and batteries. That seems fairly short-sighted at a time when were are looking to reduce energy bills and our carbon output, and even in respect of our national security. What impact assessment or review has been done on the impact of the cut in this grant?

Turn 4 · Martin McCluskey

Let me say to the hon. Member what I said to his Front-Bench colleagues: we are committed to delivering the 5 million upgrades that were promised in the warm homes plan, and I am confident we can achieve that, because we have the future homes standards, minimum energy efficiency standards and the warm homes loan scheme, all of which will be supporting people across the country to upgrade their homes this winter. I am confident we can meet that ambition of the warm homes plan, and I am always happy to work with him and his colleagues on achieving it.
