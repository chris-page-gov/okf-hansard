Turn 212 · Speaker unlabelled

Paragraph 30 allows the Secretary of State to give financial assistance to a remediation authority, with the intent to support it until it can recover the costs from the duty holder. It is not our intention to seek the recovery of cladding remediation costs from duty holders where those costs are covered by existing funding schemes. The flexibility provided in paragraph 30 ensures that no one is unduly penalised by the process or unduly profits from it.
