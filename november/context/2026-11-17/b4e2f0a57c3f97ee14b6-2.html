Turn 10 · Lord Roborough (Con)

My Lords, I also thank the Minister for bringing forward these regulations for scrutiny and for her excellent explanation of their purpose. To be classed as persistent organic pollutants, or POPs, substances must meet four criteria: a half-life of more than two months in water or six months in soil; building up in living organisms; demonstrating an ability to travel far from where they are released; and giving evidence of toxicity. The Stockholm convention restricts their use to protect our environment and our health, and we welcome the restrictions on these five POPs in this regulation. Turn 13 · Speaker unlabelled

EU divergence was mentioned by noble Lords. Both the UK and the EU are parties to the Stockholm convention and are subject to the same obligations regarding prohibition of the harmful POPs adopted under the convention. In some instances, this instrument makes amendments that are already in force in the EU and Northern Ireland. For the more recently prohibited POPs, both we and the EU have prepared equivalent draft legislation in parallel to meet the same convention deadline of 16 December 2026. We do not anticipate any significant divergence issues between this instrument applying to Great Britain and the equivalent regulation in the EU and Northern Ireland. Consultation respondents highlighted that greater alignment with the EU and the convention would likely reduce business complexity and trade barriers and provide industry with greater certainty. Turn 14 · Speaker unlabelled

Finally, the noble Lord, Lord Roborough, asked about specific impacts on the NHS or defence. The impact assessment did not expect there to be a significant distributional impact, but also that the provision of the time-limited specific exemptions would help to ensure that industry—whether defence, the NHS or any other—has time to transition away from the use of these substances and would therefore mitigate against any adverse impacts. As I say, for questions that I have not responded to, including if they were particularly technical, I will do so in writing. I commend the draft regulations to the Committee.
