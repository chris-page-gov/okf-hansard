Turn 27 · Dame Diana Johnson

It is a pleasure to serve under your chairmanship, Mr Turner, as my next-door constituency neighbour in the great city of Hull. Earlier this year, the Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026 became law. This landmark piece of legislation will deliver on the Government’s commitment to create a smoke-free generation and tackle youth vaping. As we all know, tobacco is a uniquely harmful product, and smoking still claims about 75,000 lives a year in the United Kingdom. From 1 January 2027, it will be an offence to sell tobacco products, herbal smoking products or cigarette papers to anyone born on or after 1 January 2009, helping to create the UK’s first smoke-free generation. While vapes can be an effective aid for adult smokers to quit smoking, children and non-smokers should never vape or use nicotine products.
