Turn 29 · Justin Madders

I will try to put the shadow Minister’s mind at ease. Clause 72 requires the company, before the granting of a special administration order, to notify the Secretary of State and the ORR of its intention to do that. The Secretary of State and the ORR then have a 14-day window to decide, if necessary, to make an application to court for the special administration order. The process is constructed in that way because we do not want services to be disrupted because of administration or another insolvency event. It is a safeguard to enable the ORR or the Secretary of State to step in to ensure that the highway continues to run in those circumstances. I accept that we are talking about a slightly unusual situation, but it is important to protect the continuity of services, which is why we have done it in that way. Question put and agreed to.
