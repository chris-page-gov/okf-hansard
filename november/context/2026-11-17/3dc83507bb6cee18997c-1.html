Turn 2 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero (Ms Polly Billington)

We are focused on driving down every element of energy bills. We have a strong record of removing levies. We have already moved 75% of the cost of the renewables obligation to the Exchequer and are funding hydrogen projects through public spending rather than consumer bills. No new policy costs will be put on bills until a comprehensive cost control framework is put in place at the spending review in 2027.

Turn 4 · Ms Billington

I mean, honestly! The cheap power plan would add over £1,000 in a homes tax by abandoning the future homes standard. The cheap power plan assumes that the price of gas will be stable and low rather than volatile and high, which would be the reality. The Tories are quite literally gaslighting the British people into thinking that higher bills are the result of clean power policies, when what the Tories would do would end up putting up people’s bills.
