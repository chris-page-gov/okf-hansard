Turn 193 · Pat McFadden

The benefit proposals outlined in the report talk about extra cost benefits for people who could participate more if they had more support, in addition to a local fund for short-term needs, when somebody might require short-term support. There are several levels of support for people with sickness and disability issues recommended in the report, which is right and proper. The emphasis should be on participation and work, where possible, because we want to close the disability employment gap, which has been so stubborn for so many years.

Turn 194 · Lola McEvoy (Darlington) (Lab)

The welfare state must always support those facing extraordinary adversity. It is not, however, a compensation scheme for living through 14 years of the Conservative Government, although I understand why people might feel that they deserve that. I welcome the recommendation from the former Member for Darlington for an opportunity and a right to succeed—the right for people to stand on their own two feet, the right to dignity for young people, the right to earn a living and the right to have that supported. I urge the Secretary of State to get to work with the same determination that the young people of Darlington put into trying to get a job.
