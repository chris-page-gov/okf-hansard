Turn 217 · Lord Scriven (LD)

My Lords, because of the hour, I will be very brief. I thank all noble Lords who have spoken in the debate. We have prodded, poked and tried to get answers. I appreciate that the Minister has given us an overview. To use the Tears for Fears song “Woman in Chains”, I feel that the chains of responsibility are there. To use another Tears for Fears song, we asked her to “Break it Down Again”, but at this moment she is not able to break down exactly what will happen. I understand that it is pragmatic and there may be issues that the Government will use in the special authority, but the key is that there are five months before 1 April when all this has to be in place. As the noble Baroness, Lady Thornton, said on a previous amendment, this is a Bill of moving parts. It has now become a Bill of new parts with great uncertainty. I am sure we will want to return to this before Report, and I hope …
