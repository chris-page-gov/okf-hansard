Turn 55 · Calum Miller

The hon. Gentleman is making a powerful case for why local communities should be given advance warning of both the justification and the likely costs of any large group of people being moved into their community. I have great sympathy for him and other colleagues who suffered that under the last Government, who did not provide them with that notice. He is not, however, providing evidence as to why Opposition Members and the Government will not accept my amendment, which simply asks for that information to be provided. It is not about advocating for one location or another; it simply says that when the Government seek to put more than 300 people into a site, they should have the courtesy to let the local community know what their intentions are.
