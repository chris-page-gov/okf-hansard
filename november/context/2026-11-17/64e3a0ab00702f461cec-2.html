Turn 11 · Speaker unlabelled

I have been reflecting on the Domestic Abuse Act, which quite a number of noble Lords who I see around the Committee were heavily involved in. Your Lordships’ House spent a great deal of time making that Bill as strong as it could be because they wanted to stop abuse. But there is an area of abuse not currently covered by the law: gender conversion practices and, potentially, other conversion practices. If that is not clearly identified, the support is not there for people who have suffered from it. We are not as a society taking action to stop it, and we very clearly should.
