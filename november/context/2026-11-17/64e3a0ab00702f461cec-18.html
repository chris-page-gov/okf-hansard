Turn 142 · Speaker unlabelled

Just before the general election, the previous Conservative Government made a commitment to universal liaison services by 2030. The then shadow Secretary of State for Health and Social Care matched that commitment. This is not a particularly party-political issue when you had the Government of the day and the then Opposition, and the now Government of the day and the now Opposition, making this commitment. It seems one of those issues where successive Governments have been reluctant to spend a little bit of money now to save much more money in the medium or long term, perhaps out of fear that the other side might take the credit. This is one of those issues that takes me back to the work of Buchanan and Tullock on public choice theory, when you take the romance out of politics and look at how politicians act in their own self-interest rather than the public interest.
