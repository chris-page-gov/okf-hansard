Turn 12 · Lord Wood of Anfield (Lab)

I may have to come back if I have got this wrong, and other noble Lords in the House may have a better answer, but I believe that it is approximately 50% through multilateral institutions. However, we are slightly gearing the increase in that amount, partly to compensate for bilateral cuts elsewhere. The effectiveness depends on the programme. There are certain concentrated populations who benefit extensively from focused bilateral support, but there are region-wide economic investment programmes where multilateral economic institutions, such as the development banks, are going to be crucial partners for the UK in pursuing development goals. Unfortunately, there is no single answer to the noble Lord’s question, but we are looking to leverage multilateral economic institutions much more as a result of the way that we have reprofiled our development spend.
