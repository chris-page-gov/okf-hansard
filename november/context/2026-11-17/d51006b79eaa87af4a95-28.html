Turn 219 · Florence Eshalomi

Amendment 45, which was tabled by the hon. Members for Orpington and for Broxbourne, would broaden the grounds on which a connected person can challenge a costs notice, including where the decision was based on an error of law, or was irrational or disproportionate. However, the Government do not consider the amendment necessary or appropriate. The Bill contains specific safeguards before liability can be imposed on connected persons. It requires connected persons to be given an opportunity to make representations on whether joint and several liability would be appropriate before a costs notice is served.
