Turn 9 · The Minister for Energy (Michael Shanks)

In the past two and a half years, we have been very clear that oil and gas will remain a hugely important part of our energy mix, and it is critical that we put in place a credible plan to support that workforce into the jobs of the future, while supporting oil and gas in the short term. The answer is to invest as heavily as we possibly can in renewables, carbon capture, hydrogen and other technologies in order to support them. That is what we have been doing—it is what pragmatism means—and why we made the decision on Jackdaw, as well as announcing record investment in renewables. We have to do both and I hope the hon. Gentleman will support that.
