Turn 49 · Patrick Hurley (Southport) (Lab)

It is paramount to me that Government Members do not write off yet another generation of young people. To that end, this is precisely the welfare reform for which the country has been crying out for years. Its moral clarity and plan of action is worthy of the best Labour Governments of the past. Will the Secretary of State please commit to fast-tracking the coming White Paper so that the taxpayer gets excellent value for money and every young person has a genuine route into employment?

Turn 54 · Pat McFadden

I thank my hon. Friend not only for her question but for the many conversations we have had over the past year. She has been strong and consistent in saying that we should do more to close the disability employment gap, and I fully agree with her. We have done some things so far—she will be aware that I lifted travel restrictions on disability bus passes in conjunction with my right hon.
