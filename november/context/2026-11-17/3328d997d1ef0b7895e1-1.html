Turn 4 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (Baroness Curran) (Lab)

My Lords, future electricity demand is considered as part of the National Energy System Operator’s ongoing long-term strategic energy planning. NESO’s strategic plans aim to ensure that generation will meet future demand, whether from transport, data centres or other parts of the economy. Growing demand from data centres and transport electrification will be met through significant investment in clean generation and grid infrastructure.

Turn 6 · Baroness Curran (Lab)

That is a reasonable question, and I am sure it is one that many people are considering at this moment. The UK, Scottish and Welsh Governments have commissioned the National Energy System Operator to develop a strategic spatial energy plan. This plan will set out locations for power generation and the associated networks needed to meet the Government’s objectives for a secure, affordable, low-carbon power system. In developing the plan, NESO has used the Government’s forecast for future electricity demand and will use this information to inform its work. My noble friend’s general point that there will be increasing demands for energy is right. Data centres are important, as are other demands for electrification, and we need to make sure that we have a managed response to that. NESO will set out those plans.

Turn 7 · Lord Howell of Guildford (Con)

My Lords, the Government are talking about possible blackouts in the future and people are being advised to store up and all the rest.
