Yet GPs often see patients decades younger living with frailty. Frailty means reduced physiological reserve. A relatively minor infection, a fall or a change of medication can tip someone from independent living into hospital. Preventing avoidable deterioration and preserving independence must therefore be central to how we care for people as they age.
