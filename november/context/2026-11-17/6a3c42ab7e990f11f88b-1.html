Turn 3 · Ben Obese-Jecty

I thank the Minister for his recent clarification on my questions on this topic. My concern is that of scale. We have existing wind farms in the local area that use the MM82 turbines, which are around 100 metres tall, so roughly the same height as Big Ben. The proposed Chatteris wind farm would use wind turbines that are 220 metres high, which is just about as tall as any of the buildings in central London, more than twice the height of Big Ben and two thirds the height of the Shard. This is on some of the flattest countryside in the country and the turbines would be much taller—three times the size—than nearby Ely cathedral. What assessment have the Government made of the likely impact of the scale and of the peatland that may see subsidence, as we see with roads in that part of Cambridgeshire?

Turn 4 · Michael Shanks

I am always disappointed when I open my box and there are no written questions from the hon. Gentleman to answer, so I appreciate that he is diligent on these matters and on many others. I appreciate the various comparisons he squeezed into that question as well. The national policy statements are clear that national need for infrastructure does not negate the need for an assessment of local impacts. All those considerations about scale but also about cumulative impact will be taken into account. I hope he will appreciate that I cannot comment on the particular application, but that is what the planning system exists to do.
