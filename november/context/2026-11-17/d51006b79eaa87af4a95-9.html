However, that defence does not excuse avoidable delay or inaction. Responsible entities will be expected to take all reasonable steps to secure funding and progress remediation. Where an entity delays applying for funding without a good reason, that would not in itself provide a reasonable excuse, and enforcement action could still be taken, including civil or criminal penalties. The reasonable excuse defence is therefore a safeguard, not a loophole. It protects those acting responsibly in circumstances that are generally outside their control, while allowing action to be taken against those responsible for avoidable delay or inaction. For those reasons, the Government cannot accept the amendment, and I again urge the hon. Member for Taunton and Wellington not to press it.
