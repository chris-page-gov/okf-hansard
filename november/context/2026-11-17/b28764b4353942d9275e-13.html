Turn 101 · Gareth Bacon

I beg to move amendment 26, in clause 7, page 5, line 10, at end insert— “(6) For the purposes of this section, ‘as soon as reasonably practicable’ shall be determined by reference to whether there were any shortages of— (a) qualified, professional assessors, (b) contractors, or (c) materials in the immediate vicinity of the building in question.”. This amendment would make it clear that “as soon as reasonably practicable” must be see in the context of whether it was possible to obtain the necessary assessors, contractors and materials.

Turn 103 · Gareth Bacon

The Bill rightly puts a legal duty on responsible entities to remediate, yet it establishes no fixed timescale for that. Instead, the Bill relies on the basis that the work must be completed “as soon as reasonably practicable” with the detail left to future regulations. That is despite the fact that the failure to meet deadlines and properly remediate can mean up to two years in prison or a financial penalty of £500,000. That is a serious weight on the mind of responsible people left waiting for further details. With such heavy penalties, we believe that those responsible for remediation must be afforded clarity on what the law expects of them.
