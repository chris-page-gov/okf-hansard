Turn 25 · John Slinger (Rugby) (Lab)

Does my right hon. Friend agree that the concept of responsibility is absolutely vital? Government have a responsibility, as his statement clearly demonstrates, but responsibility also resides with the whole of the state, with businesses, and, yes, with young people too, when the support and opportunity is there. When I met Lincoln Smith, managing director of Custom Heat and Cool in my constituency, he mentioned that his 70-person organisation recommends that it could take on apprentices because it can deal with the HR, the regulation and so on, and then one and two-people businesses or tradespeople could benefit from taking on and using those apprentices. Would my right hon. Friend look at this? Being an active state and enabling young people and businesses will really show that together we can overcome this problem for the long term.

Turn 26 · Pat McFadden

My hon. Friend is right to say that there are responsibilities here. There are responsibilities on us—the Government—to ensure that young people have a chance, and there are responsibilities on young people to take up suitable offers of support. We do need to do more to make things simpler than they are at the moment. I recently met the chief executive of Screwfix, a company that deals with lots of self-employed traders around the country, and he made the plea, quite rightly, to help self-employed plumbers and electricians to take on an apprentice, with us perhaps taking care of the paperwork for them. I think that is a good idea, because many small businesses want to teach people, but they might want someone else to help them with the paperwork.

Turn 30 · Pat McFadden

One of the differences between the situation today and the situation when I was last in government—I have been around for a year or two, Mr Speaker—is that the proportion of young people in the cohort that we are talking about who are suffering from mental health problems is a great deal higher today than it was when I and my right hon. Friend the Health Secretary were Ministers some years ago. It is a very serious point. The Department of Health and Social Care commissioned a review by Professor Peter Fonagy into this.
