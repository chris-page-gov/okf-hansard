Turn 194 · Speaker unlabelled

103: Clause 28, page 20, line 27, at end insert— “(b) after subsection (5) insert—“(5A) The power under subsection (1) may not be used for the purpose of establishing a body that performs functions only or mainly in respect of Wales.””Member’s explanatory statement This spells out that the power in section 28 of the NHS Act 2006 cannot be used to create a Wales-only Special Health Authority. It is needed because of the removal of the territorial restriction in section 271, which limited the exercise of the power to England.

Turn 195 · Speaker unlabelled

104: Clause 28, page 20, line 30, at end insert— “(c) after subsection (2) insert—“(3) In this section a reference to a Special Health Authority is to a Special Health Authority which—(a) performs functions only or mainly in respect of England, or(b) neither performs functions only or mainly in respect of England, nor performs functions only or mainly in respect of Wales.””Member’s explanatory statement This clarifies that the power to make regulations about the delegation of Special Health Authority functions is not exercisable in relation to Wales-only Special Health Authorities. It is needed because of the removal of the territorial restriction in section 271, which limited the exercise of the power to England.
