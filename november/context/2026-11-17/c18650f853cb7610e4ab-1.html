Turn 8 · Martin Wrigley

I agree entirely with my hon. Friend. In fact, I will go one stage further and say that trust is as important as data sovereignty, and right now we do not have a system that people trust. Recent events only reinforce concerns. Yesterday, Tom Watson circulated a letter that appears to many observers to be another example of highly aggressive lobbying. I would appreciate clarification regarding communications and briefings between Palantir and the Government immediately before and after yesterday’s statement. Recent public commentary from Lord Watson has given the impression that the continuation of the contract is somehow guaranteed, but that decision has not yet been made. The Government must be seen to act independently, and the revolving door between the Government and Palantir must be challenged.
