Turn 41 · Justin Madders

Clause 83 is about putting road users at the heart of the new regime. It requires the ORR to establish and maintain a stakeholder group. The purpose of the group is to provide advice to the ORR from the perspective of road users and other interested parties. The group will help ensure that user and stakeholder views are heard as the new regime operates and will be able to advise the ORR on the exercise of its functions under the Bill. The group is advisory. It does not take regulatory decisions and it does not replace the ORR’s responsibilities as the independent regulator, but the ORR must consider the advice it receives from the group, although it is not required to follow that advice. The clause also provides for information to be shared with the group where appropriate, enabling it to carry out its role effectively. Turn 44 · Greg Smith

I am grateful to my hon. Friend. It is almost as if she had leaned over and read the second bullet point in my notes. It is this core point: the public often look at any consultation by any stakeholder body and say, “But what has it really achieved?” Where there is no compulsion to act, follow advice or prove the consideration of any recommendation, the public will rightly ask what precisely the point is of all those hours travelling to meetings—maybe they will be on Zoom or Teams—and the time that good, willing people put into sharing their views. There is nothing, not one provision, that says the recommendations that the actual users—or the representatives of the users of any highway built under the licence regimes put in place by the Bill—make, will take effect.

Turn 47 · Speaker unlabelled

We then come to the spectre of members of the stakeholder group being paid remuneration and allowances by the ORR. Just as we cannot get to the bottom of what any toll will be under the Bill, we do not know what the cost of any remuneration for membership of the stakeholder group under clause 83 will be. We do not know exactly whose budget it will come from—is from the ORR or DFT core budgets? We need clarity from the Minister. Of course, we presume it will always come down to the taxpayer, but will it come from any toll income that the licence holder produces and passes through to the ORR? Clarity and detail are important, and the current wording of the clause does not answer any of those questions.
