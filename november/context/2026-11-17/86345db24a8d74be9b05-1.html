Turn 1 · Rupert Lowe (Great Yarmouth) (Restore Britain)

On a point of order, Madam Deputy Speaker. Yesterday, in an answer to my question on the migrant camp near Piddington, the Minister for Border Security and Asylum incorrectly told the House that through the use of former military sites, illegal migrants are “contained”. That is not true. We know that these overwhelmingly fighting-age illegal migrant men are not contained—far from it. They are entirely free to roam our streets, with no restrictions whatsoever on their movements. I gave the Minister notice that I intended to raise this point of order. Can you advise me how I might ensure that the Minister either corrects the record or, in her own words, contains these men as the British people demand?
