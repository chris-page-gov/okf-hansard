Turn 1 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Transport (Justin Madders)

At the end of last year, my predecessor informed the House that National Highways had identified an anomaly affecting the interaction between some variable speed cameras and signage on the strategic road network in England, originating from the upgrade and installation of the camera system beginning in 2019 under the previous Government. This meant that, on very rare occasions, drivers were not given sufficient time to respond to a change in speed limit or were detected as speeding when the variable speed limit had changed. This error affected cameras on around 0.2% of all roads in England, and immediate steps were taken to prevent any erroneous enforcement and redress any historic enforcement that was incorrect.
