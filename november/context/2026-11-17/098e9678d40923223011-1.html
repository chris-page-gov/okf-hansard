Turn 7 · Baroness Cass (CB)

My Lords, the Government have set themselves the welcome ambition of raising the healthiest generation of children ever, but in this Bill children are not writ large or even small; they are largely absent. I recognise that this is a Bill about structures and systems, rather than specific populations, but past experience shows that health systems are designed around adults, and children need to be the subject of explicit mention in legislation and guidance lest they be forgotten. Turn 8 · Speaker unlabelled

Noble Lords may recall me mentioning the Well Centre at Second Reading. This is a primary care-based centre serving Lambeth and Southwark for young people aged 11 to 20. It can assess problems from acne to anxiety, sexual health, trauma or depression. Dr Lamb, the GP leading the services, has received comments from her young patients such as, “Thank you for always creating a safe space for me to come to”, and “You supported me so much through the toughest of times”. Imagine the impact of services such as this across the NHS, some potentially co-located with young futures hubs. Yet Dr Lamb has to spend her spare time seeking out charitable funds to supplement her work.
