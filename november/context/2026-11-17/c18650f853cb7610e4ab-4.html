Turn 29 · Mr Frith

I thank my hon. Friend for her question. This is a decision to procure anew. It is not about other territories or one supplier and their executives holding a political view. It is a decision that is being taken to go to market for a new version—the next generation of FDP. That process began yesterday in earnest. It is not, as I have said, about where else Palantir supplies or who else it has as customers. It is about maintaining what the NHS makes possible—being able to check in sooner for cancer care, other hospital care or social care. We will build on the successes of the FDP to date. We will procure anew the next generation and ensure exit and transition for March 2029.
