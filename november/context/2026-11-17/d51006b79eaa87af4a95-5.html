Turn 35 · The Chair

I remind the Committee that with this we are discussing the following: Clause stand part. Amendment 6, in clause 8, page 5, line 18, leave out “as soon as reasonably practicable” and insert “before the end of the period of 24 months beginning with the assessment deadline under section 2(3), unless the condition in subsection (2A) is met. (2A) The condition for the purposes of subsection (2) is that a majority of tenants of the building confirm in writing to the responsible entity that they are satisfied that a required remediation step may take place during a period greater than 24 months.” This amendment replaces the subjective “as soon as reasonably practicable” standard for remediation completion with a fixed deadline of 24 months from the assessment deadline, unless tenants agree otherwise.
