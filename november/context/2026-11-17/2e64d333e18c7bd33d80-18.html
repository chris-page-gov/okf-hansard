Turn 138 · Anna Turley

I will make some progress; I have a huge amount to get through. The hon. Member for Westmorland and Lonsdale (Tim Farron) tabled amendment 78, which relates to refugee family reunion. The Government recognise the importance of family unity, but we have a duty to ensure that communities are not placed under unsustainable pressure. As the House will know, the refugee family reunion route, which had minimal requirements, was suspended in September 2025 in response to the severe pressures on local authorities. The amendment would pre-empt our ongoing work on the future framework, with further details to be announced in due course. I am afraid we cannot support an amendment that would prescribe the fundamental features of future refugee family reunion measures in primary legislation, as it would remove the flexibility needed to maintain a coherent immigration system.
