Turn 51 · Olly Glover (Didcot and Wantage) (LD)

It is a pleasure to serve under your chairship on this Committee for the final time, Mr Wishart. New clause 5 is a remarkably sensible amendment from the shadow Minister—I have not always felt able to say similar during these proceedings. The hon. Member for Mid Buckinghamshire raises a very good point: the Government are taking a totally inconsistent approach to the role of the Office of Rail and Road. This Bill is strengthening its role to ensure that things work sensibly and appropriately in relation to private toll roads, but for the railway the Government have significantly denuded the ORR’s powers, presumably because Great British Railways will eventually do more than display a livery of rather dubious, garish colours. I hope the Minister can explain the logic for why the ORR is being treated so differently in those two sectors.

Turn 56 · Speaker unlabelled

I gently take issue with the restriction to just charities and small businesses in new clause 6. Charities and small businesses are fantastic, wonderful and marvellous things that should not be negatively impacted by a decision made by the state or anyone else, but so are many other organisations in our communities. In the spirit of reaching out with an olive branch, I hope that the new clause can be improved for future rounds of this Bill, when we return to the Chamber on Report or when it goes to the other place, to recognise a much broader scope of institutions. If the supply chains of medium-sized businesses or even large businesses are completely cut off, it is not reasonable that they should just suck up a cost that is not their fault and impacts their businesses so much. That is also the case for the other institutions I have spoken about.
