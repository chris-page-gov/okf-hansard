Turn 13 · Pippa Heylings (South Cambridgeshire) (LD)

The Liberal Democrats welcome the Minister’s work in this area and her recognition that rural households off the gas grid that rely on expensive heating oil and LPG are already particularly exposed to volatile fossil fuel prices. However, from next April, they face a double blow: the end of the enhanced boiler upgrade scheme grant and the reintroduction of VAT on energy-saving materials such as heat pumps, batteries and solar panels. Our analysis shows that a household installing a heat pump, 4 kW solar array and battery could face an additional £1,530 in costs. It seems bonkers that the Government are reintroducing a solar and heat pump tax at the same time as paying households to adopt the technology. Does the Minister agree with the Liberal Democrats that, to help rural off-gas-grid homes to get off volatile fossil fuel prices, we should reverse those measures?
