Turn 47 · Baroness Cass (CB)

My Lords, my Amendment 94A seeks to require every integrated care board to appoint an executive lead for children and young people aged from nought to 25. We made the case for this very strongly in the group on children earlier. Although this is a statutory role currently under NHS England, the NSPCC did an audit and found that implementation was very patchy. Large numbers of ICBs did not have such a lead or, if they did, they had this role as part of a very wide range of other duties and there was variable expertise and backgrounds in those undertaking it. With the reducing numbers in ICBs, there is a significant risk of children and young people getting lost. That is why I propose that this should go into the Bill.
