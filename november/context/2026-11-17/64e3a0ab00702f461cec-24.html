Turn 185 · Baroness Tyler of Enfield (LD)

My Lords, the hour is late, so I shall keep it as brief as I can. I thank the Minister for the responses she has given, the questions she has answered and some of the explanations she has given, because I found them helpful. I am left, though, with the feeling—I think it was something the noble Earl, Lord Howe, said—that it really is not that straightforward. It all feels quite complicated. I am left with a sense that there is still more thinking to be done about how we are going to get this absolutely right so that these local neighbourhood plans—which are so important and need to be fully joined up, with all the right people involved, all the people who need to have a seat around the table and their voice heard—really are place-based and looking at population needs and how it all fits together. There is a lot to do and a lot to think through. I feel that we need to have another look at …
