Turn 2 · The Minister for Energy (Michael Shanks)

We will need a range of technologies to build the clean power system that will get us off fossil fuels. We are backing emerging renewable technology through innovation and investments via our publicly owned energy company, Great British Energy, which will create skilled jobs and strengthen domestic supply chains. That includes investing in floating offshore wind projects including Pentland in Scotland and Erebus in the Celtic sea.

Turn 4 · Michael Shanks

My hon. Friend is right to say that affordability will be absolutely central to the decisions that we make in allocation round 8 and to other decisions we make when it comes to investing in technologies. We have also said that through the National Wealth Fund and Great British Energy, we will continue to support the deployment of clean energy, because it is the only answer that will get us off fossil fuels, and protect bill payers in the long term and the industrialisation that is so important in communities across the country. By taking that funding alongside our continued commitment to deploy clean power, we will make sure that we have jobs in every single part of the country and the power that will get us off fossil fuels.

Turn 6 · Michael Shanks

I am really glad to hear Conservative Members championing renewables; it is really good to hear that. I hope that we can rebuild that consensus across the House. I agree with the right hon. Lady that renewables are hugely important. Of course, the great grid upgrade was started by the Conservatives, including the important transmission infrastructure that we need. The problem was that they left it too late, and that is why important projects off the east of England are not connected and delivering cheaper power to homes and businesses across the country. I am really interested in the idea of offshore co-ordinated assets. We agreed with European neighbours to look at much more offshore co-ordination—I think it is the long-term answer—but we also have to build the grid here in the UK to get the power to where it is needed.

Turn 8 · Michael Shanks

My hon. Friend is a great champion for her constituency and for the Celtic sea more generally, as she is for the important role that technologies will play in reindustrialisation and in the energy mix. I will not be drawn on what will or will not come in AR8. We will say more on the budget later this week and then the process will have to run its course. Of course, in AR7 we got the first Welsh project through in more than decade. The Erebus project is also one of the first test and demo projects of floating offshore wind in the country, and it is in the Celtic sea. We remain committed to deploying those technologies, but I will say what I have said frequently before: that technology has to come at an affordable price to make sure that we are also bringing down bills for consumers.
