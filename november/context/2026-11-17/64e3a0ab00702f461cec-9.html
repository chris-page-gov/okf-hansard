Turn 72 · Speaker unlabelled

This amendment would require ICBs to plan services with people and organisations delivering them. It is a co-creation process. It would require them to consider how commissioning decisions affect continuity of care and the long-term provision of NHS services. It would encourage NHS organisations to work together rather than unnecessarily splitting the provision between different providers. That last point is particularly important. Commissioning decisions are not simply administrative exercises. They shape the services available to patients, the relationships between professionals and the ability of local communities to access care. A decision that looks efficient on paper, just on the numbers, may have very different consequences when it breaks up an established service or makes it harder for patients to move between different parts of the health system.
