Turn 17 · Baroness Blake of Leeds (Lab)

I thank the noble Baroness for giving me the opportunity to talk about the scrutiny and oversight board that will be set up. It will go through the due process and will be independent. Obviously, we are mindful of the whole range of genuine reasons why people pay late and receive parking tickets. It is imperative that we get fairness into the system, so that it is open, transparent and, most importantly, as she says, independently verified as to the data that will have to be shared with it.
