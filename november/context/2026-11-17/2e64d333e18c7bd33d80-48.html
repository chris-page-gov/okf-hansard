Turn 377 · Speaker unlabelled

This amendment would repeal some uncommenced provisions in section 59 of the Illegal Migration Act 2023. Those provisions extend the duty in section 80A of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 to declare inadmissible asylum claims made by persons from safe countries so that it also applies to human rights claims.
