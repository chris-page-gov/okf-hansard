Turn 5 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government (Baroness Blake of Leeds) (Lab)

Goodness me, what a start to proceedings—I cannot follow that. The private parking code of practice represents the first step in strengthening protections for motorists across England, Scotland and Wales. Subject to parliamentary scrutiny, the Government intend to issue the code so that its protections can be implemented without delay. Next year, we intend to consult on the remaining Competition and Markets Authority recommendations and on debt recovery fees. Further details on the scope and timing of that consultation will be set out in due course.

Turn 7 · Baroness Blake of Leeds (Lab)

The noble Lord raises a very important point. It is always useful to recognise the history of the track of legislation; I am sure he enjoyed giving us that view. The code of practice that we intend to bring in is under scrutiny in Parliament at the moment. Assuming that there are no objections in either House, that period will end on 30 November. There will then be 21 days to bring in that part of the code of practice. There is a statutory duty on the Secretary of State to consult, and it is important to do that. We are doing this now and then moving swiftly into the next stage of consultation to bring in the rest of the actions, as the noble Lord desires.
