Turn 81 · Pat McFadden

I say the same thing to the hon. Lady as I have said to other Members who have asked a similar question: we have a higher ambition for people than a life on benefits. Let me remind her of the striking figure in Charlie Mayfield’s report: that a young person signing on for long-term benefits could end up losing £1 million of earnings across their lifetime. This is an offer for a better future. That is what young people have been waiting for and what they need, and I want to get on and implement it.

Turn 87 · Pat McFadden

My hon. Friend is right to say that the north-east has the highest rate in the country. That shows that what we are tackling is not just an opportunity problem, but an equality problem. On hidden NEETs, the truth is that we know less about them because they are not on the benefit books or the sickness benefit books. Some of them may be able to look after themselves—they are not signing on and can perhaps get work—but Alan Milburn is clear in his report that at least some of the support offer should be available to people outside the benefits system.
