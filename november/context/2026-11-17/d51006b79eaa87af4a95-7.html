Clause 9 will introduce a practical mechanism for remediation recommendations to be revised. This power is to be used where a suitable professional or Homes England determines that implementing the original recommendations would not result in the external walls being properly remediated. The clause recognises that in some cases new risks or issues may become apparent only once remediation works have commenced on site. Where that occurs, the original recommendations may no longer be sufficient to achieve proper remediation and will therefore need to be updated.
