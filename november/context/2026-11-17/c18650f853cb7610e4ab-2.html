Turn 16 · Martin Wrigley

We have seen that Palantir has been authorised to access such data, which was previously unknown and undeclared. My point is not that Palantir’s political opinions should determine procurement outcomes, but that scrutiny should be applied consistently. To replace the current approach, I believe that we need an independent NHS design authority to control the architecture of the future FDP, rather than it being controlled by the supplier; multiple suppliers, rather than a single controlling company; and open, common standards—which the Minister talked about in his statement on Monday, and which I fully support—that should be interoperable by design, so that we can take out pieces and plug in another company to replace them.
