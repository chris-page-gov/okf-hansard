This amendment removes the subjective "intolerable risk" assessment threshold in Clause 3(1)(b). It requires external wall assessors to certify whether external walls meet the objective combustibility standards of Building Regulation 7(2), regardless of height limitations in Regulation 7(4), or otherwise recommend remediation to achieve that standard.
