I now come to the point about what the maximum exposure to the taxpayer could actually be, including contingent liabilities such as guarantees and indemnities, and how any use of clause 85 would be reported back to Parliament so that Members of this House, and indeed the other place, could properly scrutinise it. If it cannot be published in draft, what confidence can any of us have when we come to support the noble aims of the Bill—not least getting the lower Thames crossing built—with a cliffhanger like this in part 6?

Turn 64 · The Chair

With this it will be convenient to discuss: Government amendment 20. Amendment 83, in clause 88, page 60, line 29, at end insert— “(4) Collection and enforcement regulations which provide for penalty charges must provide that, where the vehicle was at the relevant time let under a hiring or leasing agreement, the charge is payable by the hirer or lessee.” This amendment would require liability for unpaid toll penalty charges to be transferred from the registered keeper to the hirer or lessee where a vehicle is subject to a hiring or leasing agreement.
