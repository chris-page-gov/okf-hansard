Turn 227 · Speaker unlabelled

This amendment would mean that certain decisions of the IIAA cannot be appealed to the Upper Tribunal. The excluded decisions are: decisions of the IIAA about reviews of its previous decisions; decisions on immigration bail; procedural, ancillary and preliminary decisions; and decisions that have been set aside. The Secretary of State may by regulations add additional types of excluded decisions.
