Turn 25 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Transport (Justin Madders)

On a point of order, Mr Wishart. Fortunately, over lunchtime the lawyers have been dissecting my comments from this morning. I have been advised to make a couple of clarifications. First, clause 72 deals with a company needing to notify the Secretary of State or the ORR before the court issues permission for a voluntary winding up, not giving notice before applying for a highways administration order. I hope that is clear. Secondly, on clause 85, I need to be clear that the special administration regime is not to be used for day-to-day operations. Financial assistance under the power can be used to support the licence holder if necessary and in extraordinary circumstances.
