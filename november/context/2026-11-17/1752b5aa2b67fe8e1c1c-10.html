Turn 79 · Pat McFadden

My hon. Friend is right; this is a different approach. He is also right that cross-Government working is needed. Let me be candid with the House: if it is just the Department for Work and Pensions that is involved, it will not happen. It will happen only if the Government work together across the piece. Historically, that has not always been the easiest thing for Departments to do, and that is one reason why we are keen to have more localised delivery, rather than just national Government programmes. That is what the new youth opportunities unit will be charged with overseeing. If we get agreement on the legislation, there is a critical delivery job to be done. That is why the unit has been set up and why it has an important role to play over the next few years.
