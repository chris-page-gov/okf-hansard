Turn 2 · The Minister for Climate Transition (Katie White)

The climate crisis is a threat to our national security, our economic stability, and the safety of people in Britain and around the world. That is why this week the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero is leading negotiations in Turkey at COP31. That comes alongside the Prime Minister’s announcement of two major offshore wind projects, creating hundreds of jobs in the east of England, and securing our energy future. Britain has also agreed to increase electricity’s share of global energy consumption to 35% by 2035, accelerating the global shift to clean electricity and appointing a new special representative for climate security. Every step we take to tackle the climate crisis is a step towards greater energy independence, stronger national security, and hope for future generations.

Turn 6 · The Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero (Ms Polly Billington)
