Turn 26 · Speaker unlabelled

There is real concern in society about “transing away the gay”. I cannot emphasise enough the wisdom of the noble Baroness, Lady Cass, in drawing the distinction between sexual orientation and gender dysphoria and how much I agree with her concern. We have seen instances in the US, in Bible Belt areas, where parents are posting, due to their extreme evangelical faith, how pleased they are that their effeminate gay boy child is now the little girl they always should have had, because their own religious beliefs could not accommodate the child being gay. This is an incredibly worrying area and of course we are all concerned, and no one would disagree that coercive practices should be abolished. At the same time, there are extremely vulnerable, at-risk children who need adequate care and to be protected from the unlawful, improper and excessive prescription of these hormones.
