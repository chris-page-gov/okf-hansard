Turn 47 · Speaker unlabelled

Setting the assessment standards through regulations also means that we can keep the requirements up to date as best practice develops. Where an assessment identifies an intolerable risk, clause 3 requires us to set out a recommendation for the remediation work required, as well as any interim measures needed to manage the risk while work is under way. Those recommendations provide the basis for action under the legal duties to mitigate and remediate. That is why getting the assessment right matters. Clause 3 will help to deliver a consistent, proportionate and evidence-based approach to assessing external walls.

Turn 48 · Speaker unlabelled

Clause 4 ensures that the assessment duty is targeted appropriately. Buildings that need a detailed external wall assessment should receive one. For those who have already carried out suitable assessments, there should be no requirement for reassessment. The clause sets out two circumstances in which a responsible entity will not need to carry out a new fire risk assessment of external walls: first, where a risk assessment carried out during the assessment period confirms that no separate examination of the external walls is needed; and secondly, where a fire risk assessment of external walls has already been carried out during the assessment period and meets the Bill’s requirements for a consistent assessment, including relevant requirements relating to PAS 9980 methodology.
