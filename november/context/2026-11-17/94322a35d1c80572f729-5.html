Turn 36 · Justin Madders

That is probably a question for the shadow Minister, but I can answer for him and say that the Conservatives have absolutely no idea how to run a big construction project, as their 14 years in government showed. The point is that setting it up in this way has allowed delivery partners flexibility and encouraged innovation in how carbon reduction could most effectively and efficiently be achieved. For a long time, a significant proportion of the construction industry has included the use of alternative fuels in its bids as part of its own net zero plans. The new clause is not appropriate: it imposes a level of prescription that is not necessary in what is a framework Bill about a financial model, and it is, of course, based on a completely false premise.
