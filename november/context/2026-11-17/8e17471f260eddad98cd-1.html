Turn 1 · The Minister for Security (Dan Jarvis)

My noble Friend the Minister of State, Home Office, Lord Hanson of Flint, has today made the following written ministerial statement: It has been just over a year since the publication of the second telecommunications fraud charter, a landmark agreement between the Government and the telecommunications sector to strengthen collective action against fraud. As set out in the fraud strategy, the Home Office has committed to report to Parliament on the delivery of the charter every six months until the end of 2027. One year on from publication, and following the initial update in May, I am pleased to report strong progress across both sections of the charter, with the vast majority of commitments delivered or on track.
