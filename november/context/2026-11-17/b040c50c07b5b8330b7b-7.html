Turn 55 · Justin Madders

Clause 85 allows the Secretary of State to provide financial assistance. Assistance may be provided to enable a licence holder to carry out activities under its licence. It may also be provided where a highways administration order is in force, to help achieve the objective of the highways administration. The assistance may take different forms, including grants, loans, guarantees, indemnities, contracts or investment in a company, and may include acquiring an undertaking or asset, or incurring expenditure for the licence holder’s benefit. The Secretary of State may attach appropriate terms to the assistance, which may include repayment conditions, with or without interest, or another return. The power is additional to, and does not replace or restrict, any other power of a Minister of the Crown to provide financial assistance.
