Turn 121 · Florence Eshalomi

I thank the Committee for its consideration of the clauses. I will now address amendments 35 to 37. Amendment 35, tabled by the hon. Member for Orpington, would make waiting for an audit an explicit reasonable excuse for failing to co-operate with the audit process. However, waiting for an audit does not remove the need to co-operate with it. Homes England may need information or other co-operation before an audit can begin or while an audit is under way. Clause 19 will therefore make it an offence to intentionally fail to comply with the requirement to co-operate with an audit. That will prevent someone from deliberately withholding information or refusing to co-operate, which could delay the audit and hold up remediation.
