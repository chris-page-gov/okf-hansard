Turn 209 · Speaker unlabelled

This amendment would not stop the abolition of NHS England. It asks the Government to tell Parliament what this body will do, what its powers are, what it costs, who answers for it, and to come back to this Parliament if it lasts beyond two years. If it is truly temporary, that will cost the Government nothing to answer. If it is not, they should stop calling it temporary and give Parliament a greater understanding of what is happening. I beg to move.
