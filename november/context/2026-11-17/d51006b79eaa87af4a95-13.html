Turn 103 · Speaker unlabelled

The powers in these clauses enable regulators to impose financial penalties directly, without first having to serve a statutory notice or pursue a criminal prosecution. To issue the penalty, enforcement authorities must be satisfied beyond reasonable doubt that the responsible entity has committed an offence. The duty holder retains the ability to rely on the statutory reasonable excuse defence where appropriate. These powers give regulators a more immediate remedy, which may be a more proportionate route in some cases to secure compliance where duties have not been met. I commend clauses 13, 16 and 17 to the Committee.
