Turn 126 · Pat McFadden

I do not know whether the hon. Member was in the Chamber for my opening statement, but I very explicitly rejected the demonisation of young people and the negative narrative out there about them—that is a narrative I completely reject. Fonagy, in his review, does talk about the diagnosis debate but, as I have said a couple of times, he also said that work can play a critical role in someone’s recovery—not just after they have recovered, but in the recovery, so he is in line with the recommendations of the Milburn report. The equation that I want to break is between a diagnosis and a conclusion that that person then has to spend their life on benefits. Frankly, I have a much higher ambition for young people than that.
