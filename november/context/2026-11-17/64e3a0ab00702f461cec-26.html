Fifthly, where will the staff go at the end? According to my reading of Clause 29 of this Bill, they can be moved permanently only to another special health authority or an ICB. They can be lent to the department on a ministerial direction, but they would stay on the authority’s payroll, so the department gets the people, and the savings figures do not have to count them.
