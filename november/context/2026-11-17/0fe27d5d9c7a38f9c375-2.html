Turn 9 · Pippa Heylings (South Cambridgeshire) (LD)

We have heard compellingly from my hon. Friends on the Liberal Democrat Benches about the impact of the £1 billion cut to the warm homes plan, but it was not the only cut to the plan. In the Budget, the Government managed simultaneously to launch and to raid the warm homes loan scheme fund. The original £5 billion fund was meant to help drive electrification, enabling households across the country to do their bit by overcoming the up-front costs of installing heat pumps, batteries and solar panels through zero-interest and low-interest loans. I have asked repeatedly for transparency: how much of the financial transactions part of that £5 billion is now being diverted, and how much will actually remain available to help households to cut their energy bills and get off fossil fuels?

Turn 10 · Martin McCluskey

I would say to the hon. Lady not to talk down the warm homes fund. We have already seen significant demand for the warm homes loan scheme. The Money Co-op, which is one of the providers, has already suspended its applications because the demand was so overwhelming in the first couple of days. There are still lots of other lenders—[Interruption.] I say to the Lib Dems who are chuntering from a sedentary position that people can still apply to the eight other lenders that are still open. The warm homes loan scheme is fully funded, and we remain totally committed to it. The scheme will provide significant amounts of funding for people to upgrade their homes with heat pumps, solar and batteries.
