Turn 9 · Graham Leadbitter (Moray West, Nairn and Strathspey) (SNP)

That was backed up later in the day by the Under-Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero, the hon. Member for Inverclyde and Renfrewshire West (Martin McCluskey), who stated it in a post on X. Will the Minister confirm exactly which project off the east coast of Scotland is getting that £1 billion?
