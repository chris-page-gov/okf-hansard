Turn 6 · Blair McDougall

My hon. Friend makes an important point. BICS will support lots of small businesses as well, but we are clear—the Prime Minister has said this—that as part of the 10-year plan, we want to get to a situation in which all businesses’ energy bills are much more comparable to European averages. As well as subsidising energy bills, we need to move faster to support small businesses with, for example, electrification, so that they can become more energy efficient and reduce costs in the first place.
