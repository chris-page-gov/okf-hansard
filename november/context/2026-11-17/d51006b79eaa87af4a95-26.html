Turn 206 · Florence Eshalomi

The clause and schedule 1 provide for remediation management orders. We expect the majority of responsible entities to comply with the legal duty to remediate. Where they do not, fines and prosecution offer a robust penalty. However, a duty holder may still choose not to remediate their building, continuing to place residents at risk. Right now, regulators lack an effective way to get buildings remediated where the duty holder is simply unwilling or unable to do so. Existing legislation with step-in rights is designed for smaller interventions, such as fixing broken boilers. It does not give authorities adequate powers to remediate at scale and in buildings with complex management and ownership structures.
