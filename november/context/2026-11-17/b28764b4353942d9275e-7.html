Turn 49 · Speaker unlabelled

The assessment period begins on 16 May 2022, when the Fire Safety Act 2021 came into force and clarified that fire risk assessments must consider external walls. It will end 10 months after clause 2 comes into force, providing a greater and clear deadline for any outstanding assessments. Buildings previously assessed under this consolidated advice note will not need a new external wall assessment as a matter of course. An earlier assessment can be relied on when it meets the conditions for an exception to the assessment duty. However, if a fire risk assessment carried out during the assessment period shows that further external wall assessment is needed, it must be carried out.
