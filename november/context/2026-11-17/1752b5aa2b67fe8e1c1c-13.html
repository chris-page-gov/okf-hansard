Turn 100 · Monica Harding (Esher and Walton) (LD)

We are trying to tilt apprenticeship funding more towards young people, including through a hiring incentive of £2,000 for a small business that takes on a young apprentice; if the young person has been unemployed for six months or more, the hiring incentive goes up to £5,000.

I am a passionate believer that those can be a critical bridge between unemployment and employment. Once somebody has a few weeks of the routine, the experience and all the rest, they are much more employable candidates. Of course, the work experience has to be of the right quality, but it can play a really important role.
