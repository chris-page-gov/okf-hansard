Turn 153 · Carla Lockhart (Upper Bann) (Ind)

Around 24,000 young people in Northern Ireland are outside of education, employment or training. If the Secretary of State is to introduce the Milburn proposals, will he confirm that the new tax relief for employers will apply across the whole of the United Kingdom? What funding will Northern Ireland receive for apprenticeships and the additional mental health support that the report recommends? I want young people in Upper Bann to have exactly the same opportunities as those in England.

What discussions has the Secretary of State had with the Northern Ireland Executive to secure fair Barnett consequentials for apprenticeships and mental health support, to ensure that vulnerable young people are not penalised by welfare changes before local jobs are created?
