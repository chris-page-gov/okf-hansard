Turn 92 · Speaker unlabelled

We are here to help strengthen this Bill, as I have said many times during the sittings of this Committee. We are not opposed to the core principle of the Bill, and we want it to achieve Royal Assent—but in a way that works for motorists and road users and gets free tolling in operation on the M6, the Mersey tunnel and the existing Dartford crossings as they already have it, as well as on the lower Thames crossing. We do not oppose the Bill; this amendment is trying not to frustrate the Bill, but to strengthen it. Surely the Minister sees that the Lord Chancellor must, not just “may”, make these regulations?
