Turn 169 · Speaker unlabelled

I welcome the Government’s decision to think again about the abolition of Healthwatch. It would have moved the NHS further away from connecting meaningfully with local communities. However, in the context of the shift from hospital communities, simply retaining Healthwatch will not be enough. This is what Amendment 100 seeks to address, through mandating a focus on the wider determinants of health in neighbourhood health plans and ensuring that local people are meaningfully involved.

Turn 175 · Speaker unlabelled

So far, so much simpler—apparently; but we then need to look at the new role being given to ICBs. We know that ICBs are being reduced in number to cover a much wider footprint than they do at present. We are also told that one of their jobs will be to produce a population health improvement plan. As an ICB will typically cover an area that envelops several local authorities, each population health improvement plan will need to take account of multiple joint strategic needs assessments and neighbourhood health plans. In other words, just like in the current system, there will need to be a constant process of alignment and reconciliation between the different plans.
