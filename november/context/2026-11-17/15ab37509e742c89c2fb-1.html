Turn 1 · The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions (Pat McFadden)

Alan Milburn has today published the final report of his review into young people and work. I want to thank Alan and his review team for their outstanding work over the last year, and for the landmark report they have published. The Government strongly endorse the need for a new settlement for young people, as set out in the report. It will consider the report in full alongside the recommendations of the Timms review, which are expected shortly. I commissioned this report in November 2025 to understand the root causes of a national youth participation crisis that has been building for many years and has left nearly 1 million young people outside of education, employment or training. The report sets out the scale and causes of that crisis, and makes recommendations for how the Government, and others beyond, should respond. This Government have already begun that work.
