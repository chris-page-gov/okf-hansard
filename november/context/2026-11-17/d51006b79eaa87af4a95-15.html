Turn 113 · Florence Eshalomi

I will now speak to clauses 18 to 21. Clause 18 puts independent auditing of fire risk assessments of external walls into law. Under part 1 of the Bill, those assessments determine what action is needed to address external fire safety risks. It is critical that their recommendations are robust and properly evidenced, and that residents, responsible entities and regulators can have confidence in the decisions that follow. Homes England will lead the audit process, building on its proven track record of independent oversight across Government remediation programmes. Every assessment under clause 2 that recommends interim measures or remediation works will be independently audited. Audits will provide a clear view of the remediation that a building needs, reducing uncertainty and supporting more consistent decisions.
