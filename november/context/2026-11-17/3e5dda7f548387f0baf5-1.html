Turn 3 · Kim Leadbeater

We have many brilliant sports clubs across Spen Valley, but like many organisations they have felt the impact of rising energy bills. Clubs have taken innovative action themselves, such as Mirfield Parish cricket club fundraising for solar panels and many others in Battyeford, Scholes, Birstall, Cleckheaton and East Bierley working hard to bring costs down. However, given that these clubs are at the heart of all our communities, what are the Government doing to support them with rising costs? Will the Minister visit Spen Valley to see for himself the invaluable role that sport plays in my constituency and why it is so important?

Turn 6 · Blair McDougall

The hon. Gentleman is right. We often talk about the industrial cost of high energy prices and the opportunities lost through that, but it is important that we also talk about the opportunities lost because of the cost to sports clubs. A lot of national sports bodies that distribute lottery funds and Government funding also recognise that challenge, and they have schemes in place to support more energy efficient lights, solar panels on roofs, and that sort of intervention.
