Turn 44 · Baroness Penn (Con)

On my amendments, I appreciate that children’s digital environment can be addressed in the existing frameworks, but my question is whether it is indeed being addressed through existing frameworks. The Minister referred to guidance and training, and I would be grateful to hear more detail on that before Report, either through writing or engagement. On the child health programme, which the Minister acknowledged was crucial, as I said in my speech, there is a reference in those two reviews to screen time and healthy routines, but that was it, as far as I could find.
