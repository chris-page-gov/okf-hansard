Turn 112 · Speaker unlabelled

I remind noble Lords of a simple statistic: 80% of offending is reoffending. If we want to solve our prison crisis, it is vital that we try to reduce reoffending. Improving the healthcare of people—whether they are in prison, moving into the Probation Service’s control or whatever—is vital. The Minister accepted that those people are currently disadvantaged. The level of care and support in our prisons and other parts of the criminal justice system is, frankly, woeful. Reoffending costs this country a staggering £22.7 billion every year. If we start to reduce it and provide better treatment, real savings will come.
