My Lords, I will also speak to the North-East Atlantic Fisheries Commission (Immunities and Privileges) Order. NASCO and NEAFC are both important international organisations through which the UK works with other countries to conserve and sustainably manage fish stocks in the North Atlantic. Fish stocks, of course, do not respect national boundaries, making international co-operation essential to their effective management. Through these organisations, countries work together to agree conservation and management measures, informed by the best available scientific advice, which supports sustainable fisheries and the marine environment. Turn 8 · Speaker unlabelled

Is there an opportunity for the Centre for Environment, Fisheries and Aquaculture Science to do more in support of NASCO research and the adoption of NASCO recommendations? Will the Minister consider working more closely with the Norwegian Government and NASCO in funding greater research into the incidence of salmon by-catch in the pelagic fishery? The last studies were completed over 23 years ago and showed a meaningful by-catch of as many as 1 million post-smolt salmonids per annum. Since then, the evidence is that the pelagic fishery has moved north and is now co-located with our migratory salmon. The risk is that there is much greater by-catch now than there was then, and it is urgent that this research is brought up to date.
