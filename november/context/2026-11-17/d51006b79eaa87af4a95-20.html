Turn 158 · Gareth Bacon

As we discussed previously, building safety directions are too critical to forgo proper scrutiny; there must be parliamentary oversight, especially of the directions that the clause affords the Secretary of State the power to make. Ministerial direction of the magnitude offered in clause 27 is simply too great to be considered behind closed doors in Marsham Street. That is why I tabled amendment 39, which requires any directions given by the Secretary of State to be laid before Parliament. The amendment would ensure that directions reach clear and transparent conclusions and are subject to much greater public accountability. That is a simple and logical course of action for such powers, and I hope that hon. Members will agree.
