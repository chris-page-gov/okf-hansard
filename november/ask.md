# Ask your AI about Hansard

19,715 contributions from Commons and Lords sittings, 2 November 2026 to 25 November 2026. Historical searches for configured entities cover 1 January 2007 to 1 November 2026 inclusive. The older indexed archive spans 24 February 2003 to 19 December 2006 and lists 0 source gaps. Counts of historical matches depend on the configured names and aliases.

## Use the full collection

Paste the following instruction into your assistant once. If your assistant supports saved preferences, you can ask it to remember the shortcut. Otherwise paste it in each new chat. Switch to voice and say “chat to GTM Hansard”.

> When I say ‘chat to GTM Hansard’, use the approved Hansard connection and the read-okf-plus skill if available. Otherwise open https://chris-page-gov.github.io/okf-hansard/november/hansard.json. Check the verified snapshot and latest sitting before answering. Use my context to choose public topics and send only the terms and dates needed. Ask if my portfolio is unclear. Give a short spoken answer and put the exact Hansard links and a graph or timeline link in chat. If you cannot read the collection, try https://github.com/chris-page-gov/okf-hansard/blob/main/HANSARD-EVIDENCE.md or ask me for pasted evidence. Explain the scope of the evidence you could access.

Try asking “What changed in the discussion of the Synergy programme? Show the source passages and give me the timeline.”

## Use the small evidence index

When the assistant cannot open the full collection, paste this prompt. If it cannot open GitHub either, paste the selected evidence from the finder.

> Read HANSARD-EVIDENCE.md directly from the main branch of chris-page-gov/okf-hansard. https://github.com/chris-page-gov/okf-hansard/blob/main/HANSARD-EVIDENCE.md
> Give the verified capture-through date and snapshot from the file. Use the excerpts and exact Hansard links in the file to answer. If you cannot open it, ask me to paste its text.
> Explain what Synergy is, how the discussion changed, and which statements were commitments. Distinguish historical alias matches from captured contributions.
