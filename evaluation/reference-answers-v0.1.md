### P1-Q2 — Current EHCP rights versus proposals
**Provisional answer:** Existing statutory EHCP-related appeal rights arise under the Children and Families Act 2014, notably section 51, with tribunal jurisdiction and procedural rules. Individual Support Plans described in the November debate are proposals; a ministerial statement is not enacted legislation. A full gold answer must enumerate appealable decisions, relevant exclusions/limits, current law as at the evaluation date, and the precise Government consultation proposals. **Do not treat the MP's characterisation of the proposals as the authoritative final legal text.**
