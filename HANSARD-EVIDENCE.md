## Worked example — Synergy programme

17 distinct retained Hansard links. 6 captured contributions and 11 historical alias matches.

Programme and contract

Synergy is the Department for Work and Pensions-led shared services programme for DWP, the Ministry of Justice, the Home Office and Defra. It covers functions including finance, HR, payroll and purchasing. DWP's published business case gives a maximum potential value of £873.4 million for Capita's Business Process Services contract, including optional services, over up to ten years. This figure covers that contract. The technology platform is a separate procurement.
Source https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/8-april-2026-synergy-programme-summary-business-case/8-april-2026-synergy-programme-summary-business-case

How the parliamentary discussion developed

- On 26 October 2026, during the Commons Civil Service Pensions debate, John McDonnell asked about bringing outsourced work in-house. Sally Jameson said the Synergy contract fell within DWP's remit. Her response is linked among the earlier matches below.
- On 17 November 2026 in Westminster Hall, Andy McDonald urged the Government to review outsourcing and assess whether it could bring the services in-house. He referred back to concerns about Capita's Civil Service Pension Scheme performance raised on 26 October. Ian Lavery described the Synergy contract as worth £900 million in debate. For the published maximum contract value, use the business case above.
- In the same debate, minister Lilian Greenwood said that switching course during the transition would cause disruption and add costs. She described separate technology and transactional processing contracts, said the existing SSCL contracts ran until October 2028, and said the programme would go live once testing was assured.
- On 24 November 2026, in the Commons Civil Service Pension Scheme debate, McDonald asked whether an insourcing unit could examine both the pensions scheme and Synergy.

The archive's 22 June 2023 alias match concerns teacher training for dyslexia and autism. It is a different programme with the same name.
